## 0. Core, one line

Rightness is not a property to be located but a **test**: whether a commitment's fit to its relational field survives the transformations its claim ranges over. The test runs in three registers over fibers made of different material, and the registers determine what an embedded observer can be licensed to assert.

## 2. The test: transport and fibers

**Load-bearing.** RaDRCD §5 asks whether judging after localization agrees with localizing a richer judgment:

```
K_s ∘ p_s  =?  r_s ∘ K_X
```

The square commutes exactly when the restricted judgment is constant on every fiber of the localization. The fiber is everything the slice cannot tell apart.

This cashes out the employer case cleanly. "Treats employee E well under relations R" holds across the whole fiber, so it travels. "Good company" varies across the fiber (the fiber contains clean firms and polluting ones), so it does not. A claim moves from scope *s* to a wider *s'* only if it is invariant across what the localization discarded.

The skeleton of this is information-partition knowledge from epistemic logic (Aumann; Fagin, Halpern, Moses, Vardi). It is not new, which means it is well founded. The novelty lives in the joints, not the skeleton.

**Warrant parts from knowledge at the stakes profile.** Knowledge requires the *claim* to be fiber-invariant. Warrant requires the *action* to be robust on the fiber under a stakes profile. The organism does not know the apple is dangerous, because that claim is not fiber-invariant, but rejecting it is robust across the fiber under asymmetric loss. RaDRCD's three-way split (locally warranted, factually mistaken, pragmatically appropriate) gets a mechanism rather than a description.

**What follows rather than being stipulated:**

- Burden scales with scope. Wider scope means bigger fibers and fatter tails. RaDRCD §6 asserted this; here it is derived.
- Participation is epistemic, not merely procedural. Pooling localizations refines the partition (distributed knowledge). The neighbor's slice is the only one that cuts the fiber along the pollution direction.
- Remainder is the residual fiber after every available localization has been pooled. Future populations define directions no one present can cut.
- Capture has a signature: the engineered fattening of someone else's fiber along verdict-relevant directions.
- Remainder is not obstruction. An empty intersection is genuine conflict; a non-singleton intersection is underdetermination. Different responses, kept apart by the model.

## 6. Three registers

Sol's three regions are right in direction. The correction is that typed operators K^S, K^E, K^N read as a partition, which is the isolation the framework rejects.

**Register is an index on the closure, not a type of the closer.** It joins FISSR. A verdict carries its register as it carries its slice, and cross-register substitution becomes an undeclared index change rather than a lane violation. *Load-bearing in form; whether register is fully a FISSR dimension or only behaves like one is open.*

**What makes the registers genuinely distinct is what the fiber is made of:**

| Register | Evaluand | Fiber over | Occlusion type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural | actual configuration | actual states | referent can answer back |
| Epistemic | grounds-to-closure relation | what this observer could not distinguish | occluded from here, observable in principle |
| Normative | commitment among feasible alternatives | counterfactuals | never realized; unobservable in principle |

Actual, then occluded-but-actual, then counterfactual. Each register adds an occlusion type and keeps the prior ones, so they **nest rather than partition**. Coupling is constitutive. This also explains the difficulty ordering without appeal to mystery: normative questions are harder because their fibers are made of things that never happened.

**Candidate criterion for the board's column split: does the loop reach the referent?** In the structural register the loops feed the judge's next configuration but not the property judged; physics does not read verdicts. In the normative register the field partly consists of prior verdicts, so judging remakes it (Hacking's looping effects arrived at the same mechanism independently). The third column is the hinge: negotiable in origin, near-mechanical in force once signed. RL's stale assignment lives there. *Speculative; probably a gradient rather than a binary.*

**Consequence, close to load-bearing.** The negotiable register sits on a non-negotiable substrate. What is negotiated is ranking, routing, and signing. What is not negotiated is where consequences land: consequence-receipt as nutrition, work, pollution, or waste is a boundary fact (FO, BD), and collapsing maintained asymmetries is relational annihilation (AMM). Morality is negotiable routing over a non-negotiable substrate. That is the framework's answer to relativism.

## 7. Licensing: what any framework may assert

**The dominance/optimality asymmetry.** Dominance is existential: one admissible alternative that improves relevant conditions without corresponding loss is a witness, and exhibiting it settles the claim. Optimality is universal: it quantifies over the whole feasible set. In fiber terms, dominance needs one cut; optimality needs the fiber fully resolved. MPDC forbids the second and permits the first.

**The zone derivation.** Zone is a function of relational density, not distance from the referent, so structural questions about Zone 3 systems are also irreducible. The correct claim: **register determines the minimum zone the evaluation inherits, because register determines what the fiber is made of.** The feasible-alternatives set is not given anywhere; it is generated by the system running. By RCP that is Zone 3 by construction whenever the configuration contains Zone 3 participants, which is any configuration containing people.

**EDF does the work.** For any Zone 3 property P, external determination fails: running S makes the determiner non-external, shortcuts are foreclosed, and operating on the coupling conditions rather than determines. Substitute P as the right configuration and S as the relational field. Objective rightness is a verdict from outside the field, so it requires external determination of a Zone 3 property, and EDF forecloses every route. EDF's failure modes then predict how objective moral claims go wrong, and its canonical case (empire generating meaning′ and supplying it under the name meaning) is structurally what an imposed universal ethic is.

What EDF leaves open is route (a): you can run S. You cannot calculate P but you can participate in producing it. That is why participatory localization (RL §3b, PL) is load-bearing rather than procedural.

**RRL gives the quantitative form.** R · C_eff ≤ R_max, with EUP's B · R ≤ K the same budget on a different axis. Normative claims raise C_eff and B while R_max is fixed by substrate, so available resolution falls as scope rises. The lawful responses under RRL pressure are already written: slow, coarsen, expand capacity, or classify remainder as unresolved. Raising rhetorical certainty instead is compression collapse. **Universal moral assertion is that failure mode at maximum scope, diagnosable by the framework's own inequality.**

**The reformulation.** From "what is right" (quantifies over an unenumerable space) to "is this right" (evaluates a presented candidate, which arrives with its own trace, scope, and affected field). Search becomes verification. This is the same asymmetry as dominance versus optimality, and the candidate is witness-shaped. The certificate-checking parallel is suggestive, not a result.

**Robust to the metaphysical question.** Under Ontological Containment the limit is epistemic: a fact may exist that no embedded observer can reach. Under Transformative Complexity the decidability criteria themselves migrate, so there may be no stable fact. The license is identical either way. A metaphysical register is coherent but **inert**: its verdicts have zero transport into the other three.

**The standing limit, proposed for the corpus:**

> E² is licensed to say: this is dominated, this violates a floor, this launders across registers, this closure exceeds its warrant at the scope it claims. E² is not licensed to say: this is the right configuration. The second is not an unfinished section. It is a derived limit that applies to every framework, including this one.

## 8. Where foundation still bites

**The floors.** "Is this right" needs a comparison class and an inadmissibility test. The floors are derived, not assumed: RBoR from the six primitives, the primitives from the relational categories over matter, energy, and spacetime, with epistemic limits acknowledged throughout. What the derivation establishes is **structural necessity**: violating a primitive does not break a rule, it breaks the entity. The RBoR holds that line deliberately, in engineering-specification language (exploitation as topology failure where energy out exceeds energy in; violating systems as thermodynamically unsustainable rather than evil; floor rather than ceiling).

The one register crossing in the chain is therefore narrow and visible: from *structurally necessary for coherence* to *normatively inadmissible*. This is the M-axis transport of §4. In bare form the bridge yields a hypothetical (if this entity is to persist as a coherent entity, do not violate the conditions of its coherence), which is fully licensed and makes violations diagnosable rather than merely deplorable. What it does not yield alone is why that persistence binds an agent indifferent to it. RaDRCD §1 blocks the shortcut from the other direction by denying that stability alone establishes rightness, and §9's warning about normative results built into the dynamics and then discovered applies reflexively here.

**AMM supplies the candidate bridge, and it is strong precisely because it does not require that persistence be good.** Collapsing a system's capacity to maintain its asymmetries forecloses the conditions under which any adjudication can occur, including the adjudication by which the violator would justify the act. Condition-of-possibility rather than persistence-preference, so it reaches the indifferent agent through that agent's own standing to close. The floor is not "objectively wrong" but "forecloses the conditions under which the question can be asked." It licenses floors and nothing above them, which matches the RBoR's own floor-not-ceiling scoping. *Strong candidate, not load-bearing; transcendental arguments historically prove slightly less than they appear to.*

**Two unflagged crossings in the RBoR itself**, recorded because the corpus applies its standards reflexively. "A system is only ethical if it maximizes the capacity of its agents to define their own GCOs" and "inalienable right" are normative-register sentences inside a structurally derived document: transported rather than derived, and marking them as such strengthens the document. A milder case: falsifiability is offered as an advantage over traditional ethical frameworks, but it is a structural-register virtue, and a rival framework could accept every measurement while contesting what follows from it.

**Reflexivity.** "No observer is licensed to assert objective rightness" is itself a universal claim, and MPDC arguably falls under its own scope. The move that prevents self-defeat is not a fix but honesty about register: the claim sits in the structural and epistemic registers, is conditional on RCP and MPDC, and is closure under declared dependence. It is defeasible. Stated as frame-free it would be the exact laundering it prohibits.

**The stakes profile.** Two independent methods left the same residue. The fiber machinery cannot generate mattering; the six primitives cannot either. AMM locates it: to rank is to make and hold a difference, and non-equivalence enters where a configuration collapses asymmetries a system is paying to maintain. This is where rightness touches the framework's grounding in persistence. The interest-frustration instrument is the nearest node aimed at it.
