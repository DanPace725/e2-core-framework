# RaDRCD: mathematical and structural assessment

2026-09-11 · Exploratory assessment, not an integration or promotion into Core.

**The closest mathematical object is an adaptive hybrid network of situated evaluators, equipped with local-to-global compatibility conditions and a partially ordered space of admissible commitments.** This is a proposed composition of established formalisms, not the name of an already established theory of rightness. Its most promising ingredients are **hybrid dynamical systems**, **sheaves and valuation algebras**, **viability theory**, and **defeasible revision**.

RaDRCD appears to be approaching the relationship between **how a field becomes committed** and **what licenses that commitment across its affected field**. Those are coupled problems with different mathematical signatures. Dynamics explains production and persistence; compatibility tests whether situated descriptions can coexist; admissibility determines which commitments remain available under declared requirements. None of these, alone or together, supplies its own normative authority.

The most consequential internal ancestor is **Relational Localization**, alongside GCO, CFA/CFAR, and TCL. The most directly relevant external paper is Hansen and Ghrist's *Opinion Dynamics on Discourse Sheaves*. The less obvious neighboring formalism is **valuation algebra**, which can make the gluing problem concrete before introducing topology.

## 1. What the model must preserve

The [RaDRCD proposal](RaDRCD.md), especially §§2–12, imposes several requirements that rule out easy reductions:

| Requirement | Mathematical consequence |
|---|---|
| Configuration, closure, verdict, and adequacy differ | Separate world state, commitment transition, deposited representation, and assessment relation |
| Judgments accumulate, settle, and reopen | Continuous evolution plus discrete events, with memory |
| Grounds are observer-indexed | Observation and translation maps must be explicit |
| Wider warrant does not follow from local warrant | Extension requires additional conditions; it is not automatic transport |
| Evaluative facets interact | Coupled relations or dynamics; no default separability |
| Some failures disqualify rather than subtract points | Feasibility constraints, vetoes, or admissibility predicates |
| Some alternatives remain incomparable | A partial order, preorder, or set-valued choice correspondence |
| Verdicts change subsequent configurations | Feedback into material conditions, memory, and future decision rules |
| Legitimate closure need not exhaust uncertainty | Commitment records can preserve unresolved alternatives and revision triggers |

There is a useful correction to the proposal's own mathematical gesture. Writing \(\mathbf{x}=(J,W,C,L,F,\ldots)\) already suggests that justice, warrant, correctness, legitimacy, and fittingness are quantities of a common representational kind. Their *coupling* does not establish that. Warrant may be a relation between grounds and a claim; legitimacy a predicate on a procedure; correctness a relation between a proposition and its referent. These can interact without becoming coordinates in one numerical vector.

A typed configuration is therefore the safer starting object. Numerical state variables can represent particular operationalizations once their meaning, comparisons, and dynamics have been specified. “Non-scalar” should protect heterogeneous structure rather than merely replace one unexplained number with six.

The relevant internal sequence is thus not just **CFA → TCL → normative dynamics**. It is also **localization → licensing → closure → trace → consequence → re-localization**. RaDRCD makes that second sequence applicable to judgment more generally.

Relational Localization already explicitly entertains sheaf machinery and marks it as unresolved. A repository search found the same lineage in its June 12 staged source and integration review. The gluing suggestion therefore has established internal provenance; it is not being imported solely because RaDRCD uses the word “bundle.”

### Three inherited distinctions that need repair before formalization

**Commitment is not automatically a mathematical closure operator.** GCO invokes extensiveness, monotonicity, and idempotence. Those properties require a specified ordered domain. One can order states by accumulated determination, or order sets of remaining possibilities by reverse inclusion, but the order must be supplied. RaDRCD's withdrawal of an earlier verdict need not be monotone in accepted propositions or remaining possibilities. A workable separation is: derive consequences under fixed premises with a closure operator; revise premises with a different operation; enact commitment with a transition. Calling all three “closure” is conceptually compressive but mathematically consequential.

**An interface operator is not a topological boundary.** Boundary Dynamics' active map between relational configurations is neither automatically the boundary \(\partial U\) of a subset nor the boundary operator of a chain complex. Recursive interfaces do not imply \(\partial^2=0\). For RaDRCD, “interface,” “gate,” or a typed transition map is initially more exact. Topological language becomes earned when the underlying spaces and maps are defined.

**The TCL numbers cannot presently serve as universal normative thresholds.** Boundary Dynamics §8 promotes approximately 0.757, 0.930, and 0.289 into substrate-general boundary limits. The TCL source explicitly makes the first two parameter-dependent and restricts its stronger claim for the third to its landscape class, leaving broader generalization open. The numerical research was not independently rerun for this assessment. Its reported simulation agreement supports a research lead within the specified model; it does not calibrate warrant, reciprocity, institutional legitimacy, or every boundary. Even the cubic geometry requires precision: for \(V(x)=x^4/4-x^2/2\), \(x=\pm1/\sqrt3\) are zero-curvature locations, whereas the unforced potential's hilltop is at zero. A fold of equilibria is identified in a parameterized family, not by calling all of these the same point.

## 3. The strongest dynamical fit: hybrid systems

RaDRCD describes gradual changes in evidence, attention, and pressure, followed by an event that changes what the system is committed to doing or asserting. Hybrid systems explicitly combine continuous flows with discrete jumps and mode changes. Their flow sets, jump sets, and reset maps give the proposal a more specific home than “nonlinear dynamics.” [Sanfelice, Goebel, and Teel, *Generalized Solutions to Hybrid Dynamical Systems*](https://webpages.math.luc.edu/~rgoebel1/publications/journals/SanfeliceGoebelTeel08ESAIMCOCV.pdf).

For a proposed local model, let \(z_i=(x_i,m_i,q_i)\): evaluative state, memory, and commitment mode of evaluator \(i\). Then:

\[
\dot z_i\in F_i(z_i,y_i,\{T_{ji}z_j\},a_i),\qquad z_i\in\mathcal C_i,
\]
\[
z_i^+\in G_i(z_i,y_i),\qquad z_i\in\mathcal D_i,
\qquad v_i=\operatorname{readout}_i(z_i^+).
\]

Here \(y_i\) is locally available information, \(T_{ji}\) translates incoming signals, and \(a_i\) represents allocated resources or interventions. Continuous flow leaves the discrete mode unchanged; a jump can change mode, memory, and verdict. Sets of possible updates allow underdetermination without arbitrarily selecting a unique output. This is a modeling template, not a fitted law.

The distinction between \(\mathcal D_i\) and a **licensing condition** is essential. The former says when closure can occur in the modeled system. The latter says when closure is warranted under declared criteria. Fear, propaganda, fatigue, or coercion can cause the former without satisfying the latter. An adequate model must be able to generate an unwarranted closure; otherwise it has defined the central failure out of existence.

Hysteresis requires even less machinery. Suppose a commitment turns on when support exceeds \(\theta_{\mathrm{on}}\), but turns off only below \(\theta_{\mathrm{off}}<\theta_{\mathrm{on}}\). Identical present support can then sustain different verdicts because the mode records history. This represents stickiness without identifying stickiness with correctness. Reopening is another state transition, not necessarily a return to the original configuration: memory, reputation, and consequences remain.

TCL can contribute slow variables that modify the fast flow or its thresholds. MMPS can contribute resource stocks that make prolonged suspension and repeated revision costly. Neither requires justice itself to have a metabolic unit.

**Bifurcation theory is a sharper but narrower option.** A fold can describe the disappearance of a stable judgment regime under changing parameters. Threshold crossing, however, can also result from a decision rule or noise-driven escape without any bifurcation. The proposal would need evidence of changing stability structure before “catastrophe” or a particular normal form becomes more than a candidate. [Kuznetsov, *Saddle-node Bifurcation*](https://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Saddle-node_bifurcation).

There is also no reason yet to assume one global potential whose descent produces all judgment dynamics. Coupling does not imply a gradient flow. Cycles, asymmetric influence, delay, and ongoing inputs can matter. An attractive landscape is a useful special case, not a consequence of the word “rightness.”

## 4. The strongest distributed fit: sheaves, with valuation algebras underneath

### Local descriptions need not become identical

A sheaf organizes descriptions over local domains together with restriction maps to smaller domains. Compatible descriptions over a cover glue uniquely into a description over its union. The compatibility is agreement where the descriptions overlap, not identity of everything each description contains. This distinction is exactly what ordinary consensus pictures often lose. [Fong and Spivak, *Seven Sketches in Compositionality*, §7.3](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Applied_Mathematics/Seven_Sketches_in_Compositionality:_An_Invitation_to_Applied_Category_Theory_(Fong_and_Spivak)/07:_Logic_of_Behavior_-_Sheaves_Toposes_Languages/7.03:_Sheaves).

For the proposed application, domains should identify **which claims, relations, times, and affected parties a description concerns**. They should not simply be named after observers. Two observers can inspect the same domain, and one observer can inspect several domains. A set of scopes with inclusion maps is sufficient to begin; a smooth space is unnecessary.

RaDRCD's employer example is illuminating precisely because its judgments may be compatible. “Pays employees well” and “pollutes neighboring water” can both belong to one wider description. The error arises when either local description is promoted into an unqualified verdict that discards the other. This is loss during evaluative compression, not automatically an obstruction to gluing.

### The particularly close precedent: discourse sheaves

Hansen and Ghrist give network nodes their own opinion spaces and maps into shared edge spaces. In their linear setting, compatibility is encoded by

\[
(\delta x)_e=T_{i\to e}x_i-T_{j\to e}x_j,
\qquad H^0=\ker\delta,
\]

and diffusion can evolve both opinions and communication maps. Their paper even examines adaptation that learns to lie: reducing expressed disagreement need not improve truthfulness. This makes it an unusually close formal neighbor to RaDRCD, including its distinction between stabilization and adequacy. [Hansen and Ghrist, *Opinion Dynamics on Discourse Sheaves*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.12798), §§2–4, 8–9.

The proposed E² extension would attach provenance, grounds, scope, and affectedness to these local records, and constrain which translations and updates are legitimate. A regulator's map into a shared discussion could omit an unmeasured consequence. An employer's map could advertise generosity while hiding pollution. Both can reduce visible disagreement. Therefore **the maps themselves must remain objects of assessment**. Consensus obtained by deforming what may be expressed can be a mechanism of capture.

Do not interpret \(\|\delta x\|\) as a moral error score. It measures inconsistency in a chosen linear encoding. Ordinary vector-space sheaves always have the zero global section; “no global section” requires a more specific claim, such as no section extending prescribed local data, or none satisfying additional affine or nonlinear constraints. Remainder is not automatically a cohomology class.

### The less obvious fit: valuation algebras

Valuation algebras organize information by domain and provide operations for combining information and focusing it onto smaller domains. Abramsky and Carù connect this framework to contextuality and distinguish forms of disagreement among information sources. This brings constraint satisfaction, relational data, and local-to-global reasoning into one established mathematical neighborhood. [Abramsky and Carù, *Non-locality, Contextuality and Valuation Algebras: A General Theory of Disagreement*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.03521).

An initial RaDRCD model could use the particularly transparent case of **sets of admissible assignments**. Each local record permits some assignments to named variables. Combining records joins them on shared variables; focusing projects onto a subset. This needs neither invented distances between values nor a premature manifold.

For example, take three binary decisions \(a,b,c\), with local requirements:

\[
a=b,\qquad b=c,\qquad a\ne c.
\]

Every local requirement is satisfiable, and their projections onto individual shared variables agree: each permits both 0 and 1. Nevertheless, there is no joint assignment satisfying all three. The first two imply \(a=c\), contradicting the third. This is a concrete obstruction to joint satisfaction. Calling it “everyone sees things differently” would miss the structure.

Conversely, multiple joint assignments would indicate underdetermination; incompatible reports about one shared variable would indicate direct local conflict. These cases demand different responses. The model should preserve that distinction before attaching a single word such as “tension.”

The contextuality literature supplies formal precedents for local compatibility without an appropriate global extension. It does not establish that normative disagreement is quantum, that every conflict has this form, or that a failed gluing identifies which commitment should be relinquished. [Abramsky and Brandenburger, *The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264).

### Warrant creates a nontrivial limit on the sheaf analogy

The proposal says warrant can disappear when the slice widens. That is compatible with an extension problem: a local record need not extend globally. But warrant cannot simply be assumed to satisfy sheaf axioms. Restricting a warranted global conclusion can remove the premises that warranted it; new evidence can defeat rather than merely enlarge an earlier assessment. Restriction must preserve the dependency record, or warrant must be re-evaluated.

Start with a diagram of typed descriptions and **explicitly test** whether restrictions compose and compatible records glue. Use “presheaf” only when its functorial conditions hold, and “sheaf” only when the gluing conditions hold. If translations are lossy, contested, or route-dependent, a more general diagram of maps or relations may be the correct object. The mathematical name should follow the behavior.

## 5. A central structural hypothesis: localization and judgment need not commute

RaDRCD's strongest geometric intuition may concern the failure of an operation to commute with a change of view.

Let \(X\) be a richer configuration, \(p_s:X\to X_s\) a declared localization, \(K_X:X\to\mathcal V_X\) and \(K_s:X_s\to\mathcal V_s\) evaluative maps, and \(r_s:\mathcal V_X\to\mathcal V_s\) a justified restriction of verdict records. Then one can ask whether

\[
K_s\circ p_s\;=\;r_s\circ K_X.
\]

In words: does judging after localization agree with localizing a richer judgment? RaDRCD suggests that frequently it does not. Different available grounds, consequences, stakes, or thresholds can change the judgment. This is a **proposed diagnostic**, not a theorem inferred from embeddedness. \(X\) can be a finite reference configuration, not an omniscient world state; each composition must first be well typed.

Failure of commutation is not automatically error. Rapid food avoidance and later laboratory classification answer different practical questions. If their verdicts do not have a common restrictable meaning, the comparison is ill-typed rather than noncommuting. Where meanings do match, a discrepancy can identify a transport obligation: which premise, distinction, or threshold was lost?

The constructive counterpart is demanding: identify the transformations under which an evaluative relation **does** survive. That is where a real invariant of rightness could begin to emerge. A transportable invariant would be more informative than an elaborate space in which everything depends on everything.

This is the part most worth pursuing geometrically: **invariance and failure of invariance under declared changes of scope, resolution, representation, and history**. Calling it curvature or holonomy would require additional structure, including a defined transport law. At present, the commuting-diagram question is sufficient.

## 6. Admissibility without a universal scalar: viability and order

Viability theory studies whether trajectories can remain within a constraint set. Its viability kernel consists of starting states from which some permitted evolution can continue satisfying those constraints. It distinguishes being admissible now from retaining an admissible future. [Aubin, *A Survey of Viability Theory*](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0328044).

For RaDRCD, specify a declared requirement set \(\mathcal A\), dynamics, controls, and time horizon. Then ask whether a proposed commitment preserves feasible continuations, including maintenance and the capacity to contest or revise. A presently attractive institutional decision could eliminate every future path satisfying those requirements. The claim is conditional on \(\mathcal A\); it does not derive \(\mathcal A\) from persistence.

“Some trajectory remains viable” is weaker than “all relevant disturbances can be handled.” An application must specify uncertainty and who controls what. Otherwise a favorable hypothetical continuation can masquerade as robust permission.

Comparisons among admissible alternatives can remain partial. Let \(a\succeq_k b\) mean that \(a\) is no worse than \(b\) under declared criterion \(k\). Componentwise dominance requires this for every relevant criterion, with strict improvement for at least one. This is the familiar vector/Pareto-order idea; it does not require statistically independent criteria or a weighted sum. [Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, §4.7](https://www.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf).

For the proposed application, criteria can be ordinal or relational rather than numerical. With preordered criteria the induced relation may itself be only a preorder; a partial order requires antisymmetry, possibly after identifying equivalent alternatives. If actual judgments are cyclic, even a partial order is an empirical or procedural idealization rather than a discovered fact.

This produces three different outputs: **inadmissible**, **dominated**, and **non-dominated but unresolved**. The last is not a failure to calculate hard enough. Choosing within it may require a legitimate procedure or a substantive commitment the evidence does not uniquely determine. Preserving plurality need not mean refusing action.

## 7. Revision supplies a missing logical layer

Reopening a verdict is not just adding another variable. The system may need to withdraw accepted claims, preserve their provenance, and revise dependencies. AGM belief-change theory is an established precedent for separating expansion, contraction, and revision rather than treating every update as accumulation. [Alchourrón, Gärdenfors, and Makinson, *On the Logic of Theory Change* (1985), author's publication record](https://sites.google.com/site/davidcmakinson/).

Its relevance here is architectural, not an instruction to impose all AGM postulates on every evaluator. An affective system, a legal procedure, and a scientific claim registry do not have the same representational structure. RaDRCD needs a typed revision operation appropriate to each, plus a record of what the earlier verdict caused.

The archive can grow monotonically while the set of currently endorsed propositions changes nonmonotonically. That distinction offers a precise reading of “the verdict becomes part of the next configuration”: it remains historical terrain without remaining authoritative truth.

Adequacy also needs a sharper target than a single observed outcome. A bad outcome does not by itself prove that a probabilistic decision was unsound, and a lucky outcome does not establish adequate reasoning. In the apple case, distinguish the factual claim that the apple is safe, the action rule under uncertainty, and the eventual consequence. They can come apart exactly as configuration, warrant, and verdict do.

## 8. Other mathematical candidates, and when they would earn priority

| Candidate | What it could contribute | Why it is not the initial answer |
|---|---|---|
| Smooth manifold | Local coordinates and coordinate transformations for evaluative states | The proposal has not specified smooth structure or even homogeneous variable types; local perspective alone does not imply a manifold |
| Fiber bundle | Context-indexed state spaces with controlled local equivalence | Different contexts may have different dimensions and noninvertible translations; local triviality would be an extra assumption |
| Stratified space | Distinct regimes joined along specified incidence relations | Biological, institutional, and cultural levels are not automatically mathematical strata; hybrid modes are the cheaper first representation |
| Bifurcation/catastrophe theory | Qualitative changes in stability and discontinuous regime selection | Explains some transitions, not distributed warrant, participation, or adequacy |
| Information geometry | Comparison of probabilistic belief states under a specified statistical model | Warrant and justice are not presently probability distributions; a metric would need a reason |
| Game theory | Strategic influence, bargaining, incentives, institutional equilibria | An equilibrium can stabilize exploitation; it would model an important mechanism rather than license the result |
| Category theory of open systems | Composing interacting systems through typed interfaces | Useful when interface composition is the actual problem; general categorical vocabulary alone does not select the dynamics or ethics |

The open-systems route has a concrete precedent in Baez and Pollard's composition of open reaction networks and their associated dynamical systems. This is relevant to Boundary Dynamics and MMPS because it treats interfaces as part of compositional machinery. It does not establish that meaning or justice obey chemical conservation laws. [Baez and Pollard, *A Compositional Framework for Reaction Networks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02051).

The corpus's [CT translation of RPs](../../E2Core/Context%20Layer/CT%20translation%20of%20RPs.ormd) shows an existing interest in this route. Its claim that a six-way categorical correspondence proves primitive completeness should not be inherited as a theorem. Objects, arrows, monoidal structures, limits, monads, and functors are not a unique exhaustive six-part basis of category theory; several are additional structures a category need not possess. A meaningful translation should identify what is preserved and what follows from it.

## 9. A minimal composed model

The following architecture would preserve the proposal's distinctions while leaving substantial freedom in implementation:

```text
Relational configuration and typed consequences
                     |
           situated observation maps
                     |
    local grounds, memory, and available actions
                     |
         hybrid commitment dynamics
                     |
   verdict + scope + provenance + unresolved remainder
                     |
      compatibility and admissibility assessment
                     |
       retain / qualify / act / suspend / reopen
                     |
      altered configuration, memory, and interfaces
```

The diagram shows dependencies, not a mandatory serial workflow. Licensing may constrain an event before it happens, while later assessment may expose a failure. Some systems act before an explicit warrant assessment exists. All assessment remains situated; the modeler's comparison frame is declared rather than silently placed outside the world.

There should be at least two distinguishable relational structures: **who can communicate with whom** and **who affects or bears consequences for whom**. They may overlap, but a future population or excluded community can be present in the consequence model while absent from the conversation. A perfectly compatible discourse among current participants can therefore remain inadequate to the affected configuration. This is where Consequence Routing adds something an opinion-dynamics model alone does not provide.

The resulting object can be described provisionally as **distributed commitment dynamics under indexed grounds, compatibility conditions, and revisable admissibility constraints**. “Rightness” names the substantive question asked of that architecture, not its fixed point, energy minimum, global section, or viable kernel.

## 10. What would make this more than a correspondence exercise

The next useful artifact would be a small formal example, not a universal geometry. It should define its local scopes, records, admissibility requirements, update rules, and consequence model explicitly, then establish a few discriminating results:

1. **Compatible difference:** well-treated employees and harmed neighbors are represented together without averaging away either description.
2. **Local satisfaction without joint satisfaction:** the three binary constraints above produce an empty joint solution, while a relaxed variant produces several solutions.
3. **Closure without warrant:** altered pressure or attention triggers a commitment while its declared licensing condition remains false.
4. **Hysteresis:** the same present evidence produces different commitments under different histories; revision remains possible.
5. **Capture through translation:** changing what an interface reports reduces visible disagreement while leaving an adverse consequence unchanged.
6. **Action without total ranking:** two admissible alternatives remain incomparable, but a declared decision procedure can choose while preserving the unresolved tradeoff.
7. **Transport failure and repair:** a local verdict fails in a wider declared scope; adding the missing dependency or narrowing the claim restores a justified relation between the two.

The formalism earns value if it distinguishes these cases and identifies the assumptions responsible. It loses value if every outcome can be redescribed as coherence, every incompatibility is called topology, or the desired normative result has already been built into the dynamics and then “discovered.”

My assessment is that the proposal's strongest direction is **local-to-global admissibility coupled to revisable commitment dynamics**. The attractive basins explain why a judgment holds. The compatibility structure explains whether its situated grounds can travel. The constraint and order structure explains what remains selectable. The unresolved normative work concerns which constraints, participants, and revisions are warranted—and must remain visible as work, rather than disappearing inside the geometry.
