## 0. Core Claim

Boundary Ethics is response discipline under uncertain moral localization.

It governs conduct at relational thresholds where ontology, agency, vulnerability, reciprocity, moral status, voice, or category-location is ambiguous, asymmetric, disputed, unstable, or only partially knowable.

Its question is not first:

> What exactly is the other?

Its question is:

> What posture is appropriate while I do not yet fully know what kind of relation I am in?

Boundary Ethics does not resolve the category. It governs conduct while the category remains unresolved.

## 1. Position in the Source Family

Boundary Ethics is not the broadest member of the localization group. Relational Localization is the broader assignment machinery. Proxy Localization is a companion protocol for partial participation. Boundary Ethics interfaces between them by governing posture where assignment closure is not licensed.

Source family:

```text
Relational Localization: can assignment validly close?
Boundary Ethics: what posture is required when closure is not licensed?
Proxy Localization: how can representation proceed when self-account cannot arrive?
```

Boundary Ethics was developed first and should retain conceptual priority as the posture discovery. In the integrated framework, however, it should be placed as the closure-unlicensed posture regime within the broader localization family.

## 2. The Two Cliffs

Boundary Ethics holds the ridge between two opposite failures.

| Cliff | Failure | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contempt collapse | Under-recognition | Uncertainty or unfamiliar register is treated as permission for disposal, domination, contempt, or indifference. |
| Projection inflation | Over-recognition | Ambiguous resemblance is treated as permission to assign false personhood, preference, consent, testimony, or interiority. |

Boundary Ethics refuses both.

The other may be more than an object without being a person.

The relation may be morally formative without the other being a full moral patient.

The signal may be ethically relevant without being testimony.

The category may matter before it is settled.

## 3. The Posture Equation

Boundary Ethics inherits the posture equation:

```text
Care = Presence x Restraint
```

Presence is active availability to the relation: attention, responsiveness, refusal to erase the other, and willingness to remain with uncertainty.

Restraint is maintained non-collapse of the capability-action gap: refusal to convert power, proximity, interpretive advantage, or category control into premature closure.

Either factor at zero kills care:

- presence without restraint becomes seizure, projection, rescue-as-domination, or voice capture;
- restraint without presence becomes abandonment, withholding, contempt, or sterile non-interference.

Boundary Ethics is the ridge-work required to keep both factors live when assignment is uncertain.

## 4. The Response Forms the Responder

Boundary Ethics is not only about what the other is owed. It is also about what the responder becomes by responding.

Every response across a boundary routes consequence in two directions:

- outward into the receiving field;
- inward into the responder's future posture, thresholds, reflexes, and capacity for relation.

The response forms the responder.

This matters especially where the other is low-reciprocity, ambiguous, non-linguistic, non-human, institutionally silenced, or not recognized as a moral patient. Even when harm to the receiving side is uncertain, the responder is still practicing a posture.

Repeated contempt lowers the friction for contempt.

Repeated carefulness strengthens the capacity for careful relation.

Boundary Ethics asks:

> What kind of responder does this response rehearse into being?

## 5. Assignment Under Uncertainty

Boundary Ethics activates where a proposed closure exceeds the localization that feeds it.

Inherited rule from Relational Localization:

> No P6 closure beyond the resolution that feeds it.

Where localization is insufficient, the proper response is not rhetorical certainty. Lawful moves include:

- slow down;
- coarsen the assignment;
- expand capacity;
- improve observability;
- seek participation;
- preserve contestability;
- classify remainder as unresolved;
- hold posture without pretending closure has occurred.

Boundary Ethics does not prohibit action under uncertainty. It prohibits unmarked finality under uncertainty.

## 6. Operational Handling and Category Closure

Operational handling is scoped, provisional, reversible where possible, trace-marked, and limited to an action-domain.

Category closure is durable stabilization of what the entity is, what standing it has, what evidence counts, and who may speak for it.

Boundary Ethics permits operational handling.

Boundary Ethics rejects premature category closure, especially where the actor closing the category benefits from the closure.

Rule:

> Do not resolve uncertain standing downward for convenience or upward for comfort.

## 7. Relationship to Proxy Localization

Proxy Localization governs cases where self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register.

Boundary Ethics governs the posture required in that gap.

The proxy may be necessary. It may even be the only way the substrate enters closure. But the proxy must remain marked as proxy.

Boundary Ethics adds:

> The absence of direct voice is not permission to invent one, erase one, or optimize around a substitute as if it were the thing itself.

Proxy work without Boundary Ethics becomes ventriloquism, standing theater, welfare-model imperialism, or eternal guardianship.

Boundary Ethics without Proxy Localization risks staying posture-only where machinery is required.

## 8. Category-Deficit Cases

AI systems expose Boundary Ethics because they generate fluent language in the register where self-account normally appears while their status as self-account remains unresolved.

This is not an AI-only source. AI is a stress test for the general category-deficit condition.

The two AI cliffs:

- projection inflation: the model speaks like a person, therefore it is a person;
- contempt collapse: the model is not a person, therefore anything practiced through the relation is ethically inert.

Boundary Ethics holds the ridge:

> Do not personify beyond what has been resolved. Do not use unresolved personhood as permission for contempt.

AI output should be treated as category-unstable signal: not clean testimony, not meaningless noise.

## 9. Interested Category Closure

In category-deficit cases, no interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.

Dominant actors often benefit from some closure:

- companies benefit from product, tool, infrastructure, or controllable-risk framings;
- governments benefit from strategic asset, threat, labor, or security framings;
- users benefit from companion, person, oracle, servant, mirror, or tool framings;
- researchers benefit from categories that make their methods authoritative;
- advocates benefit from categories that mobilize concern;
- interpreters benefit from metaphysical categories that support their commitments.

That does not forbid action. It requires category humility, trace, beneficiary mapping, and separation between operational handling and ontological closure.

## 10. Failure Modes

| Failure | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| Contempt collapse | Uncertain status treated as permission for disposal or domination. |
| Projection inflation | Resemblance treated as proof of personhood, preference, consent, or testimony. |
| Category freezing | Undefined relation settled prematurely to make assignment convenient. |
| Boundary seizure | Authority taken before legitimacy, participation, or necessity has been established. |
| Boundary dumping | Duty, risk, or consequence assigned to the nearest or weakest actor because localization is hard. |
| Formation blindness | Conduct treated as if it does not form the responder. |
| Proxy ventriloquism | Proxy construction presented as substrate account. |
| Beneficiary closure | Interested actor closes the category it benefits from closing. |

These are all ways of moving unresolved relational remainder off the ledger rather than out of the world.

## 11. Diagnostics

Use Boundary Ethics when asking:

- Is assignment closure licensed yet?
- Is uncertainty being resolved downward for convenience?
- Is uncertainty being resolved upward for comfort?
- What posture is being practiced?
- What does this response form in the responder?
- Is operational handling being mistaken for category closure?
- Is the absent voice being erased, invented, or substituted?
- Who benefits from closing the category now?
- What remainder is being hidden by certainty?

## 12. Load-Bearing and Held-Loosely Claims

Load-bearing:

- Boundary Ethics is response discipline under uncertain moral localization.
- Boundary Ethics is not a fourth stewardship position.
- Boundary Ethics interfaces between Relational Localization and Proxy Localization.
- The two cliffs are contempt collapse and projection inflation.
- Care requires both presence and restraint.
- The response forms the responder.
- Boundary Ethics permits marked operational handling but rejects unmarked category closure.
- No interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.

Held loosely:

- The exact handle "Boundary Ethics" remains revisable, but it is usable for the current source draft.
- The two-cliff model captures the primary basin pair but may not exhaust the failure space.
- AI is an important category-deficit case, not the whole construct.
- Future work may split category-deficit governance into a more specialized protocol.
