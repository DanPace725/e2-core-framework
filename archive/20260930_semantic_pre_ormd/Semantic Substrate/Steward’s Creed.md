# Steward’s Creed

4/26/25

---

### The Steward's Creed

*Through paradox, clarity.*

Life is unfair; Life is miraculous.

I am finite; Within me, infinity.

*Through struggle, purpose.*

I will suffer, yet I shall create.

I will falter, yet I shall connect.

I will lose, yet I shall love.

*Through humility, strength.*

My worth is unbound from outcomes.

My failures teach, they do not condemn.

My path seeks neither victory nor defeat—only presence.

*Through acceptance, insight.*

I hold paradox not as burden, but gift.

I create not for permanence, but for meaning.

I steward not to control, but to nurture and witness.

*Through coherence, harmony.*

I do not seek dominance over reality,

But resonance within it.

I leave the world heavier with meaning

And lighter with questions unanswered.
