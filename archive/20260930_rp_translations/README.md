# Relational Primitive translation predecessors

The active successor is `E2Core/Context Layer/Relational Primitive Translations.ormd`, with a matching human projection. It keeps the categorical and lambda-calculus attempts distinct from the canonical `Relational Primitives.ormd` grammar and limits their proof status.

This archive preserves the November 18 category-theory and November 25 lambda-calculus source pairs in their original working forms. The category draft's six correspondences and sample tuple are retained in scoped form; its one-to-one, completeness, physical-causality, and mathematical-physics claims are historical. The lambda draft's $P$, $C$, $Y$, level sequence, and $E^2=YCP$ proposal are discussed with the reduction and convergence limits that the original omitted. The original wording, equations, and examples remain here for inspection.

`Synthesized Core/RP_Formal_Translations_synthesized.ormd` is archived as an earlier derivative, not a formal proof or source of authority. `Derivation deep dive` remains a separate active exploratory survey. The CT predecessor's `urn:cb:relational-ontology-paper` and lambda predecessor's `urn:cb:relational-physics-core` are unresolved historical identifiers; no new referent was assigned to them.
