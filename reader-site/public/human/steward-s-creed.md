4/26/25

### The Steward's Creed {#creed-body}

*Through [paradox](#paradox-statement), clarity.* {#paradox-header}

Life is unfair; Life is miraculous. {#paradox-statement}

I am finite; Within me, [infinity](#infinity-concept). {#infinity-concept}

*Through [struggle](#struggle-statement), purpose.* {#struggle-header}

I will suffer, yet I shall create. {#struggle-statement}

I will falter, yet I shall connect.

I will lose, yet I shall love.

*Through [humility](#humility-statement), strength.* {#humility-header}

My worth is unbound from outcomes. {#humility-statement}

My failures teach, they do not condemn.

My path seeks neither victory nor defeat—only presence.

*Through [acceptance](#acceptance-statement), insight.* {#acceptance-header}

I hold [paradox](#paradox-statement) not as burden, but gift. {#acceptance-statement}

I create not for permanence, but for [meaning](#meaning-anchor).

I [steward](#stewardship-role) not to control, but to nurture and witness. {#stewardship-role}

*Through [coherence](#coherence-statement), harmony.* {#coherence-header}

I do not seek dominance over reality, {#coherence-statement}

But resonance within it.

I leave the world heavier with [meaning](#meaning-anchor) {#meaning-anchor}

And lighter with questions unanswered.
