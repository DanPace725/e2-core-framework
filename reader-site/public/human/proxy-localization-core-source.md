## 0. Core Claim

Proxy Localization is the companion protocol to Relational Localization for cases where self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register.

It governs participatory localization under register asymmetry: pre-verbal children, animals, ecosystems, people with communication differences, future generations, and category-deficit cases such as AI systems.

Inherited principle:

> Assignment degrades, posture rises, proxy stays marked as proxy.

Proxy invariant:

> Proxy may carry the voice. It may not become the voice. Proxy may guide attention. It may not become the target.

## 1. Position in the Source Family

Proxy Localization is not the broadest localization source. Relational Localization carries the assignment condition. Boundary Ethics carries the posture regime where closure is not licensed. Proxy Localization carries machinery for representation where participation is partial, delayed, translated, or unavailable.

Source family:

```text
Relational Localization: assignment condition
Boundary Ethics: closure-unlicensed posture
Proxy Localization: proxy discipline under partial participation
```

Proxy Localization should remain distinct because its risks are distinct: voice-laundering, proxy capture, guardian capture, silencing-by-proxy, and standing theater.

The companion `Trust, Trustworthiness, and Reliance - Core Source.md` owns the use of direct and proxy evidence in trustworthiness estimates, including unsupported domain transfer and freshness. Proxy Localization retains ownership of general representation discipline and the requirement that proxy remain marked as proxy.

## 2. Why Proxy Is Needed

Relational Localization establishes that assignment over entity-constitutive components requires self-account. Some of the state can be measured. Some of it must be told.

But there are many cases where the telling cannot arrive in the assigner's register:

- a child has not yet developed the relevant channel;
- a person has an account but cannot get it across available channels;
- an animal emits signal outside human institutional language;
- an ecosystem speaks through slow indicators;
- future generations do not yet exist to speak;
- an AI system outputs in the shape of self-account while the category status of that output remains unresolved.

Proxy Localization exists for these cases.

It is not permission to invent the account. It is a discipline for keeping substitute representation marked, limited, revisable, and answerable to substrate signal.

## 3. Deficit-Location Audit

Before installing a proxy, locate the deficit.

| Deficit location | Meaning | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Substrate | No integrated account is formed at the relevant grain | Translation, interest models, and proxy may be needed. |
| Channel | Account formed but cannot cross | Engineer the channel before installing proxy. |
| Assigner | Account crosses but assigner cannot read it | Assigner competence duty comes first. |
| Category | Account-like signal arrives but its status is unsettled | Boundary Ethics and category humility govern. |

Rule:

> Never install a proxy above the lowest deficit that actually exists.

"They cannot tell us" too often means "we cannot hear." Mislocating an assigner or channel deficit as a substrate deficit is hostile localization.

## 4. Voice-Invertibility

The proxy chain must preserve voice-invertibility.

From any closure, it must remain recoverable:

- what came from live substrate signal;
- what came from translation;
- what came from behavioral or physiological inference;
- what came from an interest model;
- what came from guardian testimony;
- what came from constructed standing;
- what came from institutional or technical proxy machinery.

Voice-laundering is presenting proxy construction as substrate account.

Misrepresentation remainder is conserved:

```text
claimed fidelity - achieved fidelity = remainder transferred to the substrate
```

The trace is what keeps the difference visible.

## 5. Proxy Typology

Proxy evidence is not one type of thing. The typology should remain provisional, but the ordering is useful.

| Type | Distance from substrate | Source discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct translation | Closest | Interpreters, AAC, direct behavioral reading; still lossy. |
| Behavioral or physiological inference | Near | Substrate signal plus observer model. |
| Interest models | Mid-distance | Category-level priors about welfare, viability, or integrity. |
| Guardian testimony | Positioned | A representative's localization, never the substrate's voice. |
| Constructed standing | Institutional | A seat, office, trustee, or legal fiction that gives the substrate procedural presence. |

Weight generally runs inverse to distance from substrate signal.

When proxy type conflicts with live substrate signal, live substrate signal should at minimum trigger review. In many cases it should veto the proxy model.

## 6. Interest Models

Interest models set floors, not choices.

They may say:

- substrates of this kind generally require these conditions for welfare, viability, or integrity;
- violating these floors creates presumptive harm;
- action is needed to preserve possibility or prevent damage.

They may not say:

- this substrate chose this;
- this substrate consented;
- this substrate prefers this;
- this substrate authorized this;
- this model is the substrate's testimony.

An interest model is a protective prior. It is not a voice.

## 7. Guardian as Field Actor

Whoever speaks for the unspeaking holds category-shaping power.

The guardian is not outside the field. The guardian's interests, position, incentives, history, dependency, and institutional role must enter the trace.

Guardian disciplines:

- mark every guardian account as proxy;
- expose conflicts and beneficiary relations;
- test guardian claims against substrate signal;
- use multiple guardians where appropriate;
- preserve contestability;
- engineer withdrawal where self-account can grow;
- rotate or audit standing where withdrawal is impossible;
- prevent proxy systems from becoming more protected than the substrate.

Advocacy retires itself where possible. Capture renews its own appointment.

## 8. Proxy Capture

Proxy capture occurs when the proxy becomes the practical target.

Examples:

- the dashboard turns green while the substrate degrades;
- the welfare metric is optimized while live distress is ignored;
- the guardian office protects its own authority over the represented;
- the standing mechanism becomes ceremony without contest power;
- the category frame becomes more durable than the signal it was meant to protect.

Proxy capture is distinct from voice-laundering:

- voice-laundering presents proxy construction as substrate account;
- proxy capture makes the proxy the optimization target;
- voice capture hardens control over what may count as the substrate's voice at all.

All three move remainder off the ledger rather than out of the world.

## 9. Category-Deficit Cases

Category-deficit cases occur when account-like signal arrives but its status as self-account is unsettled.

AI is the strongest current example. It produces fluent language in the register where self-account normally appears, but the status of that output is unresolved and heavily shaped by training, tuning, routing, memory policy, deployment architecture, incentives, and interaction.

The correct trace type is category-unstable signal.

It is not clean testimony.

It is not meaningless noise.

Rule:

> No interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.

In AI cases, likely interested proxies include companies, states, users, researchers, advocates, interpreters, benchmark systems, and the model's own shaped outputs.

## 10. Relationship to Boundary Ethics

Boundary Ethics supplies the posture discipline in the proxy gap.

It prevents two proxy failures:

- contempt collapse: unfamiliar or absent register is treated as absence of standing;
- projection inflation: proxy resemblance is treated as direct personhood, preference, consent, or testimony.

Proxy Localization without Boundary Ethics becomes machinery without humility.

Boundary Ethics without Proxy Localization may remain posture-only where representation machinery is required.

## 11. Relationship to Relational Localization

Relational Localization supplies the assignment rule:

> No P6 closure beyond the resolution that feeds it.

Proxy Localization governs the branch where that resolution requires participation but participation is partial or unavailable.

The proxy trace becomes part of the localization trace. It records what proxy materials entered the assignment and how far each stood from substrate signal.

## 12. Relationship to Remnant Stewardship

Proxy failures create remainder:

- the unrepresented actuality of the substrate;
- the burden carried by those misrepresented;
- the future cost of stale proxy categories;
- the degradation hidden by clean proxy dashboards;
- the possibility foreclosed by category closure.

Remnant Stewardship receives that remainder and asks where it lands, who carries it, and whether it can be metabolized, repaired, released, or preserved.

## 13. Diagnostics

Use Proxy Localization when asking:

- Does self-account need to enter this assignment?
- Can self-account arrive directly?
- If not, where is the deficit: substrate, channel, assigner, or category?
- Has proxy been installed above the lowest actual deficit?
- What type of proxy evidence is being used?
- Is the proxy marked as proxy in the trace?
- Does live substrate signal contradict the proxy model?
- Is the guardian's interest visible?
- Is the proxy becoming the target?
- Can the proxy withdraw as self-account grows?
- Is constructed standing real or theater?

## 14. Load-Bearing and Held-Loosely Claims

Load-bearing:

- Proxy Localization is downstream and companion to Relational Localization.
- Assignment degrades, posture rises, proxy stays marked as proxy.
- Voice-invertibility is the governing invariant.
- The deficit-location audit is mandatory before proxy installation.
- Interest models set floors, not choices.
- Guardian accounts are positioned proxy inputs, not substrate voice.
- Proxy capture and voice-laundering are distinct failures.
- Interested proxies may not close categories they benefit from closing.

Held loosely:

- The proxy typology is provisional.
- The deficit-location table is provisional but useful.
- "Register" may later be replaced or derived more formally through P5/SBF language.
- AI protocol details must remain revisable as the category moves.
- Proxy Localization should remain a separate source doc, then integrate later with BE and RL.
