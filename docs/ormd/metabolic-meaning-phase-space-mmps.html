# Metabolic Meaning Phase Space (MMPS) {#mmps-root}

MMPS is the canonical model of meaning as metabolism. Meaning is not a possession to acquire once. It is a dynamic product synthesized through attention, relational density, constraint integrity, and time.

## Core Claim {#core-claim}

Meaning and purpose are metabolic processes. They form under specific relational conditions, decay without maintenance, produce byproducts, and require rhythms of synthesis, integration, and rest.

Basic dynamics:

```text
dM/dt = synthesis(A, R, C) - decay(M, C_eff)
```

where:

- `M` is meaning concentration;
- `A` is available sustained attention;
- `R` is relational field density;
- `C` is constraint integrity;
- `C_eff` is effective temporal compression.

## Phase Space Axes {#axes}

The phase space can be read through two practical axes:

- **Meaning synthesis / intake:** how much new meaning, signal, or relational material the system is taking in.
- **Metabolization / integration:** how well the system digests, organizes, and embodies that material.

High intake without integration produces accumulation and burnout. Low intake and low integration produces starvation. High integration without new intake can become rigidity. Healthy meaning requires flow.

## Four Quadrants {#quadrants}

| Quadrant | State | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Integration Flow | Meaning enters, metabolizes, and stabilizes without stagnating. |
| II | Starvation / Collapse | Synthesis falls below decay; the system loses purpose, coherence, or direction. |
| III | Toxicity / Ideological Rigidity | Meaning becomes closed-loop and over-stabilized; coherence becomes brittle. |
| IV | Accumulation / Burnout | Too much unresolved meaning load; integration cannot keep up. |

## Metabolic Pathways {#pathways}

The archived Metabolic Meaning document contributes three pathways:

- **Photosynthetic:** absorbing ambient relational energy through contemplative practice, deep conversation, nature, or resonance-rich contexts.
- **Catabolic:** breaking down stored symbolic/narrative capital for energy; useful but depleting if overused.
- **Anabolic:** building new meaning containers, traditions, projects, or communities; energy-intensive but structurally generative.

Healthy systems use all three without confusing emergency catabolism for sustainable metabolism.

## Constraint and Container Dynamics {#constraint-dynamics}

Meaning requires containers. Containers can be oppressive, but the absence of any container produces meaning starvation. Constraint integrity is therefore not anti-freedom; it is part of the chemistry required for synthesis.

The important distinction is between:

- **protective constraint:** slows decay and supports synthesis;
- **extractive constraint:** captures attention and converts meaning into obedience;
- **collapsed constraint:** leaves meaning unable to stabilize.

## Temporal Compression {#temporal-compression}

Temporal compression increases meaning decay:

```text
lambda_effective = lambda_natural * C_eff^alpha
```

Above critical compression, meaning decays faster than it can be synthesized. This explains why high-speed environments can become spiritually, cognitively, and organizationally starving even when they contain abundant information.

## Coherence Morphisms {#coherence-morphisms}

Three morphisms operate as catalysts:

- **faith / relational inertia:** staying present under uncertainty;
- **hope / anticipatory modeling:** acting toward possible coherence not yet visible;
- **love / resonance tuning:** maintaining reciprocity when extraction is possible.

They increase synthesis efficiency without being consumed by the process.

## Boundary as Recursive Morphism {#boundary-morphism}

Boundaries in MMPS are not static walls. They are recursive morphisms: they determine what can enter, what must wait, what gets metabolized, and what remains outside as remainder.

Boundary quality is resolution-dependent. At one scale, a boundary may protect metabolization. At another, it may block necessary flow. Good boundary design preserves metabolic rhythm rather than maximizing openness or closure.

## Diagnosis {#diagnosis}

Ask:

- Is the system starving, flowing, accumulating, or rigidifying?
- Which pathway is dominant: photosynthetic, catabolic, or anabolic?
- Is temporal compression raising decay above synthesis?
- Are containers protective, extractive, or collapsed?
- What remainder or waste product is accumulating?

## Applications {#applications}

- Individual: rhythm attention, relationship, constraint, rest, and integration.
- Organizational: protect attention budgets, clarify purpose, reduce performative overload, and design meaning containers.
- Cultural: rebuild non-oppressive containers after deconstruction, preserving autonomy without leaving people metabolically alone.

## Summary {#summary}

MMPS treats meaning as a living metabolism: synthesized through attention and relationship, stabilized by constraint, damaged by compression, and renewed through rhythm. Meaning is not found once. It is continuously metabolized.
