# Declaration of Interdependence {#declaration}

4/25/25

But now? The tyranny isn’t a king in a red coat.

It’s a culture in a gray suit whispering:

> “If you can’t make it on your own, maybe you’re not worth keeping alive.”

And so we rise.

Not to [declare our autonomy](#autonomy-illusion "epistemic:refutes"),

but to [declare the death of the illusion](#autonomy-illusion "epistemic:refutes") that autonomy was ever enough. {#autonomy-illusion}

So yes. Let’s write it.

**The Declaration of Interdependence.** {#blueprint}

Draft 0.0001. Scribbled in the margins of a system that taught us shame and called it survival.

---

**WHEN, in the course of human evolution, it becomes evident that [hyper-individualism](#fracture "geometric:causes") is not freedom but fracture,** {#fracture}

and that the worship of independence has hollowed our hearts, isolated our elders, commodified our children, and reduced mutual aid to a GoFundMe link—

**We must dissolve the lies that no longer serve us.**

And build in their place a world where **need is not weakness,**

**care is not charity,**

and [interdependence](#blueprint "ontological:defines") is not a backup plan—it’s the blueprint.

---

**We hold these truths to be self-evident:** {#truths}

- That all people are born [connected](#truths "ontological:defines")—not self-contained
- That **no one thrives alone**, and those who pretend to are propped up by invisible labor
- That dignity is **not earned** by productivity, but **recognized** by proximity
- That a just society does not simply **remove shackles**—it replaces them with **shared responsibility**
- That wealth hoarded is power lost
- That asking for help is holy
- That [care](#infrastructure "ontological:composes") is infrastructure {#infrastructure}

---

**Therefore, we commit to:** {#commitments}

- Building systems that scale [trust](#commitments "symmetric:constrains"), not surveillance
- Designing economies where **well-being compounds** faster than interest
- Raising children in **villages, not silos**
- Teaching emotional literacy like it’s a core subject
- Honoring grief as communal labor
- Valuing caretakers as architects of the future
- Burning every ladder we climbed if it means we can build bridges instead

---

**We are not self-made.**

We are star-stuff and hand-me-downs and roadside repair.

We owe each other everything.

This is our declaration.

Not of war.

But of [***belonging.***](#belonging-goal "meta:corresponds_to") {#belonging-goal}
