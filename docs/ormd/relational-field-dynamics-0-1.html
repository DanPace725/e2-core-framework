# Relational Field Dynamics 0.1 {#title}

10/2/25

Relational Field Dynamics (RFD) v0.1 — Axioms & Measurement Doctrine
Status: Draft • Scope: Minimal formalism to model relational/coherence dynamics without faux precision.

---

## 0) Overview {#overview}
RFD treats meaning, coherence, occlusion, attention, and temporal pressure as fields on a substrate (graph of agents/channels or a continuous domain). Dynamics [follow](#dynamics "dynamical:transforms_to") an advection–diffusion–reaction form with guarded interventions (morphisms) and a built‑in [resolution doctrine](#resolution-doctrine "epistemic:measures") (Rayleigh‑style) to keep ethics and measurement honest.

RFD is [compatible with](#cip-manifest "meta:corresponds_to"): MMPS (metabolic meaning + boundary negotiation), Exposure Protocol (action/state duality via morphisms), MPDC (epistemic humility), AOMI (anti‑gaming + integrity), Temporal Compression (time‑pressure ↔ resolution).

---

## 1) State, Observables, and Domains {#state}
Fields (continuous) or Node variables (graph):
- **M(x,t) ∈ [0,1]** — [Meaning density](#state "ontological:defines") (stock)
- **κ(x,t) ∈ [0,1]** — [Coherence index](#state "ontological:defines")
- **Ω(x,t) ∈ [0,1]** — [Occlusion/uncertainty density](#state "ontological:defines")
- **u(x,t)** — [Attention/interaction flow](#state "ontological:defines") (vector field) or edge flow uᵢⱼ(t) on graphs
- **C_eff(x,t) ∈ [0,∞)** — [Temporal compression](#state "ontological:defines") (higher = more crushed time)

Shared Field (for views & protocols):
- **TI = 1/(1 + C_eff)** (Temporal Integrity ∈ (0,1])
- **J_M = uM - D_M \nabla M** (Meaning flux)

> **Bounds**: All tracked scalars [live in](#state "symmetric:constrains") compact sets; saturation operators ensure boundedness under interventions.

## 2) Dynamics {#dynamics}

### 2.1 Continuous (PDE) form
For $\Phi \in \{M, \kappa, \Omega\}$:
$\partial_t \Phi + \nabla\!\cdot(u\,\Phi) \;=\; D_\Phi\,\nabla^2\!\Phi \;+\; R_\Phi(\text{local}) \;-\; \Gamma_\Phi(\Omega, C_{\rm eff})\,.$

Components [composing](#dynamics "ontological:composes") the field:
- **Advection**: [transport by](#state "dynamical:interacts_with") attention/interaction flow.
- **Diffusion**: dispersion/smoothing.
- **Reaction**: local synthesis/decay (from [MMPS](#cip-manifest "meta:corresponds_to"), etc.).
- **Attenuation**: occlusion/pressure terms.

Local reaction for M (MMPS proxy):
$R_M \;=\; \eta\,[\,0.5\,A + 0.4\,R + 0.1\,C\,] \;-\; \lambda\,M\,.$

Flow constitutive law: potential/viscous blend
$u = -\alpha\,\nabla\Phi_u \;+\; \nu\,\nabla^2 u\,,\quad \Phi_u := f(C_{\rm eff},\,\nabla M,\,\nabla\kappa)\,.$

### 2.2 Graph form (preferred for teams/channels)
For node i with neighbors j (weights $w_{ij}$):
$\dot M_i = \eta_i F_i - \lambda_i M_i - \chi\,\Omega_i M_i \;+\; D_M\!\sum_j w_{ij}(M_j - M_i) \;+\; \sum_j u_{ij}M_j \,.$

## 3) Boundaries & Interfaces (MMPS) {#boundaries}

## 4) Resolution Doctrine (Rayleigh‑style) {#resolution-doctrine}
Operator: $\mathcal{M}$. Two states $S_1, S_2$ [are distinguishable](#resolution-doctrine "epistemic:measures") only if:
$\|S_1 - S_2\|_\mathcal{M} \;>\; \Theta\big(C_{\rm eff}, \Omega\big),$

**Implication**: Interventions below resolution [are discouraged/blocked](#ethics "symmetric:constrains") (ethics with teeth).

---

## 5) Measurement Doctrine (Ordinal > Cardinal) {#measurement-doctrine}
Use [ordinal/relational embeddings](#measurement-doctrine "epistemic:measures") (ranks, pairwise comps) for $\Phi$; any monotone transform is acceptable. We track shape and flow, not absolute units.
Add [topological summaries](#measurement-doctrine "meta:corresponds_to") (e.g., persistence diagrams on interaction graphs) to capture multi‑scale structure without fragile scales.
Numeric coordinates [act as](#measurement-doctrine "ontological:defines") charts for calculus rather than claims of absolute magnitude.

---

## 6) Ethics & Guards (AOMI + Reverent Stewardship) {#ethics}
Guards [act as](#ethics "symmetric:constrains") pre‑commit hooks on dynamics and interventions:
- **Boundedness**: saturate to domains.
- **Budgeting**: total action cost/time caps; [throttle](#dynamics "symmetric:constrains") u and policy stacks.
- **Anti‑gaming (AOMI)**: [detect exploit patterns](#ethics "epistemic:measures"); project state back to integrity set.
- **Resolution guard**: [block actions](#resolution-doctrine "symmetric:constrains") under Rayleigh threshold.

Lyapunov regularization (monotone recovery under λ): choose
$V = \tfrac{1}{2}\sum_i \big(\lambda M_i^2 + \chi\,\Omega_i M_i^2\big) + \tfrac{D_M}{2}\!\sum_{ij} w_{ij}(M_i - M_j)^2 - \eta\sum_i F_i M_i$

---

## 7) Interventions as Morphisms (Exposure Protocol) {#interventions}
Let Policies be small [transforms on state](#state "dynamical:transforms_to"); Meta‑morphisms are transforms on policies (gain, caps, scheduling).

Canonical policies (local):
- **ψ (Restore)**: $\dot \eta > 0$
- **φ (Paradox)**: temporary $\Omega \uparrow$ to crack closure, then $\kappa \uparrow$
- **λ (Integrate)**: $\dot C_{\rm eff} < 0$ (decompress time)

Composition: left‑to‑right with declared costs; [guards gate](#ethics "symmetric:constrains") commit. Budgets cap total cost per horizon.

---

## 8) Regimes (macro‑modes) & Predicates {#regimes}
- **Flow**: $\dot M > 0$ and $\kappa > 0.7$
- **Rigidity**: $u \approx 0$ and $\kappa \to 1$
- **Overload**: $C_{\rm eff} \gg 1$ and high $\Omega$ / compression
- **Collapse**: $\dot M < 0$ and $\kappa \to 0$

Transitions [trigger](#interventions "geometric:causes") guardrails or scheduled policies (e.g., λ when Overload persists).

---

## 9) Dimensionless Numbers (for comparison & falsifiability) {#dimensionless-numbers}
- **Relational Reynolds**: $Re_R = \frac{\|u \nabla \Phi\|}{\|D \nabla^2 \Phi\|}$ — [measures](#dynamics "epistemic:measures") advection vs dissipation.
- **Compression**: $Co = \frac{C_{\rm eff}}{C_{max}}$ — [measures](#state "epistemic:measures") compressibility/time‑crunch.
- **Resonance**: $Rs = \frac{\eta F}{\lambda M + D \nabla^2 M}$ — [measures](#dynamics "epistemic:measures") synthesis vs diffusion.
- **Occlusion**: $Oc = \chi \Omega$ — uncertainty attenuation strength.
- **Stewardship**: $St = \frac{\text{Guard Action}}{\text{Raw Flow}}$ — [measures](#ethics "epistemic:measures") damping fraction by guards.

## 10) Algorithmic Loop (Graph substrate) {#algo-loop}
1. **Sense** → [build ordinal embedding](#measurement-doctrine "epistemic:measures") for $\Phi$; estimate $u$ from interaction flows.
2. **Evolve** → [integrate graph dynamics](#dynamics "dynamical:transforms_to") with diffusion and reaction terms.
3. **Guard** → [apply resolution, budgets, and AOMI projections](#ethics "symmetric:constrains") before committing state.
4. **Regime logic** → [evaluate predicates](#regimes "epistemic:measures"); schedule ψ/φ/λ as needed.
5. **Evaluate** → track forecast error and $V$ under λ; [compute dimensionless numbers](#dimensionless-numbers "epistemic:measures") over time.
