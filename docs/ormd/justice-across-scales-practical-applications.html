**Section 1: The Diagnostic Stack** {#diagnostic-stack}

**Step 1: Identify Your Scale** {#identify-scale}

**Question**: What system are you trying to understand or intervene in?

| **Scale** | **Characteristics** | **Examples** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Micro** | 1-20 people, direct interaction, high resolution | Individual conflict, family dynamics, small team |
| **Meso** | 20-10,000 people, mediated interaction, aggregate data | Organization, department, neighborhood, city |
| **Macro** | 10,000+ people, indirect/statistical, low resolution | Corporation, nation, industry, civilization |

**Quick diagnostic**:

- Can you name everyone involved? → [Micro](#micro-scale "ontological:defines")
- Do you work with metrics and reports? → [Meso](#meso-scale "ontological:defines")
- Are impacts generational or demographic? → [Macro](#macro-scale "ontological:defines")

**Step 2: Assess Your Epistemic Situation** {#epistemic-situation}

**The Three Gradients Checklist**:

**Uncertainty (Ω)**:

- [ ]  Can predict outcomes with reasonable confidence (LOW)
- [ ]  Some predictability, some emergence (MEDIUM)
- [ ]  Fundamentally unpredictable, [MPDC](#mpdc-ref "meta:corresponds_to") applies (HIGH)

**Resolution (R)**:

- [ ]  Can see individual actions and their effects (HIGH)
- [ ]  Can see patterns in aggregates (MEDIUM)
- [ ]  Can only see statistical correlations (LOW)

**Temporal Lag (Δt)**:

- [ ]  Feedback within days/weeks (SHORT)
- [ ]  Feedback within months/years (MEDIUM)
- [ ]  Feedback across decades/generations (LONG)

**Your epistemic profile**: [Uncertainty: ___ / Resolution: ___ / Lag: ___]

**Step 3: Check the Morphisms** {#check-morphisms}

**Which capacity is failing?**

**φ (Relational Inertia / Faith)**:

- [ ]  System maintains presence under uncertainty
- [ ]  People/institutions stay engaged despite not knowing outcomes
- [ ]  No panic collapse at first sign of unpredictability

**Failure signs**: Abandonment at first difficulty, "I'm done" reactions, institution hopping, emergency responses that [make things worse](#failure-modes "dynamical:transforms_to")

**ψ (Anticipatory Modeling / Hope)**:

- [ ]  System acts despite inability to guarantee results
- [ ]  Signals transmitted into uncertain futures
- [ ]  No paralysis waiting for certainty

**Failure signs**: "We need more data before we can act," endless studies with no decisions, learned helplessness, resignation

**λ (Resonance Tuning / Love)**:

- [ ]  System maintains reciprocity under pressure
- [ ]  No extraction or zero-sum dynamics
- [ ]  Field coherence increases rather than drains

**Failure signs**: Hoarding resources, extractive relationships, "I got mine" attitudes, winner-takes-all structures

**Step 4: Calculate Your Compression Status** {#compression-status}

**The Resolution-Responsibility Check**:

`|= R * C_eff <= R_max |`

**Estimate C_eff** (temporal compression):

- How many decisions per time unit?
- How rushed do things feel?
- Scale: 1 (leisurely) to 10 (constant crisis)

**Estimate R** (resolution requirement):

- How fine-grained does attribution need to be?
- Scale: 1 (aggregate/statistical) to 10 (individual action)

**Estimate R_max** (system capacity):

- Resources available for observation/response?
- Scale: 1 (minimal) to 10 (abundant)

**Check**: Does `|= R * C_eff <= R_max |`?

- **YES**: System can maintain required resolution → Use precision tools
- **NO**: System is [over-compressed](#compression-status "symmetric:constrains") → Must slow down, coarsen grain, or expand capacity

**Step 5: Map Your Occlusion** {#map-occlusion}

`|= Ω = f(C_eff, Δt, R) |`

**Temporal occlusion**:

- How compressed is decision-making? (C_eff)
- Is there time to see consequences before next decision?

**Topological occlusion**:

- How many layers between decision and impact?
- Can decision-makers feel consequences?

**Cognitive occlusion**:

- Is the system too complex to understand?
- Are metrics gamed or divorced from reality?

**Occlusion score**: Low / Medium / High / Extreme

**Who benefits from high Ω?**

- List stakeholders who gain from consequences staying invisible
- This tells you where resistance will come from

**Section 2: Mode-Specific Toolkits** {#mode-toolkits}

**Micro Scale (Direct Responsibility Mode)** {#micro-scale}

**Context**: 1-20 people, high resolution, personal relationships

**Core Tools**

**1. The 3-Touch Rule** (for consequences) {#three-touch}

- **First touch**: Signal ("The dishes need doing")
- **Second touch**: State natural consequence ("We'll run out of plates")
- **Third touch**: Let consequence occur (use paper plates, natural hunger)
- **You're done**: Let reality teach, don't become the consequence

**When to use**: Family dynamics, small teams, any situation where [natural consequences](#three-touch "geometric:causes") exist

**2. Reverent Stewardship Framework** {#stewardship}

- **Formula**: `|= Care = Presence * Restraint |`
- **Presence**: I'm here, I'm paying attention
- **Restraint**: I'm not taking over, forcing, or rescuing

**Practices**:

- Signal before entry (don't barge in)
- Wait for resonance (let them invite you deeper)
- Match fragility (soft touch for fragile systems)

**3. Coherence Triage System** {#coherence-triage}

| **State** | **SCIA/T Level** | **What To Do** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Green** (8-10) | High | Can engage deeply, offer support, take on complexity |
| **Yellow** (5-7) | Medium | Maintenance mode, simple boundaries, witness without engagement |
| **Red** (0-4) | Low | Survival mode, minimal interaction, consequences return to sender |

**Check your coherence before engaging**. Don't try to fix others when you're in Red.

**4. Consequence Provenance Audit** {#provenance-audit}

Ask:

- Whose pressure am I holding that isn't mine?
- Where am I being a "pressure dam"?
- What consequences belong to others that I've absorbed?

**Then**: Systematically [return consequences](#provenance-audit "dynamical:transforms_to") to their proper source.

**Failure Modes to Watch For** {#failure-modes-micro}

**Enmeshment**: Boundaries dissolve, can't tell whose consequence is whose

- **Fix**: [3-touch rule](#three-touch "symmetric:constrains"), [consequence provenance audit](#provenance-audit "epistemic:measures"), increase personal coherence

**Pressure Dams**: One person absorbs everyone else's consequences

- **Fix**: Stop being shock absorber, let pressure find natural level

**Occlusion by Speed**: Moving too fast to see impacts

- **Fix**: Slow down deliberately, build in pause protocols

**Worked Example: Family Conflict** {#example-family}

**Situation**: Teen not doing homework, parent stressed, grades dropping

**Diagnosis**:

- Scale: [Micro](#micro-scale "ontological:is_a") (family dyad)
- Resolution: High (can see individual actions)
- Morphism failure: Parent absorbing teen's consequences ([λ failure](#check-morphisms "ontological:defines") - extracting coherence)
- Compression: Medium (parent feels rushed to fix)

**Intervention**:

1. **Recognize the pattern**: Parent is pressure dam, preventing natural consequences
2. **Apply 3-touch**:
    - Touch 1: "Homework needs doing"
    - Touch 2: "You'll face the teacher/grades without it done"
    - Touch 3: Let natural consequence occur (failing grade, teacher conversation)
3. **Maintain presence**: Available if teen asks for help, but not forcing
4. **Return consequence**: This is teen's responsibility, not parent's
5. **Check coherence**: Is parent in Green/Yellow to stay non-reactive?

**Expected outcome**: Short-term grades may drop, but [consequence-action loop reconnects](#example-family "dynamical:transforms_to"). Teen learns from reality rather than parent's anxiety.

**Meso Scale (Systems Stewardship Mode)** {#meso-scale}

**Context**: 20-10,000 people, medium resolution, organizational systems

**Core Tools**

**1. CFA Balance Monitoring** {#cfa-dashboard}

Track three variables:

**C (Constraint)**: Policies, processes, structure

- Too high (→1): Rigidity, bureaucracy, can't adapt
- Too low (→0): Chaos, nothing sticks, constant churn

**F (Fluctuation)**: Change, innovation, disruption

- Too high (→1): Thrash, constant crisis, exhaustion
- Too low (→0): Stagnation, boredom, decay

**A (Attention)**: Focus, strategic priority, energy

- Fragmented: Spread thin, nothing gets deep attention
- Concentrated: Some things get focus, others starve

**Healthy state**: Dynamic balance, A channels F into productive C

**Dashboard template**:

C: [0--------X-----10] (Current constraint level)

F: [0-----X----------10] (Current fluctuation level)

A: [Distribution map showing where attention is focused]

Warnings:

- C approaching 1 + F increasing → Lock-in imminent
- F approaching 1 + C low → Thrash state
- A spread across >7 major domains → Fragmentation

**2. AOMI (Adversarial Occlusion & Mechanism Integrity)** {#aomi-logic}

**Core principle**: Make high-occlusion pathways riskier, not less risky

**Implementation**:

**Paradoxical Policing**:

`|= Audit_Probability = g(Ω) |` where `g'(Ω) > 0`

Translation: The less visible something is, the more likely it gets audited.

**Steps**:

1. **Map occlusion**: Where are consequences invisible?
2. **Calculate Ω**: For each decision pathway, estimate occlusion level
3. **Inverse scrutiny**: High Ω paths get [enhanced monitoring](#aomi-logic "symmetric:constrains")
4. **Gaming detection**: Watch for:
    - Threshold dancing (staying just under limits)
    - Explanation complexity (convoluted justifications)
    - Coordination overhead (suspiciously complex processes)
5. **Reserve capacity**: Budget 20-30% slack to absorb gaming without collapse

**Red flags**:

- Processes that get more complex over time without clear reason
- Metrics that improve while reality worsens
- Decision-makers insulated from consequences
- "Unresolved" clusters that benefit specific stakeholders

**3. Occlusion Reduction Strategies** {#occlusion-reduction}

**Temporal compression reduction**:

- Add review buffers before critical decisions
- Slow-path requirements for high-impact choices
- Quarterly reflection cycles (not just annual)

**Topological distance shortening**:

- Decision-makers visit impact sites
- Participatory budgeting from affected communities
- Rotation programs (executives work on front lines)

**Cognitive clarity improvement**:

- Simple dashboards (resist metric proliferation)
- "Unresolved" states explicitly tracked (CLP-style)
- Explanations required for all major decisions

**4. The WIP Limit System** {#wip-limits}

**Constraint on Work-In-Progress**:

- Calculate team capacity realistically
- Set hard limits on concurrent projects
- When at limit, must complete or abandon something before starting new work

**Effect**: [Reduces C_eff](#compression-status "symmetric:constrains") (compression), increases resolution on what's actually happening

**5. CLP-Style Query Framework** {#clp-query}

For any organizational decision, require:

**Query structure**:

Intent: [What are we trying to determine?]

Frame: [What domains are relevant?]

Resolution thresholds: [min_support, confidence_level]

Attention budget: [How much can we spend investigating?]

**Response structure**:

Results: [What we found]

Explain: [Why we believe this, with evidence]

Unresolved: [What we couldn't determine]

- Why unresolved
- Who benefits from it staying unresolved
- What would be needed to resolve it

Telemetry: [System health metrics]

**Key feature**: "Unresolved" is a valid output, not a failure

**Failure Modes to Watch For** {#failure-modes-meso}

**KPI Theater**: Metrics improve, reality decays

- **Fix**: [AOMI paradoxical policing](#aomi-logic "symmetric:constrains"), unresolved tracking, inverse scrutiny

**Responsibility Laundering**: Everyone points to someone else

- **Fix**: Clear consequence ownership, provenance tracking, fewer handoffs

**Deadline-Driven Occlusion**: Compression creates blind spots

- **Fix**: [WIP limits](#wip-limits "symmetric:constrains"), slow-path requirements, compression budgets

**Gaming**: Sophisticated manipulation of metrics

- **Fix**: [AOMI implementation](#aomi-logic "symmetric:constrains"), reserve capacity, exploitability tracking

**Worked Example: Organization Policy Change** {#example-org}

**Situation**: Company implementing new performance review system, concerns about fairness

**Diagnosis**:

- Scale: [Meso](#meso-scale "ontological:is_a") (500 person organization)
- Resolution: Medium (can see departmental patterns, not individuals)
- Occlusion: High (leadership doesn't see impact on front-line workers)
- Compression: High (quarterly reviews, rushed implementation)
- Morphism failure: [ψ failure](#check-morphisms "ontological:defines") (no one modeling long-term consequences)

**Intervention**:

**Phase 1: Occlusion Mapping**

1. Calculate Ω for decision:
    - Temporal: High compression (must implement in Q1)
    - Topological: Leadership 3 layers removed from impact
    - Cognitive: Complex rubric, unclear criteria
    - **Total Ω: High**
2. Identify who benefits from high Ω:
    - Middle managers (can game metrics)
    - Leadership (insulated from complaints)

**Phase 2: AOMI Implementation**

1. **Inverse scrutiny**: Because Ω is high, trigger enhanced review
2. **Slow-path activation**: Add 90-day pilot before full rollout
3. **Reserve capacity**: Budget 30% management time for handling issues
4. **Gaming detection**: Monitor for:
    - Explanation complexity increases
    - Threshold dancing around rating cutoffs
    - Complaints suppressed rather than resolved

**Phase 3: CLP-Style Process**

1. **Frame**: [HR.policy, employee.experience, manager.practice]
2. **Resolution thresholds**:
    - min_support = 5 (need 5 independent validators per claim)
    - confidence = 0.85 (85% confidence required)
3. **Query**: "Does this system create fair attribution of performance?"

**Phase 4: Pilot with Monitoring**

1. Deploy to 3 departments (different cultures)
2. Weekly check-ins with front-line workers
3. Bring leadership to observe actual review sessions
4. Track "unresolved" clusters:
    - Criteria that can't be consistently applied
    - Rating disagreements that can't be reconciled
    - Cultural mismatches in performance definition

**Phase 5: Decision**

- If unresolved clusters < 20%: Proceed with modifications
- If unresolved clusters > 20%: System needs fundamental redesign
- Either way: Make unresolved clusters visible, don't force resolution

**Expected outcome**: Either system works with known limitations, or [leadership learns](#example-org "epistemic:supports") why it won't work before damaging entire organization

**Macro Scale (Possibility Preservation Mode)** {#macro-scale}

**Context**: 10,000+ people, low resolution, generational impacts

**Core Tools**

**1. Multi-Horizon Review System** {#multi-horizon}

**Principle**: Different decisions need different time horizons

**Implementation**:

| **Decision Type** | **Review Horizon** | **Example** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operational** | 1-3 months | Budget adjustments, hiring |
| **Tactical** | 1-3 years | Program launches, facility changes |
| **Strategic** | 5-10 years | Major infrastructure, policy shifts |
| **Existential** | 20-50 years | Constitutional changes, climate action |

**For each tier**:

- Appropriate review cycles
- Different stakeholders consulted
- Longer Δt = [more conservative change threshold](#multi-horizon "symmetric:constrains")

- Explicit [future generations](#existential-tier "ontological:defines") consideration for existential tier

## 2. Curvature Equilibrium Monitoring {#curvature-monitoring}

**Question**: Is [power distribution](#curvature-monitoring "epistemic:measures") net-supporting or net-extracting?

**Metrics to track**:

### Resource flows {#resource-flows}

- [Where does value originate?](#resource-flows "epistemic:measures")
- [Where does it accumulate?](#resource-flows "epistemic:measures")
- [Are origins replenished or depleted?](#resource-flows "epistemic:measures")

### Gradient analysis {#gradient-analysis}

- [Wealth concentration trends](#gradient-analysis "epistemic:measures")
- [Opportunity access by geography/demography](#gradient-analysis "epistemic:measures")
- [Intergenerational mobility patterns](#gradient-analysis "epistemic:measures")

### Field effects {#field-effects}

- [Do wealthy regions require poor regions to maintain wealth?](#field-effects "geometric:causes")
- [Does system stability depend on some groups bearing costs?](#field-effects "geometric:causes")
- [Are "winners" winning by extracting from "losers"?](#field-effects "geometric:causes")

### Red flags {#red-flags}

- [Increasing concentration without productivity increase](#red-flags "epistemic:refutes")
- [Regions/groups persistently drained of resources](#red-flags "epistemic:refutes")
- [System stability requiring someone's exploitation](#red-flags "epistemic:refutes")

### Intervention {#intervention-curvature}

**Intervention**: When [curvature becomes extractive](#intervention-curvature "dynamical:transforms_to"):

- [Regenerative resource return](#intervention-curvature "dynamical:transforms_to") (to sources)
- [Redistribution mechanisms](#intervention-curvature "dynamical:transforms_to") (graduated taxation, public goods)
- [Structural changes](#intervention-curvature "dynamical:transforms_to") (break monopolies, strengthen labor)

## 3. Option Space Preservation Checklist {#option-space}

**Principle**: At high uncertainty, [don't collapse to single path too early](#option-space "symmetric:constrains")

**Questions before major decisions**:

- [ ]  Are we [eliminating future options](#option-space "symmetric:limits")?
- [ ]  Could we be wrong about this?
- [ ]  What's the cost of reversing if we're wrong?
- [ ]  Are we preserving diverse approaches?
- [ ]  Have we consulted perspectives we disagree with?
- [ ]  Is there irreversible harm if this fails?

**If many "yes" answers**: [Slow down](#option-space "symmetric:constrains"), preserve alternatives, pilot before scaling

## 4. Participatory Sensing Networks {#participatory-sensing}

**Problem**: At macro scale, [leadership can't see ground truth](#participatory-sensing "epistemic:limits")

**Solution**: [Distributed observation capacity](#participatory-sensing "epistemic:supports")

**Implementation**: {#sensing-implementation}

1. [Citizen assemblies](#sensing-implementation "ontological:composes"): Randomly selected groups review policies
2. [Community reporting](#sensing-implementation "ontological:composes"): Direct channels from affected populations
3. [Anthropological embedding](#sensing-implementation "ontological:composes"): Researchers in communities for extended periods
4. [Cross-scale feedback](#sensing-implementation "ontological:composes"): Front-line workers to executive direct lines

**Key**: Make [consequence proximity](#participatory-sensing "symmetric:constrains") part of the system design, don't rely on it emerging naturally

## 5. The "Unresolved States" Framework {#unresolved-states}

**At macro scale, much remains genuinely undecidable**

**Practice**: Maintain honest registries of:

### Unresolved Policy Questions {#unresolved-questions}

- [What we're trying to determine](#unresolved-questions "epistemic:measures")
- [Why it's currently unresolved](#unresolved-questions "epistemic:measures") (insufficient data, conflicting values, [MPDC limit](#unresolved-states "symmetric:limits"))
- [Who benefits from it staying unresolved](#unresolved-questions "epistemic:measures")
- [What would help resolve it](#unresolved-questions "epistemic:measures")
- [Current best guesses with confidence levels](#unresolved-questions "epistemic:measures")

**Example**: {#unresolved-example}

Question: What's the optimal urban density for human flourishing?

Status: UNRESOLVED

[Confidence: 35%](#unresolved-example "epistemic:measures")

Why unresolved:

- Cultural variation high
- Long-term health impacts unclear
- Economic vs. social tradeoffs uncertain
- Climate context shifting

Beneficiaries of non-resolution:

- Developers (can build whatever)
- NIMBYs (can block everything)

Current best practice:

- Preserve diversity of density levels
- 10-year longitudinal studies underway
- Let cities experiment with guidelines
- Don't mandate single solution

Next review: 2028

## Failure Modes to Watch For {#failure-modes}

### Premature Optimization {#failure-premature}

[Collapsing to single solution too early](#failure-premature "dynamical:transforms_to")

- **Fix**: [Option space preservation](#option-space "symmetric:preserves"), pilot-first mandates, reversibility requirements

### Paradigm Capture {#failure-paradigm}

[One worldview dominates, alternatives unthinkable](#failure-paradigm "symmetric:limits")

- **Fix**: Institutionalize dissent, fund alternative approaches, mandatory red teams

### Temporal Arbitrage {#failure-temporal}

[Pushing costs beyond political cycles](#failure-temporal "geometric:causes")

- **Fix**: Multi-horizon review, generational accounting, citizen assemblies

### Meta-Occlusion {#failure-occlusion}

[The paradigm itself is invisible](#failure-occlusion "epistemic:limits")

- **Fix**: Cross-cultural comparison, history education, future scenario planning

## Worked Example: Climate Policy {#climate-example}

**Situation**: Nation designing climate adaptation strategy

**Diagnosis**: {#climate-diagnosis}

- Scale: Macro (200 million people)
- Resolution: Very low (can see national trends only)
- Uncertainty: Extreme (climate + social + tech unknowns)
- Temporal lag: Generational (50+ year impacts)
- [MPDC: Fully applies](#climate-diagnosis "symmetric:limits") (can't see full meta-pattern)

**Intervention**:

### Phase 1: Acknowledge Epistemic Limits {#climate-phase-1}

1. Document what we genuinely don't know:
    - Regional climate impacts (50% confidence)
    - Technology development timelines (30% confidence)
    - Social adaptation capacity (40% confidence)
    - Economic cascade effects (25% confidence)
2. [Make uncertainty visible in all planning](#climate-phase-1 "epistemic:supports")
3. Don't pretend precision we don't have

### Phase 2: Multi-Horizon Structure {#climate-phase-2}

[Phase 2](#climate-phase-2 "geometric:precedes") follows Phase 1.

1. **Immediate** (1-3 years):
    - Emergency response capacity
    - No-regret investments (efficiency, resilience)
    - Monitoring infrastructure
2. **Near-term** (5-10 years):
    - Major infrastructure decisions
    - Economic transition support
    - Community adaptation programs
3. **Long-term** (20-50 years):
    - Existential risk mitigation
    - [Preserve option space](#option-space "symmetric:preserves")
    - Avoid irreversible harms

### Phase 3: Option Space Preservation {#climate-phase-3}

1. **DON'T** mandate single solution nationwide
2. **DO** support [diverse regional approaches](#climate-phase-3 "ontological:composes"):
    - Coastal retreat in some areas
    - Hardening in others
    - Managed migration experiments
    - Multiple technology paths
3. **Monitor** which approaches work, preserve variety

### Phase 4: Curvature Analysis {#climate-phase-4}

1. [Who bears costs?](#climate-phase-4 "epistemic:measures") (Often: poor, rural, minority communities)
2. [Who captures benefits?](#climate-phase-4 "epistemic:measures") (Often: wealthy, urban, majority communities)
3. **Intervention**: [Regenerative investment](#climate-phase-4 "dynamical:transforms_to")
    - Communities bearing climate costs get first-priority adaptation funding
    - Extractive industries fund transition programs
    - Benefits distributed based on burden carried

### Phase 5: Participatory Sensing {#climate-phase-5}

1. [Citizen assemblies](#climate-phase-5 "ontological:composes") from affected communities
2. Indigenous knowledge integration
3. Front-line worker input (farmers, coastal residents, etc.)
4. Regular rotation of assembly membership

### Phase 6: Honest Unresolved States {#climate-phase-6}

Maintain public registry:

RESOLVED (high confidence):

- Climate is warming
- Human activity is primary cause
- Impacts will be severe without action

UNRESOLVED (genuinely uncertain):

- Optimal carbon price level
- Regional migration patterns
- Technology breakthrough timing
- Social cohesion under stress
- International coordination feasibility

APPROACH:

- No-regret actions immediately
- Diverse experiments regionally
- [Preserve future options](#option-space "symmetric:preserves")
- Adapt as we learn
- Review every 5 years

**Expected outcome**: Not "solving climate change" (impossible from embedded position), but [maintaining conditions for collective learning](#climate-example "dynamical:evolves") and adaptation across multiple possible futures

## Section 3: Cross-Scale Patterns {#cross-scale-patterns}

### Pattern 1: Scaling Up (Increasing Complexity) {#scaling-up}

**What happens when you move from micro → meso → macro**:

**Resolution drops**: {#resolution-drops}

- Micro: See individual faces
- Meso: See departmental patterns
- Macro: See statistical trends
- **Adaptation**: [Accept coarser grain, use aggregates honestly](#resolution-drops "epistemic:supports")

**Uncertainty rises**: {#uncertainty-rises}

- Micro: Generally know why things happen
- Meso: Emergent effects appear
- Macro: [Fundamental unpredictability (MPDC)](#uncertainty-rises "symmetric:limits")
- **Adaptation**: [Shift from control to option-space preservation](#option-space "dynamical:transforms_to")

**Feedback slows**: {#feedback-slows}

- Micro: Days/weeks
- Meso: Months/quarters
- Macro: Years/decades
- **Adaptation**: [Build in longer review cycles, multi-horizon thinking](#feedback-slows "geometric:causes")

**Tools change**: {#tools-change}

- Micro: 3-touch rule, direct repair
- Meso: AOMI, CLP queries, CFA monitoring
- Macro: [Curvature analysis](#curvature-monitoring "meta:corresponds_to"), [unresolved registries](#unresolved-states "meta:corresponds_to")
- **Adaptation**: [Match intervention to scale](#tools-change "symmetric:constrains")

### Pattern 2: Scaling Down (Reducing Complexity) {#scaling-down}

**What happens when you move from macro → meso → micro**:

**Common mistake**: [Bringing macro habits to micro scale](#scaling-down "meta:corresponds_to")

**Examples**:

- Using statistical language for individual people ("according to the data...")
- Deferring personal accountability to "systems"
- Waiting for studies instead of addressing immediate harm
- Over-complicating what should be simple

**Fix**: When you [scale down](#scaling-down "dynamical:transforms_to"):

- Increase directness
- Take personal responsibility
- Use felt consequence
- Simplify explanation

### Pattern 3: The Gaming Gradient {#gaming-gradient}

**Gaming sophistication increases with scale**:

**Micro**: Simple avoidance, blame-shifting {#gaming-micro}

- **Detection**: Usually obvious to participants
- **Fix**: Direct confrontation, natural consequences

**Meso**: Metric manipulation, responsibility laundering {#gaming-meso}

- **Detection**: Requires [AOMI-style monitoring](#template-aomi "epistemic:measures")
- **Fix**: Inverse scrutiny, reserve capacity, exploitability tracking

**Macro**: Paradigm capture, meta-gaming {#gaming-macro}

- **Detection**: Very difficult, requires outside perspective
- **Fix**: Institutionalized dissent, cross-cultural comparison, long memory

**Key insight**: As scale increases, [assume more sophisticated gaming](#gaming-gradient "epistemic:supports") and build in stronger resistance

## Section 4: Templates & Checklists {#templates}

### Template 1: Justice Diagnostic Worksheet {#justice-diagnostic}

**System under examination**: ________________________

**Scale identification**:

- [ ]  Micro (1-20 people)
- [ ]  Meso (20-10,000 people)

## Section 5: Common Failure Modes & Fixes {#failure-fixes}

### Failure Mode 1: Scale Mismatch {#failure-scale}

**Pattern**: [Using tools designed for one scale at another scale](#failure-scale "meta:corresponds_to")

**Example**: Demanding individual accountability for systemic outcomes

- "If people just tried harder..." ([micro thinking about macro problems](#scaling-up "meta:corresponds_to"))

**Example**: Treating individual harm as statistical noise

- "That's just one data point..." ([macro thinking about micro harm](#scaling-down "meta:corresponds_to"))

**Recognition**:

- Intervention feels wrong to participants

- [More effort](#effort-input "dynamical:interacts_with") [produces worse results](#diminishing-returns "dynamical:transforms_to")
- Frustration and resentment increase

**Fix**:

1. Re-diagnose the scale you're actually at
2. [Match intervention](#intervention-strategy "meta:corresponds_to") to [appropriate mode](#scale-matching "symmetric:constrains")
3. Accept limitations of that scale's resolution

## Failure Mode 2: Morphism Collapse {#failure-mode-2}

**Pattern**: One or more [morphisms](#morphism-definition "ontological:composes") fail under stress

### φ Collapse (Faith/Inertia) {#phi-collapse}

- **Symptom**: [Abandonment, panic reactions, emergency measures](#phi-symptoms "epistemic:measures")
- **Example**: "This is too hard, I'm done" or "We need to act NOW"
- **Fix**:
    - Slow down deliberately
    - Increase structural support
    - Build in tolerance for uncertainty
    - Practice staying present without knowing

### ψ Collapse (Hope/Anticipation) {#psi-collapse}

- **Symptom**: [Paralysis, waiting for perfect information, learned helplessness](#psi-symptoms "epistemic:measures")
- **Example**: "We need more data before we can decide" (indefinitely)
- **Fix**:
    - Set decision deadlines
    - Accept "good enough" information
    - Start with reversible experiments
    - Lower stakes for initial action

### λ Collapse (Love/Resonance) {#lambda-collapse}

- **Symptom**: [Hoarding, extraction, zero-sum thinking](#lambda-symptoms "epistemic:measures")
- **Example**: "I got mine" or "Every man for himself"
- **Fix**:
    - Make reciprocity visible and valuable
    - Increase consequence proximity
    - Build in regenerative mechanisms
    - Track field coherence, not just local wins

## Failure Mode 3: Compression Runaway {#failure-mode-3}

**Pattern**: [C_eff rises uncontrollably](#compression-dynamics "dynamical:transforms_to"), occlusion increases, system [loses ability to self-correct](#homeostasis-loss "symmetric:constrains")

**Stages**: {#compression-stages}

1. [Initial compression](#stage-1 "dynamical:transforms_to") (deadlines tighten)
2. [Reduced visibility](#stage-2 "dynamical:transforms_to") (no time to see impacts)
3. [More mistakes](#stage-3 "dynamical:transforms_to") (decisions made blind)
4. [More compression needed](#stage-4 "dynamical:transforms_to") (to handle mess)
5. [Even less visibility](#stage-5 "dynamical:transforms_to")
6. [**System collapse or gaming takeover**](#stage-6 "dynamical:transforms_to")

**Recognition**:

- Constant crisis mode
- Metrics gamed because no time for real measurement
- "We'll fix it later" becomes permanent
- People burned out, leaving
- Quality degrades despite more effort

**Fix**:

1. **Emergency brake**: [Stop taking on new commitments](#commitment-limit "symmetric:constrains")
2. **WIP reduction**: Finish or abandon current work before starting new
3. **Compression budget**: [Calculate C_eff, set hard limits](#compression-limit "symmetric:constrains")
4. **Reserve capacity**: Build in 20-30% slack permanently
5. **Slow-path protocols**: Certain decisions must have time buffers

**Critical**: This is one of the few times "slow down" is *required* not optional

## Failure Mode 4: False Precision {#failure-mode-4}

**Pattern**: [Claiming resolution](#resolution-claim "epistemic:refutes") you don't have, forcing closure prematurely

**Examples**:

- Performance reviews pretending to measure individual contribution in complex systems
- Impact assessments giving exact numbers for uncertain outcomes
- Policies claiming to solve problems beyond their resolution capacity

**Recognition**:

- Numbers look precise but feel arbitrary
- Reasonable people have wildly different assessments
- Gaming increases because metrics don't track reality
- Interventions fail despite "data-driven" approach

**Fix**:

1. **Honest uncertainty**: Use ranges, confidence intervals, "unresolved" states
2. **Resolution check**: [Does R × C_eff ≤ R_max?](#resolution-formula "symmetric:constrains") If not, accept coarser grain
3. **CLP approach**: [Make what's genuinely unknown explicitly unresolved](#clp-method "epistemic:measures")
4. **Probabilistic thinking**: "60% confident that..." not "The data shows..."

## Failure Mode 5: Paradigm Capture {#failure-mode-5}

**Pattern**: At macro scale, one [worldview](#paradigm-definition "ontological:composes") becomes invisible because it's everywhere

**Examples**:

- Economic growth as unquestioned goal
- Technological progress as inevitable good
- Western models as universal applicable
- Current values as timeless truths

**Recognition**:

- Alternatives seem not just wrong but unthinkable
- Questioning basics gets you labeled crazy or dangerous
- Young people don't even know alternatives existed
- [Meta-pattern (MPDC) is invisible](#mpdc-occlusion "epistemic:measures")

**Fix**:

1. **Historical perspective**: What did people believe 100 years ago? What will they believe in 100 years?
2. **Cross-cultural comparison**: How do other cultures approach this?
3. **Institutionalized dissent**: Fund people to argue for alternatives
4. **Future scenarios**: What if we're wrong? What's the cost?
5. **Option preservation**: Keep paths open even if you disagree with them

## Section 6: Integration Exercise {#section-6}

**Working a Real Case Through the Framework**

Let's walk through a complete analysis using the tools:

**Case**: [Tech company (5,000 employees) considering AI-powered productivity monitoring](#tech-case "ontological:defines")

### Step 1: Diagnostic {#step-1-diagnostic}

Scale: Meso (organization)

Epistemic profile:

- Uncertainty: High (AI outcomes unpredictable)
- Resolution: Medium (can see department patterns)
- Temporal lag: Medium (effects over months)

Morphisms:

- φ: [At risk](#phi-risk "ontological:composes") (employees uncertain about AI)
- ψ: [Present](#psi-present "ontological:composes") (company acting despite uncertainty)
- λ: [Failing](#lambda-failure "ontological:composes") (extractive relationship, company benefits, employees bear costs)

Compression: [R × C_eff ≤ R_max?](#compression-check "symmetric:constrains")

- R = 7 (trying to track individual productivity)
- C_eff = 8 (fast-paced tech environment)
- R_max = 6 (limited HR capacity)
- 7 × 8 = 56 > 6 = **VIOLATION**

Occlusion:

- Temporal: High (fast decisions, slow impacts)
- Topological: High (leadership insulated from surveillance experience)
- Cognitive: High (AI black box, complex metrics)
- **Overall Ω: Very High**

Who benefits from high Ω:

- Leadership (easier control, plausible deniability)
- Tech vendors (sell system)
- Shareholders (short-term productivity gains)

### Step 2: Apply AOMI {#step-2-aomi}

[High Ω triggers paradoxical policing](#aomi-trigger "geometric:causes"):

- This decision should face ENHANCED scrutiny, not rushed
- Red flag: Leadership wants fast implementation
- Suggests gaming of normal review process

### Step 3: CLP Query {#step-3-clp}

Intent: [Determine if AI monitoring increases productivity](#clp-intent "epistemic:measures") without unacceptable costs

Frame: `[employee.experience, productivity.metrics, privacy.rights, organizational.culture]`

Resolution requirements:

- min_support: 10 independent studies
- confidence: 0.90
- attention_budget: 3 months investigation

Results:

RESOLVED:

- AI monitoring can increase certain metrics
- [Support: 7 studies](#clp-support "epistemic:supports")
- Confidence: 0.75
- Note: "Certain metrics" ≠ actual productivity

UNRESOLVED:

- Does it increase real productivity? (Confidence: 0.40)
- Why: [Metric gaming, Goodhart's law effects](#gaming-cause "geometric:causes")
- Beneficiaries: Vendors, management
- What are psychological costs? (Confidence: 0.35)
- Why: Long-term effects unknown
- Beneficiaries: Those not monitored
- Culture impact? (Confidence: 0.30)
- Why: [Emergent, nonlinear effects](#emergence "meta:emerges_from")
- Beneficiaries: Executives (insulated)

Telemetry:

- Coherence: 0.42 (low)
- Occlusion: Very High
- Gaming indicators: Detected (vendor lobbying)

### Step 4: Decision Tree {#step-4-decision}

Given:

- [R × C_eff violation](#violation-ref "symmetric:constrains") (system can't handle this resolution under compression)
- Very high Ω (decision-makers won't feel consequences)
- λ morphism failing (extractive dynamics)
- Major unresolved clusters (< 50% confidence on key questions)
- Gaming detected

**Recommendation**: [DO NOT PROCEED](#recommendation-stop "geometric:causes") with full implementation

**Alternative approaches**:

**Option A: Slow Down + Pilot**

1. 6-month pilot with 50 volunteers
2. Leadership also monitored (reduce topological occlusion)
3. Weekly check-ins with participants (reduce Δt)
4. Clear reversibility criteria
5. Full transparency on data use

**Option B: Coarsen Grain**

1. Don't track individuals (violates R × C_eff)
2. Use aggregate team metrics only
3. Voluntary opt-in
4. Focus on workflow tools, not surveillance

**Option C: Expand Capacity First**

1. Increase R_max (hire more HR, reduce manager loads)
2. Reduce C_eff (slow down pace, fewer concurrent initiatives)
3. Then reassess if monitoring still needed

**Option D: Address Root Cause**

1. Why do we think we need monitoring?
2. What actual problem are we solving?
3. Are there less invasive approaches?
4. Might this be treating symptom not cause?

### Step 5: Implementation (choosing Option A) {#step-5-implementation}

**Pilot structure**:

Weeks 1-4:

- Recruit 50 volunteers (truly voluntary, no pressure)
- Include executives in monitoring (essential for λ morphism)
- Set clear success criteria (what would make us stop?)
- Establish weekly feedback loop

Weeks 5-12:

- Monitor both metrics AND experience
- Track unresolved clusters:
    - What's improving? (Data with confidence levels)
    - What's degrading? (Data with confidence levels)
    - What can't we tell? (Honest "unresolved")
- Watch for gaming (threshold dancing, explanation complexity)

Week 13-24:

- Expand to 200 if (and only if):
    - Real productivity increase (not just metrics)
    - Psychological costs acceptable to participants
    - No gaming detected
    - Executives remain monitored
- Otherwise: Discontinue or redesign

### Step 6: Ongoing Monitoring {#step-6-monitoring}

AOMI dashboard:

- Ξ (exploitability index): Track monthly
- Gaming indicators: Continuous detection
- Employee wellbeing: Quarterly surveys
- Productivity: Multiple measures (don't trust single metric)

CFA balance:

- C (constraint): Has system become rigid?
- F (fluctuation): Can people still innovate?
- A (attention): Is this consuming disproportionate energy?

Morphism health:

- φ: [Are people staying engaged](#phi-health "ontological:composes") despite uncertainty?
- ψ: [Are we acting adaptively](#psi-health "ontological:composes") with incomplete information?
- λ: [Is this regenerative or extractive?](#lambda-health "ontological:composes")

**Expected outcome**: Either genuinely useful tool with acceptable tradeoffs (rare) or [early detection](#early-warning "epistemic:measures") that this doesn't work before damaging entire organization (likely)

## Conclusion: From Tools to Practice {#conclusion}

You now have:

**Diagnostic frameworks** to understand what you're looking at

**Mode-specific toolkits** for each scale of operation

**Templates and checklists** for systematic application

**Failure mode recognition** so you know what's breaking

**Worked examples** showing the full process

**The pattern**: [Justice across scales](#justice-pattern "meta:corresponds_to") isn't mysterious. It's:

1. **Recognize your scale** (and its epistemic limits)
2. **Check your morphisms** (what's failing?)
3. **Calculate your compression** ([respecting R × C_eff ≤ R_max](#compression-law "symmetric:constrains"))
4. **Map your occlusion** (where is harm invisible?)
5. **Choose appropriate mode** (Direct/Systems/Structural)
6. **Apply scale-matched tools** (not one-size-fits-all)
7. **Monitor for failure modes** (especially gaming and compression runaway)
8. **Accept honest uncertainty** ("unresolved" is valid output)

**Start small**: Pick one situation you're actually facing. Work through the diagnostic. Try one intervention. See what happens.

**Scale up**: As you get comfortable with the tools, apply them to larger systems.

**Adapt**: These are patterns, not rigid rules. Modify for your context. What works in your domain?

**Remember**: The framework isn't about achieving perfect justice (impossible given epistemic limits). It's about:

- Seeing clearly what's actually happening
- Knowing what's possible at each scale
- Intervening effectively within those limits
- Accepting what remains genuinely unresolved

[**Justice is practice**](#justice-definition "meta:corresponds_to"), not perfection. This gives you a way to practice with clarity.
