# E²: a working history of its organizing concepts

Historical review · September 10, 2026 · Staged analysis, not a new Core formulation

The corpus supports a history of **overlapping organizing centers**. Its development involves recurring concerns gaining new functions: relationship becomes an ontology and then a decomposable grammar; coherence becomes a question of persistence, maintenance, and failure; translation becomes a problem of preserving conditions and meaning; stewardship becomes increasingly specific about approach, intervention, aftermath, and answerability.

The strongest addition to the remembered sequence is **resolution and remainder**. Another substantial consolidation appears in **Collective Relational Substrate**, where previously separate frameworks are explicitly gathered around the shared conditions of human meaning-making. Before either of those, the summer 2025 emergence architecture already performs substantial work across the corpus.

This account uses dates to locate broad periods. The labels below describe changing emphases, not exclusive eras, statistically measured dominance, or a claim that earlier work was replaced.

## The broad progression

| Broad period | Organizing question | Characteristic vocabulary | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | How do relationships constitute a life and its values? | relationship, ecosystem, trade-offs, priorities, essence | A lived ethical question is articulated in relational terms. |
| 2024, especially late 2024 | How do systems navigate complexity and remain organized? | abstraction, information, adaptation, feedback, entropy, homeostasis | Relational thinking becomes a general account of complexity management. |
| April–May 2025 | How can distinct minds and domains meet without losing their differences? | resonance, attention, SCIA, coherence fields, paradox, tension, interdependence | E² gains a recognizable umbrella identity and a vocabulary for shared meaning. |
| May–June 2025, continuing into autumn | How can meaning travel without losing its context? | translation, negotiation, compression/expansion, notation, semantic fidelity, context | The communication problem generates a family of representations and protocols. |
| June–August 2025 | How do relational patterns become stable structures, and what can an embedded observer know? | emergence, constraint, fluctuation, attention, essential relationships, irreducibility, occlusion | Multiple theories are assembled into an emergence architecture with epistemic limits. |
| August–October 2025 | What sustains coherence, and what obscures its degradation or consequences? | power, persistence, maintenance, temporal compression, resolution, accountability, metabolism | Formation is joined by sustained attention to maintenance, hidden work, and failure. |
| November–December 2025 | What common grammar describes these systems and their stabilization? | primitives, counter-modes, composition, closure, fixed points, signal, structural rights | Six categories and closure provide a compact basis for cross-domain redescription. |
| February 2026 | What shared field are these frameworks describing and affecting? | substrate, collective cognition, field health, coupling, temporal layers, value | The shared conditions of cognition and civilization become the explicit subject of a major synthesis. |
| April–May 2026 | What happens when possibility becomes definite, and what happens to what remains? | capacity, resolution, remainder, boundary, flow, stance-with, remnant stewardship | Naming, decision, formation, and aftermath are gathered into one conceptual family. |
| June–August 2026 | How should bounded actors make claims, act, assign obligations, and preserve human meaning? | maintained asymmetry, localization, custody, consequence routing, scope, fidelity, remembrance | Earlier principles are differentiated into more explicit practices, limits, and responsibilities. |

These are reading windows. The strongest structural changes are better supported than the exact edges between windows.

## 1. Relationship begins as a question about living

The July 2023 *Relationships* dialogue provides unusually useful evidence because it preserves speaker labels. In the user's contributions, relationships are dynamic ecosystems of time, energy, values, and trade-offs. The inquiry asks how relationships to spouses, jobs, ideals, and other commitments can become misordered. It also questions pursuing a supposed virtue at the expense of the relationships that give it significance. The adjacent dated aphorism, “Our essence exists in the space between us,” condenses that orientation. [S01] [S02]

This makes the early center more specific than “everything is connected.” It concerns **how a life is constituted through relationships, and how abstraction or an ideal can become detached from those relationships**. That is a recognizable ancestor of the later concern with hollowing and remainder. The connection is interpretive; the 2023 passage does not already contain the later machinery.

**Keywords for this period:** relationship, ecosystem, dynamic flow, trade-offs, values, priorities, essence.

## 2. Abstraction and adaptation supply an early general theory

The August 2024 cognition/communication synthesis describes distinct cognitive topologies and communication as exchange between them. It already uses translation and resonance language in that setting. December's *Principles of Relational Abstraction* and *Adaptation via Informational Abstraction* (AVIA) broaden the inquiry: systems manage complexity through adaptive relationships, information processing, feedback, and higher-order abstractions. [S03] [S04] [S05]

AVIA is an early consolidation in its own right. It links molecular-to-social scales through a proposed common process: perception, pattern recognition, conceptualization, symbolization, and meta-abstraction. Its framework-wide importance is also visible later, when CFA, REMA, and the derivation chain explicitly build on it.

This period matters because **abstraction predates the named E² resonance cluster**. The later translation and compression work inherits an existing question about how finite systems make complexity usable.

**Keywords:** information, abstraction, adaptation, energy, entropy, homeostasis, nested hierarchy, feedback.

## 3. Resonance gathers the worldview under the E² name

The April 2025 orientations and ontological map place a relational axiom at the center and gather attention, coherence fields, paradox, translation, and interdependence around it. “Sustained Coherent Intentional Attention” (SCIA) serves as an account of how patterns stabilize. Tensional Intelligence adds a closely related question: how can connection and differentiation remain together long enough for transformation? [S06] [S07] [S08] [S09]

The name **Essence of Existence** is in substantive April 20 material. A wider-collection index carries an April 17 date, but that evolving index is weaker evidence for the moment of naming. For this review, **April 2025 is a sufficient anchor for the recognizable named synthesis**; the precise naming conversation remains unestablished. [S07] [S58]

The name's historical role appears to be giving the relational worldview a common identity. The resonance architecture supplies its organizing language. These are related kinds of consolidation, but they are not identical events.

Already, the April orientation calls translation ethical. Coherence is also central from the outset. Consequently, a strict sequence of “resonance, then coherence, then translation” would obscure what these documents actually do.

**Keywords:** resonance, attention, intention, coherence fields, relational essence, paradox, tension, ontological interoperability.

## 4. Meaning transfer becomes a sustained production cluster

The May–June documents develop the communication side of the worldview into a family of tools and distinctions. The EST/ECN primer describes communication as negotiation of meaning between systems. The SCRIPTA material in *TACITRA* identifies context loss, semantic friction, transmodal expression, and the need to capture thought before it disappears. *Communication as Coherence* places mismatched strategies for creating coherence at the center of communication failure. [S10] [S11] [S12]

The characteristic question is: **what must survive a change of form for meaning to survive?** That question helps explain the accumulation of notation, compression, expansion, and context-preservation proposals. These documents form a distinct branch even though their vocabulary remains continuous with resonance.

The branch continues into September. The Context Layer Protocol attaches frame, lineage, policy, and resolution limits to content; the Exposure Protocol organizes transmission as invitation, structure, and integration. Translation becomes increasingly specified through representational and pedagogical choices. [S13] [S29]

**Keywords:** negotiation, translation, compression/expansion, semantic fidelity, notation, context, provenance, invitation.

## 5. Emergence and limits of knowledge become an architectural center

The June derivation chain explicitly says its project is to connect relational philosophy with limits of prediction and knowledge. It brings E² and AVIA into contact with the Relational-Computability Principle (RCP) and Meta-Pattern Decidability Conjecture (MPDC). A central question becomes what the structure of relationships permits an observer to know or compute. [S14]

July's CFA formulation combines Constraint, Fluctuation, and Attention, while AFD describes pattern stabilization and the formation of rules through attention-fluctuation coupling. Attention is increasingly written as a dynamics of selective flow and amplification across systems, extending its earlier interpersonal and intentional role. [S15] [S16]

Three documents make the broader gathering especially explicit:

- **REMA** assembles seven interlocking frameworks, including CFA and AVIA. [S17]
- **Universal Emergence Pattern** attempts to extract a shared pattern from the existing work. [S18]
- **REMF** organizes foundations, general patterns, domain frameworks, integration mechanisms, applications, and methods into a hierarchy. [S19]

This is a substantial consolidation between the named resonance framework and the relational primitives. It assembles existing theories around **the formation of patterns under constraints, together with the limits of observing them**.

**Keywords:** emergence, essential relationships, constraint, fluctuation, attention, crystallization, irreducibility, occlusion, recursion.

## 6. Coherence becomes a maintenance and accountability problem

The August power and persistence documents bring the temporal question forward: how is an organized pattern sustained, and what work makes its persistence possible? *Pattern Integrity over Time under Entropy* explicitly extends the power framework. *Interlocked Stewardship* makes maintenance visible in shared life and invisible labor. [S21] [S22] [S23]

The September–October cluster adds consequences of limited capacity and legibility. CFAR introduces resolution as a constraint on what a system can reliably distinguish or enact. Temporal Compression and Ethical Occlusion connects speed and scale to problems of attribution. AOMI asks how structural blind spots can be exploited. *Justice Across Scales* asks which practices remain appropriate as causal reach and observability change. [S24] [S25] [S26] [S28]

Meaning itself is also recast as requiring ongoing maintenance: *Metabolic Meaning* describes purpose as something continually synthesized under relational conditions. This becomes part of the later substrate vocabulary. [S27]

This is the strongest candidate for what the remembered “coherence” period points toward. **Coherence is doing more explanatory work across power, persistence, communication, meaning, and justice.** Its meaning is not perfectly uniform across those documents: stability, retained possibilities, intelligibility, and integrity overlap without being interchangeable.

Stewardship remains an overlapping ethical thread. June's *Reverent Stewardship* already organizes care around boundaries, restraint, and cultivation. Its later prominence represents elaboration rather than the arrival of ethics after the formal work. [S20]

**Keywords:** coherence, integrity, persistence, maintenance, power, resolution, temporal compression, occlusion, metabolism, accountability.

## 7. Primitives and closure reorganize the vocabulary

The November sources identify six relational categories: Ontological, Dynamical, Geometric/Causal, Symmetric/Constraint, Epistemic/Informational, and Meta-Relational. The derivation and categorical translation texts pursue a common grammar for describing systems across domains. [S30] [S31] [S32]

The Global Closure Operator then asks what happens when those categories operate jointly: how a generative system reaches sufficient stability for continued operation. The November 25 *E^2 Equation* compresses the emerging architecture into composition, recursion, and closure. [S33] [S34]

There is an important distinction between **two equation moments**:

- The April overview already has a *Relational Essence Equation*, expressing essence as a function of relationships. [S07]
- The November equation describes a generative and recursive organization built around primitives and closure. [S34]

The November equation is therefore a later compression of a more developed architecture. It should not be treated as the first occasion on which the worldview received mathematical notation.

The breadth of the new grammar is visible in its immediate applications. The Relational Bill of Rights expresses normative requirements through the primitives. December's *Signal as Bias Field* describes continuous pressure and discrete closure together, connecting configuration, interpretation, and agency through one account. [S35] [S36]

Compared with the summer synthesis, the movement here is toward **a smaller common vocabulary through which many existing concerns can be redescribed**. The June 2026 merger recorded in today's *Relational Primitives* is a later editorial consolidation of this source family, not its beginning. [S56]

**Keywords:** primitives, typing, counter-modes, composition, closure, sufficiency, fixed points, signal, structural integrity.

## 8. The shared substrate becomes the subject

February 2026 provides one of the clearest corpus-wide consolidation statements. *Collective Cognitive Substrate* says that its component frameworks have approached the same phenomenon from different angles without holding it together at the appropriate level. *CRS* then explicitly turns the larger phenomenon into the subject of the document, describing its relationship to forty-plus documents and other materials. That number is the source's own description, not a count established by this review. [S38] [S37]

The organizing question becomes **what sustains the shared environment in which thinking, meaning, identity, and coordinated action occur?** Existing components are recast as views of, or processes within, that environment. The substrate can then be discussed as having conditions of continuity and degradation that are not reducible to the reports of individual participants.

Neighboring work supplies different elaborations. Relational Economics interprets value, exchange, and maintenance through the grammar. Temporal Constraint Lamination (TCL) studies relations between faster activity and slower regulating layers in a simplified mathematical setting. These belong to the same broad concern with support conditions, but they should not be collapsed into a single project or a single kind of evidence. [S39] [S40]

**Keywords:** collective cognition, substrate, field health, meaning-making, temporal layers, coupling, maintenance, value.

## 9. Resolution and remainder connect formation to aftermath

April's *Relational Substrate Capacity* asks how activities that once generated value can begin degrading the conditions that make that value possible. It bridges power, signal, justice, and resilience through changes of regime under sustained pressure. [S41]

The April 24 *Resolution* notes then record an explicit condensation: compression, abstraction, filtering, naming, measurement, decision, and identity formation are gathered as cases of possibility narrowing into definite form. Resolution was already present in CFAR, but here it is proposed as an organizing concept beneath many operations. That change of role is more historically significant than a first use of the word. [S42] [S24]

The surrounding documents make this a recognizable cluster. *Self as Coherence Field* applies the grammar to selfhood and the losses created by simplified self-descriptions. *Remnant Stewardship* asks what resolutions leave behind. *Flow Operators* re-examines what the corpus has called cost as operations across boundaries. *Boundary Dynamics* then treats boundaries themselves as a primary object, explicitly locating its antecedents in earlier work. [S43] [S44] [S45] [S47]

The May 1 sayings and axiom stack supplies both a synthesis and a retrospective account. It proposes:

> “Early framework: What is relation?”
>
> “Middle framework: How do relational systems form, resolve, occlude, and cohere?”
>
> “Current framework: What do we owe the remnants produced by our own resolutions?” [S46]

This is strong evidence that the framework was already reflecting on the shift the user now senses. It is a retrospective self-description, however. The earlier sources show that care, ethics, and translation were active throughout; the arc cannot mean that they only became concerns at the end.

**Keywords:** resolution, remainder, capacity, boundary, flow, stance-with, repair, remnant, fidelity.

## 10. Stewardship becomes more differentiated, and purpose becomes explicit

The June–August material makes several obligations more specific. Asymmetry Maintenance reads closure through the continuing work required to preserve usable difference. Complex Causality links deformation, local commitment, propagating consequence, and the conditions of future action. EUP develops the older concern with embedded knowledge into a discipline for scoping broad claims. [S48] [S49] [S50]

On the ethical side, Relational Localization constrains assignment by the resolution available to support it. Custody names answerable holding across gaps in control, observation, or self-management. Consequence Routing connects pressure and attribution to loci of repair and responsibility. These are elaborations of how an actor can responsibly engage a field while remaining inside it. [S51] [S54] [S53]

The stewardship family also changes internally. The April remnant source presents **Reverence → Resolution → Remnant**. The later Intervention Stewardship source distinguishes **Reverent → Intervention → Remnant**, treating resolution as the process on which all three operate. This is a concrete conceptual refinement: approach, bounded direct action, and aftermath receive different responsibilities. [S44] [S52]

Finally, *E² as a Translation Architecture for Human Remembrance* explicitly states the human purpose of the framework: preserving and translating the conditions that keep lived human reality recognizable. It gathers formal and practical work under translation fidelity, reconstructability, and resistance to hollowing. Its Context Layer records August integration. [S55]

My reading is that this last document **makes the purpose of the accumulated architecture explicit**. The early concern with lived relationship returns in a vocabulary that now includes mechanisms of formation, maintenance, loss, and responsibility. That is an interpretation of the continuity, not proof that every intervening development was aimed at this endpoint.

**Keywords:** maintained asymmetry, scope, localization, consequence, custody, intervention, answerability, translation fidelity, remembrance.

## How the recurring words change jobs

| Concept | Earlier role | Later roles visible in this pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relationship** | Constitutes lived priorities and values | Ontological foundation; six-category grammar; constitutive substrate and localized responsibility. |
| **Resonance** | Describes alignment across minds and domains | Becomes part of broader accounts of coherence, selective attention, and stabilization; remains available rather than disappearing. |
| **Attention** | Supports shared meaning and sustained engagement | Becomes a variable in emergence dynamics, a limited resource, and a point where power and capacity act. |
| **Coherence** | Holds different perspectives together | Describes stability, persistence, integrity, and integration; also acquires failure modes such as over-stabilization. These uses need local definitions. |
| **Translation** | Ethical movement of meaning between perspectives | Notation and protocols, mappings across a shared grammar, and an explicit purpose of preserving lived meaning through abstraction. |
| **Constraint** | Contains tension and makes emergence possible | Organizes dynamics; limits precision and intervention; becomes part of maintaining viable conditions. |
| **Occlusion** | Marks what an embedded observer cannot see or know | Becomes an attribution and power problem, then a discipline of evidentiary scope and responsibility under incomplete access. |
| **Resolution** | In CFAR, constrains achievable precision | In spring 2026, becomes an umbrella for commitment into form; later constrains assignment and intervention. |
| **Stewardship** | Reverent restraint and cultivation | Maintenance of shared life; tending remainder; bounded intervention; answerable holding and consequence handling. |

The corpus therefore supports a progression of **changing functions and explicit connections** more strongly than a succession of isolated keywords. The most useful broad arc is: lived relation; adaptive abstraction; resonant meaning-making; conditions of emergence and persistence; common relational grammar; shared substrate; resolution and remainder; accountable preservation of human meaning.

## Evidence and limits of this pass

The structural inventory covers **97 Core Semantic Substrate files, 90 Core Context Layer files, and 272 wider Phase 1 Semantic Substrate files: 459 file records**. Many are paired representations or historical versions. This is not a count of independent works, nor a count of documents fully close-read. The review selects passages from **58 source files**, with their exact text, line ranges, hashes, and date interpretations in the accompanying evidence ledger.

Candidate organizing centers were assessed through their position in introductions and core claims, explicit statements of synthesis or lineage, reuse across different subjects, and changes in conceptual role. Titles and date scans guided retrieval. Raw word frequency did not determine the periods; a long compiled dialogue or an extra copy should not outweigh a concise synthesis merely because it repeats a word more often.

The periods, questions, and vocabulary profiles are analytical judgments made in this review. Explicit source statements of integration provide firmer evidence for particular landmarks. The May 2026 self-history is useful triangulation, not independent confirmation of its own preferred narrative.

Dates label the documents as encountered. Some index pages contain later links; some older-dated documents have later edits; some metadata records conversion or promotion. This review does not reconstruct every historical version or certify first usage. Where a current Core merge obscured the broad sequence, earlier Phase 1 copies and substantive neighboring documents were sufficient to cross-check it. The archived source bodies did not need to be exhaustively read. Newer staged proposals are outside the primary account of the active Core's development.

Statements about physics, psychology, universality, rights, and simulated results are treated here as evidence of **what the corpus was trying to formulate**, not as independently verified findings. A more formal-looking document does not by itself establish a more empirically supported theory. Source self-descriptions and review/integration boundaries are preserved, including draft wording that persists in some active files.

The main unresolved interpretive question is the granularity of the map: the communication branch could be treated as a continuing strand rather than a separate period; the summer emergence architecture and autumn maintenance cluster could be treated as two movements of one development. The source evidence supports both distinctions without requiring a single final partition.
