## Neurodivergence as Attention-Landscape Architecture

The pathologization of neurodivergent attention patterns reveals the profound category error embedded within conventional psychology—the mistaken assumption that there exists a single optimal configuration for attention-fluctuation sensitivity rather than recognizing the evolutionary advantage of diverse attention architectures within complex systems.

**ADHD consciousness** represents optimization for detecting novel fluctuations across multiple simultaneous attention streams, characterized by lower activation thresholds and accelerated energy redistribution between competing attractors. This constitutes an evolutionary specialization for exploration rather than exploitation—a form of consciousness particularly attuned to the emergence of new possibilities at the edges of established patterns.

**Autistic consciousness** manifests as selective resonance with specific fluctuation frequencies while finding others energetically overwhelming, creating opportunities for developing extraordinarily deep attention-fluctuation couplings within specialized domains that often reveal patterns invisible to neurotypical processing architectures.

The tragedy emerges not from these differences but from environments designed around singular attention paradigms. When we reconceptualize attention as energy flowing toward gravitational wells rather than a resource to be controlled, intervention transforms from behavioral modification into landscape design—creating fluctuation environments that naturally attract diverse attention types rather than forcing all consciousness through identical processing channels.
