# Intelligence Field - REMA / SCF Review

Status: staged, noncanonical review; sources inspected 2026-09-29.

## Question and disposition

The active human/ORMD pair, `E2Core/Semantic Substrate/The Intelligence Field Framework.md` and `E2Core/Context Layer/The Intelligence Field Framework.ormd`, should not be merged into REMA or treated as a consciousness account. Its distinct, potentially useful contribution is a task-oriented model of intelligence as navigation through a changing cognitive landscape. The best retention is a **scoped functional intelligence/navigation note**, kept distinct from the Self as Coherence Field (SCF) capacity profile. If a later integration pass cannot identify an operationally distinct use beyond existing active intelligence/attention material, retain it as a historical precursor and archive only after that comparison and reference audit.

Do not retain the source’s consciousness/agency definitions as established claims. Observable performance and adaptive navigation can support hypotheses about functional capacities; they do not establish awareness, subjective experience, qualia, volition, welfare, or moral standing. Do not revive REMA’s ladder or its proposed qualia mechanism through this source.

## Evidence and confidence limits

This is a source-comparison review, not empirical validation. The Intelligence Field source offers a coherent vocabulary for a possible functional research question, but no study or operational protocol in the inspected pair establishes that its dimensions measure a unified construct, generalize across tasks or substrates, predict outcomes, or distinguish intelligence from attention, expertise, resources, or task familiarity. The archived REMA/REMF claims cannot supply that missing evidence. SCF’s boundary is the appropriate current constraint: observed capacity is assessable only under a specified scope; subjective experience and moral status remain separate, unresolved matters.
