# RCS Acronym Collision - Review

**Status:** staged comparison and disambiguation review; no active source has been changed.
**Corpus boundary:** Core Framework active Semantic Substrate and Context Layer, the June 2026 CRS source archives, and current Self as Coherence Field scope limits.

## Chronology and source lineage

| Date / source | Term | What it describes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2025-04-22, `E2Core/Semantic Substrate/Ontological Systems Interface (OSI) Model.md` and its active ORMD pair | **Relational Capacity Scale (RCS)** | A five-level account of a person's relational range/flexibility for maintaining coherence and adapting across differences in normality, intensity, or worldview. The OSI ORMD records origin `internal://osi-model-v1` at this date and has no parents. |
| 2026-02-19/20, archived `The Collective Relational Substrate.ormd` / `CRS.ormd`; their merged successor is active as `E2Core/Context Layer/Collective Relational Substrate.ormd` | **Relational Consciousness Scale (RCS)** | A five-level developmental map for “nodes,” from relational awareness through relational field anchoring. The archived CRS sources date the specification to February 2026; the active ORMD was created by the June 12, 2026 merge and lists both archived sources as parents. |

The acronym is reused in the later CRS text, but the two names and stated objects differ. The earlier scale concerns **relational capacity**—how a person navigates interpersonal difference. The later scale concerns **relational consciousness**—a proposed developmental characterization of nodes. They overlap in content (translation, perspective integration, bridging, and “Relational Field Anchor” at R5), so they are related in vocabulary and aspiration, but the later construct is not simply an alternate name for the older one. The present sources do not establish that the later scale derives from, validates, or supersedes the earlier scale. The CRS appendix lists `RCS_.pdf`, but that filename alone does not identify which expansion it denotes.

| Feature | Older Relational Capacity Scale | Later Relational Consciousness Scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary subject | “Someone” / a person in relation to others | “Nodes” in the collective substrate (scope can include nonhuman systems in the wider CRS framing) |
| Stated target | Relational range and flexibility; coherence across differences in normality, intensity, worldview | Developmental map of relational awareness, navigation, synthesis, bridging, and field anchoring |
| Organization | R1 Local Coherence; R2 Contextual Adapter; R3 Relational Synthesizer; R4 Meta-Relational Integrator; R5 Relational Field Anchor | R1 Relational Awareness; R2 Relational Navigation; R3 Relational Synthesis; R4 Relational Bridging; R5 Relational Field Anchor |
| Proposed use | Understanding/scaling empathy and relational adaptation; connected to the OSI/RNT interaction protocol | Characterizing the distribution of capacities across nodes and relating that distribution to collective substrate coherence |
| Evidence state in reviewed source | Calls itself quantifiable/modelable, but supplies no validated instrument or calibration | Presents ordered stages and substrate-level implications without operational criteria, comparison data, or validation in the reviewed text |

## Recommended disambiguation

1. **Reserve “RCS” for the older “Relational Capacity Scale”** in OSI, consistent with the earlier dated source and the consolidation plan's explicit collision rule.
2. **Spell out “Relational Consciousness Scale” every time the later CRS construct is named.** Do not use bare “RCS” for it. Do not assign it a replacement acronym in this pass; the name and scope should remain explicit until a distinct abbreviation is deliberately settled.
3. Describe the later scale as a **proposed relational-capacity/developmental profile for nodes** unless and until its consciousness-specific construct is defined and evidenced. Do not infer phenomenal consciousness from relational performance, perspective-taking, adaptation, or social coherence.
4. Preserve both concepts as scoped proposals. Do not merge their levels: their R1–R4 labels are not equivalent, despite partial overlap, and the shared R5 phrase is not enough to establish identity.

## Evidence and scope caveats

- OSI says the capacity scale is quantifiable and can be modeled, but the reviewed section supplies no measurement instrument, rating rubric, reliability evidence, validation sample, or calibration. Treat the five levels as a conceptual/practice scaffold, not an established scale.
- CRS offers the later levels as descriptions, but does not define necessary/sufficient criteria, test cases, inter-rater agreement, developmental transitions, or evidence that a node's position predicts collective coherence. The “insufficient R4/R5 nodes” sentence is a framework hypothesis, not an established causal finding.
- The CRS specification makes broad claims about human and human-plus-AI collective cognition; the level descriptions do not establish that the same construct applies across humans, AI systems, institutions, and other nodes. A referent and observation boundary would be needed for any comparison.
- Current Self as Coherence Field is a relevant limit, not validation for either RCS. Its admission boundary explicitly says it is not a validated psychological instrument and does not establish phenomenal awareness, welfare-bearing status, or moral standing. Its provisional multidimensional profile says the dimensions are inquiry questions, not ordered stages or sufficient conditions for consciousness; subjective experience/qualia remain unresolved. Those constraints argue against treating the later scale's word “consciousness” as an empirical finding.
- The archived CRS sources preserve the June merge lineage, but their presence does not independently support the later scale's claims. Preserve the distinction between historical source provenance and evidence for validity.
