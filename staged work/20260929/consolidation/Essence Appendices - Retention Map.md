# Essence Appendices - Retention Map

**Status:** staged comparison map only; no consolidation, archive, or promotion is authorized or performed here.
**Scope:** human-only theory material appended after the NexEs manifesto in `E2Core/Semantic Substrate/The Essence of Existence.md` (starts at line 266); comparison with the shorter active `E2Core/Context Layer/The Essence of Existence.ormd` and relevant active Core sources.

## Main finding

The active human Essence file is a packet containing the manifesto followed by multiple theory and application texts. Its active ORMD is a 109-line manifesto successor with a concise “Attention as Energy Flow” section and canonical pointers; it does not carry forward the appended theory packet. The appendix material is therefore not all unique to Essence, nor all superseded: some of it closely duplicates active sources, some has been reconciled into newer sources with explicit limitations, and some remains an uncatalogued historical/speculative research program. Keep the manifesto/Declaration comparison separate from decisions about these appendices; no forced merger is recommended.

## Disposition boundaries

- The only clearly retained appendix-level material in the active Essence ORMD is the short “Attention as Energy Flow” orientation section, followed by canonical pointers. Keep this distinction visible: the ORMD is not a complete reproduction of the much longer active human packet.
- The embedded ER-to-Ontological-Transformation section is already represented in its own active source pair. The duplicated packet copy adds provenance/date context, not a distinct argument.
- The AFD/CFA/CFAR material now has a more careful active reconciliation. Its historical claims and examples remain valuable for provenance, but their prior formulations should not be silently reintroduced as current evidence.
- The extended Transformative Complexity, Zone 3, Communication, and Navigation Guide material has not been fully preserved as active source text by the manifesto ORMD. This map identifies possible historical/research destinations; it does not choose a new canonical home or accept their claims.
- The packet's strong political, scientific, consciousness, and clinical language should remain attributed to its original context. No claim gains empirical status from being appended to a manifesto or summarized by an ORMD.
