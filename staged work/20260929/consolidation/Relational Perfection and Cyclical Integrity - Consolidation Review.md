## Finding

The sources share a useful relational account of situated coherence, but they do different work. The manifesto offers a poetic, present-centered reassurance; the framework develops a more qualified analytical account with explicit questions about purpose, cost, constraints, and affected parties; Cyclical Integrity adds a system-level account of rhythmic change and resilience. The active Cyclical Integrity ORMD also embeds a Relational Perfection text and a distinct cultural manifesto, “The Unstable Ecosystem.” That composition should not be mistaken for proof that all three registers form one validated theory.

**Recommendation:** retain the three reader-facing works as distinct for now. Do not force a merged destination in this pass. A later source-mapping review may separate or clearly label the embedded Relational Perfection and Unstable Ecosystem registers within the Cyclical Integrity source, but must first establish their provenance and intended status. Keep the manifesto's reassurance distinct from analytical or ethical approval, and retain the framework's “for what, at what cost, to whom, and within what constraints?” checks.

These defects affect navigation and historical context. They do not establish that the target materials never existed; keep the missing-link question open until source discovery or an explicit unresolved-link disposition is recorded.

## Proposed later disposition

1. **Keep the manifesto and framework separate.** They have complementary rhetorical and analytic functions. If the framework continues to refer to the manifesto, repair or intentionally retire its missing companion link only after tracing the original child source. Preserve the manifesto's reassuring register without importing its absolutes as settled framework propositions.
2. **Keep Cyclical Integrity as its own candidate work.** Retain the cycle/transition model and its limits. Before editing, map the origin and status of the embedded Relational Perfection and Unstable Ecosystem material; decide whether the source should remain a clearly labeled composite or whether its components merit separately linked destinations. Do not remove either register based solely on overlap.
3. **Do not merge all three into one theory.** Shared language about relational/contextual coherence is insufficient to establish that the cyclical model follows from Relational Perfection or that the ecosystem manifesto is a consequence of either.
4. **Defer title, filename, anchor, archive, and reader-catalog changes** to the plan's later dependency/migration pass. Add explicit lineage metadata only when the actual source relationships are established.
