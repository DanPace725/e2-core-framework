It records an April 24, 2026 discussion and explicitly describes itself as an “engine-room document, not public-facing.” The ORMD is a deterministic preserve conversion of the human source (May 13, 2026), with no lineage parents and title “Resolution: Synthesis Notes.” The two bodies preserve substantially the same content; the ORMD adds metadata and heading anchors. The notes make a broad load-bearing claim: resolution is an invariant family of operations that narrows a possibility space along a signal gradient into committed actuality, while reducing degrees of freedom. They then connect that claim to language, information, thermodynamic entropy, phase changes, life, identity, meaning, power, GCO, MPDC, and a coaching/intake practice. Some sections are labeled tentative or provisional, but other cross-domain claims are stated as mechanisms or universal facts. Their provenance and evidence are not uniform. ## Is “Resolution Invariant” a Defensible Name?

**Current answer: no, not yet.** The notes nominate “resolution” as an invariant, but do not identify a quantity or relation shown to remain invariant under a specified class of transformations. “Resolution” currently does multiple jobs: process, degree of distinguishability, narrowing, commitment, maintenance, terminal closure, and residue. “Invariant” alternates between a mathematical property and a broad claim of recurrence across domains.

An invariant normally needs a defined object and transformation class, together with a property preserved by every allowed transformation. The current claim instead groups heterogeneous processes by verbal resemblance. “All of these reduce degrees of freedom” is not testable until the possibility space and degrees of freedom are defined consistently in each domain. If “invariant” only means a recurring organizing description, it should be named as a cross-domain pattern or hypothesis and not presented as a demonstrated invariant.

The September staged Primitive Resolution uses “resolution” in a more bounded procedural sense: distinguish aspects of an account, recombine them into a scoped reading, state a practical commitment, and retain remainder. That gives a possible **method-level use** of the term. It is not the same as a law that all natural, cognitive, social, and ethical change is resolution. It also reports conceptual/architectural success for one exercise, not empirical effectiveness or universal scope.

Possible provisional title for a future theory draft: **Resolution as a Family of Scoped Operations**. This avoids asserting invariance before its test. “Resolution Invariant” should remain a question or hypothesis label until the criteria below are met.

## Relation to Nearby Sources

### Relational Primitives

The current six primitives are defined as relation categories and a shared grammar for asking what relations operate. The canonical source explicitly says the primitives do not replace domain science, ethics, or practice. A resolution theory could use them to type relevant relations (e.g., dynamical process, geometric/causal embedding, symmetric constraints, epistemic distinguishability, meta-relational mapping), but it cannot reclassify them as six measured axes without argument. The notes' possible active/characterizing roles are not part of the settled definitions.

### Global Closure Operator

GCO is a specifically defined operator on a relational model, with a fixed-point condition and stated closure properties (extensiveness, monotonicity, idempotence). The notes call GCO “terminal resolution” or a point where further primitive application changes no qualitative structure. These may be related descriptions, but they are not automatically identical: a closure operator, a process of narrowing, and an ethical or practical commitment have different types and stopping conditions. A future theory must define the mapping, or explicitly leave them as an analogy.

### E² Equation

The active human Equation source contains a broad, dialogue-derived convergence/ethics interpretation. The consolidation plan treats the Equation as a separate historical/scope review and warns against inheriting its high confidence. A staged counterexample (`f(x) = -x`) shows that iteration alone need not converge. That undermines a generic “resolution always reaches a stable state” claim; it does not by itself disprove every narrower resolution process.

### E² Axioms and EUP

The axioms are the canonical proposition layer; the primitives are not a shortcut for establishing each axiom. EUP distinguishes broad applicability from completeness. A family-level resolution theory needs transport warrants for each cross-domain extension, and cannot infer universality from a shared vocabulary.

### Metabolic Meaning and entropy sources

MMPS and related sources offer domain-specific accounts of meaning and metabolism. They may be comparison points, but terminology such as energy, entropy, signal, and attention must keep its physical, information-theoretic, functional, and metaphorical senses separate. The April notes do not supply a common equation or measurement that joins those senses.

### Staged Primitive Resolution

This staged exercise offers a concrete method for one corpus interpretation and documents its scope, source coverage, counterexamples, and remainder. It supports retaining a practical procedure as one scoped use of “resolution.” Its own source ledger disclaims proposition-level review of the whole corpus and its main document disclaims empirical effectiveness, uniqueness, and completion. Do not cite it as proof of the larger invariant hypothesis.

## Definitions Needed Before a Standalone Theory

At minimum, a defensible draft must specify:

1. **Domain and target:** What systems or tasks does “resolution” cover? Is the proposal about descriptions, decisions, models, physical states, organisms, social processes, or a narrower subset?
2. **State space:** What is a “possibility space” for a given case? Who defines the admissible alternatives and at what level of description?
3. **Resolution event/operator:** What maps an initial state/account to a later state/account? Is it a physical transition, an observer's inference, a decision, or an intervention? Can one event combine these?
4. **Dissolution and recombination:** Are these necessary phases, an optional analysis heuristic, or a historical/etymological framing? Give criteria for identifying each phase independently.
5. **Signal and gradient:** Define signal, gradient, direction, and selection criterion. What makes a narrowing non-arbitrary, and how can it be observed or estimated?
6. **Degrees of freedom:** Define the relevant degrees of freedom and measure before/after; distinguish state-space dimension, number of options, entropy, uncertainty, and behavioral flexibility.
7. **Commitment and actuality:** Specify whether these are ontic state changes or observer-relative commitments. Define what counts as a committed state and how reversibility/reopening works.
8. **Invariant candidate:** Name a precise quantity, structure, or relation that is preserved across a declared operation family. State the transformation class, assumptions, proof burden, and counterexample conditions.
9. **Threshold and resolution quality:** Define under-, over-, and wrong-axis resolution without circularly classifying outcomes as “wrong” because they are harmful or unsuccessful. State whose objective and evidence set judge adequacy.
10. **Entropy and energy accounting:** If retaining physical claims, define system boundaries, reservoirs, quantities, units, and balance relations. Distinguish thermodynamic entropy, Shannon entropy, model uncertainty, and figurative “entropy.”
11. **Cross-domain mapping:** For each claimed transfer (optics → cognition; phase transitions → development; metabolism → meaning; thermodynamics → ethics), state the mapping and what it preserves, plus where it fails.
12. **Primitive mapping:** Define whether P1–P6 are types, roles, dimensions, or measurements in this theory. Reconcile the two competing role assignments in the notes.
13. **Use and safety scope:** For coaching or clinical-adjacent practice, define population, informed participation, practitioner competence, contraindications, outcomes, and risks. A general systems metaphor cannot establish therapeutic efficacy.

## Evidence and Tests Needed

- **Conceptual necessity/sufficiency:** Apply the proposed definition to clear positive, negative, and borderline cases. Identify cases called “resolution” by ordinary/domain usage that do not narrow the stated possibility space, and narrowing events that are not resolution under the proposed account.
- **Counterexample search:** Test whether the claimed property actually holds for decision, measurement, abstraction, compression, phase change, metabolism, forgetting, decay, and identity formation as separate process types. A typology cannot treat one similarity as proof they instantiate one mechanism.
- **Formal result:** If mathematical invariance is claimed, give a formal model and prove preservation under the specified operation class, or report a counterexample. Show how nonmonotonic exploration, branching, reversals, and increased options are represented.
- **Thermodynamic grounding:** For life/entropy claims, identify the system boundary and energy/matter flows, state applicable balance relations, and separate local organization from entropy export. Do not infer semantic or ethical conclusions from thermodynamics alone.
- **Operationalization and reliability:** For any cognitive/practice construct, specify observable variables, coding rules, inter-rater reliability, and measures that can distinguish under-, over-, and wrong-axis cases before evaluating effectiveness.
- **Empirical evaluation:** For coaching claims, define outcomes, comparison conditions, sample/population, adverse effects, follow-up, and preregistered failure criteria. The anecdote of a productive conversation is not evidence of client effectiveness.
- **Transport checks:** Each domain mapping needs a case where the mapping works, a case where it fails, and a reason to transport the result. Shared metaphors or primitive labels are not sufficient.
- **Provenance/version audit:** Recover the original conversation and the ten-cluster source material if available; distinguish the user's claims from assistant-generated analogies, names, and generalizations. The note records a conversation arc but does not itself provide a transcript or citations for its etymological, Rayleigh, thermodynamic, or Friston claims.
