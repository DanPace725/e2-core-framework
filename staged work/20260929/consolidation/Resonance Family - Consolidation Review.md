## Working Read

The family contains at least three different facets, not three interchangeable versions of one theory:

1. **April 17 — epistemic/translational architecture:** diagnosis of fragmented knowledge systems; proposals for shared scaffolding that lets different frameworks communicate without forced unification; resonance, attention, context, paradox, and a symbolic seven-node dashboard.
2. **April 24 — ontological and conceptual map:** organizes the relational axiom with a set of related concepts (SCIA, coherence fields, relational truth, interoperability, translation infrastructure, paradox tolerance), plus applications and a “way of being.” It is the most compact organizing map, but also contains the strongest consciousness and physics-metaphor assertions.
3. **April 26 — interaction/design proposal:** focuses on approach, pacing, boundaries, threshold crossing, negotiation across layers, and protection from manipulated connection. Its distinctive proposal is the Relational Approach Vector (RAV) plus selective threshold design; its cellular and protein comparisons are analogies, not evidence that human consent can be inferred from biological fit.

The first two overlap enough to support a **scoped orientation candidate**. The April 26 proposal has a different job and should remain a separate historical/candidate source unless its approach and threshold concepts are independently defined and reviewed. None of the three should be treated as empirical evidence for a general physical “resonance” law.

## Proposed Retention Plan

### Candidate shared orientation from April 17 + April 24

A future, newly scoped source could combine only these defensible common facets:

- relational framing as the framework’s starting premise, linked to the canonical axiom source;
- plural frameworks may exchange descriptions while retaining differences;
- translation requires stated context and criteria for what must survive;
- shared inquiry can require patience, attention, mutual testing, and correction;
- paradox can be held during inquiry without assuming every contradiction is productive or true;
- examples of cross-domain exchange are illustrations, not evidence of universal transfer.

The new source should state whether it is an epistemic orientation, a method, or a conceptual map. It should not merge those genres by implication. The dashboard belongs only as a proposal for future operational design unless each construct, scale, observable, and validation method is supplied.

The unique April 17 “galaxies” framing and transmission sequence can be retained as historical framing if useful; the April 24 concept map can supply labels and structure. The relational axiom itself should be a pointer to Axioms, not a claim newly derived by this document.

### Separate April 26 candidate

Retain the source’s distinct focus on approach and thresholds as a historical proposal pending a separate practice review. Before promotion, define what RAV’s components mean in observable terms; how they differ from interpersonal preference, trust, or safety; how a threshold is chosen; and how the framework protects explicit consent and refusal. Keep the biological and protein comparisons labeled analogy unless tested mappings are demonstrated.

## Evidence Limits and Claim Strength

- **Resonance is overloaded.** Across the family it can mean metaphorical fit, mutual comprehensibility, attention-supported alignment, coherence, emotional connection, a proposed field mechanism, or a physical analogy. A successor must specify the sense in each claim.
- **Truth claims outrun the evidence presented.** “Truth is cohered into being” and “all truths are situated” can mean truths become knowable in relation, or that truth itself is produced by attention. Those are different epistemic and ontological claims. The sources do not establish the stronger reading.
- **Attention/SCIA as causal engine is not demonstrated here.** The material offers analogies to temperature, gravity, energy, and phase transition, but no variables, units, threshold criteria, or controlled evidence showing SCIA causes truth or meaning to stabilize across domains.
- **Metaphor does not establish physics.** Gravitational wells, oscillation, field, signal, phase transition, entropy, and “resonant proof” should not be read as physical mechanisms without equations, measurements, and validated mappings.
- **Consciousness claims are especially underdetermined.** The April 24 map says consciousness emerges in the space between systems and is not located within individuals. The documents provide no operational definition, comparison with individual functional capacities, or evidence concerning subjective experience. This claim must stay a speculative interpretation or be removed from a scoped orientation.
- **Proof types cannot be flattened.** Logical proof, empirical evidence, instrumental success, phenomenological report, narrative coherence, and symbolic resonance have different warrants. Mutual experience of resonance does not verify external truth by itself.
- **Dashboard is not a metric.** The seven dimensions do not have operational definitions, scales, thresholds, reliability results, or demonstrated predictive/decision validity. Its status is symbolic design proposal only.
- **RAV and thresholds require ethical safeguards.** Felt compatibility cannot substitute for explicit consent. “Field-level consent,” nonverbal fit, and system-level resonance may be mistaken, manipulated, or asymmetric; absence of resonance is not proof of unsafe conduct, and resonance is not proof of safety.
- **Biological analogies are limited.** Membrane permeability and protein binding do not show that social systems should admit people by resemblance or compatibility, or that their dynamics are ethically equivalent.
- **No active predecessors/archives were found.** The three source ORMDs have `parents: []`; the comparison ledger reports no archive ancestors. The existing Synthesized Core is the only identified prior synthesis. It does not replace keeping the individual source histories available.
