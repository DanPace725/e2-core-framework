## Distinct material that would need a destination before archiving OSI

1. **Seven-layer interface checklist.** Physical/perceptual, emotional, cognitive, relational/intentional, cultural, reflective, and coherence/emergence layers each have a reader-facing diagnostic question. This is an analogy to a network stack and a proposed practice scaffold, not a proven dependency hierarchy. No reviewed destination reproduces it.
2. **Relational Capacity Scale.** OSI's R1 Local Coherence through R5 Relational Field Anchor concerns a person's flexibility across different norms and experiences. Its within-level horizontal/vertical growth and failure patterns (refusal to scale, overstretch, burnout) are not retained in Communication or Primitives. The document calls the scale quantifiable but supplies no instrument, calibration, reliability, or validity evidence; retain it as a conceptual rubric only.
3. **RNT procedure.** Detect mismatch, estimate needed adaptation, choose Attune/Bridge/Buffer, exchange, and monitor attention. The specific modes and bidirectional translation aim could become a scoped communication-practice section or a separate interface note. The protocol should not imply that translation succeeds simply because either participant reports resonance.
4. **η, κ, Λ, and RAV vocabulary.** OSI proposes affective tuning, cognitive pacing, an ambient baseline, and an approach orientation, plus examples in human relationships and AI interfaces. These are useful distinctions but are mostly analogies and design prompts; the source does not measure the parameters or specify a stable mapping from observation to setting. The seven-layer model and four-variable sketch are not shown to be equivalent.
5. **Formal/implementation sketches.** The `η: ℝⁿ → ℝⁿ` inequality leaves the norm, signal encoding, threshold, estimation procedure, and criterion for successful communication undefined. Later logistic/ratio language and `COHERENCE_FLOOR` claims refer to a proposed implementation, but the reviewed human text provides no calibration or evaluation. Preserve the sketches as hypotheses, not an implemented or validated mathematical specification.
6. **Situated examples and cautions.** Neurodivergent, illness, trauma, conflict, generational, education, and human–AI examples explain the author's intended use. They are illustrative and can be retained with consent, power, context, and participant feedback in view; they do not establish clinical effectiveness or justify inferring a person's internal state from expression alone.

## Disposition and sequence

**Keep the OSI pair active for now.** The proposed archive condition is unmet: the unique interface, capacity, and RNT material does not yet have a named, reviewed successor. A simple fold into Relational Primitives would change that source's role from primitive typing to a human communication practice. A simple fold into Communication as Coherence would import an unvalidated ordinal scale and parameter language without its caveats.

For a later extraction, the narrowest candidate is a distinct **relational interface and translation practice** source, with the seven layers as questions, RNT as a revisable procedure, RCS as an unvalidated capacity rubric, and η/κ/Λ/RAV as explicitly provisional handles. Compare that candidate against both active representations of OSI, then audit index rows, CRS §10 references, reader human/ORMD selection, inbound links, and lineage before archiving either active OSI file. Keep the 2025-04-22 origin and the RCS chronology visible.

## Evidence and wording limits

- The network OSI analogy does not make the proposed layer order necessary, exhaustive, or applicable to all human and AI interactions.
- η is called both a variable transformer and a “constant”; its mathematical role is unsettled. RNT's norm/threshold notation is a proposal without defined measurement units or data.
- The source's “quantifiable,” “mathematical foundation,” “both transformations preserve essential meaning,” and “engineering layer of reality” claims exceed the supplied examples and formulas. Neither model agreement nor analogy is validation.
- Emotional safety, cognition, attention, and communication access must not be inferred from a single proxy or calibrated universally across neurotypes, cultures, or models. Reciprocal feedback and the possibility of pausing are part of the proposed practice, not evidence of successful transformation.
- Preserve the later CRS scale's distinct name and its unresolved evidence limits. The identity of the historical `RCS_.pdf` appendix is still unconfirmed.

**Next decision:** whether the intended retained destination is a separate scoped practice source or an identified section of Communication as Coherence. That choice should be made with the actual retained text in view; it does not block other consolidation packages.
