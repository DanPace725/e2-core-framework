# Eidosemantic Systems Theory: a conceptual reference

**Status:** staged, source-attributed account of a developing idea. This is a reference for its conceptual questions, not an adopted Core theory or a notation specification.

## Central idea

The starting observation is that a word or sentence can be understood as one visible choice within a larger field of related possibilities. An action such as *write* can be connected to drawing, believing, experiencing, or loving; a *sentence* can be considered alongside a word, paragraph, book, or language. These are not interchangeable synonyms. The point of the author's May 22, 2025 sketch is to make the relations and alternative paths available for exploration, instead of treating the chosen phrase as an isolated, final unit of meaning.

The sketch also treats meaning as temporal. The same apparent subject can be encountered at different slices of a developing process. What changes between slices matters as much as the words used at either end. Sustained attention, branching associations, and indirect paths may let an insight emerge that a straight-line explanation would miss. This is a proposal about how to *notice and navigate* relationships in thought and expression; it does not show that all words contain a measurable or recoverable field.

## Communication and interpretation

Later EST and ECN material develops a related proposal: communication is an adjustment between differently situated interpreters, rather than the delivery of a fixed meaning that both already share. A speaker can offer words, context, intent, uncertainty, and possible associations; a recipient still has to interpret them from their own position. A useful exchange may preserve a live question or tension while the parties test and revise what they understand.

That broader formulation comes chiefly from subsequent model-written explanations, including the *Primer* and the material quoted in *TACITRA*. It is a plausible conceptual direction, not an independently established property of every human or AI interaction. The name **Eidosemantic Systems Theory** also arose in that model-mediated development as a working name. Using it here identifies the source family; it does not settle whether the author adopts every later definition.

## Relationship to the E² Core

This reference is most useful as a focused question within existing E² work: how can an expression retain enough of its relations, history, and unresolved possibilities for another person to work with it? *Communication as Coherence* already addresses translation between different meaning-making approaches. *E² as a Translation Architecture for Human Remembrance* supplies the aim of translating without erasing the path back to lived context. The Context Layer Protocol addresses context, lineage, and resolution metadata for documents. EST's distinctive contribution at this stage is the author's temporal and associative *field sketch* as a way to inspect meaning formation, not a replacement for those active accounts.

## Scope and limits

- This document carries the theory and conceptual questions only. It does not select or reproduce ECN, PLFN, SCRIPTA, or other notation variants.
- Neither the May sketch nor later summaries demonstrate semantic preservation, faithful compression and expansion, or transfer across people, models, or other media. Such outcomes would need defined tasks and comparison criteria.
- The available first-person explanation refers to whiteboards that are absent from this staged set. Later model responses elaborate and sometimes conflict with one another. They should not be read back into the original sketch as if the author had specified them there.
- Relational interpretation is a conceptual account here. It does not establish claims about subjective experience, AI consciousness, or a universal substrate-independent theory of meaning.
