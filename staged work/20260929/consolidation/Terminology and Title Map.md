### TC / EO / EOTC audit

The active corpus uses `TC` for **Transformative Complexity** in the Essence/ontological-transformation lineage, and also uses `TC/EO` shorthand for temporal compression plus ethical occlusion. `EO` appears as the distinct phrase **Ethical Occlusion**; `EOTC` names **Ethical Occlusion via Temporal Compression**. These are not globally interchangeable: use EOTC only where the reference is specifically that established mechanism; retain TC for Transformative Complexity and spell both concepts out where combined shorthand obscures which one is meant. Preserve quoted historical `TC/EO` language with a clarifying note when needed.

Direct active references occur across the paired EOTC documents, CCS/CRS and collective substrate texts, `Relational Derivation Chain`, MRIE synthesis, `Self as Coherence Field`, and supporting sources including AOMI, `From Essential Relationships to Ontological Transformation`, MPDC, OSI, `Global Closure Operator`, `Intervention Stewardship`, `Proxy Localization`, `Relational Localization`, and `Remnant Stewardship`. The generated Context Layer index also repeats many terms and must be regenerated by its prescribed process after any accepted edits, never hand-edited.

### Acronym collisions and title policy

- **RCS:** the older active OSI section defines **Relational Capacity Scale** (with RCS section/heading IDs and keywords). The later Collective Relational Substrate uses **Relational Consciousness Scale** for a developmental map. Treat this as a true collision. Reserve RCS for the older capacity usage during review; spell out the later consciousness term until its construct and a distinct acronym are settled. Do not change anchors or infer that one scale succeeds the other.
- **TI:** the active Tensional Intelligence source uses TI as its short name (including a `TI Guide` reference), while `Relational Field Dynamics 0.1` defines `TI = 1/(1 + C_eff)` as **Temporal Integrity**. This is a genuine same-acronym collision across active files. Spell out the local term on first use and avoid adding bare TI in reader-facing titles or indexes until a collision policy is recorded.
- **Established acronym in title:** EOTC already appears in its descriptive title and is an established name. Other acronym-bearing titles (e.g., CCS/CRS, MRIE, RIF, EUP, HAIC, MPDC, MMPS, OSI, RPE, CLP) should be checked against acronym index and source usage before any additions or expansions. This review did not treat a generated index as authority.

### Version, date, and provenance labels

Visible versions (`V1`, `V2`, `v2`, `0.1`, `v0.2`, `Draft 2`) may distinguish a real active iteration, as in CLP Starter Spec v0.2 or RFD 0.1. Date-like filename suffixes on resonance sources (4.17.25, 4.24.25, 4.26.25) appear provenance-bearing and are not automatically title text. `Core Source` is mostly a filename/status convention; source status belongs in metadata/body and must not be lost when a title is shortened. Check each body and lineage before moving any marker.
