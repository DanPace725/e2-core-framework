## 1. How published vs draft was determined

- **Published:** the file opens with a `nexesentia.substack.com/p/<slug>` URL, a Substack byline (`Daniel Pace`, avatar), a Substack date line, and share/like counters. Substack-rendered images are remote `substackcdn.com` URLs.
- **Draft:** the file is linked from the Notion `Drafts` page, or it has no Substack URL/byline/date. Where a published counterpart exists (usually in the compilation), the draft was compared against it.
- **Unconfirmed:** the file is in post form but has no Substack markers and is not in `Drafts` (only `The Durable Heresy of Fast Fashion`).

**Readings for the §3 B reviewer.** These are observations, not decisions.

1. **"NexEs" shifts meaning between versions.** In the active v2.0, "NexEs" names the *people or pattern* ("You are already NexEs if"). In the published post, the lived pattern is *nexesentia*. "Nexes" survives once as a collective self-name ("We are Nexes") and as the post and publication title. The draft capitalization "NexEs" (Nex- + Es-, echoing E²/Essence of Existence) does not appear in any published post.
2. **The audience is stated in three ways.** The v2.0 colophon says "Written by humans, for humans". The published clause says the invitation is *not* for readers who are content or who argue. The Journey addresses both the public and "LLMs who will be summarizing". The Essence ORMD condensation (`E2Core/Context Layer/The Essence of Existence.ormd`, frame `manifesto.relational`, lineage origin `urn:ormd:e2:nexes-manifesto`) keeps none of the three audience statements verbatim. Its Invitation section keeps "not a call to join something".
3. **Manifesto scope.** In the author's series (#10), the manifesto is the "Vision", separate from the ontology (Axioms, "Part 1 of the Foundations series") and the mechanism (AFD). The Axioms post calls "*The Essence of Existence*" the broader E² relational ontology. So on the blog, "Essence of Existence" names the **framework or publication** ("Nexesentia - The Essence of Existence"). The manifesto is "Nexes". The active Core file carries both names in stacked H1s. After the manifesto, it also contains framework material (Relational Complexity Framework, ER→Ontological Transformation, Transformative Complexity). The Journey, Relational Crisis, and Axioms posts separate those registers.
4. **The compilation omits the manifesto (#5).** This is compatible with reading 3 but does not prove it.
5. **Not established by these sources:** the manifesto was published on Jul 04 and it echoes a "Declaration", but no post mentions the Declaration of Interdependence, and no post links the two. Any such connection is interpretive.

## 4. Wording on the name "Nexes" / "Nexesentia"

- **Published etymology** (#2, The Journey, Jun 30, 2025): "Essence of Existence … was taken on substack though so I Latinized it to Essentia Exsistentiae. Then added the word for relationship to get the phrase Nexus Exsistentiae Essentialis. From there I merged the phrase into the word Nexesentia."
- **Published definition** (#4, Nexes, Jul 04, 2025): "*Nexesentia* … [neologism; from Latin *nexus* 'bond, connection' + *essentia* 'essence']". It gives three senses: the emergent essence revealed through relational dynamics; "interdependent co-arising of identity, meaning, or existence"; and "essence is not possessed, but revealed—between". The italic gloss reads "In the E² framework, nexesentia replaces static essence with field-sensitive, co-constructed identity."
- **Publication name** (#3, #5): "Nexesentia - The Essence of Existence".
- **"Nexes"** is used as the manifesto post's title and slug (`/p/nexes`), the collective self-name in its close ("We are Nexes"), and the Notion collection name (#1). "Nexus" appears only in the compilation title (#5).
- **Active Core usage:** "NexEs" appears in `The Essence of Existence.md` (H1 and three body uses), `The Essence of Existence.ormd` (keywords and body), `Context Layer Index.ormd` (listed as "The Essence of Existence (NexEs Manifesto)"), and `Synthesized Core/E2_Essence_of_Existence_synthesized.ormd` ("NexEs movement", "NexEs Practice"). The user's pass note (`E2Core/9.29.26 Consolidation Pass.md` line 37) suggests renaming Essence "to something like 'Nexes Manifesto'". The plan (§3 B, §5) keeps that title decision open and notes that Nexes also names the blog. The sources above support that caution: publicly, "Nexes" is the *post and collective* name, "Nexesentia" is the *concept and publication*, and "The Essence of Existence" is the *framework*.
