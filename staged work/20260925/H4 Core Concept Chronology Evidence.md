## Earlier analogous patterns (not retroactive renamings)

- **Reverent Stewardship (2025-06-26)**, active Semantic source, already centers restraint, boundary care, and avoiding intervention before invitation. This is a dated predecessor/posture, not Intervention Stewardship under an earlier name.
- **The Stewardship Architecture: A Summary (2025-11-21)**, legacy `Phase 1/Semantic Substrate`, frames asymmetric risk of transmitting causal power and proposes a three-ring containment structure (“share physics, contain execution”). It is an antecedent disclosure-governance pattern; do not collapse it into the later, more formal GDD.
- **Metabolic Meaning Phase Space (MMPS)** in active Core names “Boundary as Recursive Morphism” as a section and is cited by Boundary Dynamics as an earlier structural insight. Its current ORMD lineage carries `urn:ormd:mmps:pass-3b`, timestamp **2026-06-12**. This postdates the boundary_dynamics ORMD origin timestamp, so it supports conceptual overlap/precursor lineage but does not establish that this exact MMPS text predates the standalone Boundary Dynamics draft. Preserve that date-order ambiguity.
- **Flow Operators (provisional)** says “Session date: April 30, 2026” and catalogs operations at boundaries under “flow.” Boundary Dynamics describes this as earlier operational work whose label was too narrow. As its date precedes May 13, it is the clearest dated operational analogue for the standalone theory.
- **Solution Stewardship** is described by the June 9 Intervention draft as an earlier draft superseded because it treated the healthier-basin mode as the genus. No dated source for that draft was found in the inspected Core tree; report only the relative ordering.
