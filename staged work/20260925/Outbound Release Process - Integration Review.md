## Working Read

**Load-bearing claim.** A corpus release should be a traceable offer to a particular receiver, with its claim and limits intact, followed by a time-bounded observation of whether that receiver uses and eventually maintains it independently. The steward controls eligibility and release; the receiver controls uptake. The candidate turns the Exposure Protocol's transmission pattern into a release, registration, and feedback practice.

**Provisional type:** practice, with a bridge to existing boundary and stewardship concepts. The name is provisional and has no proposed symbol. It fits inside the existing framework; it does not propose a seventh primitive.

**New operational distinctions:** internal source status versus external carry; release versus uptake; `ignored`, `misread`, `hollowed`, `carried`, and `promoted`; lossy translation versus loss of structural traceability; and receiver-maintained use versus use sustained by the corpus. These distinctions are useful even if the particular status words change.

## Collision And Risk Review

1. **Status collision inside the draft.** The first “Promotion Event” definition (lines 25-34) says external, traceable use is promotion. Line 36 and the status table (lines 50-51) call that state `carried` until the receiver sustains it without corpus effort. Define `carried` first, then `promoted` as a later, independently maintained state. Say whether an observation window can close as `carried` with future promotion still open.
2. **Evidence overreach.** Line 23 calls promotion “whole-level evidence” that tests what the broad model does beyond narrower frameworks. Receiver use can establish usefulness or portability of a *piece* in one setting. A claim of incremental explanatory or predictive value over narrower frameworks needs a registered comparison, discriminating outcomes, and independent evaluation. Uptake is neither truth nor a whole-framework test by default; step 13 already acknowledges part of this.
3. **Gate transfer.** Intervention Stewardship limits its promotion gate mainly to Solution and partly Transition modes. The draft generalizes the gate to all outward forms without explaining the changed meaning of `promoted`. A receiver maintaining a vocabulary or practice is observable in principle, but may be less direct than a system maintaining a new condition.
4. **Observation and attribution.** “No detectable uptake” is not the same as no uptake. Keep `ignored` explicitly scoped to the chosen channels/window. Records should distinguish witnessed action, receiver report, inference, and absence of signal. No AI restatement, authored demo, view count, paper appearance, or public deployment alone proves `carried`.
5. **Operational gap.** The process requires a named receiver and preregistration, but offers no minimal schema, evidence types, or criteria for the end of an observation window. One receiver *class* per release may be practical; a specific adopter cannot always be named before an open publication. Specify what counts as sufficiently named and how downstream intermediaries are recorded.
6. **Scale of review.** A 13-step procedure may be too expensive for small releases. The draft's proposed short form is promising, but it must still carry eligibility, claim/source trace, receiver, status limits, preregistered observation, and beneficiary disclosure when relevant. A reduced form should not waive the high-stakes checks.
7. **External collision check remains undone.** The AI Weather example lists possible external parallels but explicitly says they are unverified. No novelty claim about this process or its test case is supported yet.

## Proposed Integration Method

Keep the original draft intact. Revise a candidate copy or new practice draft to: (a) resolve `carried`/`promoted`; (b) narrow the evidence claim; (c) state which parents are active and which are legacy; (d) specify a minimal registration and observation record; and (e) test the process on one bounded outward artifact with an actual receiver and frozen source version. The project scan in `E2 Projects - Outbound Fit and Readiness.md` supplies candidates, not uptake outcomes.

If that test is coherent, place the practice under or adjacent to Exposure Protocol as a candidate source first. A Context Layer conversion and generated index update would be later steps under the inward integration process. Do not make the staged release procedure self-authorizing.

## Open Questions

- What evidence of independent maintenance suffices for `promoted`, and over what duration?
- How should indirect uptake through intermediaries be traced without surveillance or manufacturing a signal?
- Which kinds of release require the full review, and what is the smallest safe review for a routine bounded instrument?
- When should repeated misreadings reopen the underlying source claim rather than only the translation?
- How can a project with excellent reproducibility but no independent receiver be represented honestly as release-ready rather than as “ignored”?
