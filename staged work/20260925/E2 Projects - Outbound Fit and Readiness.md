## How To Read The Comparison

The staged Outbound process evaluates **one piece for one receiver**, not an entire repository. The table therefore identifies a plausible first piece and asks what the project already provides. It does not classify any whole project as `carried` or `promoted`.

Marks refer to the inspected project material, not to a completed Release Review:

- **A** — a bounded outward artifact or demonstrable method exists.
- **T** — the claim can be traced to inspectable source, method, or receipts.
- **R** — a receiver and its stakes are identified at least at role/community level. A generic “public” or imagined audience gets `~`.
- **B** — the outward artifact states material evidence/status limits that must survive translation.
- `Y` = substantially present, `~` = partial or implied, `—` = not located in inspected material.

**Across the sample:** no completed per-piece Release Review, pre-release registration, or named observation window was established in the inspected project documents. This matrix now includes user-reported family use, workplace use, external feedback, contest submission, public response, and deliberate non-disclosure alongside repository receipts. They are distinct kinds of evidence; none alone establishes independent, sustained maintenance of a particular piece. Public access, an authored test, a paper or demo, internal cross-project reuse, and AI discussion do not by themselves meet the draft's receiver-side criterion. No external collision search was performed for these projects. A `Y` in A/T/R/B is not a claim that release is eligible or safe. **Relationship to E² and contact with the world are separate dimensions:** a piece can be E²-influenced without observed uptake, or widely used without a demonstrated E² lineage.

## What The Scan Suggests For The Outbound Draft

1. **Add a pre-release “piece selection” prompt.** Repositories often contain multiple plausible outward items with different forms and evidence. Condition Map's app and schema, for example, should not share one registration.
2. **Rate readiness separately from uptake.** A source-linked, runnable artifact can be ready for a bounded trial while its outcome remains unobserved. Conversely a popular demo is not automatically traceable or faithful. Use explicit fields for `candidate`, `release status`, `observed signal`, and `outcome inference`.
3. **Record evidence provenance at the level the receiver can use.** Research projects need version/config/seed/data/run provenance and a line between proxy, simulation, digital replay, physical test, and external deployment. Educational and practice projects need a distinction between intended learning or benefit and observed change.
4. **Specify observation channels without chasing.** A named receiver can be a role or institution, with a stated channel for voluntary feedback and a chosen window. Record unknown or invisible uptake as unknown; do not convert it into an `ignored` verdict.
5. **Make “doing work” form-specific.** Running a tool, adapting a protocol, teaching a lesson, incorporating a design result, and arguing with a formal claim leave different receipts. One person changing one practice may establish `carried`; sustained independent maintenance requires further observation before `promoted`.
6. **Give small, bounded releases a short review.** The proposed short form should keep eligibility, claim/source trace, receiver, status limits, preregistered signals/window, and beneficiary disclosure. It can defer a full collision map where novelty is not claimed and consequences are modest.
7. **Represent responses that change the next piece.** REAL Neural Substrate's peer reviews and WDW's church collaboration both returned information, but in different ways. The record needs to distinguish critique, invitation, co-development, use of the original artifact, and later independent maintenance. A course can remain deferred while its underlying framing helps start another practice.
8. **Include author-operated field use.** ACE, DocFlow, and VideoTrans show that sustained real-world work may precede any non-author carrier. Record duration, users, workflow changes, benefits, difficulties, and maintenance burden without relabeling author operation as `promoted`.
9. **Record deliberate withholding as a valid pre-release state.** Nexes, RP-Lang, and the paperclip/optical research show why release eligibility is a real decision. A candidate can be technically strong and intentionally private because of capability, accountability, or unresolved disclosure questions. The matrix's A/T/R/B marks should never serve as pressure to publish.
10. **Separate lineage from worldly response.** The Fast Fashion essay has explicit E² influence and a dated reader-response receipt; MindiAI and the immigration tool have non-author use without an established E² lineage. Add independent fields for `conceptual lineage` and `observed receiver action`, with source and confidence for each.
11. **Track maintenance, retirement, and credit.** MindiAI's extensive father use ended in an exported-data handoff when maintenance became infeasible; the history parser came from another person's idea and Daniel's improvements. An Uptake Record should preserve who originated, built, used, maintained, and retired a piece, plus the receiver's ability to keep using or exit it.
