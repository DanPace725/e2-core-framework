## Working Read

The load-bearing claim is that E² is best understood not as a totalizing explanatory system but as a translation architecture: a way to carry lived perception, moral intuition, relational awareness, and pattern sensitivity across minds, systems, disciplines, technologies, and scales without severing the path back to human reality.

The document names failed reconstructability as “hollowing”: a surface-sign, metric, ritual, institution, or abstraction remains while the interior relations that gave it meaning can no longer be recovered through it.

Provisional integration type: framework-level bridge with interpretive posture. It orients the formal, ethical, communicative, and applied layers without adding a new primitive.

## Collision And Risk Review

- **Primitive-layer risk:** “translation architecture” must not be read as a replacement grammar or a seventh primitive. It is an orientation across the existing architecture.
- **Mechanism risk:** “hollowing,” “signal,” and “path back” are interpretive language here. They should not be equated automatically with a formal signal field, EOTC mechanism, or measurable property.
- **Evidence risk:** “recognition, not mere proof” does not waive empirical or formal standards. Claims that are predictive, mechanistic, or universal remain governed by Lawfulness, EUP, and their source-specific evidence.
- **Source-reference risk:** phrases such as “freedom as capacity” are useful compressions in this document but do not currently name a separate canonical source document. They should remain examples unless developed through their own review.
- **Scope risk:** “human remembrance” is normative and interpretive, not a claim about a measured cognitive memory process.
- **Authority risk:** the submitted root document is preserved as provenance; the promoted Semantic Substrate and Context Layer pair are the active source artifacts.
