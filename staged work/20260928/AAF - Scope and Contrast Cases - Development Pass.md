# AAF — Scope and Contrast Cases

*September 28, 2026 · staged development pass · supports AAF v0.4, not an empirical validation or promotion decision*

**Disposition update (2026-09-28):** Daniel accepted the scoped cross-substrate term **attention** without a standing “attention-like” qualifier. [AAF is now active Core](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/Attentional%20Access%20and%20Formation%20-%20Core%20Source.md). The alternatives below record the pre-promotion deliberation; empirical and mechanism-specific limits still apply.

## Proposed nucleus

**Attentional access is the state-, history-, or context-sensitive modulation of which among available differences can influence a finite system's focal resolution.** A claim must name the system boundary, competing candidates, the focal resolution, a change in differential influence, and the conditions under which that change occurs. The same episode may later form terrain, but formation is not required for each instance. Conscious intention, felt experience, care, and moral standing are separate questions.

This is a **functional bridge** within the existing architecture. [Signal as Bias Field](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/Signal%20as%20Bias%20Field.md) already has conditional signal uptake; [Resolution Synthesis](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/resolution_synthesis.md) and [GCO](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/Global%20Closure%20Operator.md) already own resolution and closure. AAF's contribution is to specify *differential access to a named resolution*, decompose its episode/terrain/organization forms, and ask what repeated access changes leave behind. It does not claim a mechanism unique to all domains called attention.

**Disposition rule.** “Supported at this scale” requires more than a changed output: available alternatives, a focal resolution, and evidence or a fully specified design showing that the alternatives' influence changes with state/history/context. “Excluded at this scale” requires a sufficiently described focal mechanism that lacks this differential modulation; it does not declare the containing organism or institution attention-free. If the mechanism, comparison, or boundary is missing, say “unresolved,” not “excluded.” An unmeasured resource allocation, stimulus detection, or downstream response cannot on its own decide the case.

## Scope cases

These cases exercise the proposed language. Only the PLTi row relies on an observed study; the others are **designed contrasts** or under-resolved reports, not new experiments.

| Focal case | Provisional disposition | What the contrast shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mouse spatial selection with PLTi silencing.** Target and task-relevant flankers compete to guide a response. | **Supported as a selective-access interpretation at this task boundary.** [Kothari et al. (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72340-9) found impaired competitive target selection while the tested single-target discrimination and motor measures lacked corresponding impairment. | A change in competitive influence can be distinguished from those measured functions. The experiment does not prove AAF's cross-substrate definition, uniquely locate Gate/Ward, or demonstrate every property of GCO. |
| **A fixed optical or software filter** always passes one feature and blocks another, regardless of task, history, or state. | **Excluded as a stand-alone attention system at that boundary.** | It is a component gate. If a wider system dynamically changes when or how this filter bears on a focal resolution, assess the wider system anew; do not transfer the exclusion upward. |
| **A specified two-sensor controller** receives competing moisture and temperature readings, changes their weights with its operating state, and the change alters which reading guides its next act. | **Supported as attention-like at the controller's decision boundary, if the stated design is actually implemented.** | Dynamic differential access can be described without consciousness. It does not establish that the controller Tends the plant, or that the plant itself attends. A generic single-sensor thermostat would not meet this description. |
| **A reported plant response to a sound** changes a downstream physiological output, but the available account does not show competing differences being differentially admitted to a focal resolution. | **Unresolved, not an attention finding.** | Response and state change alone leave detection, regulation, and access modulation indistinguishable. A candidate plant mechanism would need its own competing cues, comparison, and scale. |
| **An institutional agenda** lets some registered proposals influence a committee decision while others are deferred; priority can be revised with context and the record shows the effect. | **Potentially supported at the institutional decision process, conditional on that trace.** | Agenda access can be functionally attention-like without positing a group mind or treating an institution's agenda as every member's inner Lean. A bare list of scheduled items remains under-resolved. |

The computational and institutional rows are deliberately broad. If either seems too broad for the word *attention*, that is the principal naming/scope judgment to make before promotion. The operational distinctions still hold if these are called *attention-like access* rather than attention simpliciter.

## Distinctions under load

| Contrast | Reading that survives | Reading to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registered but not influential** versus the same candidate admitted after a priority change | Registration answers whether the difference can be distinguished; attention answers whether it can matter to this focal resolution. A changed decision can support the latter when detection and action alternatives are held suitably comparable. | A null outward response is not proof that nothing was registered, nor proof of Ward. |
| **Phone notification while reading.** The reader is habitually pulled toward it but keeps reading; later the current focus is released or redirected. | **Lean** is the standing disposition toward the phone; **Gate/Ward** is the current exclusion. **Release** loosens an allocation already held; **Redirect** favors another candidate. The same visible “ignored phone” can result from several of these. | Treating Lean as the gate operation, or treating Ward, Release, and Redirect as interchangeable verbs. |
| **Selecting a passage for current reading** and **committing to a final interpretation** | Pick can settle an allocation and therefore be a *local* closure. GCO names the wider closure principle; the eventual interpretive commitment may be another closure with different scope. Local and wider closures can nest, overlap, or be reopened. | A compulsory pipeline in which Pick is always pre-closure, or a claim that each Pick has been shown to satisfy GCO's formal fixed-point properties. |
| **One episode alters later defaults** versus a transient episode with no durable effect | Formation is a possible consequence: to call the changed default Lean/terrain, name what persists, for whom, at what timescale, by what evidence and maintenance mechanism. [Condition as Typed Terrain](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/Condition%20as%20Typed%20Terrain%20-%20Core%20Source.md) supplies that trace. | Making formation an admission condition for every attention episode, or inferring a shared inner Lean from a shared interface. |
| **Timer, responsive plant-care controller, and engagement recommender** | Timer: no feedback. Controller: feedback and perhaps attention-like access; **Tend remains unresolved** unless the plant's viable condition can revise the controller's policy with suitable restraint, not merely satisfy a fixed proxy. Recommender: responsive to users but oriented to engagement can be attention-like without Tending them. | Treating responsiveness, good performance, or repeated return as sufficient evidence of Tend or care. [Boundary Ethics](../../E2Core/Semantic%20Substrate/Boundary%20Ethics%20-%20Core%20Source.md) keeps presence and restraint separate. |
| **Genuine and forged recognition signs** produce similar recipient uptake | Uptake shows that the sign affected the receiver. Backing asks whether the sign actually tracks the claimed attending operation. This is a separate property of signs, not an admission criterion for attention itself. | Treating the sign's social effect as proof of attention, or treating lack of an outward sign as proof no attention occurred. |

## What this pass resolves and leaves open

The examples support a **scoped conceptual source**: AAF can name a recognizable function, carry negative and unresolved cases, and preserve its parent sources' distinct roles. They do not validate a universal natural kind or a cross-substrate instrument. The PLTi case is valuable because it separates some measured functions in one experiment; the designed cases expose where the proposed criterion would apply if their mechanism is implemented.

For promotion, the substantive judgment is whether E² wants the term **attention** to cover qualifying computational and institutional processes, with “attention-like” marking unestablished cross-domain cases. My recommendation is yes to the function at a declared boundary, with the qualifier and explicit evidence state. Restricting *attention* to cognitive organisms would be a defensible alternate naming policy, but it would change AAF's intended architectural reach. That choice belongs to Daniel; no active Core authority is assigned by this pass.

The remaining research program—immune and plant mechanisms, measures of effective access, formal Gate/Lean dynamics, quantitative CFA/CFAR mapping, and clinical or group-level extensions—can continue after a scoped source decision. None should be smuggled into the source as an already established consequence of the admission trace.
