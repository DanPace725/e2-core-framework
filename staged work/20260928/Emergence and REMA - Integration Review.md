# Emergence and REMA - Integration Review

Status: scoped integration into existing active nodes; older syntheses archived on 2026-09-28. This review is noncanonical provenance.

## Decision

- Preserve the *Universal Emergence Pattern*'s comparative questions as a cross-primitive inquiry note in the active Relational Primitives source and Context Layer pair. The five-step universal law, equation, cross-domain validation claim, conversion thermodynamics, and predictive or engineering promises were not integrated.
- Preserve REMA's graded inquiry as a provisional multidimensional capacity profile appended to the active Self as Coherence Field source and Context Layer pair. The ordered consciousness ladder, CFA phase-state thresholds, qualia mechanism, and attribution of sentience or consciousness to example systems were not integrated.
- Archive the active Universal Emergence Pattern, REMF, and REMA document families and their three dependent Synthesized Core summaries intact under `archive/20260928_emergence_synthesis_reconciliation/`. REMF's multi-perspective comparison protocol remains a historical research-method candidate, not an accepted full-framework architecture.
- Archive RCF and its dependent summary after tracing its triadic morphism to the Exposure Protocol. Its distinct cross-role feedback questions enter the provisional SCF appendix; its consciousness criterion, untyped meta-morphisms, inherited temporal formula, and self-demonstration claim do not.

## Placement and limits

The six Relational Primitives remain the grammar; emergence is a question asked across them, not a seventh primitive. EUP requires a declared scope and a transport warrant for cross-domain comparisons. The SCF profile asks about differentiation, selective access, integration and retention, boundary and continuity, reflexive modeling, and functional valuation at a named system boundary and observation window. These dimensions are neither a single score nor a consciousness test. Subjective experience, welfare-bearing status, and moral standing remain separate questions requiring their own definitions and evidence. AAF supplies a narrower functional test for selective access.
