# Boundary Measurement Protocol - Skeleton

Status: staged skeleton

Purpose: separate boundary analysis, measurement, and evaluation from the general theory of boundaries and horizons.

Proposed role:

```text
Boundary Dynamics = structural/operator theory.
Boundary and Horizon = epistemic/access theory.
Boundary Conditions and Layers = stratification and contact conditions.
Boundary Measurement Protocol = how to analyze a boundary claim from a declared observer position.
```

## 1. Core Measurement Problem

Boundary measurements are Zone 3 measurements when agents are involved.

The observer, measured field, boundary, substrate, and participants may all be coupled.

Measurement without declared position risks mistaking:

- observer horizon for field reality,
- agent compensation for substrate health,
- silence for repair,
- agreement for coherence,
- contact for carrying,
- stability for truth,
- low access for absence.

## 3. Boundary Identification

Questions:

- What regions are being distinguished?
- What relation constitutes the boundary?
- What does the boundary admit, refuse, transduce, reflect, leak, or dissipate?
- At what resolution does this boundary appear?
- What changes if the resolution changes?

Possible outputs:

- boundary description,
- region pair or region network,
- active modes,
- observer-position caveat.

## 4. Mode And Capacity Scan

Use Boundary Dynamics' six modes:

- stance-with,
- stance-against,
- settlement,
- depletion,
- trade,
- consequence-receipt.

Questions:

- Which modes are active?
- Which modes are saturated?
- Which modes are missing?
- Which modes are being substituted for others?
- Which mode-capacities are being mistaken as interchangeable?

## 5. Selectivity And Permeability Scan

Core questions:

- Does the boundary distinguish nutrition, pollution, work, and waste?
- Does permeability vary by source power?
- Does pressure from some sources transduce while pressure from others dissipates?
- Is selectivity high, low, source-distorted, or performative?

Connections:

- Boundary Dynamics selectivity.
- Sign-Mediated Flow Routing selectivity tensor.
- AOMI capture analysis.

## 6. Horizon And Access Scan

Questions:

- What does this boundary make visible from this position?
- What does it make inaccessible?
- What remainder exceeds current access?
- Where might redshift be occurring?
- Which signals may be present but unreadable as signal?

Outputs:

- known horizons,
- likely redshift zones,
- occlusion risks,
- observer humility notes.

## 7. Layer And Condition Scan

Candidate conditions:

- repetition,
- memory,
- cross-dyadic transfer,
- normative inertia,
- load transfer,
- repair landing capacity,
- remainder storage,
- temporal continuity.

Questions:

- Which layers are present?
- Which layers carry which functions?
- Which conditions are missing?
- Which layer failure would collapse the boundary?

## 8. Viscosity / Carrying Scan

Purpose:

Evaluate whether boundary-mediated contact is low-viscosity, trap-viscosity, or carrying-viscosity.

Questions:

- Can friction remain contactful?
- Can difference move without expulsion or merger?
- Does repair land?
- Does holding preserve movement?
- Does stability require suppression?
- Do agents have to personally fund the field's coherence?

Regime indicators:

- low viscosity: abrasion, fragmentation, repair failure,
- trap viscosity: immobilization, false harmony, exit cost, closure theater,
- carrying viscosity: protected movement, metabolizable friction, selective boundary function.

## 9. Remainder And Recovery Scan

Questions:

- What remainder is being produced?
- Where does it go?
- Is it metabolized, stored, displaced, dumped, suppressed, or redshifted?
- What happens after load exits?
- Does the field recohere?
- Does it require heroic agents to hold together?

Possible proxy families:

- recovery dynamics,
- remainder metabolization,
- operator-valid routing,
- contact depth,
- boundary selectivity.

## 13. Guardrails

- Do not treat measurements as the underlying object.
- Do not compare measurements across contexts without matching load, observer position, and resolution.
- Do not interpret agreement as coherence by default.
- Do not interpret silence as repair by default.
- Do not interpret high constraint integrity as carrying-viscosity by default.
- Do not ignore observer stake.
- Do not erase remainder to make the evaluation tidy.
