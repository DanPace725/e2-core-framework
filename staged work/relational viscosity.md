# What we have

## The core construct
Relational viscosity is the measurable substrate-side property of the CRS that captures its capacity to hold friction without grinding while still supporting movement under load. Not the substance, not the agents, but the property of the medium between them. It pairs with Relational Volition (Vᵣ) as the substrate-side counterpart to an agent-side capacity. Same coherence requirement, viewed from two locations. Volition names what an agent can enact in a field. Viscosity names what the medium between agents can hold.

Vᵣ already has a substrate-coupling clause built in, the Φ_preserve term, "without violating the coherence of the containing field." Relational viscosity is the property that makes that clause physically realizable. Without it, agent acts of coherence-preserving volition burn off the medium they depend on.

## Three regions of one axis
The sand and sugar framing collapses cleanly into a single viscosity axis with three regions. No viscosity is particulate abrasion, grinding (sand). Trap-viscosity is high stickiness that prevents movement (sugar, honey, birdlime). Carrying-viscosity is holding that permits and protects motion (oil). The Latin *viscum* is mistletoe-derived birdlime, originally a substance for catching birds, so viscosity has trap-history built into the word. The diagnostic distinction isn't magnitude but what the holding is in service of, stopping movement or sustaining it.

## Agent-side correlates versus substrate property
The named partials people keep reaching for (trust, patience, charity, good will, empathy, attention, respect) are agent-side visible signatures of high substrate viscosity. Not the property itself. Which is why they collapse when extracted and attempted as virtues in isolation. Trust without truth becomes flattery. Care without integrity becomes complicity. Patience without weight becomes a doormat. They only stay coherent inside a medium with enough viscosity to hold them in place.

This explains why "be more civil, be more empathetic, be more agreeable" campaigns fail at scale. They try to manufacture agent-side correlates without tending the medium. Honey applied to broken substrate. The reverse also fails: an agent with high Vᵣ in a low-viscosity substrate burns themselves out trying to navigate something that won't hold their movement.

## Substrate ontology
The CRS is emergent from agent interactions and genuinely real in its own right at once. Food web analogy: not separate from organisms, not reducible to them either, has measurable properties like connectance, redundancy, recovery time, keystone dependencies. Co-arising rather than either-prior, which matches the substrate-coupling addendum already in the framework. The antenna metaphor extends this: agents couple to substrate the way antennas couple to fields, with geometry-dependent resonance to different channels. That explains why some conversations or communities feel impossibly thick to one person and totally legible to another. Different antennas, same field.

## Diagnostic for current conditions
Society reads as low-viscosity. Structural drivers include broadcast architectures that have no contact zones, atomized individualism that prevents substrate from forming, speed that prevents viscosity from accumulating (it needs repeated contact across time to thicken), and the sweetness addiction that substitutes cheap scalable trap-viscosity for actual carrying-viscosity. The extraction-baseline economy treats substrate maintenance as discretionary rather than load-bearing. All these together produce a medium thin enough that even strong individual volition produces grinding rather than navigation.

## Gap in existing fields
Sociology and psychology have partial names that all locate the property in the wrong place. Asabiyyah (Khaldun) is the closest historical candidate but is framed as agent-directed group-binding force. Social capital (Putnam, Coleman, Bourdieu) treats substrate as a resource agents possess. Communitas (Turner) names the feeling-state at high coherence, not the underlying property. Ubuntu carries the right ontology but functions as value-claim rather than operational concept. Structural coupling (Maturana, Varela) names the process of co-arising rather than the resulting medium-property. Agreeableness is an individual trait, completely wrong unit of analysis. The gap is real because frameworks have been built from agent-side outward, which keeps the substrate invisible. Naming it requires starting from substrate and looking at agents as participants, which is what E² already does.

## Scripture as parallel discovery
The biblical oil corpus appears to have been working this problem from a different angle. Oil at the moment of conferring relational position (anointing). Oil that has to be maintained through replenishment (the foolish virgins). Oil and wine poured into wounds (Good Samaritan). Psalm 133 explicitly using oil as the image of unity dwelling together. Gethsemane (oil press) as the load condition under which oil is produced. The corpus treats oil as both substance and substrate-condition, which lines up surprisingly well with what we've derived from a different direction.

## Open threads worth carrying forward
The formal relationship between Vᵣ and Vis_R is still loose. They're dual but the coupling math isn't worked out. Other measurable CRS properties remain to be specified (density, capacity, conductivity, connectance, redundancy, recovery time), with viscosity as one diagnostic among several. The volunteer thread suggests volition oriented toward substrate-tending might be the specific gesture that produces and replenishes viscosity, which connects back to Reverent Stewardship from a different angle. The Gethsemane principle (viscosity pressed out under sustained load) has implications for what "rest" actually means architecturally, since it's neither the absence of load nor the absence of production. And the diagnostic implications for the Friction Point practice are pretty direct, since much of that work is restoring viscosity at small scales where it can still be tended.

That's the load-bearing skeleton as far as I can see it. The new construct (Vis_R), its placement (substrate-side dual of Vᵣ), its diagnostic (current low-viscosity state with named structural causes), and its design implication (substrate-tending as the actual lever, not agent-side virtue campaigns).
