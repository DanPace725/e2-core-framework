## 0. Core Claim

Asymmetry Maintenance and Complex Causality should be promoted together because they describe the same landscape at two different temporal resolutions.

Asymmetry Maintenance names the persistence reading of the Global Closure Operator. It asks how a usable difference is held open against decay.

Complex Causality names the propagation reading of that same field. It asks how a deformation commits, travels, accumulates, and becomes terrain.

Short form:

> Asymmetry Maintenance explains how difference is held. Complex Causality explains how held and released differences move.

Compressed grammar:

```text
AMM:        maintain the gradient
Causality: deformation -> closure -> propagation -> terrain
```

Neither document adds a new primitive. The pair gives the existing corpus a shared mechanics for persistence, attribution, consequence, responsibility, and remainder.

## 1. Non-Inflation Rule

The first integration discipline is negative:

> AMM is not a new primitive and Complex Causality is not a new root metaphysics.

AMM is GCO under the persistence lens:

- the operator is the closure operation already present in the corpus;
- the maintained asymmetry is the closed configuration held over time;
- asymmetry maintenance is the repeated work required to keep the configuration from collapsing into unusable sameness.

Complex Causality is the causal grammar read on the same surface:

- cause deforms;
- effect commits;
- consequence propagates;
- consequentiality weighs;
- condition stabilizes;
- attribution selects;
- responsibility obligates;
- accountability responds.

The promotion succeeds only if the pair sharpens existing machinery without inflating the ontology.

## 2. Asymmetry Maintenance

Meaningful relation requires usable difference. A flat field has no salience, no direction, no resolution path, and no action gradient.

Asymmetry Maintenance is the continuous, energy-costing work of holding a usable difference open against decay so that directed flow, interpretation, resolution, and action remain possible.

Creation and maintenance are distinct:

- creation breaks uniformity and introduces difference;
- maintenance holds difference against decay.

Under CFA, Fluctuation creates the opening, Constraint holds the shape, and Attention funds the maintenance under a finite budget.

The single load-bearing addition over ordinary relational ranking is this:

> A maintained gradient is not a stored state. It is a perishable structure requiring continuous upkeep.

That one claim explains why margin matters. A system without margin cannot maintain asymmetry long enough to reach stable closure. It collapses toward cheap attractors: certainty, outrage, apathy, compliance, projection, or premature finality.

## 3. Complex Causality

Causality is the propagation of asymmetry through a constrained landscape over time.

In the paired frame:

- a cause is a deformation of the landscape;
- an effect is local closure into a basin;
- a consequence is closure that continues to travel;
- consequentiality is the field-weight of the traveling deformation;
- a condition is a consequence stabilized into terrain.

The causal loop:

```text
landscape deforms
system commits
commitment propagates
propagation stabilizes
stabilized deformation becomes the next landscape
```

This is why yesterday's consequences become tomorrow's causes. A fast event becomes slow terrain when it crosses the promotion threshold.

Causality here is emergent, not fundamental. The grammar applies where systems are complex, open, constrained, and driven far enough from equilibrium for asymmetries to propagate in attributable chains. At the base layer there may be fluctuations before there are causes.

## 4. Shared Surface

The pair lives on the free-energy / signal landscape already developed by the corpus.

GCO supplies the closure machinery. Signal as Bias Field supplies the continuous pressure and deformation vocabulary. TCL supplies operating-window constraints. Temporal Compression and Ethical Occlusion explains how attribution fails under compression. MMPS explains why unmetabolized consequence persists as latent deformation. Remnant Stewardship explains what remains when closure finishes without actually clearing the field.

In this surface:

- AMM is persistence of deformation against decay;
- causality is deformation moving through commitment and consequence;
- responsibility is assignment to a controlled deformation;
- blame is moral closure over attributed deformation;
- accountability is re-deformation by repair, restraint, redesign, or restitution.

This keeps blame from doing the work of causality. Blame may follow causal resolution; it may not substitute for it.

## 5. Tilt and Reshape

Two causal modes should be preserved:

| Mode | Structure | Typical form | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tilt causation | Biases which basin is preferred without changing the option-space | Prioritize A over B | Can over-amplify existing attractors |
| Reshape causation | Changes the option-space itself | Reorganize the whole structure | Fragile, delay-sensitive, and harder to attribute |

Tilt operates inside an established landscape. Reshape changes the landscape.

This distinction matters ethically because high-power actors often claim to be merely tilting behavior while actually reshaping the categories in which responsibility, option-space, and future attribution will occur.

## 6. Operating Window and Attribution

Complex Causality gives TCL an attribution reading:

- below the viability floor, causal stasis: the field does not propagate enough for assignment;
- above the chaos ceiling, causal incoherence: propagation occurs but cause-effect maps decohere;
- beyond the parametric wall, causal illegibility: the option-space is reshaped faster than maps can update.

The operating window is the band where causation can be effective and attributable.

Justice, in this frame, has two coupled tasks:

1. Keep causal systems inside the attributable window.
2. Prevent remainder from being dumped down-gradient when attribution becomes difficult.

Responsibility laundering is the deliberate or structural movement of the system outside the attribution window while benefits still route up-gradient.

## 7. Margin, Entropy, and Remainder

The pair gives a mechanics for the recurring corpus line:

> The books balance because the remainder left the ledger, not the world.

Large systems often maintain their coherence by exporting entropy, cost, confusion, and responsibility down-gradient. The local system then loses the margin required for AM. It cannot hold usable difference, cannot sustain interpretive friction, cannot route consequence, and cannot reach clean assignment.

This is not primarily stupidity or failure of will. It is saturation.

When there is no margin for AM, the field cannot reach MA. When the field cannot reach MA, closure becomes cheap, brittle, outsourced, or externally captured.

Remnant Stewardship is downstream of this mechanics. It asks where the unheld remainder lands and whether it is metabolized, preserved, restored, released, or merely displaced.

## 10. Load-Bearing and Provisional Claims

Load-bearing:

- AMM is GCO under the persistence lens, not a new primitive.
- A maintained gradient is a perishable structure requiring continuous upkeep.
- AM and MA are action/state readings of one process.
- Complex Causality is deformation, closure, propagation, and stabilization over time.
- Effect, consequence, consequentiality, and condition are roles in a promotion chain.
- Blame must not substitute for causal resolution.
- Responsibility laundering can be described as pushing systems outside the attributable window while preserving up-gradient benefit.
- Remainder does not vanish when it leaves the ledger.

Held loosely:

- Whether AMM is the final handle. - AMM is fine for now
- Whether the free-energy landscape is literal beyond physical and cognitive model classes.
- Whether TCL constants can be used directly in social systems. - Use the concepts of TCL, while holding the literal values loosley. 
- Whether the cause/effect/consequence/condition roles remain clean under every live case. No, but can be understood using other parts of the framework at proper scope and resolution. 
- Whether "causality is emergent" should be stated as a hard metaphysical claim or a scoping rule for this framework. Scoping rule, but settled until proven otherwise or disputed.
