Engine-room node. Captured June 2, 2026. Consolidates the earlier causality consolidation with the recent manifold, TCL, Signal-as-Bias-Field, and AMM connections. 0. Core, one line
Causality is the propagation of asymmetry through a free-energy landscape over time: a cause deforms the landscape, an effect commits to a basin, a consequence propagates the deformation, and a condition is a deformation that stabilizes into the terrain itself.

1. The geometric definition
The earlier pass had causality as "the patterned propagation of asymmetry through constrained relational fields" and gave the stack cause-field → effect → consequence → condition → new cause-field. The manifold frame names what the field is and turns the stack into a loop on it.

The arena is the state-space manifold.
The base landscape is the free-energy surface. Its unmodified flow is descent to the global minimum, which is equilibrium, which is flat, which is death.
A cause is a deformation of that landscape, a tilt or reshape that redirects the trajectory away from bare descent. "Cause biases" means cause deforms.
An effect is a closure event: the system commits to a basin, the GCO fires, the marble drops into a valley.
A consequence is closure that travels: the commitment propagates outward, deforming neighboring regions and recruiting relations.
Consequentiality is field-weight: whether the propagating deformation is large enough to cross thresholds other systems must now route around.
A condition is a consequence that stabilizes into the landscape itself, promoted from a fast-layer event to slow-layer terrain. This is the recursion: yesterday's consequences become tomorrow's causes because stabilized deformations become the next base landscape.

So the causal loop is the TCL slow/fast loop and the Signal-as-Bias deformation, unified: landscape deforms (cause) → system commits to a basin (effect) → commitment propagates as deformation (consequence) → deformation stabilizes into terrain (condition) → new base landscape (new cause-field). Causality is the dynamics of deformation and commitment running over time.

Condition stabilizes. (promotion from event to terrain)

3. Two causal modes
TCL's two coupling types and Signal-as-Bias-Field's two signal types are the same distinction, and it is a distinction the causal grammar did not have on its own.

Tilt causation (additive). The cause biases which basin is preferred without changing the option-space. In the signal doc this is "high-coherence signals amplify existing patterns," deepening a basin you already have. Robust, delay-tolerant. "Prioritize A over B."
Reshape causation (parametric). The cause changes the option-space itself, adding or removing basins. In the signal doc this is "adversarial signals suppress the fluctuations that would produce competing patterns," raising barriers around alternatives. Fragile, and hard-limited: TCL's parametric wall sits at about 0.289, a pure geometric constant, far below the additive window of 0.757 to 0.930. "Reorganize the whole structure."

Consequence: structural causation, changing the conditions themselves, is far more fragile and far more limited than biasing causation within fixed conditions. You can tilt hard. You cannot reshape much before the system becomes fatally delay-sensitive.

4. The operating window is the band of attributable causation
The three TCL constants are three causal-failure regimes, and the window between them is where causation is both effective and attributable.

Below the viability floor: causal stasis. The cause-field is too weak to produce any transition. Nothing propagates. The system sits in one valley.
Above the chaos ceiling: causal incoherence. Causation still happens, but high coupling plus inevitable delay makes the cause-effect map decohere. Boundary Dynamics labels this threshold the point where the capacity to discriminate is overwhelmed, and attribution is discrimination, so this is the physical floor under TC/EO occlusion and responsibility laundering.
Above the parametric wall: causal illegibility. The option-space is reshaped faster than anyone can track, so everyone reasons with an outdated map. This is topology sculpting made into a hard limit.

Inside the window, consequences propagate and can be traced to causes. That is the band where justice is possible. Justice, which the scale doc defines as reducing occlusion and making consequences visible, is the work of keeping the causal system inside its window.

5. Causation is emergent, not fundamental
The cosmology turn put a floor under the whole system. At the base there is no causation. Spontaneous symmetry breaking produces asymmetry from an unstable symmetric state under an arbitrarily small perturbation, and the perturbation can be a quantum fluctuation, which on many readings is genuinely acausal rather than a hidden classical push. The microscopic laws are largely time-symmetric. The causal arrow is the thermodynamic arrow.
So the causal grammar describes an emergent regime, not a fundamental one. Causation turns on only where systems are complex, open, and driven far enough from equilibrium that asymmetries propagate through a constrained landscape in attributable chains. Below that, there are fluctuations, not causes. "What caused the first asymmetry" is likely malformed the way "what is north of the north pole" is malformed. This is the scoping discipline for the whole causality system: it applies in the complex driven regime, and asking it to reach the base is a category error.

6. The moral layer, grounded
Blame is moral closure over an attributed causal relation. Attribution requires being inside the operating window, because outside it the cause-effect map is incoherent (above the chaos ceiling) or the option-space is moving (above the parametric wall). When attribution fails, blame does not disappear. It lands on the most visible low-power node rather than on the deformation source. That is blame capture.
Responsibility laundering then has a precise definition: it is driving the causal system out of its operating window, up past the chaos ceiling with coupling and delay, or past the parametric wall by reshaping conditions faster than they can be tracked, while benefits still route up-gradient. This is the social instance of the entropy gradient from EOTC section eight: benefits concentrate up-gradient where the energy goes in, while entropy and responsibility disperse down-gradient. The powerful maintain their asymmetry by dumping unattributable remainder downward. The books balance because the remainder left the ledger, not the world.
Justice, in this frame, is two coupled moves: keep the causal system inside the attributable window, and reverse the down-gradient dumping so the remainder is metabolized rather than offloaded.
Guardrail, carried from the first pass: never let blame do the work of causality. Blame must follow adequate causal resolution, not replace it, and adequate resolution is only possible inside the window.

7. Effect, consequence, consequentiality, condition
The earlier distinction, now locked to the dynamics.

Effect is local closure. The valley-commitment.
Consequence is closure that keeps traveling. The deformation propagating outward.
Consequentiality is field-weight: whether the deformation is large enough to reshape the terrain, that is, large enough to be promoted from a fast-layer event into the slow-layer landscape.
Condition is a consequence with enough consequentiality to become terrain. The promotion gate is consequentiality.

A consequence that is not promoted and not metabolized does not vanish. It persists as latent deformation, untracked, which is the Power document's latent field tensions and the consequence-routing remainder. MMPS gives the rate: the meaning field equation dM/dt = synthesis minus λM, where λ is the relaxation of a maintained deformation back toward the bare landscape. Untracked consequence is deformation no one is paying to hold or to clear, accumulating where it can no longer be passed on.
