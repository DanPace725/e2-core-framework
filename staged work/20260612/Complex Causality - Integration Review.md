## Collision And Risk Review

- Model-overreach risk: TCL constants should not be used as direct social thresholds without the appropriate model class.
- Moralization risk: the staged document correctly warns that blame must not do the work of causality.
- Dependency risk: this doc leans on AMM and Maintenance Window, which are also staged.
