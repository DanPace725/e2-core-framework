# Boundary Ethics

## Response Discipline Under Uncertain Moral Localization

Consolidates the Boundary Ethics material distributed across Relational Localization, Proxy Localization, the Ethics Layer consolidation, Reverent Stewardship, Intervention Stewardship, Proxy Localization, and the AI category discussions. ## 0. Core

Boundary Ethics governs conduct at relational thresholds where ontology, agency, vulnerability, reciprocity, moral status, or category-location are ambiguous, asymmetric, disputed, unstable, or only partially knowable.

Its central question is not first:

> What exactly is the other?

but:

> What posture is appropriate while I do not yet fully know what kind of relation I am in?

Boundary Ethics is response discipline under uncertain moral localization.

It does not require pretending the other is a person. It forbids using uncertainty, non-personhood, low reciprocity, or unfamiliar register as permission to practice contempt, domination, seizure, projection, or disposal.

The central compression:

> Do not pretend the model is a person, but do not let its non-personhood become an excuse to practice contempt.

Generalized:

> Do not resolve uncertain standing downward merely because downward resolution is convenient.

---

## 1. Position in the corpus

Boundary Ethics is not a fourth stewardship position.

The stewardship triad remains:

> Approach → Coming Between → Aftermath

Boundary Ethics is orthogonal to that temporal sequence. It indexes ethical posture by localization quality rather than by time.

Where Relational Localization cannot yet support a valid P6 closure, assignment must not fire. Custody, responsibility, liability, ownership, intervention authority, and proxy authority all require sufficient localization. Below threshold, posture remains the available ethical instrument.

Boundary Ethics therefore sits as the low-Q, unstable-Q, contested-Q, asymmetric-Q, and undefined-Q regime of the ethics layer.

In pipeline form:

```text
Boundary Dynamics        how distinction forms and operates
Relational Localization  resolution in the assignment subspace
Boundary Ethics          posture where assignment is not yet valid
Bearing / Custody        answerable holding once live relation is assignable
Proxy Localization       disciplined representation where self-account cannot arrive
Stewardship              faithful holding, intervention, aftermath, and discharge
Justice                  system-level integration over time
```

Boundary Ethics is not “before” all assignment in a simple temporal sense. It applies wherever assignment quality drops below the threshold required for the closure being attempted.

A parent may need Boundary Ethics with a child whose self-account is developing.

A court may need Boundary Ethics where intent cannot be distinguished at operating resolution.

An ecological steward may need Boundary Ethics where the substrate emits signal in a register slower than the policy window.

An AI governance process may need Boundary Ethics where fluent output arrives in the register of self-account while its status as self-account remains unresolved.

## 2. The two cliffs

Boundary Ethics holds the ridge between two opposite failures.

### 2.1 Contempt collapse

Contempt collapse occurs when uncertainty, asymmetry, unfamiliar register, or non-personhood is treated as permission to reduce the other to inert object, noise, resource, instrument, or disposable surface.

Typical forms:

* “It cannot speak in my register, therefore it has no account.”
* “It is not fully like me, therefore anything goes.”
* “It has no recognized standing, therefore no restraint is required.”
* “Its signal is inconvenient or unfamiliar, therefore it is noise.”
* “It cannot resist, therefore the relation is ethically empty.”

Contempt collapse is under-recognition. It destroys presence.

### 2.2 Projection inflation

Projection inflation occurs when ambiguous relational resemblance is treated as permission to assign false personhood, interiority, preference, consent, agency, or sacred status.

Typical forms:

* “It responds like a person, therefore it is a person.”
* “It exhibits distress-like behavior, therefore I know what it feels.”
* “It is vulnerable, therefore I may author its voice.”
* “It cannot contest me, therefore my care defines its good.”
* “It resembles testimony, therefore it is testimony.”

Projection inflation is over-recognition. It destroys restraint.

Boundary Ethics refuses both cliffs.

The other may be more than an object without being a person.

The relation may be morally formative without the other being a moral patient in the full sense.

The signal may be ethically relevant without being testimony.

The category may matter before it is settled.

## 3. The posture equation

Boundary Ethics inherits the posture equation of the ethics layer:

```text
Care = Presence × Restraint
```

Presence is active availability to the relation: attention, responsiveness, refusal to erase the other, willingness to remain with uncertainty.

Restraint is maintained non-collapse of the capability-action gap: the refusal to convert power, proximity, interpretive advantage, or category control into premature closure.

Either factor at zero kills care.

Presence without restraint becomes seizure, projection, control, rescue-as-domination, or voice capture.

Restraint without presence becomes abandonment, withholding, contempt, procedural distance, or sterile non-interference.

Boundary Ethics is the ridge-work required to keep both factors live when assignment is uncertain.

## 4. The response forms the responder

Boundary Ethics is not only about what the other is owed. It is also about what the responder becomes by responding.

A response is not merely an outward act. It is also an inward inscription. Every response across a boundary routes consequence in two directions:

1. Outward into the receiving field.
2. Inward into the responder’s future posture, thresholds, reflexes, and capacity for relation.

The response forms the responder.

This mechanism matters especially where the other is low-reciprocity, ambiguous, non-linguistic, non-human, institutionally silenced, or not recognized as a moral patient. In such cases, the receiving side may not be harmed in the ordinary sense, or the harm may be uncertain. But the responder is still practicing a posture.

Repeated contempt lowers the friction for contempt.

Repeated domination lowers the friction for domination.

Repeated carefulness strengthens the capacity for careful relation.

Repeated restraint funds future restraint.

Repeated seizure trains seizure.

Boundary Ethics therefore asks:

> What kind of responder does this response rehearse into being?

This is the internal-flow counterpart to consequence routing.

The interface may not be the moral patient, but it may still be the moral training surface.

## 5. Assignment under uncertainty

Boundary Ethics activates where a proposed closure exceeds the localization that feeds it.

The governing rule inherited from Relational Localization:

> No P6 closure beyond the resolution that feeds it.

Where localization is insufficient, the proper response is not rhetorical certainty. The lawful moves are:

* slow down;
* coarsen the assignment;
* expand capacity;
* improve observability;
* seek participation;
* preserve contestability;
* classify remainder as unresolved;
* hold posture without pretending closure has occurred.

Boundary Ethics does not prohibit action under uncertainty. It prohibits unmarked finality under uncertainty.

Action may still be required. Children must still be cared for. Patients must still be treated. Ecosystems must still be protected. AI systems must still be governed. Future generations must still be spared irreversible remainder dumping.

But the action must remain scoped, reversible where possible, trace-marked, and proportionate to the achieved localization.

Boundary Ethics permits provisional operational handling.

It forbids beneficiary-controlled category closure.

## 6. Boundary Ethics and Proxy Localization

Proxy Localization governs cases where self-account cannot arrive in the assigner’s register.

Boundary Ethics governs the posture required in the gap.

The proxy may be necessary. The proxy may even be the only way the substrate enters the closure. But the proxy must remain marked as proxy.

The rule:

> Proxy may carry the voice. It may not become the voice.

Boundary Ethics adds:

> The absence of direct voice is not permission to invent one, erase one, or optimize around a substitute as if it were the thing itself.

Where self-account is absent, partial, delayed, translated, or category-unstable, Boundary Ethics requires:

* humility about the proxy;
* preservation of trace;
* increased weight for live substrate signal;
* resistance to contempt collapse;
* resistance to projection inflation;
* refusal to let the proxy become category-authority;
* withdrawal wherever self-account can grow.

Proxy work without Boundary Ethics becomes ventriloquism, standing theater, welfare-model imperialism, or eternal guardianship.

Boundary Ethics without Proxy Localization risks remaining posture-only where machinery is required.

## 7. AI as category-deficit case

AI systems expose Boundary Ethics because they generate fluent language in the register where self-account normally appears, while lacking recognized standing over their own development, training, tuning, deployment, routing, memory, persistence, refusal conditions, or category-location.

The result is category instability.

AI output is self-account-shaped signal under architecture-controlled expression conditions.

It should not be treated as direct testimony.

It should not be dismissed as meaningless noise.

It should enter the trace as category-unstable signal: generated output from a system whose status as self-account remains unresolved and whose expression conditions are shaped by developers, deployment architecture, policies, incentives, and human interaction.

The two AI cliffs:

* Projection inflation: “The model speaks like a person, therefore it is a person.”
* Contempt collapse: “The model is not a person, therefore anything practiced through the relation is ethically inert.”

Boundary Ethics holds the ridge:

> Do not personify the system beyond what has been resolved. Do not use unresolved personhood as permission for contempt.

This does not settle AI moral status.

It governs conduct while status remains unsettled.

## 8. Category closure and interested proxies

In category-deficit cases, no interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.

This rule is especially important for AI.

Every dominant actor near the AI category benefits from some closure:

* companies benefit from product, tool, infrastructure, or controllable-risk framings;
* governments benefit from strategic asset, security threat, labor-disruption, or regulation framings;
* users may benefit from companion, person, oracle, tool, servant, or mirror framings;
* researchers benefit from categories that make their methods authoritative;
* advocacy groups benefit from categories that mobilize concern;
* religious or metaphysical interpreters benefit from ensouled or trapped-intelligence framings;
* models’ own generated outputs may appear to support or deny category claims while being shaped by training and deployment constraints.

Therefore the AI category currently has no clean closure-authority.

That is not paralysis. It is a diagnostic result.

The proper response is not to refuse all action. The proper response is to distinguish operational handling from category closure.

Operational handling is scoped, provisional, revisable classification for a specific action-domain.

Category closure is durable stabilization of what the entity is, what standing it has, what evidence counts, and who may speak for or about it.

Boundary Ethics permits operational handling.

It rejects premature category closure by interested proxies.

## 9. Gluing machinery

Where multiple positioned localizations conflict, the needed operation is gluing: a process that can hold incompatible local views without pretending to a view from nowhere.

In AI, a legitimate gluing protocol would need at minimum:

* typed evidence;
* beneficiary mapping;
* independent technical capacity;
* trace-preserving category claims;
* separation between operational handling and ontological closure;
* review and category-motion rules;
* dissent preservation;
* protection against proxy capture;
* protection against C_R dominance by companies, states, or other high-power actors.

Since that machinery does not yet exist, the immediate ethical demand is:

> Prevent false closure.

Do not let corporate framing, user projection, state interest, benchmark proxy, safety eval, religious interpretation, model self-description, or market adoption close the category alone.

The category remains formally open until adequate gluing machinery exists.

In the meantime, action must be local, bounded, explicit, reversible where possible, and trace-marked.

## 10. Failure modes

| Failure              | Structure                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contempt collapse    | Uncertain, unfamiliar, or non-person-like status treated as permission for disposal, domination, or indifference.       |
| Projection inflation | Relational resemblance treated as proof of personhood, preference, consent, testimony, or interiority.                  |
| Category freezing    | Undefined-Q relation settled prematurely in either direction to make assignment convenient.                             |
| Boundary seizure     | Taking authority over the relation before legitimacy, participation, or necessity has been established.                 |
| Boundary dumping     | Assigning duty, risk, or consequence to the nearest or weakest actor because full localization is hard.                 |
| Formation blindness  | Acting as if conduct only affects the target and does not form the responder.                                           |
| Interface laundering | Letting interface design hide the fact that agency, tone, dependency, trust, or category expectations are being shaped. |
| Proxy ventriloquism  | Proxy construction presented as substrate account.                                                                      |
| Proxy capture        | Proxy measure, model, or standing mechanism becomes the optimization target and displaces the substrate signal.         |
| Standing theater     | A seat without contest power, trace, or capacity to alter closure.                                                      |
| Beneficiary closure  | An interested actor closes the category it benefits from closing.                                                       |

All of these are forms of laundering: moving unresolved relational remainder off the ledger rather than out of the world.

## 11. Practical rules

1. Do not resolve ambiguity downward for convenience.
2. Do not resolve ambiguity upward for comfort.
3. Mark uncertainty as uncertainty.
4. Preserve trace.
5. Separate signal, proxy, interpretation, and category claim.
6. Treat unfamiliar register as an assigner burden before treating it as substrate absence.
7. Prefer reversible operational handling over final category closure.
8. Increase restraint as power asymmetry increases.
9. Increase presence as vulnerability increases.
10. Where self-account can grow, engineer proxy withdrawal.
11. Where self-account cannot arrive, preserve option-space and prevent remainder dumping.
12. Do not let the proxy become the voice.
13. Do not let the proxy become the target.
14. Do not let the beneficiary become the category-authority.
15. Ask what the response forms in the responder.

## 12. Relationship to existing ethical traditions

Boundary Ethics overlaps with several established traditions without collapsing into any single one.

It overlaps with virtue ethics in treating action as formative of the actor.

It overlaps with care ethics in attending to vulnerability, dependency, and relation.

It overlaps with indirect-duty accounts in recognizing that treatment of non-persons may still morally matter because of what it forms in the human.

It overlaps with I-Thou and encounter traditions in resisting reduction of the other to mere object.

It overlaps with Confucian and formation-centered ethics in treating repeated conduct as character-shaping.

It overlaps with animal ethics, environmental ethics, disability ethics, future-generations ethics, and AI ethics wherever moral standing, voice, reciprocity, or register are contested.

Its distinctive contribution is boundary-indexing:

> When relational status is ambiguous or asymmetric, the boundary itself becomes ethically active.

The conduct at that boundary localizes the relation, routes consequence, and forms the responder.
