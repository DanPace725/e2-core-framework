0. Working name
The core object of this thread is the noun/verb pair MA and AM. Proposed working handle:
Asymmetry-Maintenance Morphism (AMM). The endomorphism that holds a configuration against decay. Its fixed points are maintained asymmetries (MA), the configurations it returns unchanged because they are already stable. Its iterated application is asymmetry maintenance (AM), the continuous work of returning the configuration to that fixed point against the second-law pull toward flat.
Important discipline: AMM is not a new primitive. It is the persistence reading of an operator you already built, the GCO. A closure operator is an idempotent endomorphism; MA is its closed set; AM is the GCO firing again and again to hold it there. AMM names that operator under the lens of persistence-over-time. Calling it new would be the one-operator inflation this thread has been careful to avoid.
Alternatives if AMM does not sit right: Maintenance Closure, Persistence Closure, or simply keeping MA/AM with the note that they are two resolutions of one process, not two things. Your call.

2. The core, stated whole
Meaningful existence requires usable difference. A flat field has no gradient, no salience, no direction. Movement begins where symmetry breaks. Asymmetry maintenance is the continuous, energy-costing work of holding a usable difference open against decay so that directed flow, interpretation, resolution, and action remain possible.
Two coupled moves stay distinct. Creation breaks a uniformity and injects a difference. Maintenance holds that difference against the pull back toward sameness. CFA maps onto this: Fluctuation creates asymmetry, Constraint maintains it, Attention powers the maintenance under a finite budget.
The operator is substrate-invariant (AVIA). The organism holds a chemical and structural gradient against equilibrium. The mind holds an asymmetry in signal space (this matters more than that) against the pull toward flat. Literacy holds usable asymmetries across symbolic fields. Same operator, different substrate. The parameters vary by system and scale: gradient shape, boundary permeability, modulation capacity.
The single claim that makes this load-bearing rather than poetic: a maintained gradient is not a stored state, it is a structure held against continuous decay, and the holding costs energy without pause. That is the one thing AMM adds over plain relational ranking, and everything downstream (the endurance floor, why cheap attractors win, the margin analysis) descends from it.

3. Key insights, grouped
Existence and identity. Exist = run the maintenance loop. State is a composite (P1+P3+P4), not a primitive, the same way signal is a composite of all six. Identity is relational precipitate (Yoneda, formally). Noun and verb are resolution slices of one process.
Valuation. The braid is one operator: relational ranking, equivalently the typed permeability of the reception boundary, equivalently a standing bias field below awareness. It generates the gradient resolution falls along, so it is not a pipeline stage but the slope the stages obey. Value splits into stability (valere, *wal-) and flexibility (worth, *wer-, held loosely), which is CFA localized to appraisal.
Interpretation and literacy. To interpret is to reconstruct usable asymmetry across a boundary. Bad interpretation carries the symbol and loses the gradient. Literacy is context-specific interpretive agency: maintaining, interpreting, evaluating, and acting on the meaningful asymmetries inside a symbolic field. The modifiers (financial, civic, media) name the field, not a different operation.
The endurance floor. Holding the friction open, the layer-1 capacity most literacy failures never pass, is maintaining a local asymmetry against the symmetrizing slide. It costs energy, so it fails first when energy is low. Cheap attractors (outrage, certainty, apathy) win because they are the differentiated gradient collapsing onto one steep cheap axis. Premature collapse is the GCO firing early when signal intensity exceeds modulation capacity (Signal as Bias Field).
Substrate and scale. Literacy needs three live couplings (self, other, world / C_body, C_cog, C_eco). The literacy crisis is C_cog degradation at node resolution, a cross-section of CRS triple-coherence failure, often downstream of C_eco foreclosure. You cannot fix C_cog with C_cog interventions.
The margin and the gradient. Systems maintain coherence by dumping entropy down the power gradient onto individuals (EOTC section eight). Saturated individuals have no margin for AM, so cannot reach MA, so collapse to cheap attractors, so become available for MRIE capture. Remainder cascades and accumulates untracked.
The ethics. Relation is maintained asymmetry held without annihilation. Collapsing another's asymmetry-maintenance capacity is relational annihilation, the basic harm the RBoR names. The whole ethical test reduces to: which way is this actor moving the margin. Regenerative restores it, extractive consumes it.
