## Working Read

The candidate claim is that Asymmetry-Maintenance Morphism (AMM), maintained asymmetry (MA), and asymmetry maintenance (AM) name the persistence reading of an existing operator rather than a new primitive. The staged document's own discipline is load-bearing: AMM is the GCO read as persistence-over-time; MA is the maintained fixed point; AM is the repeated work of returning a configuration to that fixed point against decay.

Provisional integration type: construct, conservatively classified as a GCO specialization / bridge.

The new distinction is not "there is another primitive." It is that a maintained gradient is not a stored state. It is a continuous, energy-costing relation between state, operator, and substrate margin.
