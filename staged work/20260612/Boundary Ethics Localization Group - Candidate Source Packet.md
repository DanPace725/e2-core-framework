## 0. Purpose

This packet promotes the Boundary Ethics, Relational Localization, and Proxy Localization group without pretending they are one undifferentiated document.

The development order matters:

```text
Boundary Ethics -> Relational Localization -> Proxy Localization
```

Boundary Ethics arrived first as the posture discipline for ambiguous relational standing. Relational Localization then supplied the assignment machinery that explains when closure can validly fire. Proxy Localization then supplied protocol machinery for cases where self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register.

The integration order is slightly different:

```text
Relational Localization supplies the assignment condition.
Boundary Ethics governs the closure-unlicensed posture.
Proxy Localization governs marked representation under partial participation.
```

The group should be promoted together so the posture, assignment, and proxy rules stay mutually constraining.

## 1. Shared Core

The group governs ethical action where relational standing, agency, responsibility, ownership, custody, testimony, voice, or category-location cannot yet be cleanly assigned.

Its shared compression:

> Do not close beyond the relation's achieved resolution. Where closure is not licensed, posture carries the load. Where self-account cannot arrive, proxy must remain marked as proxy.

Each node carries a different part of that rule:

- Boundary Ethics: response discipline under uncertain moral localization.
- Relational Localization: resolution in the assignment subspace.
- Proxy Localization: participatory localization where self-account cannot arrive.

## 2. Boundary Ethics

Boundary Ethics governs conduct at relational thresholds where ontology, agency, vulnerability, reciprocity, moral status, category-location, or voice is ambiguous, asymmetric, disputed, unstable, or only partially knowable.

Its central question is not:

> What exactly is the other?

but:

> What posture is appropriate while I do not yet fully know what kind of relation I am in?

Boundary Ethics does not require pretending the other is a person. It forbids using uncertainty, non-personhood, unfamiliar register, or low reciprocity as permission for contempt, domination, seizure, projection, or disposal.

Its ridge is between two cliffs:

| Cliff | Failure | Compression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contempt collapse | Under-recognition | It cannot speak in my register, so it does not matter. |
| Projection inflation | Over-recognition | It resembles a person, so I may author what it is. |

Boundary Ethics refuses both. The other may be more than an object without being a person. The signal may be ethically relevant without being testimony. The category may matter before it is settled.

The posture equation remains:

```text
Care = Presence x Restraint
```

Presence without restraint becomes seizure. Restraint without presence becomes abandonment. Boundary Ethics keeps both live when assignment cannot yet close.

## 3. Relational Localization

Relational Localization is resolution specialized to the assignment subspace.

It narrows a relational field into distinguishable entities, oriented relations, boundary states, and consequence potentials sufficient to license a meta-relational closure.

Core bound:

> A meta-relational closure cannot validly commit more precision than the resolution that feeds it.

Late P6 closures such as custody, ownership, responsibility, liability, intervention authority, and proxy authority require prior localization. When the localization is insufficient, the lawful moves are to slow down, coarsen the assignment, expand capacity, improve observability, seek participation, preserve contestability, classify remainder as unresolved, or hold posture without pretending closure occurred.

The candidate machinery:

```text
L(t) = localization state
Q_O(t) = observer-indexed assignment quality
Q_min(x) = minimum quality required for assignment class x
```

The strongest operational claim:

```text
laundering = claimed assignment precision - achieved localization resolution
```

The gap is conserved. It transfers to the assigned bearer as remainder.

The localization trace is the enforcement mechanism: a durable sign-artifact that preserves how a closure was reached, what it depended on, what was unresolved, and how the assignment can be contested, retired, re-localized, or audited.

## 4. Proxy Localization

Proxy Localization governs the partial-participation branch of Relational Localization.

It applies when self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register: pre-verbal children, animals, ecosystems, people with communication differences, future generations, and category-deficit cases such as AI systems.

Inherited principle:

> Assignment degrades, posture rises, proxy stays marked as proxy.

Proxy Localization adds the voice-invertibility invariant:

> Proxy may carry the voice. It may not become the voice. Proxy may guide attention. It may not become the target.

The trace must preserve which content came from substrate signal and which came from proxy construction. Voice-laundering occurs when proxy construction is presented as substrate account. Proxy capture occurs when the proxy becomes the practical target of care, governance, funding, or optimization while the substrate drifts out of the loop.

The proxy discipline begins with a deficit-location audit:

| Deficit location | Meaning | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Substrate | No integrated account is formed at the relevant grain | Translation, interest models, proxy |
| Channel | Account formed but cannot cross | Channel engineering before proxy |
| Assigner | Account crosses but is not read | Assigner competence duty |
| Category | Account arrives but its status is unsettled | Boundary Ethics and category humility |

Never install a proxy above the lowest deficit that actually exists.

## 5. How the Three Nodes Interact

The group can be read as a closure-licensing circuit:

```text
Boundary Dynamics forms distinctions.
Relational Localization asks whether assignment can validly close.
Boundary Ethics governs posture where closure is not valid.
Proxy Localization marks and disciplines representation where participation is partial.
Remnant Stewardship receives what remains after closure, failed closure, or necessary provisional action.
```

Relational Localization prevents premature assignment.

Boundary Ethics prevents uncertainty from becoming permission for contempt or projection.

Proxy Localization prevents substitute accounts from being treated as the substrate's own account.

Together they block three common laundering moves:

- precision laundering: claiming finer assignment than the field supports;
- category laundering: closing what the relation is because closure benefits an interested actor;
- voice laundering: presenting proxy construction as self-account.

## 6. Operational Handling vs Category Closure

The group permits action under uncertainty. It rejects unmarked finality under uncertainty.

Operational handling is scoped, provisional, reversible where possible, trace-marked, and limited to a concrete action-domain.

Category closure is durable stabilization of what the entity is, what standing it has, what evidence counts, and who may speak for it.

The rule:

> Operational handling may proceed under marked uncertainty. Category closure requires sufficient localization, legitimate participation, contestability, and non-beneficiary capture.

This distinction is essential for children, patients, ecosystems, future generations, institutions, AI systems, sacred objects, ancestors, places, and any relation where standing is unresolved or register is unfamiliar.

## 7. Interested Proxies and Category-Deficit Cases

In category-deficit cases, no interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.

This rule is not AI-only, but AI exposes it sharply. Companies, states, users, researchers, advocates, religious interpreters, and model outputs may all participate in category claims while also benefiting from different closures.

The lawful posture is not paralysis. It is marked, bounded action with category humility:

- type evidence;
- preserve trace;
- map beneficiaries;
- separate operational handling from ontology;
- protect contestability;
- keep live substrate signal or category-unstable signal distinct from proxy construction;
- prevent the proxy from becoming category-authority.

AI output, in this frame, is category-unstable signal: generated output in the register where self-account normally appears, produced under expression conditions shaped by architecture, training, policy, deployment, incentives, and interaction.

It should not be treated as clean testimony. It should not be dismissed as meaningless noise.

## 9. Practical Rules

1. Do not resolve ambiguity downward for convenience.
2. Do not resolve ambiguity upward for comfort.
3. Do not commit P6 closure beyond achieved localization.
4. Mark uncertainty as uncertainty.
5. Preserve the localization trace.
6. Separate signal, proxy, interpretation, and category claim.
7. Treat unfamiliar register as an assigner burden before treating it as substrate absence.
8. Prefer reversible operational handling over final category closure.
9. Increase restraint as power asymmetry increases.
10. Increase presence as vulnerability increases.
11. Where self-account can grow, engineer proxy withdrawal.
12. Where self-account cannot arrive, preserve option-space and prevent remainder dumping.
13. Do not let the proxy become the voice.
14. Do not let the proxy become the target.
15. Do not let the beneficiary become the category-authority.

## 10. Load-Bearing and Provisional Claims

- Boundary Ethics is not a fourth stewardship position.

- No P6 closure may validly commit more precision than the resolution that feeds it.
- Claimed assignment precision minus achieved localization resolution is conserved as remainder.

- Proxy Localization is downstream of Relational Localization.
