## 0. Core, one line

Relational Localization is resolution (RS) specialized to the assignment subspace: the narrowing of a relational field into distinguishable entities, oriented relations, and weighted consequence potentials sufficient to license a meta-relational closure. Custody, ownership, responsibility, liability, and intervention authority are late-stage P6 closures over that resolved structure, and the governing bound is:

> **A meta-relational closure cannot validly commit more precision than the resolution that feeds it.**

Laundering is the violation of the bound, and the gap is conserved: claimed precision minus achieved resolution does not vanish, it transfers to the assigned bearer as remainder.

The remedies are lawful and few: coarsen to the supportable grain, localize participatorily where the state must be told rather than measured, keep the trace visible, re-localize when signs go stale, and hold posture where assignment cannot yet fire.

## 1. Extension, not addition

v0.1 positioned localization as a new operation between Boundary Dynamics and the bearing family. The deeper identification follows the corpus's own template: SMFR is the general routing operation and CR is its agentic specialization; the same move applies one level up. Resolution is the general operation, the invariant under the whole reduction family (RS), and localization is resolution running in the subspace where the output is assignment-relevant structure. A localization event is a GCO firing on the attribution-relevant components of the signal field. "He is holding the bowl" is sign-formation in the attribution subspace (SMFR): a closure-artifact that converts ungovernable relational ambiguity into governable, contestable, auditable relation. The localization trace (§12) is that sign-artifact made durable.

Everything established for resolution transfers without re-derivation: the dissolution-recombination arc, the Rayleigh criterion, the three pathologies, observer-relativity, the prior-dissolution requirement (RS). The ordinary-language observation survives intact and gains a sibling: hold, bear, carry, keep, answer-for are the slow-layer continuous-relational verbs (FO) performing low-resolution localization closures fast enough for practical cognition. Two documents independently found English grammar operating as a closure protocol; that is triangulation, and the two threads are one thread.

## 2. The state and the functional

v0.1 carried three notations for one object, and one was circular (Q appeared both as a component of the state and as a function of the state's other components). Corrected:

**Localization state:**

```
L(t) = ⟨ D, G, Θ, Χ, W ⟩
```

distinguishability, geometric-causal placement, relation orientation, boundary coupling state (BD), consequence-weight profile (CR).

**Assignment quality** is not part of the state. It is a positioned functional on the state:

```
Q_O(t) = q( L(t); Π, Λ_g, 𝒪, Ω, R_obs, Δt, C_eff )
```

proximity, legitimacy gradient, observability, occlusion (EOTC's combined form), the observer's resolution, the attribution window, effective compression. The subscript O is load-bearing: Q is observer-indexed (FO observer clause), which is what makes localization conflict (§8) a well-formed object rather than an error state. Two observers can hold the same L(t) and different Q.

## 3. The typing of the state (EDF)

**Load-bearing, new in v0.2.** The components of L(t) are not epistemically uniform. They split into:

**External-resolvable components.** Position, causal contact, flow, boundary state, timing: the P3-heavy facts. These can in principle be driven to high Q by instrumentation, proximity, and time.

**Substrate-constitutive components.** Intent, meaning, identity, agency, interiority: emergent properties of the localized entity's own Zone 3 dynamics. Per EDF these can be conditioned and modeled from outside but never determined. There is no external route to them; every apparent external determination is substitution, an L′(t) supplied under the name L(t).

Three consequences follow immediately:

**(a) The substitution identity.** Hostile localization and EDF substitution are the same operation. Gaslighting, forced redefinition, algorithmic categorization, credentialism: an external system imposes identity′, intent′, meaning′ under the original names. The RBoR prohibits the operation (P1 forced redefinition, P6 meta-cognitive hijacking); EDF explains why it always yields a counterfeit rather than a determination; and EDF's reversibility-by-recognition clause supplies the mechanism by which the counterfeit breaks: substitution is sustained by recognition failure, so the localized entity's voice is structurally what defeats it.

**(b) The participatory requirement.** Q over substrate-constitutive components cannot reach assignment threshold by observation alone, structurally, ever. Valid assignment over them requires **participatory localization**: the substrate's own self-account entering the closure as a first-class input, not a courtesy. Contestability is therefore not procedure but the only available route to valid Q for this component class. This was proposed as a floor condition in working sessions; with EDF underneath it, it moves to load-bearing.

**(c) The triangulation.** Mens rea. Criminal intent is a substrate-constitutive property of the defendant. The adversarial trial, the right to be heard, the right to confront accusers, are the participatory requirement institutionalized centuries before its derivation existed. Independent arrival at a conclusion already entailed by the framework: external triangulation, logged as such.

**(d) The partial-participation principle.** Many substrates cannot deliver self-account in the assigner's register: pre-verbal children, animals, ecosystems, people with communication differences, AI systems, future generations. Where participation is impossible or partial, three rules govern, jointly:

1. **Assignment degrades.** Q over the substrate-constitutive components is structurally capped, so the admissible assignment class coarsens lawfully (§4). The cap is not a deficiency to be argued around; it is the Rayleigh limit of the relation.
2. **Posture rises.** The closure deficit transfers to the posture budget (BE, EL): presence and restraint carry the load that assignment cannot. As participatory access falls, the admissible grain coarsens and the posture obligation rises; the two move inversely.
3. **Proxy stays marked as proxy.** Guardian accounts, advocate testimony, behavioral inference, and welfare models may enter the localization, but the trace must record them as proxy. An unmarked proxy account is EDF substitution: the guardian's L′(t) supplied under the entity's name. Marking is the anti-substitution discipline, and it is what distinguishes advocacy from capture.

The full protocol branch (per-substrate protocols, guardianship structures, proxy typing and weighting, advocacy-capture failure modes, the guardian's own C_R risk) is a separate extension node, queued. The principle lives here; the machinery lives there.

## 4. The bound and the typed thresholds

The assignment condition is not new law; it is the Resolution-Responsibility Law (RRL, EOTC, Justice) generalized from the routing domain to all P6 closure, with EDF now sitting beside it in the law stratum (EL §2). The admissibility form:

```
A_x admissible  iff  Q_O(t) ≥ Q_min(x)
```

with three structural commitments:

**Q_min is a Rayleigh threshold (RS).** Each assignment class x has a minimum resolvable separation. Where candidate bearers cannot be distinguished at operating resolution, the correct behavior is coarsening: joint liability, collective responsibility, institutional accountability are not failures of individual assignment but Rayleigh-correct assignment at the supportable grain. Fabricated individual blame under unresolvable conditions is sub-Rayleigh assignment, which is laundering by precision-fabrication.

**Classes inherit conditions from their governing regimes (EL).** The thresholds are not a flat list. Intervention authority must prospectively pass the margin and withdrawal tests (Intervention). Custody requires the bearer-capacity check against the substrate's operator-capacity vector (CR). Ownership carries the consequence-weight and custody-obligation riders (v0.1 §12, retained). Assignment classes touching substrate-constitutive components additionally require the participatory condition (§3b). All classes are floored by the RBoR: an assignment that violates the floor is inadmissible at any Q.

**Degradation is lawful, not optional.** When R_obs · C_eff > R_max (RRL), the system slows, coarsens, expands capacity, or explicitly classifies remainder as unresolved. Increasing rhetorical certainty instead is the compression-collapse failure (CR), and under MMPS it has a metabolic reading: localization is synthesis work requiring Δt_avail, so Quadrant II systems, compressed and starving, lose localization capacity structurally. Crisis-mode institutions misassign even in good faith because the work cannot be funded.

## 5. The bearing family, derived

v0.1 §6 presented hold/contain/carry/bear/charge/custody/stewardship as a free-standing catalog. It is not one. The bearing relations are flow-operator compositions (FO) with accumulating P6 answerability:

| Bearing term | Composition |
|---|---|
| Holding | stance-with, assignment-salient |
| Containing | stance-with + stance-against (maintained interiority, refused diffusion) |
| Carrying | stance-with under P2 displacement |
| Bearing | stance-with ∘ consequence-receipt over time (why it hurts) |
| Charge | bearing + open settlement obligation back to a delegator |
| Custody | bearing + standing answerability: exposure to future consequence-routing (CR) |
| Stewardship | custody + the regenerative criterion, run under the posture (EL) |

This is strict condensation: the family derives from FO rather than standing beside it, the ladder is a ladder of operator-composition plus accumulating answerability, and it partially settles FO's open thread on per-operator primitive decomposition by exhibiting the operators under P6 dominance.

## 6. The capacity axes, identified

v0.1 §7 introduced three axes; all three are existing objects:

- **C_B (bearer capacity)** is the substrate operator-capacity vector (CR): what the candidate bearer can stance-with, stance-against, settle, deplete, trade, and receive, bounded by the TCL constants.
- **C_K (consequence capacity)** is the pressure profile π of the held relation (CR), multi-component, conserved component-wise.
- **C_R (reshape capacity)** is Power: field-curvature capacity, the ability to deform the category-space in which future localization occurs. In TCL terms it is parametric modulation, and it is **wall-limited**: reshape is hard-capped near 0.289, beyond which the coupled system becomes fatally delay-sensitive. Category capture is therefore structurally bounded and self-betraying: an actor reshaping the localization landscape toward the wall produces rising delay-sensitivity as a detectable signature.

The genuinely new content of v0.1 §7 survives as a rule rather than an axis-set: **higher C_R raises Q_min.** Actors with category-shaping power cannot be localized by first-order actions alone; their relation to the field includes how they bend the categories by which assignment will later be made, and the wall gives the rule a quantitative anchor.

## 7. De-localization and stale assignment

**Load-bearing, absent from v0.1.** Every resolution has a prior dissolution (RS: resolvere, loosen first, recombine into a form fewer things can be). Localization inherits the requirement: when L(t) moves, the old closure must dissolve before a new one validly commits. The bowl example contained it all along: holding becomes harm not by the bowl changing essence but by the localized relation shifting, and the shift demands re-localization.

The failure mode is **stale assignment**, and its mechanism is sign-localization decoupling (SMFR): signs are durable, L(t) is not, so the sign-artifact outlives the localization that justified it. Criminal records, credit scores, diagnoses, reputations, treaties drawn for fields that no longer exist. Stale assignment is where the framework touches lived injustice most directly.

The rights basis: the Right to Self-Determination (P6) plus the Right to Closure (RBoR capstone) jointly entail standing to force re-localization when L(t) has moved. Expungement, credit repair, reputational recovery stop being mercies and become structural requirements. The half-life of a localization, and the conditions of sign-retirement, are open (§16).

## 8. Localization conflict and gluing (SBF)

SBF's fourth lens is already named the **Localization lens**: locally coherent patterns that may or may not glue into global coherence, with GCO attempting the gluing and gluing failure experienced as incoherence or perceived dishonesty. The assignment operation of this document runs inside local patches; **localization conflict is the gluing question.**

Two observers localize the same field differently, both with Q ≥ Q_min from their positions, and neither is wrong (MPDC, FO observer clause). Courts, custody disputes, arbitration, border commissions, and most marital arguments are **gluing protocols**: institutional machinery for producing a shared closure from incompatible positioned localizations without pretending a view-from-nowhere exists. Where no global section exists, the lawful outcomes are a constructed coarser shared closure (quotient), or measured, bounded disagreement (SBF), never fabricated agreement.

Three sources of conflict, only two of which sharpening can address: resolution difference (one party under-instrumented), position difference (genuinely different patches), and **embedding difference** (Power): observers cannot see dynamics at their own organizational level, so some disagreements are embedding-level and survive any improvement of either party's instruments. Adjudication design should triage which kind it is facing before deciding whether better evidence can help at all.

## 9. The adversarial overlay (AOMI)

Localization is an accountability mechanism, and the Gaming Inevitability Principle applies: any sufficiently sophisticated accountability mechanism will be gamed by actors who understand its structure. The attack surface is L(t) and Q component-wise: topology sculpting attacks G, cognitive jamming attacks R_obs, temporal arbitrage attacks Δt, evidence dilution and frame confusion attack the gluing process itself. The deeper structure is directional, two attack families:

**Occlusion attacks: suppress Q to escape assignment.** Fragment decisions across actors and time so no entity crosses the attribution threshold. In this document's terms, **sub-Rayleigh engineering**: deliberately manufacturing indistinguishability so assignment must coarsen, then hiding inside the collective closure. The Rayleigh-correct coarsening of §4 is exploitable, and the exploit must be priced in.

**Seizure attacks: force false localization to dump assignment.** Manufactured urgency, scapegoating under compression, premature superposition collapse (Power): commit the field to a closure before the field supports one, with the assignment landing where the attacker aims. Note the rhyme with the two cliffs (BE, EL): one family prevents closure, the other forces it.

**The counter-move, load-bearing:** AOMI's paradoxical policing translated into assignment terms. **When low Q is manufactured, the manufacture is the assignable act.** The occluded harm cannot be localized, but the production of unassignability can, because gaming creates exposure: engineered occlusion carries coordination overhead, explanation complexity, and pattern inconsistency that natural occlusion lacks, and uncertainty has beneficiaries that field analysis can surface (AOMI Layers 4 and 5). Attribution moves up one level instead of failing. This answers v0.1's research question 7 (distinguishing legitimate unresolved remainder from strategic ambiguity): beneficiary analysis plus exposure factor, with audit intensity rising, not falling, with Ω.

## 10. The localizer is in the field

v0.1 treated localization as reading; it is perturbation. Knowing is causal (Reverent Stewardship's epistemic ethic); attention focusing and measurement demands collapse superposition with efficiency loss (Power), so localizing power partially destroys what it measures; and the act of localizing rehearses a posture in the localizer (BE: the response forms the responder, applying to the mapping as much as to the act). Running the diagnostic protocol on a person, a marriage, an institution, or an AI system is a flow across the boundary into the field being mapped, and at sufficient C_R it is seizure with a clipboard.

The protocol therefore carries a standing clause rather than a new stage: **the localizer is inside the field; the mapping is a perturbation; the act forms the mapper.** The posture (EL: reverence, Care = Presence × Restraint) modulates every stage, and Boundary Ethics governs whenever closure is unlicensed. "Low Q" was shorthand; licensing fails in at least five ways:

- **Insufficient Q**: magnitude below threshold, the plain case.
- **Unstable Q**: L(t) drifting faster than the assignment window; any closure would be stale on arrival (§7).
- **Ungluable Q**: positioned localizations that do not glue (§8); contested, with no global section available.
- **Asymmetric Q**: one party localizes the other far better than the reverse (RBoR black-boxing, Power embedding blindness), so any closure carries substitution risk.
- **Undefined Q**: category instability. Q is computed relative to an assignment-class taxonomy, and sometimes the taxonomy itself holds no stable class for the relation. The facts may be operationally clear while the moral classification remains boundary-active. This is not ignorance; it is unresolved category-space, and the AI interface is the canonical case: external-resolvable components well measured, the kind of relation unsettled.

In every mode the consequence is the same: no P6 closure validly fires, posture is the ethical instrument that remains, and which mode is operating determines what would re-license closure (instrumentation, waiting, gluing protocol, symmetrization, or category stabilization respectively). That diagnostic difference is why the typology earns its keep: the five modes have five different exits.

A client failing Rayleigh and an institution laundering custody are the same pathology at different scales, which is why the practice's diagnostic apparatus ports directly.

## 12. Laundering, quantified; the trace as enforcement

The quantitative core of the document, promoted from aside to center:

```
laundering = claimed assignment precision − achieved localization resolution
```

The gap is conserved (CR component-wise conservation): it does not vanish, it transfers to the assigned bearer as unheld remainder (Remnant). This makes v0.1's axiom a theorem under the bound of §0 and unifies the corpus's five-plus independent derivations of the anti-pattern (EL §7): moving remainder off the ledger rather than out of the world, in assignment costume.

The discriminator is invertibility, and the **localization trace** is invertibility operationalized: every major claim, duty, ownership assertion, liability judgment, custody transfer, or intervention authorization preserves the localization pathway that justified it, as a durable sign-artifact (SMFR). The trace is thereby promoted from design implication (v0.1 §14.1) to the enforcement mechanism of the ethics layer: it is what makes assignments contestable (§3b), retirable (§7), gluable (§8), and auditable against manufactured occlusion (§9).

## 13. Position in the corpus

The pipeline, restated with the posture-field over it:

```
Boundary Dynamics        how distinction forms and operates (BD)
Relational Localization  resolution in the assignment subspace (this node)
Bearing relations        flow-operator compositions with accumulating answerability (§5, FO)
Flow / Routing           what crosses, how it is signed and routed (FO, SMFR, CR)
Assignment               P6 closures licensed by Q ≥ Q_min(x) (§4)
Stewardship positions    approach → coming between → aftermath (EL triad)
```

with reverence as a field over the whole pipeline (EL), Boundary Ethics as the posture's closure-unlicensed regime at any rung (§10, five modes), and the adversarial overlay (AOMI) across all of it.

**The Q-gated handoffs, load-bearing and new.** The stewardship triad's positions are time-indexed, but the transitions between them are Q-indexed. The Reverent-to-Intervention crossing is a localization judgment: restraint cannot be known to have become abandonment (GDD withholding violation) without localizing the vulnerability, the capacity, and the consequence weight; crossing requires Q ≥ Q_min(intervention authority). The Intervention-to-Remnant handoff requires the remainder itself to be localized: what cannot be distinguished cannot be tended. Time moves the steward along the arc; localization quality licenses the crossings. Below threshold the system defaults to the approach position, where posture is the whole instrument: Boundary Ethics derived from a second direction. This supplies Intervention's open thread 1 with its missing variable.

**The justice modes, derived.** A mode of justice (Justice) is the finest assignment class whose Q_min is satisfiable at the operating scale: Mode 1 where individual attribution resolves, Mode 2 where only systemic attribution does, Mode 3 where assignment is structurally unavailable and possibility preservation is what remains. Mode-mismatch becomes formally diagnosable as demanding a class above the satisfiable threshold, which is laundering by fabricated precision in the upward direction and agency-dissolution in the downward one.

**AI governance, restated (TCL).** Public localization systems (courts, regulation, shared language) are the slow layer; high-consequence AI relations are the fast layer. "Relations mutate faster than public localization can resolve them" is a lamination failure: effective slow-layer coupling below the viability floor (~0.757). The remedies are the lawful RRL moves: slow the fast layer, strengthen slow-layer coupling, expand R_max. And the predicted oscillation between over-control and under-control is the chaos regime (~0.930): strong coupling plus inevitable delay produces incoherent oscillation, not control. Heavy regulation with long feedback loops is predicted to whiplash. This falls out of the constants, not out of political intuition, and is one of the node's testable claims.
