## Working Read

The candidate claim is that Proxy Localization supplies protocol machinery for participatory localization when self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register. Its governing invariant is voice-invertibility: proxy may carry the voice, but may not become the voice; proxy may guide attention, but may not become the target.

Provisional integration type: practice plus specialization.

This is downstream of Relational Localization. It operationalizes the partial-participation branch and should not be treated as an independent root construct.
