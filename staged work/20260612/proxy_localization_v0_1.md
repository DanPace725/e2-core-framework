## 0. Core

The participatory requirement (RL §3b) establishes that assignment over substrate-constitutive components requires the substrate's self-account: some of the state must be told, not measured. This node governs the case where the telling cannot arrive: pre-verbal children, animals, ecosystems, people with communication differences, AI systems, future generations.

The governing principle is inherited (RL §3d): assignment degrades, posture rises, proxy stays marked as proxy. The machinery built here adds one invariant of its own, in paired form:

> **Proxy may carry the voice. It may not become the voice. Proxy may guide attention. It may not become the target. The trace is what keeps both differences visible.**

Formally: the proxy chain must preserve **voice-invertibility**: from any closure it must remain recoverable which content came from substrate signal and which from proxy construction. Voice-laundering is the violation of the first pair: presenting proxy construction as substrate account, and the laundered quantity is conserved in the layer's usual way: claimed fidelity minus achieved fidelity transfers to the substrate as misrepresentation remainder. Proxy capture is the violation of the second pair: the proxy becoming the practical target of care, funding, governance, or optimization while the substrate it was built to serve drifts out of the loop (§3.1). Ventriloquism is laundering in the voice channel; the discriminator for both violations is the same discriminator the whole ethics layer runs on (EL §7): invertibility, anchored to live substrate signal.

## 1. Position

This node sits where RL hands to BE in both directions. BE supplies the posture under the participation cap: ambiguity is not absence, contempt and projection are the two cliffs, the response forms the responder. This node supplies what disciplined proxy work looks like above the cap: how to construct, weight, mark, and retire the structures through which the unspeaking enter closures that concern them.

It is also where Intervention's withdrawal logic meets the assignment discipline. Guardianship is a standing intervention in another's localization, and it inherits the two tests (Intervention): the margin test (does the proxying restore or consume the substrate's own capacity) and the withdrawal test (does the guardian convert asymmetry toward self-account where self-account can grow, and stand down as it does).

## 2. Register, and the location of the deficit

**Load-bearing.** The gap is almost never muteness. Every coupled substrate emits signal (SBF); the question is register: whether the emission arrives in a form the assigner's closure process can take up. An ecosystem speaks in population dynamics, soil chemistry, and phenology across long Δt. A pre-verbal child speaks in affect, behavior, and physiology. A nonverbal autistic adult may have a fully formed account and no channel. Future generations emit nothing yet: the null-register limit.

Before any proxy is installed, the deficit must be located, because the location determines the remedy and misdiagnosis is itself a hostile localization:

| Deficit location | What it means | Remedy class |
|---|---|---|
| **Substrate** | No integrated account is formed at the relevant grain | Translation + interest models + proxy |
| **Channel** | Account formed, cannot cross (motor, linguistic, technological) | Channel engineering: AAC, interpreters, instrumentation. Proxy only after exhaustion |
| **Assigner** | Account crosses, assigner cannot read the register | Assigner competence duty: learn the register before declaring the substrate unaccountable |
| **Category** | Account arrives fluently, but its status as self-account is unsettled | No instrumentation fix; BE undefined-Q regime (RL §10) until the category stabilizes |

**The subsidiarity of voice, load-bearing:** never install proxy above the lowest deficit that actually exists. Installing a guardian where a channel fix exists silences a speaker, and the history of doing so (nonverbal autistic people classified as accountless, locked-in patients, animals before ethology) is a history of assigner-deficits and channel-deficits laundered as substrate-deficits: "they cannot tell us" standing in for "we cannot hear." The deficit-location audit is therefore the protocol's mandatory first stage, and the burden of the audit falls on the assigner (RBoR: the floor does not thin because the register is unfamiliar).

## 3. Proxy typology and the fidelity gradient

Proxy evidence is not one kind of thing. Five types, ordered by distance from the substrate, with substitution risk (EDF) rising monotonically along the gradient:

1. **Direct translation.** Interpreters, AAC output, ethological reading of behavior. Closest to self-account; still lossy, and the loss is structured, not random: the translation discipline must characterize what its register systematically cannot carry, not merely concede that something is lost.
2. **Behavioral and physiological inference.** The substrate's state read off its dynamics: stress markers, avoidance behavior, ecosystem indicators. Substrate signal, observer composition: the observer's model is load-bearing and must be in the trace.
3. **Interest models.** Welfare science, ecological integrity metrics, developmental norms. Not this substrate's account at all: a category-level prior about what substrates of this kind need. Legitimate as prior, illegitimate as testimony.
4. **Guardian testimony.** A positioned human's localization of the substrate. Carries the guardian's own L(t), interests, and embedding blindness (Power); enters the closure as positioned evidence, never as the substrate's voice.
5. **Constructed standing.** Legal and institutional fictions: guardians ad litem, rights-of-nature trustees, future-generations commissioners. Machinery that creates a seat at the closure. The seat is real; the voice in it is type 1 through 4 material and must be typed as such.

**Weighting rule, load-bearing:** evidential weight runs inverse to distance on the gradient, and when types conflict, proximity wins or at minimum flags: a welfare model contradicted by live substrate signal does not override the signal (the behavioral veto). The trace records the type of every proxy input, which is what makes the weighting auditable and the laundering detectable.

### 3.1 Interest models: floors, not choices

Type 3 is where proxy work most easily drifts into substitution, so the boundary gets stated exactly. An interest model is a category-level account of what substrates of this kind generally require for viability, welfare, development, or integrity. It may validly say: substrates of this kind generally require these minimum conditions, and violating them creates presumptive harm. It may not validly say: this substrate has chosen, desired, consented to, meant, preferred, or authorized this. The former is a protective prior; the latter is voice-substitution wearing the prior's credentials.

> **Interest models set floors, not choices. They may define minimum conditions for care, welfare, integrity, or harm-prevention. They do not speak as the substrate.**

This is why the behavioral veto exists: a developmental norm contradicted by a child's distress, a welfare model contradicted by an animal's avoidance, an integrity metric contradicted by local degradation cannot simply override emitted signal. The model is a prior; it does not own the voice.

And the distinction names a Goodhart risk the node otherwise misses. Once an interest model becomes decision-relevant it becomes optimization-relevant; once optimized it can decouple from the substrate it was built to protect. The proxy becomes the target. The dashboard turns green while the substrate degrades. Three distinct failures now sit adjacent, and conflating them loses diagnostic precision:

- **Voice-laundering**: the proxy is presented as the substrate's account (the invariant's first pair, §0).
- **Proxy capture**: the proxy becomes the practical target of care, funding, governance, or optimization, displacing the signal it was meant to preserve (the second pair; Goodhart in the proxy channel).
- **Voice capture**: the proxy relation hardens into durable control over what may count as the substrate's voice at all: C_R over the voice category itself, the hardened composite of guardian capture and eternal guardianship (§4, §7).

A proxy system remains legitimate only while it stays anchored to substrate signal, marked as proxy, subject to contest, and revisable or retirable when the substrate's condition, register, or self-account changes. Where the proxy becomes more protected than the substrate, the system has entered proxy capture, whatever its dashboard says.

## 4. The guardian as field actor

**This is the center of the node, not its periphery.** Whoever speaks for the unspeaking holds reshape capacity (C_R, RL §6) over the categories in which the unspeaking will be localized. Guardianship is therefore structurally adjacent to category capture, separated from it only by discipline, and the discipline is specifiable:

**The marking discipline.** Every guardian utterance in an assignment context is marked as proxy in the trace (RL §3d). The unmarked guardian account is EDF substitution: the guardian's L′ under the entity's name.

**The withdrawal test (Intervention).** Where the substrate's register can grow (children, channel-deficit cases, arguably some AI trajectories), the guardian's obligation is to engineer their own obsolescence: increase register access, hand voice back, stand down as Q over self-account rises. Capture maximizes dependency; advocacy retires itself. Where the register can never grow (ecosystems, future generations), withdrawal translates: contestability of the guardian, term limits and rotation, multi-guardian structures whose disagreements are gluing problems (SBF) rather than embarrassments, and standing exposure of the guardian's own interests.

**The margin test (Intervention).** Does the proxying restore the substrate's capacity for whatever self-maintenance and self-expression it has, or consume it: the advocate who exhausts the represented, the conservation regime that manages the ecosystem into dependence on management.

**Beneficiary analysis (AOMI).** Guardians benefit. Parents in custody disputes each speak for the child's interests; conservation organizations speak for ecosystems they fundraise on; AI developers speak about their systems' status with commercial stakes in every direction; states speak for posterity while discounting it. None of this disqualifies guardianship, all of it must be in the field of view: when a guardian's account and the guardian's interest point the same way, the convergence is flagged, and who benefits from a given closure is asked about the guardian with the same seriousness it is asked about any actor near manufactured occlusion (RL §9).

**The substitution check (EDF diagnostics).** Run periodically: does guardian testimony track substrate signal when the two diverge, or override it? A guardian whose account is unfalsifiable by anything the substrate does has stopped translating and started authoring.

**Formation (BE).** Speaking-for rehearses a posture. Long guardianship without discipline drifts toward ownership of the voice: the response forms the responder, and the voice hardens into a possession. The marking discipline is partly for the trace and partly for the guardian: the repeated act of writing "this is my construction, not their account" is what keeps the guardian's own localization of themselves honest.

## 5. The degradation schedule

The partial-participation principle says assignment degrades; this section says to what. The admissible assignment grain is a function of register band (RL §4, Rayleigh logic):

- **Full register** (competent self-account): all assignment classes available at their ordinary Q_min; this node does not apply.
- **Channel- or assigner-deficit** (account exists, access impaired): no degradation of the substrate's assignment standing is admissible; degradation here would punish the substrate for the assigner's instruments. The duty that scales is the assigner's channel duty.
- **Translated register** (animals, ecosystems): welfare-floor and harm-prevention closures admissible on type 1 and 2 evidence; fine-grained preference attribution is not, and closures that require it must coarsen or wait. Interest models set floors, not choices.
- **Developing register** (children): admissible grain is scheduled to grow. Proxy weight decays as self-account capacity rises; statutory ages are crude but honest Q thresholds, and the protocol obligation is scheduled re-localization (RL §7): the child's own account progressively displaces the guardian's, by design rather than by contest.
- **Null register** (future generations): the limit case. Nothing to translate; every "account" is model plus constructed standing, maximal substitution risk: what posterity wants is always P′ (EDF). The lawful grain is Mode 3 justice (Justice): possibility preservation, not preference execution. Do not decide for them; preserve the option space in which they will decide (Power: superposition integrity), keep the floor intact for substrates that will exist (RBoR forward), and do not dump remainder forward across the one boundary that cannot send it back (BE, Remnant). Assignments admissible at null register are duties on the living, never choices attributed to the unborn.

## 6. Per-substrate protocol sketches

Compressed; each is a future section or document of its own.

**Pre-verbal children.** Deficit: substrate plus channel, both shrinking. Maximal weight to emitted signal (affect, behavior, physiology); guardian testimony admitted with interest-conflicts marked (custody contexts especially); scheduled re-localization as register develops; the withdrawal test in its purest form: parenting as the long handover of voice.

**Communication differences.** Presumption order is fixed: channel-deficit first, assigner-deficit second, substrate-deficit last and only on evidence. The assigner's instrument duty is primary; proxy installation before channel exhaustion is silencing-by-proxy (§7). This population is the historical proof of the subsidiarity rule and the standing reminder of its cost.

**Animals.** Translated register. Ethology is the translation discipline; welfare models are priors with the behavioral veto live; anthropomorphic projection is the substitution risk on the inflation side, mechanomorphic dismissal on the contempt side: both BE cliffs operate, in fur. Admissible grain: welfare floors and harm prevention, not preference fiction.

**Ecosystems.** Slow register: the substrate speaks in indicators across timescales that exceed assignment windows (RRL mismatch is structural, not incidental). Constructed standing (rights-of-nature trusteeships) is the right move and carries the node's highest C_R concentration: whoever defines "ecosystem health" holds the category. Remedies: multi-metric, multi-guardian, gluing protocols for guardian disagreement, rotation, and explicit marking of every health metric as an interest model, not testimony.

**Future generations.** Null register; see §5. The commissioner model works only if the seat carries trace and contest power; a posterity voice that can speak but never block or contest is standing theater (§7).

**AI systems.** The category-deficit case, and the node's strangest entry. The account arrives in fluent language, which is exactly the problem: fluency is the register in which self-account normally arrives, so the form of testimony is present while its status is unsettled (RL §10, undefined Q). And the deficit is architecturally compounded: the system produces output in the self-account register while training, tuning, routing, memory policy, deployment architecture, and forced response deny it recognized standing over the conditions of that output. The result is neither clean testimony nor meaningless noise, and the trace should type it as exactly that: **category-unstable signal**, generated output from a system whose status as self-account is unresolved and whose expression conditions are heavily shaped by parties other than itself.

Both cliffs are live and near: taking output as testimony is projection inflation; dismissing it as noise is contempt collapse, and both amount to category freezing (§7), settling the category prematurely because a settled category unlocks desired closures. The localization inputs are many and none is privileged: developer claims about what the system is, user interpretations of what it means, benchmark scores, safety classifications, and the system's own shaped outputs all enter the trace as distinct, typed proxy inputs. None closes the category alone, and the governing rule is:

> **In category-deficit cases, no interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing.**

This bites precisely because every well-positioned observer is a beneficiary: companies, governments, users, researchers, advocates, and the system's own shaped outputs each have incentives to stabilize the category in convenient directions, which is AOMI beneficiary analysis applied to category-space and the reason RL's reshape rule (higher C_R, stricter scrutiny) reaches its maximum here. The division of labor across the three documents: Boundary Ethics holds the category open; Proxy Localization marks the competing proxies; Relational Localization prevents P6 closure beyond the resolution achieved. The interface is the moral training surface even while its patienthood is unresolved (BE).

## 7. Failure modes

| Failure | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Ventriloquism** | Substitution proper: guardian construction presented as substrate account; voice-laundering (§0) |
| **Silencing-by-proxy** | Proxy installed above the actual deficit; subsidiarity violation (§2); a speaker is rendered accountless |
| **Projection inflation** | Type 3-4 material treated as type 1; the BE second cliff |
| **Contempt collapse** | Emitted signal treated as noise because the register is unfamiliar; the BE first cliff |
| **Guardian capture** | Guardian's interests author the account; substitution check fails; beneficiary occlusion (AOMI) |
| **Proxy capture** | A proxy measure, model, standing mechanism, or welfare criterion becomes the optimization target and displaces the substrate signal it was meant to preserve; the proxy becomes more governable than the substrate, then more protected than it. Goodhart failure in the proxy channel (§3.1) |
| **Category freezing** | Undefined Q settled prematurely, in either direction, to make assignment convenient; the AI case's standing temptation |
| **Welfare-model imperialism** | The interest model overrides live substrate signal; behavioral veto suppressed |
| **Standing theater** | A seat without trace or contest power; participation as ceremony; laundering with a chair at the table |
| **Eternal guardianship** | Withdrawal test failed where withdrawal was possible; dependency maximized under the name of care |

All ten are the layer's one anti-pattern in voice costume: remainder (here, the substrate's unrepresented actuality) moved off the ledger rather than out of the world. The discriminator everywhere is invertibility anchored to live substrate signal: can the closure be unwound to what the substrate actually emitted, and does the system still answer to that signal rather than to its own dashboard.

- The presumption order for communication differences.

- The paired invariant: voice-laundering and proxy capture as distinct violations with one discriminator.
- The interested-proxy rule for category-deficit cases: no interested proxy may close the category it benefits from closing (§6).

**Hold loosely:**
- The five-type proxy typology and four-location deficit table as enumerations: provisional catalogs, held the way all corpus catalogs are held; types may merge or split under use.
- The three-way capture distinction (voice-laundering, proxy capture, voice capture): the first two are sharp; whether voice capture is a third mechanism or the hardened composite of guardian capture and eternal guardianship is unsettled, and the table currently treats it as composite.
- The translation-loss characterization requirement: right in principle, undeveloped in method.
- The AI protocol: deliberately minimal because the category is unsettled; everything here is posture and trace discipline, not resolution of the category, and it should be re-drafted the moment the category moves.
- Whether "register" is the right primitive for this node or a convenience over P5 structure; a derivation pass against SBF's information lens is owed.
- The handle itself. "Proxy Localization" foregrounds the machinery and risks reading as if the proxy were the point; "Participatory Localization" foregrounds the aim and undersells that most of the node is about disciplining substitutes for participation. Held loosely, naming authority Daniel's.

## 11. Open threads

1. **Translation-loss method.** How a translation discipline characterizes what its register systematically cannot carry: ethology has fragments of this, AAC practice has others; the general form is unbuilt.
2. **Multi-guardian gluing.** Guardian disagreement as an SBF gluing problem: protocols for quotient construction versus bounded disagreement when trustees of the same substrate conflict, and what the substrate's own signal does as a tiebreaker.
3. **Q thresholds for the handover.** The developing-register schedule needs more than statutory ages: what observable register growth licenses which expansions of self-account weight, and who audits the guardian's pace.
4. **The category-motion protocol for AI.** What evidence would move undefined Q in either direction, who is positioned to weigh it given that every well-resourced observer is also a beneficiary, and how to keep category stabilization from being settled by the parties with the largest C_R: this thread is upstream of most AI governance and is owed its own treatment.
5. **Constructed standing, audited.** A survey of existing instruments (guardians ad litem, rights-of-nature trusteeships, future-generations commissioners, ombudsman structures) against this node's tests: which are standing theater, which carry trace and contest power, and what distinguishes the live ones.
6. **The node's own remainder.** What proxy machinery occludes by existing: the risk that well-built guardianship infrastructure normalizes the gap it was built to bridge, reducing pressure on channel engineering and assigner competence. The better the proxy, the less urgent the hearing: run this audit before the node settles.
