## Working Read

The candidate claim is that Boundary Ethics governs conduct at relational thresholds where ontology, agency, vulnerability, reciprocity, moral status, or category-location are ambiguous, asymmetric, disputed, unstable, or only partially knowable. Its central question is what posture is appropriate while the relation is not yet sufficiently localized for valid assignment.

Provisional integration type: construct plus practice.

Boundary Ethics is not a fourth stewardship position. It is orthogonal to the temporal triad and indexes posture by localization quality: low-Q, unstable-Q, contested-Q, asymmetric-Q, undefined-Q, or closure-unlicensed relation.
