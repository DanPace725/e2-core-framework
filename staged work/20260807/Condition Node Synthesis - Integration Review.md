## Working Read

The load-bearing candidate claim is that a condition is not an intrinsic kind of thing. It is a stabilized feature of relational terrain typed, from a declared observer position, as bearing on a named outcome.

Provisional integration type: specialization and bridge with an applied-practice component.

The document combines three layers that should be kept distinct:

- a causal specialization: stabilized consequence functioning as terrain;
- an epistemic/assignment discipline: referent, register, observer, resolution, and freshness must be declared;
- an applied Condition Map interpretation: notice, name, mark, and tend conditions over time.

It does not add a seventh primitive. Its strongest contribution is the indexing and freshness discipline applied to an already active causal role.

### Relational Localization

Primary connection:

- `E2Core/Semantic Substrate/Relational Localization - Core Source.md`

Integration implication:

A condition assertion should record at least terrain or evidence, referent outcome, register, observer/frame, resolution, timestamp, and re-localization trigger. ## Source-Status And Evidence Boundary

The CCC triad, condition inventory, conditional-effect analysis, and field-kit appendix do not exist as independent source artifacts inside the Core Framework or broader Phase 1 tree. Their provenance and recoverable content are now recorded in `staged work/20260807/Condition Materials - Context Capture.md`.

That capture resolves the references as context, not authority. Daniel has confirmed that these materials are not particularly load-bearing for the broader framework and that the Condition synthesis already absorbed what mattered. They should therefore support lineage and interpretation without becoming required parents for the candidate bridge.

Current etymological references support English `condition` primarily through Latin `condicio` / `condicere`, meaning agreement or stipulation. Late-Latin confusion with the separate `conditio` / `condere`, meaning making or founding, is documented. That history supports a marked interpretive parallel, but it does not establish that the modern concept has two jointly authoritative roots or independently prove "settlement become terrain."

The etymology can illuminate the candidate. It cannot promote the causal typing or serve as mechanism.

## Collision And Risk Review

- Stale-status risk: the causal four-role grammar is already load-bearing in the active Complex Causality source.
- Ontological-floor collision: a terminal substrate floor conflicts with the later open-domain audit.
- Primitive-typing risk: stabilized terrain plausibly involves P2, P3, and P4; describing it simply as a P3 object may be too narrow. The P5/P6 separation is explicitly unresolved.
- Observer-independence risk: an underlying configuration may persist unobserved, but calling it terrain and selecting its stable scale already involves modeling choices.
- Drawer-collapse risk: if any relevant state can be typed as a condition, the node needs a strict minimum trace rather than only a conceptual definition.
- Register collision: possibility, probability, sustainability, ethical permissibility, and epistemic licensing are not obviously one homogeneous list.
- Binary-origin risk: "did somebody decide this?" is a useful coaching probe but not a general discriminator. Emergent conventions have no single decider, and decided constraints may become practically irreversible.
- CCC flattening risk: treating containers and constraints as merely far-promoted conditions can erase their distinct topology and constraint operation.
- Etymological-authority risk: the dual-root interpretation is a parallel, not promotion-grade evidence.
- Applied-layer scope risk: product UI, acquisition, retention, and coaching claims require their own practitioner/application artifact and should not become source-level ontology.
- Provenance-boundary risk: the CCC and inventory materials now have a context capture but no independent source record; claims unique to them remain non-load-bearing.

## Proposed Integration Method

Do not promote the current synthesis unchanged as a standalone core node.

Create a compact candidate bridge that:

1. Starts from the already-canonical Complex Causality role: condition as stabilized consequence functioning as terrain.
2. Defines conditionality as observer-indexed typing of that terrain against a named outcome.
3. Specifies a minimum condition trace: referent, register, position, resolution, evidence, timestamp, and re-localization trigger.
4. Treats freshness and stale condition labels as a Relational Localization specialization.
5. States promotion and un-promotion through AMM variables and keeps quantitative thresholds open.
6. Keeps the etymological seam in an explicitly interpretive section.
7. Replaces the terminal substrate floor with observational and practical stopping rules.
8. Moves CCC, UI, coaching, and Condition Map claims into a separate applied-practice note.
