## 1. CCC triad (July 8 session)

The triad: **containers** as legal and structural boundary holders, **constraints** as shaping rules operating within them, **conditions** as the relational and environmental field state that determines what actually emerges. Framing claim: effective business design is less about engineering a machine and more about creating an environment where outcomes become natural attractors.

The structural wrinkle, raised same session: the three are not cleanly parallel categories. Containers contain constraints; conditions arguably encompass both. Resolution reached: the triad is not three kinds of thing but **resolution slices of one organizational manifold**, discriminated by rate of change and temporal grain. A condition that stabilizes becomes a constraint; a constraint surface that closes becomes a container. Independent convergence noted with Stewart Brand's pace layering.

Open remainder flagged at close: why three slices rather than more or fewer. Working answer: three is the pragmatic minimum for diagnostic work, not a theoretical necessity.

Superseded reading (July 29): the triad is **three positions on one promotion continuum**. Containers and constraints are conditions that promoted so far they hardened into objects; an LLC is terms spoken together and filed. Also flagged there, held loosely: container may not be genuinely distinct from constraint; a container may be a constraint set that closed into a loop and acquired an inside, which would make the triad two positions plus a topological property.

## 2. Conditional-effect analysis (GPT-side)

The four registers a condition can bear on an outcome through:

- **Possibility**: can the outcome occur at all
- **Probability**: how likely is it
- **Permissibility**: is it licensed or defensible
- **Sustainability**: can it continue without degrading

This list powers the mandatory-indexing discipline in the node synthesis: a state does not receive the name "condition" until indexed to a referent and a register. "Trust is low" is a fragment; "trust between the two partners, bearing on whether the referral loop is sustainable" is a condition.

Known asymmetry, open thread: possibility, probability, and sustainability are modal properties of the outcome; permissibility is a property of an observer's relation to it. One list or two, unworked; the asymmetry may be correct given P5's observer-indexing.

Lawfulness was placed relative to this list: not a claim in the probability register but the licensing condition on that register, a second-order condition in the permissibility family.

## 3. Condition inventory (GPT-side, "the inventory session")

Two products:

- **A functional typology of roughly twenty condition types** (known members: preconditions, sustaining, protective, licensing, invalidating, threshold; licensing was "type twelve," implying a numbered list). Status per the synthesis: a useful diagnostic family, not a derived one; instinct says it compresses to five or six with the rest as subtypes; unworked.
- **Domain inventories**: catalogs of where conditions tend to be found across domains. Status per the synthesis: a search index, not a taxonomy, because every entry is written in unindexed intrinsic form (no referent, no register).

Also attributed to inventory work: **condition freshness** (temporal inventory) and **ongoing defensibility** (defensibility inventory), which the synthesis identifies as the same object as stale assignment (RL) and stale setpoint (chemotaxis): four vocabularies, one invertibility failure.
