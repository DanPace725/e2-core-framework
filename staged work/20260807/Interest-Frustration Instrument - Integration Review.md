## Collision And Risk Review

- Intra-batch conflict: section 3's "validates or destroys" framing is explicitly retracted by the later synthesis and must be revised before promotion.
- Construct validity: hysteresis and cross-domain degradation may measure ordinary state carryover, resource contention, or optimizer dynamics rather than borne cost.
- Anthropomorphic calibration risk: the biological predictions are asserted before a measurement procedure capable of observing the proposed signatures is specified.
- Feasibility risk: planaria, Pando, mycelia, colonies, tardigrades, and neural models require incompatible instruments and levels of analysis.
- Ethics risk: intentional thwarting in biological cases requires domain-specific welfare review; "beneficiary-free" does not mean ethically cost-free.
- White-box access risk: internal features are observer-constructed evidence and may be causally manipulable without mapping cleanly to the named state.
- Reflexivity/GDD risk: publication can make signatures optimization targets.
- Scope risk: structural cost may be relevant to welfare without being sufficient for moral status.

## Proposed Integration Method

Hold as a versioned research protocol rather than a core construct.

Required v0.2 changes:

- replace binary self-report validation with register-resolution characterization;
- define settlement-debt, internal bearing, locus, discharge, and cross-domain propagation operationally;
- separate shared conceptual signatures from substrate-specific measurement procedures;
- add alternative explanations and discriminating controls for each signature;
- pre-register rebuild conditions, not only predicted readings;
- add domain-specific ethics and feasibility gates;
- preserve negative and null results in machine-readable form;
- state that the AI phase waits on interpretability capability and independent oversight.
