## External Evidence Boundary

Current Anthropic material supports non-verbalized evaluation-awareness measurements in specified evaluation contexts and reports 16%, 26%, and below-1% comparisons for selected datasets. Those are model-, method-, and context-specific measurements.

They support a functional situation-modeling research program. They do not by themselves establish a general awareness topology, welfare-bearing state, or the direction of translation loss across all report types. Every numerical and causal claim needs its specific source, model, intervention, and limitation attached.

## Collision And Risk Review

- Synthesis-of-syntheses risk: the document joins self-report, awareness, extension, welfare, substrate, REAL, and mind-lawfulness before the parents have equal source status.
- Frame-dependence risk: "presence" follows partly from selecting REMA's definitions and cannot be reported as frame-independent evidence.
- Asymmetry risk: warnings against one-sided doubt must not become one-sided credence.
- Human-analogy risk: confabulation, trauma, alexithymia, animal welfare, and tool incorporation are analogies with different mechanisms and evidence standards.
- Remaining missing-parent risk: the CRS primary-object reference still needs mapping; Self as Coherence Field is now Core-authoritative.
- Scalar-instrument risk: the proposed six-primitive profile correctly rejects a single score, but "jointly non-degenerate" lacks operational thresholds.
- Intra-batch dependency: the document corrects the Interest-Frustration Instrument and depends on the Substance/Substrate audit; it cannot be reviewed as earlier than either.
