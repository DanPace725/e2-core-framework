## Working Read

The feed capture contains a candidate construct, but its strongest contribution is not a complete definition of trust. It is a discrimination program separating:

- evidence about another party's trustworthiness;
- willingness to accept vulnerability under uncertainty;
- the reliance or coupling act that exposes something to that uncertainty;
- custody obligations governing what is held across monitoring gaps;
- proxy confidence formed from agreement rather than diagnostic interaction.

Provisional integration type: construct / bridge / practice.

The load-bearing candidate is that trust becomes most diagnostically visible where vulnerability and interests can diverge. Behavior under mild divergence can supply better evidence than shared identity or agreement. That should not be expanded into the claim that trust exists only in adversarial or divergent regions.

## External Construct Collision

Established organizational-trust work distinguishes perceived trustworthiness from trust and risk-taking behavior. Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman treat ability, benevolence, and integrity as antecedents of trust and distinguish trust from the resulting risk-taking. Rousseau, Sitkin, Burt, and Camerer synthesize trust around willingness to accept vulnerability based on positive expectations. The Berg, Dickhaut, and McCabe investment experiment separately measures trusting transfer and reciprocal response under controlled risk.

Primary anchors:

- [Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman, “An Integrative Model of Organizational Trust”](https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/amr.1995.9508080335)
- [Rousseau, Sitkin, Burt, and Camerer, “Not So Different After All”](https://doi.org/10.5465/AMR.1998.926617)
- [Berg, Dickhaut, and McCabe, “Trust, Reciprocity, and Social History”](https://iro.uiowa.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Trust-reciprocity-and-social-history/9984962889802771)

Integration implication:

The feed capture currently calls trust the P5 assessment and reliance the P4 coupling act. That split is directionally useful but compresses three objects into two. A cleaner candidate architecture is:

1. **Trustworthiness estimate:** observer-indexed assessment of relevant ability, intention, integrity, and prior behavior.
2. **Trust:** willingness or intention to accept specified vulnerability under uncertainty, informed but not determined by the estimate.
3. **Reliance:** the actual coupling, dependency, transfer, or exposure; it can occur with low trust because of coercion, necessity, monopoly, or lack of alternatives.
4. **Custody:** answerable holding across a gap, owned operationally by `Custody - Core Source.md` rather than redefined here.

This four-part split should be tested rather than treated as settled vocabulary.

## Collision And Risk Review

- **Definition narrowing:** divergence-region behavior is diagnostic evidence, not the whole of trust. Competence, benevolence, integrity, vulnerability, risk, and institutional guarantees may matter differently by domain.
- **Trust/trustworthiness collapse:** an assessment of another is not identical to willingness to become vulnerable.
- **Trust/reliance collapse:** reliance may be coerced or structurally unavoidable and therefore cannot be read as evidence of trust.
- **Custody inflation:** not every trusted interaction involves a discrete entrusted object; custody is a specialization, not a synonym.
- **Proxy dismissal:** shared identity may sometimes carry relevant historical information. The failure is unsupported transfer across domains, not the use of all proxies.
- **Testing ethics:** `small invertible experiments` can become manipulative loyalty tests. Consent, proportionality, mutual exposure, and repair must constrain them.
- **Counterfactual observability:** behavior under serious divergence cannot always be sampled safely. Claims must preserve uncertainty rather than manufacture a test.
- **Reputation feedback:** a trustworthiness assignment changes opportunity, monitoring, and future evidence; it is not inert terrain.
- **Phenomenology overreach:** permission to rest vigilance is a plausible signature, not a universal measure or proof of achieved custody.
- **Etymological authority:** `trust`, `troth`, `truce`, `entrust`, `betray`, and related roots may supply interpretive structure only after direct historical-linguistic verification. Word history cannot establish the construct.

## Proposed Integration Method

Do not promote the feed capture unchanged.

Create a candidate source organized around:

1. trustworthiness estimate, trust, and reliance as separate Trust-side objects, with an explicit handoff to the separate Custody source;
2. a typed trust trace identifying parties, domain, accepted vulnerability, evidence window, divergence class, reliance state, custody interface where applicable, and re-localization trigger;
3. divergence-region evidence as a high-value but non-exclusive diagnostic;
4. proxy evidence with explicit domain-transfer limits;
5. reputation and stale-assignment dynamics;
6. ethical constraints on trust experiments;
7. an interpretive etymology appendix, not an etymological derivation.

The candidate should include negative cases:

- high reliance with low trust;
- high perceived trustworthiness without accepted vulnerability;
- successful custody produced by institutional controls rather than interpersonal confidence;
- affinity without domain-relevant trustworthiness evidence;
- one domain of trustworthy behavior failing to transfer to another.
