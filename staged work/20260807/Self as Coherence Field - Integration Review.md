The load-bearing claim is that selfhood can be modeled as a metastable composite configuration of the six relational primitives, with recognizable partial projections appearing when the joint configuration contracts under load. The source does not require a new primitive. Its strongest practical distinction is between self as a common causal denominator and self as a common interface through which differently sourced loads are routed.

Provisional integration type: construct / bridge / interpretive parallel.

## Collision And Risk Review

- **Primitive inflation:** resolved by admitting the source explicitly as a six-primitive composite.
- **Metaphor/mechanism collision:** “field,” “metabolic budget,” “signal,” and “dataset” are useful structural metaphors here; they do not establish physical mechanisms without independent evidence.
- **Empirical overreach:** claims of cross-tradition convergence and characteristic suffering are interpretive parallels, not empirical validation of the ontology.
- **Cultural compression:** “Western” and “collectivist” passages are diagnostic ideal types, not exhaustive descriptions of cultures or persons.
- **Observer closure:** the source's “original signal” language must remain bounded by MPDC and resolution; no complete observer-independent self-state is made available.
- **Instrument risk:** the six-axis description is a profile, not a clinical scale, diagnostic instrument, or single score.
- **Mind-lawfulness overreach:** the source can parent later work, but does not itself establish consciousness, phenomenal awareness, welfare-bearing cost, or moral standing in humans, animals, or artificial systems.
