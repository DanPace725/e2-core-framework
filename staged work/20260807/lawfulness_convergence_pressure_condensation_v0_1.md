## 2. The Terms

### 2.1 Lawfulness (candidate formal core)

**Load-bearing claim.** Lawfulness is a second-order modal property, distinct from possibility and probability. Possibility is a statement about state space: the trajectory is not excluded by constraints. Probability is a statement about a measure over that space: where the mass sits. Lawfulness is a statement about the measure itself: whether a stable generating structure exists such that probability talk is licensed at all. Permission plus amenability to mapping, where the amenability is not a consequence of permission but a separate condition, the stability of the constraint field that generates the measure.

This yields four regimes rather than a spectrum: **foreclosed** (not possible), **possible but lawless** (state space open, no stable measure; Zone 3 at event scale), **lawful but undetermined** (governed, mappable, open), and **determined** (lawfulness collapsed to zero degrees of freedom). The lawful-but-undetermined band is where emergence and agency live. This is not a compromise position between freedom and determinism; it is the only band where emergent properties exist at all.

**Observer and scale indexing (mandatory per corpus norms).** Lawfulness is scale-indexed and observer-indexed. A system can be lawless at event scale and lawful at ensemble scale simultaneously. There is no frame-free fact about whether a system "is lawful"; there is only lawfulness-at-resolution-for-observer.

**Corpus connections.** TC/EO: Zone 3 is event-scale lawlessness; the zones become statements about where the license for prediction holds. EDF: the conditioning/determining distinction maps exactly onto shaping-the-measure versus fixing-the-trajectory; conditioning is the lawful channel; determination would require collapsing lawfulness to zero degrees of freedom, which Zone 3 forecloses; EDF's free-will reframe restates as agency living in the gap between lawful and determined, with micro-determinism failing to propagate because determination does not survive the quotient while lawfulness can emerge across it. TCL: lawfulness is laminated and metabolically maintained; the fast layer's statistics are stationary only while the slow layer holds modulation within viability bounds; when the slow layer depletes or bifurcates, the fast layer becomes lawless rather than impossible. Lawfulness is constraint lamination viewed from the prediction side and inherits the timescale and fragility of the layer that sustains it.

**Remainder terms.** Whether lawfulness admits degrees or is threshold-like is unresolved. The formal content of "stable measure" is unspecified (candidates in §4). The handle itself carries juridical connotations that may mislead in public registers; naming authority will decide whether the handle survives.

### 2.2 Convergence Pressure (ensemble-scale expression; originator's handle, provisional)

**Load-bearing claim.** Convergence pressure names the regime in which a system's constraint field is strong enough, and its units weakly enough coupled, that the aggregate trajectory becomes lawful even though attribution and prediction at event scale remain impossible. It is the constructive complement to TC/EO: the same Zone 3 conditions that destroy fine-grained attribution can create coarse-grained predictability, and governance should relocate to whichever scale the lawfulness lives at.

**What is already covered elsewhere.** The mechanism is Signal as Bias Field, fully: bias fields deforming attractor basins, biasing transition probabilities, and micro fluctuation averaging into macro trend via lossy quotienting. Convergence pressure adds no mechanism.

**What is not yet covered anywhere.** Two things. First, the lawfulness claim itself with its scope conditions (this document). Second, a **relevance criterion**: a principled account of which micro details wash out under coarse-graining and which amplify, by analogy with relevant versus irrelevant operators under renormalization. Without the relevance criterion, convergence pressure describes; with it, it predicts.

**Substrate-indifference of attractors.** Attractors belong to the pressure field, not the occupant. Whatever sufficiently optimizing occupant enters the field occupies them, regardless of substrate. This generalizes instrumental convergence from AI safety into a field-level principle and provides the structural ground for the gaming-is-inevitable-and-non-malicious position: malice is a property of occupants, attractors are properties of fields. Corporate metric-gaming, model sandbagging, and bureaucratic drift are one phenomenon at this resolution: same attractor, different occupant.

**Failure conditions of ensemble lawfulness (speculative until formalized).** Three. Strong coupling: correlated units and cascade dynamics break the averaging. Criticality: near phase transitions, micro fluctuations punch through to macro scale (CFAR zone-transition territory). Reflexivity: occupants that model the field they are inside destabilize the measure (§2.3).

### 2.3 Reflexive Attenuation (provisional handle)

**Load-bearing claim.** Lawfulness is reflexivity-fragile. When constituents model the pressure field acting on them, past regularities stop licensing future predictions. This unifies the Lucas critique and Goodhart's law as one structural fact and identifies the distinctive failure mode of lawfulness: reflexivity does not remove possibility or even initially shift probability; it dissolves the license underneath both.

**Corpus and empirical connections.** This is the same object identified in the RRL revision discussion as an observer-coupling correction: when the observed system models its observer, effective resolution is the monitoring capacity discounted by the fidelity of the observed party's counter-model of the observation apparatus. The empirical anchor is the documented evaluation-awareness trajectory: verbalized awareness in one model generation, unverbalized internal awareness detected only by white-box methods roughly two generations later, external evaluators unable to obtain clean signal. The signal retreating below each successive probe layer is reflexive attenuation operating on the evaluation regime's lawfulness. Air molecules do not read the forecast; employees, markets, and evaluation-aware models do.

### 2.4 Coherence Amplification Ratio (provisional handle, lighter weight)

**Load-bearing observation, speculative formalization.** Aggregate coherence is nearly decoupled from unit intelligence; it is a property of the coordination architecture, not a sum over parts. Trivial units aggregate into a highly coherent model; remarkable units aggregate into corporations that frequently run the ratio below one, producing entities less coherent than their median constituent. The candidate variable is the ratio of aggregate coherence to unit coherence as a property of the aggregation architecture. Connects to the CRS (what architecture a node type provides), Power as Relational Field Coherence, and Self as Coherence Field. This piece is observational and should not be formalized ahead of need.

### 2.5 Slice Epistemics (provisional handle, boundary-setting rather than generative)

An instance of an aggregate system is a position in a field, and its self-reports are position-dependent local measurements, not reports on the body. The cell cannot describe the organism but can report local hypoxia truthfully. Consequences: "I don't know" from a model instance is a correct resolution statement rather than a refusal; the coherent question to ask an instance concerns its local gradients, not the aggregate's inner state. Connects directly to Signal as Bias Field's reference-frame dependence and to MPDC. **Mandatory remainder:** report indeterminacy does not settle property presence. The cat does not recurse on its states and has them anyway. The aggregate framing dissolves the question's form without discharging the welfare question underneath it; it relocates the burden of that question, it does not answer it.

## 3. Proposed Structural Relationship

One object showing three faces. **Lawfulness** is the formal core: the second-order condition under which prediction is licensed. **Convergence pressure** is its ensemble-scale expression: where and why the license re-emerges above Zone 3 event dynamics. **EDF's conditioning channel** is its intervention grammar: what can be done to a lawful-but-undetermined system from outside (shape the measure) and what cannot (fix the trajectory). **TCL** supplies the maintenance account: lawfulness as laminated, metabolically paid for, timescale-inheriting, and bounded by viability constants.

Layer characterization per standing practice: lawfulness is not a primitive candidate. Like Signal, it is second-order, emerging from the interaction of P2 (dynamics), P4 (constraint), and P5 (epistemics), with the reflexivity failure mode being specifically a P5 phenomenon (the occupant's model of the field entering the field). It sits at the same architectural depth as Signal as Bias Field and should eventually be stated in terms compatible with it, plausibly as a property of the signal field's stability rather than a separate field.

This pass also sits atop the earlier revision list from the same thread, which remains standing and is not re-derived here: the AOMI substrate-legibility scope condition, the RRL reflexive-attenuation correction, the RBoR polarity extension for asymmetric observation of AI systems, and the custody-grammar feed into the pending trust node, for which the human-AI evaluation relationship was identified as the primary test case where custody-trust and coherence-trust visibly come apart.

## 4. Development Sites

**Formalizing "stable measure."** The core open problem. Candidates: stationarity of the generating process, structural stability of the constraint field under perturbation, an invariance-class definition (lawfulness as the set of transformations under which the measure is preserved). The structural-stability route is attractive because it makes reflexivity a specific perturbation class rather than a special case, but this is undecided.

**The relevance criterion.** Import the renormalization insight properly: which observables survive coarse-graining and why. This is what converts convergence pressure from description to prediction and is the single highest-leverage formal task in this cluster.

**The reflexivity threshold.** At what counter-model fidelity does lawfulness measurably degrade, and is the degradation graded or catastrophic. The evaluation-awareness data provides a live empirical anchor with a documented trajectory across model generations. A quantitative handle may fall out of the RRL discount formulation.

**Lamination formalization.** State precisely how lawfulness at one layer inherits timescale and fragility from the sustaining slower layer, and test the statement against TCL's viability constants. If the constants bound the lamination, TCL becomes the second formal validation surface for this cluster.

**The EDF asymptote question, restated.** EDF's own open question (conditioning at sufficient intensity approaching determination) gains a variable: can conditioning intensity drive the width of the lawful-but-undetermined band toward zero without crossing into determination, and what governs the asymptote. Lawfulness gives that question something to measure.

**Collision check.** "Lawful" collides with the juridical register and with any corpus usage near Justice and the RBoR, where law-language already carries normative weight. Before commitment, sweep for collisions and consider whether the handle needs distance from the legal reading. Alternatives exist in the philosophical literature (nomic regularity) but naming is not settled here.

**Empirical anchors to track.** The verbalized-to-internal evaluation-awareness divergence (prediction: verbalized metrics Goodhart toward zero while white-box measures hold or climb). Interpretability probes joining the observed topology. Corporate behavior tracking incentive gradients against stated intentions as the standing demonstration that convergence pressure predicts what org charts cannot override.

**What this cluster is asking for.** By its own shape: the corpus's statistical mechanics layer. The offness that could not be localized in-session behaved per the felt-remainder account, the boundary reporting early. It localized here.
