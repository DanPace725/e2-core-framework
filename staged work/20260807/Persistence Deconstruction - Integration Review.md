- Etymology risk: the `*sta-` family can probe structural parallels but cannot ground the mechanics; the document itself acknowledges reconstruction limits.
- Bundle risk: topology, stance-with rate, settlement status, interest-frustration, reliance, reciprocity, and accountability should not be compressed into a replacement mega-construct.
