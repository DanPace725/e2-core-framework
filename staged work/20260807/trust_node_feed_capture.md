## Findings from the July 8 probe

- **Two-root structure.** Trust via Old Norse traust from \*deru- (firm, steadfast; also tree, true). Rely from \*leig- (to bind; ligament, obligation, ally, league, likely religion). The split independently delivers a two-operator distinction the collapsed modern definition obscures: **trust as assessment of a counterparty's firmness (P5 model of structural capacity); reliance as the coupling act that assessment licenses (P4 interface decision).** Load-bearing candidate.
- **Custody grammar.** Entrust as placing-into-holding; betray from tradere, to hand over: custody violation, the surrender of what was held. Custody is richer than coherence because it names divergence-region behavior: holding something of mine when I cannot watch you hold it.
- **Truce** as plural of trew, pledges: regeneration protocol with ancient linguistic precedent.
- **Phenomenal signature.** Germanic consolation cluster (Trost, troost): trust as permission to rest vigilance.
- **Hollowing arc.** The 1877 corporate trust sense: the word's own semantic history enacting the destruction of the commons it named.

## Findings from the August sessions

- **Trust is a counterfactual claim** about divergence-region behavior: how someone acts when their interests diverge from yours. The convergent region needs no trust. Load-bearing candidate.
- **Proxy measurement failure.** Trust is unmeasurable directly, so people read it off cheap proxies (shared religion, politics, taste). But proxies are sampled from the convergent region, so the estimate is a wrong-axis resolution failure: sharp on the visible axis (agreement), blind on the causal axis (behavior under conflict). Explains why in-group betrayal shocks: high felt confidence, zero divergence-region data.
- **Mechanism of proxy vividness** (held loosely): proxy alignment licenses self-simulation ("you run my generating structure, so I predict you by consulting myself"); the fluency of the simulation is experienced as trust.
- **Identity entanglement.** Shared proxies do double duty as evidence about the other and as components of one's own localization. Divergence perturbs the holder's own coordinates; defensiveness is re-localization cost misattributed as attack. Since trust was never measured directly, proxy breakage zeroes the estimate rather than degrading it: the intermediate state was never instrumented.
- **Direct measurement exists but is expensive:** small invertible experiments under mild conflict of interest, iterated, stakes growing with the record. Another arrival of the invertibility invariant and of the unformalized "pick one invertible experiment" principle.
- **Pledge cluster.** The social branch of \*deru- (trust, truth, troth, betroth, truce, tryst) is entirely pledge-words: firmness voluntarily installed into a relational channel by commitment, not intrinsic hardness. Truth's older sense was troth, fidelity; propositional accuracy is a later P5-ward migration. Trust and truth were historically one thing: pledged firmness. Trust-as-divergence-region-commitment is literally troth.
- **Precipitate grammar.** A \*deru- attribution is the P1 precipitate of a sustained \*sta- process, minted when the maintenance history compresses out of view and residual coherence reads as property. Every such attribution carries a silently dropped observation-window index. "Trustworthy" formally: an assertion that divergence-region behavior has been sampled over a sufficient window to be treated as terrain. Legitimate when the settlement history exists. **Proxy trust is counterfeit terrain: the precipitate minted without the process,** which is why it zeroes on breakage; there was never a payment stream to interrupt, only a label. Pathology generally: precipitate without ongoing payment; the label persisting after the process stopped is stale assignment (RL §7) in lexical form. Betrayal's retroactive sting: discovering the terrain had stopped being maintained some unknown time ago.
- **Caution:** the precipitate is not inert. Reputations do causal work once minted, feeding back into what gets maintained; the precipitate is a coherence-field object with its own dynamics. Probable second half of the node.
