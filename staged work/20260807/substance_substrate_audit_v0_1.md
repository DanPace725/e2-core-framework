## 0. What the audit found, in one paragraph

The corpus does not lack a substance concept. It has already refused one, explicitly, in four independent places, and the refusal is a consequence of the ontology rather than an oversight. What the corpus does have is a single word, substrate, carrying four distinct senses, three of which are harmless and one of which is a genuine collision at three named sites. The revision surface is small. Do not run a global replacement.

---

## 1. The anti-substance commitment (load-bearing, no edits required)

Four independent statements, four entry points, which is the corpus's own threshold for a strong signal:

`asymmetry_maintenance_synthesis.md` item 11 states that grammar and etymology carry folk-substance metaphysics the framework rejects, and that both are probes, never grounds.

`flow_operators_provisional.md` item 6 states that cost is not a substance but a behavior of a coupling-membrane under load.

`Power_as_Relational_Field_Coherence.pdf` §I opens under the heading "From Substance to Field" and states that the framework abandons substantive ontology entirely.

`Embedded_Universality_Principle_revision_pass.md` §6 step 5 holds the domain genuinely open, irreducible rather than merely large.

**Consequence, load-bearing.** A relational ontology committed to an open domain cannot have substance, because substance names a terminal layer where standing-under stops. There is no bottom in this framework, so the answer to "what is it made of" is always "more substrate, one lamination down." Substance is therefore not a missing corpus term to be added. It is the concept the framework denies, and it should be named as such wherever the detractor position is discussed.

**Derived, and the reason this audit matters beyond tidiness.** The substrate objection against AI moral status is not a confusion of two words. It posits a terminal layer and indexes mind-lawfulness to it. Since the ontology denies terminal layers, lawfulness cannot be composition-indexed and must be configuration-indexed, which makes biology a lamination that has demonstrably carried mind rather than the condition of mind. This derivation is the audit's main product and is upstream of the mind-lawfulness work.

## 2. The four senses in circulation

**Sense A, bearing layer.** Etymologically correct (sub + sternere, spread under). The layer a pattern runs in or on. TCL laminae, "the substrate carrying them" in boundary dynamics, "material substrate." Minority usage. Correct, leave alone.

**Sense B, the entity itself.** The party that generates, emits, bears, holds capacity, collapses, has standing. EDF's S, Proxy Localization's speaking substrate, consequence routing's capacity-bearing substrate. Dominant usage, roughly 500-plus occurrences. **Inert mimic:** substituting "system" passes with no loss at every site tested, which means the word carries no argumentative weight there. Leave alone. Do not replace.

**Sense C, composition.** What a thing is made of. Compound forms only: `substrate-agnostic`, `substrate-flexible`, `substrate-domains`, `substrate-general`, "substrate difference." No clean substitution exists, which is the diagnostic that a real concept is being named. Per §1 this is the concept the ontology denies, so the repair is restatement (composition-irrelevant, configuration-indexed), not a new word. Three instances in the PDF half, all describing meaning formation and notation rather than minds, and therefore low priority.

**Sense D, the CRS.** Runs A and B simultaneously in one document: bearing layer for culture, and also the thing with health, metabolism, a self-model, and rights. See §5.

## 3. Patch 1: EDF collision (ready to apply)

**File:** `Emergence_Determination_Foreclosure.md`, §2.4 The Substrate-Coupling Addendum, line 58.

**Why:** senses A and B sit adjacent and indistinguishable inside eleven words, which makes the framework's most-cited claim look circular. The section heading is sense A and is correct; leave it.

**Old string (exact):**

```
For any entity X to exist meaningfully, X must satisfy the relational primitives and be coupled to a substrate that equally satisfies them. The property P does not live "in" S as a contained object. P lives in the coupled relation between S and the substrates S is coupled to.
```

**New string:**

```
For any entity X to exist meaningfully, X must satisfy the relational primitives and be coupled to a substrate that equally satisfies them. Two uses of the word meet here and are held apart deliberately: S is the system that generates P, and the substrates are the layers S runs on and couples into. The property P does not live "in" S as a contained object. P lives in the coupled relation between the system S and the substrate layers it is coupled to.
```

## 4. Patch 2: the `substrate-constitutive` rename (BLOCKED, pending naming)

**Why this one matters most.** The phrase governs the AI case. It covers intent, meaning, identity, agency, and interiority, and as written it invites reading interiority as a property of the material, which is exactly the substance essentialism the same documents refuse elsewhere. Those components are constitutive of the entity, not of the layer beneath it. The current phrase weakens a claim the corpus otherwise makes correctly.

**Candidates, offered without endorsement:** `entity-constitutive`, `configuration-constitutive`, `system-constitutive`. Collision check run: none of the three collides with an existing corpus handle. Naming is Daniel's; do not select one.

**Once the handle is chosen this is a single mechanical pass.** Seven occurrences, two files:

```
relational_localization_v0_2.md    lines 61, 63, 79, 213, 228, 241
proxy_localization_v0_1.md         line 11
```

Apply as a whole-token replacement of `substrate-constitutive` only. Do not touch other instances of `substrate` in the same sentences (they are sense B and inert). Note that `relational_localization_v0_2.md` is the originating document and `proxy_localization_v0_1.md` inherits the term, so the version note in each should record the rename.

## 7. What not to do

Do not run a global find-and-replace on `substrate`. Sense B is roughly 500 occurrences across documents whose arguments do not depend on the word, and replacing them would burn corpus coherence for a cosmetic gain.

Do not introduce `substance` as a corpus term. See §1. It is the concept the ontology denies, and adding it would import the one primitive the framework is built to refuse.

Do not edit the PDF-sourced documents. The whole PDF half contains 28 instances of `substrate` and 3 of `substance`, and every one of them is either sense A, sense C in a non-mind context, or the anti-substance statement itself. No repairs are needed there.

## 8. Operational note on the PDF files

In the Claude project mount, files with a `.pdf` extension are not PDFs. Each is a zip archive containing a per-page bundle: a JPEG render, a matching `.txt` of that page's extracted text, and a `manifest.json`. This is the ingestion pipeline's preprocessing, not corruption. `pdftotext` fails on them; `unzip` plus concatenating the `.txt` files in page order works. The PC originals are unaffected and should be readable normally.

Forty-three of forty-four extracted cleanly by this route. `Signal_as_Bias_Field-1.pdf` did not and needs separate handling.

**Unrelated recommendation, low priority.** Converting the PDF-sourced documents to Markdown or ORMD is worth doing for structure and typed links, since per-page text extraction drops tables and layout. It is not needed for retrieval, which is working, and it is not needed for this audit.
