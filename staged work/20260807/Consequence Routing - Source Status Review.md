## Working Read

The load-bearing claim is that Consequence Routing specializes the Global Closure Operator to agentic signal: typed consequence-pressure passes through sign-interfaces toward substrates with typed receiving capacity, leaving remainder wherever routing does not become accountable substrate-work.

Provisional integration type: construct and bridge with metric/property components.

The document supplies the missing structural parent presumed by `consequence_routing_constraint_field_synthesis_v0_1.md`. It is substantially closer to a source document than the later session synthesis, but it remains staged rather than canonical.

Its main proposed contributions are:

- causal, agentic, moral, and resource-flow decomposition of consequence-pressure;
- operator-typed receiving capacity;
- four coupling conditions for valid routing;
- typed interface behavior and selectivity;
- component-wise and operator-wise accounting;
- power as deviation in routing geometry;
- remainder dynamics;
- justice as a system-temporal property rather than a single verdict.

## Collision And Risk Review

- Canonical-status gap: the parent now exists, but only in staged work.
- Duplication risk: significant formal material already appears in active Boundary Dynamics.
- Conservation-form risk: norms are not generally additive. The equations require nonnegative measures over disjoint routed portions, or they should be presented as ledger identities rather than natural conservation laws.
- Semantic-conservation risk: causal, moral, and responsibility "mass" is a formal accounting commitment, not an empirically established conserved physical quantity.
- Operator-conversion risk: interfaces and downstream mechanisms may transform demands; non-interconvertibility must be defined as ledger typing rather than assumed mechanism.
- Baseline collision: routing proportional only to causal proximity conflicts with the document's own agency, capacity, and interface conditions and with Justice Across Scales.
- Observer overreach: MPDC does not entail that true accounts are unavailable; it limits completeness claims by embedded observers.
- Persistence dependency: node persistence `T_i` and P2 persistence language must be revised through the companion persistence deconstruction.
- Threshold overreach: the TCL values are not established as universal across substrate domains.
- Formal incompleteness: the failure table leaves `Overrouting` undefined; section numbering moves from 6.2 back to 5.3.
- Measurement gap: permeability proxies, curvature, moral-pressure norms, and justice factors are domain-specific constructs without calibration.
- Notation collision: sign-interface, signal-field, work, pressure, and remainder symbols need a single typed glossary.
