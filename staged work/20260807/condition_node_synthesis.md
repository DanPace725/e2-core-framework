# Condition

## A working synthesis of the condition node

---

## Status

This document consolidates a multi-session thread that ran through etymology, the CCC diagnostic triad, the condition inventory, and the causality lineage. It is a candidate corpus node. The handle "condition" is provisional and naming authority rests with Daniel. The document's claims are marked load-bearing or held loosely per house discipline, and observer positions are indexed where they matter.

## 1. The Definition

A condition is settled terrain, read under a typing operation, from a position.

Expanded: a condition is a feature of stabilized relational configuration (terrain), typed by an observer as bearing on the possibility, probability, permissibility, or sustainability of some outcome (the typing operation), where the typing is performed from a declared frame (the position).

The three components are separable and the separation is load-bearing:

The terrain is real without any observer. A stabilized deformation in the landscape is P2 and P3 doing ordinary work. Trust between two people, a cash balance, a body's sleep debt: these persist whether or not anyone reads them.

The bearing is not intrinsic. Nothing is a condition on its own. Conditionality is a P6 operation applied to a state under an outcome: relations-on-relations in the strict sense, because typing a relation as consequential for another relation is a claim about what kind of relation a relation is.

The position is P5. Different observers, resolutions, and frames legitimately identify different conditions over the same terrain. A condition statement without a declared position is unfinished.

Compressed signature: **a P3 object under a P6 operation from a P5 position.**

Plain-language compression, suitable for Layer 1 use: a condition is something currently true that matters to what happens next.

## 2. The Etymological Ground

English "condition" descends from a medieval merger of two Latin words with different roots, and the merger is structural rather than accidental.

*Condicio*, from *condicere* (con + dicere, to speak together), meant terms agreed upon: stipulation, compact, and by extension the circumstances those terms establish.

*Conditio*, from *condere* (to found, establish, lay down, put together), meant a founding: what has been laid down and now stands.

Scribes merged the spellings; English inherited the seam. The word's notorious overload (contract condition, necessary condition, living conditions, medical condition, conditioned response, air conditioning) is the seam showing.

The two roots jointly specify the object: **a condition is a settlement that has become terrain.** Terms were articulated (condicere), the articulation stopped, and the terms hardened into ground (condere). The word carries the forgetting inside it: the transition from active speaking to standing structure is the transition from negotiable to ambient.

Load-bearing: the two-root reading and the settlement-to-terrain compression. Held loosely: any claim that Latin speakers experienced the two words as one concept; the merger is doing conceptual work for us, not historical work for them.

## 3. Convergence with the Causality Lineage

The causality system synthesis independently derived the same object dynamically. Its sequence: cause deforms, effect commits (local closure), consequence propagates (closure that keeps traveling), consequentiality is field-weight, and **condition is a consequence with enough consequentiality to become terrain.** The promotion gate is consequentiality.

The etymological derivation and the dynamical derivation match at the exact phrase. "Settlement that has become terrain" and "consequence promoted to terrain" are the same claim from a philological substrate and a free-energy substrate respectively. Neither derivation knew about the other.

Consequences of the convergence:

The causality synthesis marks its four-term typing as held loosely. This convergence is promotion-grade evidence for it. Recommended: the typing moves toward load-bearing in the next revision pass of that node, with this document cited as the independent derivation.

Background conditions are not a separate family. They are ordinary conditions far enough past the promotion gate that nobody is still speaking the terms. The condicere-to-condere transition is the mechanism of their invisibility, which the causality synthesis names (promotion) but does not explain (why promoted conditions disappear from view).

The promotion gate's formalization in TCL variables remains the open formal target it already was (causality synthesis open thread 3). This document adds no formal progress on it, only a second reason to want it.

## 4. Mandatory Indexing

Because conditionality is a P6 operation applicable in principle to any state under any outcome, everything can be typed as a condition, and therefore everything will be. This predicts the word's two-thousand-year semantic collapse and predicts the same collapse inside the corpus unless indexing is enforced.

The discipline: **a state does not receive the name "condition" until it is indexed to a referent and a register.** The four registers, from the conditional-effect analysis:

Possibility (can the outcome occur at all), probability (how likely is it), permissibility (is it licensed or defensible), sustainability (can it continue without degrading).

"Trust is low" is a fragment. "Trust between the two partners, bearing on whether the referral loop is sustainable" is a condition. The indexing is what makes the statement checkable rather than nodable-at.

The register list carries a known asymmetry: possibility, probability, and sustainability are modal properties of the outcome, while permissibility is a property of an observer's relation to it. Whether this is one list or two is an open thread. The asymmetry may be correct given P5, since the corpus already treats all assertions as observer-indexed, but it has not been worked.

The functional typology developed in the inventory session (preconditions, sustaining, protective, licensing, invalidating, threshold, and the rest of the twenty) is a useful diagnostic family, not a derived one. Instinct says it compresses hard, perhaps to five or six with the rest as subtypes; unworked. The domain inventories generated in the same session are a search index (places where conditions tend to be found), not a taxonomy of objects, because every entry is written in unindexed intrinsic form.

## 5. Individuation Without Isolation

Conditionality being relational forbids isolating a condition from its relata. It does not forbid individuating one within a named frame, and the coaching practice depends on the difference.

The gradient analogy carries it: a gradient cannot exist at one point, it is defined across a separation, yet "the gradient between these two points is steep and shrinking" is a well-formed, checkable claim. Naming a condition is a localization: assigning a sign to a field property at a chosen resolution from a specified observer position. This routes directly through Relational Localization, and inherits its named failure mode, stale assignment: the sign persists after the localization has moved.

Stale assignment, stale setpoint (chemotaxis grounding), condition freshness (temporal inventory), and ongoing defensibility (defensibility inventory) are the same object surfacing in four vocabularies. All are instances of the invertibility invariant: a condition statement that can no longer be traced back to live terrain has lost something load-bearing, and continuing to operate on it is the ordinary, everyday form of hollowing.

## 6. Recursion and Termination

Conditions have conditions, and this costs nothing structurally. The P6 operation takes its own output as input: a second-order condition is the same operator with a first-order condition's status serving as the outcome. There is no tower of exotic types, only one operator recursing on shifted referents.

Lawfulness sits here natively: a second-order condition in the permissibility register, governing whether a stable generating structure exists such that probability talk is admissible at all. It maps onto existing structure (licensing-type condition, one level up) rather than opening new structure.

The recursion terminates in both directions:

**Theoretical floor:** substrate. Sleep, calories, physics, biology. Condere without condicere: laid down, never spoken, carrying no P6 load, not renegotiable by re-articulation. This is the REAL addendum's shape arriving again: the regress runs until it hits something that was laid down rather than agreed, then stops.

**Practical floor:** controllability. Diagnostic work stops climbing at the first level where something can be tended this week. Without this stop rule, condition analysis becomes infinite drilldown terminating at "the real problem is capitalism," which is true, unactionable, and fatal to any instrument built on the analysis.

Climbing is legitimate only if descent remains possible. A second-order insight that cannot be traced back to a first-order condition someone could touch is not deeper diagnosis; it is laundering of the fact that nobody wants to do the tending. Invertibility holds at every level of the recursion.

## 7. The Promotion Continuum and the CCC Triad

The CCC triad (containers, constraints, conditions) is a Layer 1 diagnostic instrument, pragmatically minimal rather than metaphysically necessary. This synthesis gives it a mechanism it previously lacked.

Conditions do not emerge from containers interacting with constraints; the arrow runs the other way. **Containers and constraints are conditions that promoted so far they hardened into objects.** An LLC is literally terms spoken together and filed: a settlement that stabilized until it acquired an inside and could be treated as a thing. Policies, roles, budget ceilings, contracts: settlements that went quiet and became structure. Trust, by contrast, is imported into a container fully formed, with its own generative history; sleep derives from nothing in the triad at all.

So the triad is three positions on one promotion continuum, distinguished by distance past the gate and current renegotiability, not three kinds of stuff. This is consistent with, and strengthens, the existing note that three slices is the minimum for useful discrimination rather than a claim about the world.

The two roots then split constraints into two genuinely different kinds, with a client-runnable test: **did somebody decide this?** If yes, it is a settlement (spoken, went quiet, re-speakable in principle even when expensive). If no, it is substrate (route around it). This repairs a thumb on the scale in earlier product framings: "stop railing against constraints, tend conditions" is correct counsel only for substrate constraints. For settled constraints, railing is sometimes exactly right, and an operating agreement is nothing but settled constraints.

Held loosely, flagged as a live open thread: whether container is genuinely distinct from constraint, or whether a container is a constraint set that closed into a loop and thereby acquired an inside. If the latter, the triad is two positions plus a topological property, a cleaner geometry than the current one.

## 8. Neighbors and Non-Collisions

**Boundary.** Not the same object, and the earlier suspicion of collision is withdrawn. A boundary is a relation between regions, an operator on their engagement, structurally located in R and typed P2 through P6; when the regions stop engaging, the boundary ceases to exist. A condition is promoted terrain in the slow layer; it persists whether or not anything is currently crossing it. Boundary is where flow crosses; condition is what flow moves across. Sign-interface, as a specialization of boundary, inherits the non-collision.

The differing typing signatures (boundary: structural object in R typed P2 through P6; condition: P3 object under P6 operation from P5 position) are candidate formal discriminators between corpus objects. If signatures of this kind hold up across other nodes, the corpus graph gains a typed comparison operation that does not depend on argument. Unworked; promising.

**Consequence.** A condition is downstream of consequence via promotion, but consequence itself is never intrinsic to a system. It lives in the embedding: a boulder is simple, a boulder above a village is enormously consequential, and the consequence is located nowhere in the boulder. Any analysis plotting a system's own complexity against its consequence commits a category error and will produce a cloud. The apparent decline of consequence at high complexity is better read as consequence becoming unattributable (topological occlusion rising) while consequence itself continues to climb; the gap between consequence and attributable consequence is AOMI territory.

**Lawfulness, convergence pressure, EDF's conditioning grammar.** Previously proposed as one object showing three faces. This synthesis places lawfulness cleanly (second-order licensing condition). Whether convergence pressure resolves to a condition-family object as well is plausible and unworked; if it does, the drawer-collapse warning of Section 4 applies with force.

## 9. Drift, Un-Promotion, and the Applied Layer

Intervention Stewardship supplies the maintenance dynamics: an intervention that crosses the promotion gate becomes condition, maintained by the system itself; one held forever is a tax. Its open thread 4 states the reverse process: **a promoted condition with no funded defender gets un-promoted.** Degradation is therefore not a vague decline but a checkable structural event: the maintenance loop lost funding.

This gives the condition-mapping product its native mechanism. The applied claims, stated compactly:

The map is the acquisition surface ("why is this not working" is a question people ask; "log your decisions" is not). Drift detection is the retention surface, because a finished map decays immediately and the only reason to return is that something in it has stopped being true. The product's philosophical core, earned from the etymology rather than imposed: condition mapping is re-speaking terms that were settled and then went silent.

UI consequences: every condition carries a register tag and a referent, and an untagged entry is flagged as not yet a condition. Containers and constraints render as lists (discrete, enumerable, rarely touched); conditions render as gauges (field-valued, moving). Decay rate and lead time jointly yield a computable fragility triage (fast to decay, slow to rebuild sorts first) that does not depend on accepting the framework. The practical verb set is four: notice, name, mark, tend, with controllability as the stop criterion. The instrument must be able to deliver the structural verdict (dissolve the container, the constraint binds, stop using this tool), or it is a comfort object with a schema.

## 10. Load-Bearing vs Held Loosely

**Load-bearing:**
The definition and its three-part separation (terrain, bearing, position). Conditionality as a P6 operation, not an intrinsic property. The two-root etymology and the settlement-to-terrain compression. The convergence with the causality synthesis and its promotion-grade weight. Mandatory indexing (referent plus register) as the discipline preventing drawer collapse. Stale assignment / stale setpoint / condition freshness / ongoing defensibility as one object; the invertibility invariant holding across the recursion. The promotion continuum reading of CCC and the did-somebody-decide-this test. The boundary non-collision. Un-promotion via defunded maintenance as the mechanism of drift.

**Held loosely:**
The four-register list as complete, and the permissibility asymmetry (one list or two). The twenty-type functional typology and its conjectured compression. Whether the P5 position is a genuine third component or collapses into the P6 typing (typing arguably requires a typist; possible double-count). Container as closed constraint-loop. Typing signatures as a general corpus discriminator. Convergence pressure as condition-family.
