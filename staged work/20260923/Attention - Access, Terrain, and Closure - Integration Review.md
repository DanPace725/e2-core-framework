## Working Read

### 1. Load-bearing claim

The useful new claim is that **attention can be distinguished by its modulation of effective access to a system's resolving processes**, rather than identified with energy expenditure, sensory registration, the final commitment, or care. This supplies a common interpretation for several operations already called attention in the corpus without requiring them to be identical mechanisms.

The strongest short formulation is the candidate's own:

> Can it be distinguished? Can it matter? What closes?

The contribution becomes more substantial when joined to the second claim: present access is shaped by historically formed conditions, and present episodes can alter those conditions. The note gives this recurrence a useful organization:

```text
terrain -> attentional modulation -> local closure
   ^                                  |
   +--- stabilization <- consequence -+
```

This diagram is a functional account, not a compulsory sequence. Attention may change effective distinguishability, and a local allocation may itself be a closure. The review recommends retaining that qualification prominently.

The candidate's success is architectural clarification. It brings access, persistence, history, interpersonal organization, and power into one inspectable account. It does not yet establish a general scientific theory of attention or a validated cross-substrate diagnostic.

### 2. Provisional integration type

**Primary type: bridge. Secondary type: specialization, with a candidate functional construct.**

The bridge connects Resolution Synthesis, Signal as Bias Field, GCO, Asymmetry Maintenance, Complex Causality, Condition as Typed Terrain, and Boundary Ethics. Its mechanisms specialize existing modulation, boundary, persistence, and reopening operations. “Attention as access modulation” is a candidate construct insofar as its admission criteria discriminate cases that those parent descriptions leave conflated.

It is not presently a metric/property: no observable scale for effective access, fitted model, identification procedure, or calibrated decision threshold is supplied. The etymological material is an interpretive parallel. The plant, immune, developmental, and group-complexity extensions have separate evidential burdens.

No seventh relational primitive is needed. The six attention-grammar terms are also not the six Relational Primitives, and their provisional count should not imply a derivation from that grammar.

### 4. What is genuinely sharpened

1. **Registration, effective access, and commitment are distinguishable.** Seeing or representing a difference need not give it meaningful influence over the next resolution.
2. **Lean and Gate can differ in the same episode.** A standing inclination can coexist with temporary suppression; deliberate admission can oppose a standing inclination.
3. **Ward, Release, and Redirect answer different questions.** Preventing admission, dissolving an existing allocation, and favoring another candidate should not be inferred interchangeably from the same outward behavior.
4. **Attention can leave changes in future access policy.** The payload is broader than a preference for an object: thresholds, suppression, search templates, persistence, and ease of release may change.
5. **Tend is an organization of attention.** Responsiveness, orientation toward the tended relation, and restraint distinguish it from bare contingent feedback.
6. **Attention-signs can outlive or misrepresent attention.** Operation, observable artifact, and the artifact's effect on a receiver should remain separate.
7. **Shared terrain can coordinate attention without a single group mind.** This is a useful way to discuss institutions, interfaces, and persistent traces while preserving individual and collective scales.

These distinctions justify further candidate development even if several empirical illustrations and stronger equivalences are removed.

## Collision And Risk Review

### 1. Resolution, attention, and GCO need a level distinction

Candidate §§4–5 distinguish access from closure, while §7.4 calls Pick the GCO firing. Both can stand if the allocation is a **local attentional closure** that constrains later interpretation or action. They conflict if the account requires Pick to precede all closure categorically. Similarly, RS already includes commitment and recombination, not only discrimination.

**Recommended clarification:** distinguish discriminability, access modulation, local allocation closure, and subsequent closures, while allowing recurrence and overlap. A functional distinction is sufficient; a new mandatory processing pipeline is unnecessary.

### 2. Admission still needs a negative-control boundary

The revised criterion is better than generic resource allocation. Yet state-sensitive filtering is widespread. The note must show what distinguishes attention-like modulation from a fixed filter, history-dependent detector, simple adaptive controller, or downstream actuator at the chosen scale.

For each proposed case, record the system boundary, competing differences, process being influenced, access-changing mechanism, history/state dependence, comparison condition, observation window, and what would count against admission. A static filter may implement Gate as a component without the whole filter being an attention system. A resource response following detection is not evidence that access itself changed.

The immune test remains a good adversarial next case. It is not completed by listing receptors, amplification, suppression, and memory. Plant and computational cases deserve the same local discipline. A positive functional classification does not settle consciousness, welfare, or agency in every other sense.

### 3. Lean, Gate, valuation, and standing bias are not established identities

The distinction between Gate now and Lean over a longer window is strong. The stronger assertion that ranking, permeability, and bias are equivalent at terrain resolution needs a specified equivalence relation. Two mechanisms can produce the same observed ranking and diverge under intervention, changed inputs, or changed timescale.

**Recommended wording direction:** they may be alternative terrain-level descriptions of a localized access disposition, while their equivalence remains conditional on what is preserved by the chosen coarse-graining. Keep internal disposition, interface behavior, and shared environmental terrain individually traceable.

### 4. The grammar cannot make Tend necessary for every causal cycle

Candidate §11 correctly allows attention without Tend. Section 7.4 then maps Tend to consequence propagation in the general attention cycle. Attention can have consequences without tending anything, including adversarial or careless attention.

Retain the table as a partial correspondence, and state that **consequence follows attentional episodes generally; Tend is one organization that governs which consequences are sought, noticed, and corrected**. Likewise, “no attention without an attended” should apply to directed allocation without denying that nondirectional Alert is an enabling component.

### 5. Topology, closure, and care must stay separate

The directed/reciprocal/mutual/joint distinction is valuable. However, “only a loop can settle” and “mutual attention is the first attention structure” to which settlement applies (§9) overreach unless the relevant boundary and kind of settlement are specified. A person can close a decision about an unresponsive object. Internal feedback and reciprocal interpersonal attention are different loops.

An exploitative recommender may be genuinely reciprocal and may meet the proposed functional attention criterion. Its ethical failure need not make the return arc fictitious. “Counterfeit” should name a false representation of care, regard, or orientation where that is what is counterfeited. **Mutual attention does not entail Tend.** This preserves the force of the candidate's later answerability/orientation distinction.

Stigmergy also need not include joint awareness. A persistent trace can coordinate later behavior without anyone representing that another attends to the same object. Keep “one structure at two resolutions” as a proposed mapping with a stated lost feature, not an established identity.

### 6. Pull/reach origin does not by itself determine capture or recoverability

The note correctly says that the pull-precipitate route can produce ordinary formation. Its five signatures subsequently become too categorical: chosen practices can lose recoverable origin; imposed dispositions can be endorsed later; involuntary learning can be revised; deliberate practice can become rigid or exploitative.

Keep provenance, endorsement, reversibility, beneficiary, and maintenance burden as **separate diagnostic axes**, not a classification rule already established by origin. Mixed origins and unknown histories must remain admissible. Cost borne by someone and benefit accruing elsewhere require actual tracing.

### 7. Signal role, sign credibility, and sign governance need separate tests

The operation/artifact/role distinction is worth retaining. The following stronger claims should be revised or scoped:

- The active SBF describes a relational bias field; reducing signal to emitter-side push would narrow that account. “Signal is a role” is a compatible interpretive extension, not its complete quoted definition.
- A sign of attention can influence a receiver without accurately representing attention. Credibility needs evidence about reliability and the generating process; cost alone is neither a sufficient warrant nor a necessary feature of every reliable sign.
- A gaze or fleeting response may be an attention cue without constituting the durable, auditable sign needed in accountability routing.
- The archived SMFR's invisibility is relative to systems that operate on signs. It does not show that every unsigned physiological process lacks control or that unattended flow must accumulate as burden.
- The neuronal and breath-work examples should remain analogies or separately sourced mechanisms. Their physiology does not by itself establish an alternation of attention and sign at every interface, or an attention-based explanation of the whole effect.

“Nobody sends their attention into another system” works as a distinction between an operation and its effects/signs. Its necessity does not follow from the etymology of stretch or from treating every physical push as release. Mark the scope as functional description.

### 8. Formalism and empirical evidence have different limits

The schematic in §5.3 usefully names dependencies but does not yet explain them quantitatively:

- `Φ(P_i, R_i | L)` is unspecified and therefore does not imply additive combination of Pull and Reach.
- A positive common Alert multiplier does not change candidate ranking by itself; an effect on absolute thresholds or dynamics must be specified.
- Alert and Gate are not separately identifiable from their product alone without additional observations or constraints.
- Normalization, capacity coupling, units, ranges, and a readout from priority to allocation remain absent.
- A product of gains is not a general stability test without dynamics, feedback sign, delay, saturation, and a definition of gain.
- Slow accumulation can change a landscape without proving it avoids a parametric instability. The “sidestepping the wall” claim remains a modeling hypothesis.

These are reasons to retain the expression as an illustration, as the candidate itself initially does. They are not reasons to discard the underlying distinctions.

### 9. Group complexity is a promising extension, not a consequence of group size alone

For a labeled directed graph without self-links, `n(n−1)` possible edges and `2^(n(n−1))` binary configurations are correct counts; at ten nodes this is `2^90`, approximately `1.24 × 10^27`. Real attentional configurations may be constrained and many counted configurations may be unreachable.

The counting argument does not prove computational irreducibility, unpredictability, or a universal transition at three parties. The three-body, social-triad, and joint-attention examples involve different state spaces and explanatory problems. “Combinatorics × reflexivity” is a useful qualitative prompt, not a derived criterion.

Forward prediction limits and retrospective attribution are distinct questions worth retaining. Their proposed identification with “Zone 3 proper” and a universal attention chaos ceiling needs its own review. CC and TCL supply source vocabulary and a model lead, not that conclusion. Active Boundary Dynamics also makes stronger universality claims than AMM, CC, and TCL's model-class caveats; this source tension must be recorded rather than silently resolved in favor of the strongest wording.

### 10. Etymology and developmental parallels should not carry proof burden

Word histories can guide naming and expose forgotten senses. They cannot establish the anatomy of attention, a shared mechanism, a prohibition on attentional “push,” or the validity of a care criterion. The active Condition source [S06] already states this discipline explicitly; the staged AMM predecessor does too.

The note's “framework validates it” wording needs to distinguish internal compatibility from empirical validation. The proposed six-stage developmental correspondence is not an independent test without definitions and predictions fixed before matching examples. The acknowledgement in §18 that Sol had read Claude's synthesis is good provenance and should be retained. Agreement in that sequence is not independent replication.
