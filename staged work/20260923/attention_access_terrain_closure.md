## 0. Core, one line

Attention is the dynamic differential modulation of which relational differences gain effective access to a finite system's processes of resolution and closure; it operates through fast pressures and gates acting on historically formed terrain, and in operating it helps form the terrain through which later attention will resolve.

Shortest form: **Can it be distinguished? Can it matter? What closes?** Resolution, attention, GCO, embedded in the loop terrain → attention → closure → consequence → precipitation → terrain.

## 3. Etymological recovery

Etymology generates candidate structure; the framework validates it. Convergent threads are trusted; single-witness threads are flagged.

**The *ten-* family, to stretch.** *Attendere* (stretch toward), *intendere* (stretch into, aim), *tendere* as incline (tendency), and *tenere* (to hold), whence maintain (*manu tenere*, hold in the hand), sustain, contain. Greek *teinein* gives *tonos*, a string's tension and so its pitch, whence tone and its variant tune. *Attenuare*, to make thin: attention and attenuation are siblings, so a reach-versus-thickness trade sits inside the word itself. English *tend* in the caring sense is the aphetic form of *attend*.

**The pull family.** *Trahere*, to draw (attract, distract, abstract; distract originally meant to pull asunder). *Salire*, to leap (salient). *Rapere*, to seize (rapt). *Capere*, to take (captivate). *Fascinare*, to bewitch. Every member has the field acting on the system. The working handle is **Pull**, adopted over Draw because Draw forks in reading (a picture, a tied game, a drawer) and Pull does not, and because Pull exposes its conjugate.

**The *leg-* family, to gather or pick.** *Seligere* (select), *diligere* (to single out, esteem, love), *neglegere* (not to pick up), *intellegere*, *eligere* (already touched by the elegance pass). This family names the partition, and neglect is literally not-picking.

**Guarding and waking.** Heed (Germanic *hodjan*, likely related to hood; deeper root not verified). Regard, *re-garder*, to watch over. Ward, to guard or keep off. Vigil, *vigil*, awake, with Germanic cognates wake and watch.

**Care.** Old English *caru*, grief or burden, not Latin *cura*. The burden-bearing sense is native.

**Focus.** Latin *focus*, hearth, borrowed by Kepler in 1604 for the burning point of a lens: the place where rays converge and energy concentrates.

**The locus word.** *Interest*, *inter-esse*, to be between. Attention lives in the between.

**The return prefix.** Mutuality is marked by *re-*: regard, respect (*respicere*, look back at), relate (*re-latus*, carry back), reciprocal, respond (*re-spondere*, pledge back; *spondere* also gives spouse and sponsor). **Answer** is Old English *andswaru*, *and-* (in return) plus *swerian* (to swear): a sworn reply.

**The emitter families.** Push, from Old French *pousser*, from Latin *pulsare*, frequentative of *pellere*, to drive or beat: pulse, impulse, compel, expel, repel, propel. Appeal, *appellare*, built on *ad-pellere*, to drive toward. *Mittere*, to send: emit, transmit, message.

**Loosening.** Release, *relaxare*, to loosen again. Resolve, *resolvere*, to loosen again. The two share the same etymological motion, which the RS already predicted: every resolution has a prior dissolution.

**Findings that carry weight.**

The *ten-* identity: the root that names attention also names maintenance. Asymmetry maintenance is hold with no particular attended; attention is hold with one.

The two-family split: attention's two directions are named by different families. The system stretches (*ten-*); the field pulls (*trahere*, *salire*, *rapere*).

The pledge convergence: three unrelated roots put a pledge at the center of the returning relation. *Deru-* (trust, troth, truce) from the trust pass, *spondere* under respond, *swerian* under answer. This is about as strong as etymological convergence gets.

**Single-witness or uncertain.** Heed's deeper root. Whether *nota* (mark, sign) connects to *noscere* (to know); if it does, noticing literally names sign-formation. Tendril's link to *tendere*.

**Registry entries proposed.** *\*ten-* and *\*leg-* for the Etymology Registry, alongside the seeded *\*sta-*, *\*deru-*, *\*wal-*, *\*sel-*, *\*strenk-*, the carry family, and *\*deik-*. *\*ten-* sits adjacent to *\*sta-*: standing firm and holding taut.

## 4. The central distinction: resolution, attention, closure

Attention sits between distinguishability and commitment. Three questions separate:

1. **Resolution capacity.** Can this difference be distinguished at this resolution? This is the RS and Rayleigh question.
2. **Attention.** What effective access does this distinguishable difference have to the system's resolving processes?
3. **Closure.** What configuration actually becomes determinate enough for action, interpretation, or persistence? This is the GCO.

A difference may exist without being resolvable by a system. It may be resolvable without being attentionally consequential. It may become consequential without determining closure, and it may influence closure without winning it outright.

This is a functional distinction rather than a strict temporal pipeline. Attention can operate after registration (all five objects on the desk are seen; one is attended), and it can also alter effective resolution through gain, gating, amplification, or sustained inspection. The gain is conceptual separation: perception is not attention, attention is not closure, resource allocation is not attention, and attention can modulate all three.

The distinction also keeps attention from collapsing into generic GCO. The GCO asks which possibility becomes closed. Attention asks which differences get enough effective access to the closure process to shape that resolution in the first place.

**The central shift.** Attention is not fundamentally the thing being spent. It is the modulation of access by which differences become consequential to closure. Resources may follow attention, and attention consumes resources, but resource movement is neither necessary nor sufficient to establish it.

**Load-bearing:** the three-question separation; access rather than expenditure as the central shift.

## 5. Grammar and mechanics

### 5.1 The grammar

The first pass produced six terms: **Lean, Pull, Reach, Pick, Hold, Tend.** They identified real structure but did not sit at one level. The first pass had already typed them differently (standing, event, vector, partition, rate, delivery) without asking what that heterogeneity meant. Sol's reading is that they span layers: Lean is terrain, Pull and Reach are pressures on it, Pick is resolution into allocation, Hold is persistence, and Tend is a higher-order organization.

That is why the six read as a progression. They ascend through layers of organization, and human development builds the layers in roughly the same order: innate biases and the orienting response present at birth, visually guided reaching around four to five months, joint attention around nine to twelve months, sustained attention maturing through adolescence, and care for others as the mature form. The ordering was not deliberately built, so the developmental match is the independent check rather than the listing.

The six are kept as an **attention grammar**: descriptively valuable, recognizable, and portable. The corpus already has a causal grammar in the causality synthesis, so the genre fits. A smaller or differently organized set carries the mechanics.

### 5.2 The mechanics

**Alert → Bias [Pull and Reach on Lean] → Gate [Ward] → Resolve [Pick] → Sustain [Hold] → Release**

These name functional questions, not mandatory stages. Several may operate at once, recursively, or on different clocks.

**Alert.** The system's current readiness for any difference to acquire attentional gain. Non-directional: it answers "how available is this system for something to matter right now," not "what matters." It varies with arousal, fatigue, vigilance, metabolic condition, threat state, expectancy, and circadian phase. Failure runs both ways: hyporesponsiveness at one end, and at the other hypervigilance, which is not "stronger attention" but altered readiness across a whole class of possible pulls. Alert maps onto the alerting network in Posner and Petersen's three-network model (alerting, orienting, executive control) and onto the adaptive gain theory of the locus coeruleus (Aston-Jones and Cohen), in which a tonic mode favors exploration and distractibility and a phasic mode favors exploitation and task focus. Tonic Alert is therefore the attentional form of search pressure, which ties this layer to the parked noise thread.

**Pull.** A change in candidate priority originating outside the currently controlling attentional loop. "Outside" is boundary-relative, not organism-external: a sound, pain, hunger, an intrusive memory, a notification, and another person's gaze can all pull. What matters is that the current controller did not issue the bid. **Pull is a bid on access, not capture.** A candidate can pull strongly and still be warded, outcompeted, or released.

**Reach.** Priority pressure issued from the currently controlling side of the system toward a candidate: searching, scrutinizing, recalling, listening-for, checking, deliberately focusing. Its geometry is focus and aperture; its limit is attenuation. **Intention is reach represented reflexively by the system as its own directed act**, so reach does not require consciousness, and conscious intention is one specialization of it. The two-origin structure survives intact: the field can raise a candidate's priority from one side, and the system can raise it from the other.

**Lean** enters the mechanics as the surface both pressures act on, not as a third pressure. See §7.

**Gate and Ward.** Gate modulates how effectively a candidate may participate in attentional competition: differential admission by modality, location, feature, class, or relation. Its suppressive pole is **Ward**, which reduces gain, permeability, priority, feature weighting, channel access, or downstream influence. Gate can also be positive, selectively opening permeability. See §6.

**Pick, read as Resolve.** The resolution of an underdetermined priority field into an allocation determinate enough to constrain downstream processing. The allocation need not be binary; it may be singular, distributed, graded, alternating, hierarchical, or multimodal. Pick is the attentional localization where access becomes allocation; the GCO is the general closure principle governing the commitment that follows. Two parameters belong here rather than becoming operators: **aperture** (how broad or narrow the attended field is) and **multiplicity** (how many candidates receive meaningful allocation at once). Neglect remains Pick's shadow, understood as opportunity cost under finite capacity rather than a strictly conserved quantity.

**Hold, read as Sustain.** Sustaining an allocation against drift, decay, interruption, competition, and noise. Hold has duration, energetic cost, decay rate, interference tolerance, margin requirement, and a characteristic timescale, which can range from milliseconds to developmental periods. Too little Hold means distractibility and failure to keep a distinction long enough to integrate or use it; too much becomes fixation or capture. AMM's warning applies directly: when maintenance margin collapses, systems lose the ability to hold distinctions apart and fall toward cheaper closures.

**Release.** Active loosening of an existing allocation enough to permit re-resolution. Release is not Hold passively decaying to zero; those are different events. **Hold preserves attentional closure; Release reopens it.** Release is RS's prior-dissolution requirement at the scale of a single episode. Posner's orienting model separates disengage as its own stage, which is an external convergence for Release as a distinct operation.

### 5.3 The priority schematic

The first draft wrote Bias = Lean + Pull + Reach. That treats three peers, but Lean is not a peer: it is the landscape the other two act on, and the same pull has no context-independent force (a bang pulls a soldier and a toddler very differently, as SBF would predict). A better schematic:

```
Π_i = A · G_i · Φ(P_i, R_i | L)
```

where L is Lean terrain, P is Pull, R is Reach, G is Gate, A is Alert, and Π_i is the effective priority or access of candidate i.

This is illustrative rather than established mathematics. The structural point: Pull and Reach are evaluated on Lean terrain, which is the additive, biased-competition part (Desimone and Duncan); Alert and Gate act as multipliers on the result, a global gain and a selective permeability. Neural data show both forms. Attention scales tuning curves multiplicatively (McAdams and Maunsell), biases competition additively, and Reynolds and Heeger's normalization model combines the two. The schematic also answers the additive-versus-multiplicative question Daniel raised: additive within the priority field, multiplicative in gain and gating.

SMFR adds where multiplication compounds. Within one interface, permeability, friction, reflectivity, and leakage sum to one (an additive partition of what arrives). Along a serial chain of interfaces, throughput is the product of permeabilities. Around a loop, loop gain is the product of the gains, so loops above unity run away and loops below it damp.

### 5.4 Table of forms

| Form | Placement | Core question |
|---|---|---|
| **Alert** | Mechanic: gain | How ready is the system for anything to matter? |
| **Lean** | Terrain | What is already easier to treat as relevant? |
| **Pull** | Mechanic: field-side pressure | What is bidding for access from outside the current controller? |
| **Reach** | Mechanic: controller-side pressure | What is the system actively seeking or privileging? |
| **Gate / Ward** | Mechanic: access control | What is admitted, amplified, attenuated, or refused? |
| **Pick** | Mechanic: allocation | What becomes attentionally resolved? |
| **Hold** | Mechanic: persistence | What allocation remains live? |
| **Release** | Mechanic: reopening | What allocation is actively dissolved? |
| **Push** | Coupling: emitter side | What is emitted to alter another attentional field? |
| **Tend** | Organization | What is kept responsively attended for the sake of its ongoing condition? |

This is the cleanest current localization, not a final ontology.

## 6. Suppression, release, and the three ways of not attending

A system can reduce a candidate's influence without pushing attention away from it. Negative weighting is not negative movement. X can simply be denied enough effective access to shape closure.

Ordinary language compresses three distinct operations into "moving attention":

- **Ward:** X is prevented from gaining access, or downweighted, before allocation.
- **Release:** X was attended, and the allocation is dissolved.
- **Redirect:** Y receives enough reach or pull to become the next allocation.

One event may involve all three, but none reduces to the others. An ignored notification can mean weak pull, low lean, active ward, strong reach elsewhere, pick elsewhere, or rapid release after brief capture. Each looks the same from outside and is mechanically different.

**There is no push-away vector at the attentional level.** Refusal is not reverse uptake; disengagement is not emission; redirecting does not require throwing the prior attended away. Wegner's thought-suppression studies support this for internal attention: trying to push attention off a thought produces rebound, because the monitoring process that checks for success keeps pulling the thought back. What works is reaching elsewhere, a chosen focal replacement. Inhibition of return, a brief bias against re-picking a just-attended location, is best read as a transient ward serving search, not a repulsive force. Even aversive stimuli pull attention while pushing the body away, which is why people rubberneck. Inhibitory mechanisms may implement Ward in neural tissue without push becoming the attentional-level description.

**Ward and stance-against.** Ward looks like the attentional form of stance-against from the Flow Operators: refusing what the boundary should not admit, a threshold quantity with a capacity. Gate as a whole is SMFR's interface permeability and selectivity tensor applied at the attentional boundary. A captured gate is one whose selectivity varies sharply by source, which is where engineered pull does its work.

**Naming note.** An earlier pass used "ward" for Tend's shadow, watching over in the guard sense. Sol's use has better etymological support ("ward off") and is adopted. Watching without tending is simply called surveillance.

**Load-bearing:** Ward, Release, and Redirect as distinct operations; no push-away vector at the attentional level.

## 7. Terrain: how attention forms its own conditions

### 7.1 Lean is terrain

Lean first appeared as a sub-reflexive operator, a standing bias toward some relations over others. That remains descriptively correct, but its structural role is clearer as terrain rather than event:

> **Lean is the standing geometry that makes some attentional trajectories easier than others before the present episode begins.**

It can include learned salience, valuation, expectation, habit, receptor sensitivity, boundary permeability, prior formation, inherited disposition, cultural conditioning, persistent suppression, prior reward or punishment, and embodied state. AMM already licenses the move: noun and verb are resolution slices of one process, so Lean as terrain-state rather than operator is that principle applied.

**The AMM braid, localized.** AMM's valuation section states the braid as an identity: relational ranking, "equivalently the typed permeability of the reception boundary, equivalently a standing bias field below awareness." AMM's core section lists boundary permeability as a parameter, beside gradient shape and modulation capacity. Both passages are in the node, and they sit at exactly the joint this pass probed. The resolution is that both are right at different resolutions:

> **Relational ranking, typed permeability, and standing bias are equivalent at terrain resolution and decompose into different mechanisms at episode resolution.**

At short timescales Lean and Gate differ sharply. A system may lean toward X and ward against it for now; it may have little lean toward Y and deliberately open its gate to Y. At slower timescales, persistent gating precipitates into the very terrain that later appears as Lean.

```
Gate_t ≠ Lean_t
persistent Gate → Lean_(t+n)
```

Compression: **Gate is one process that can shape Lean. Lean is the terrain produced by whatever shaping has persisted.**

### 7.2 Precipitation into a control policy

Attentional episodes do not only occur against terrain; they help produce the next terrain. The causality synthesis supplies the grammar: cause deforms, effect commits, consequence propagates, condition stabilizes into terrain. Attention fits inside it.

The first version of the claim was: **tendency is what intention leaves behind once it has been selected enough times.** That is condition promotion in attention-space, and Aristotle's *hexis* is its classical witness: we become just by doing just acts. It needed three corrections and one generalization.

**Three selection routes on different clocks.** Habit within a lifetime, transmission across a culture, selection across lineages. The orienting reflex is precipitated reach, but it was precipitated in ancestors.

**Not all lean is precipitated reach.** Some is precipitated pull (§8).

**Healthy precipitates stay invertible.** A formed tendency should be re-dissolvable into intention: lifted into view, questioned, re-chosen. That is reflection at personal scale and reform at institutional scale. A disposition that can be reopened is faithful abstraction; one that cannot is hollowing. The invertibility discriminator applies directly.

**Generalization: history precipitates into policy, not only direction.** Repeated episodes can modify Lean, Gate, Alert thresholds, Reach templates, Pull sensitivity, Hold duration, and Release difficulty. An episode can teach "this matters," "this predicts irrelevance," "this is dangerous," "this is worth searching for," "this may safely remain background," or "this must never be released." Attention history precipitates into an **attentional control landscape**.

```
Attention episode → Consequence → Precipitation → Terrain → Future attention
```

Habituation, which first appeared as a natural brake on precipitation, is itself a precipitate: repeated pull settling into lowered gate or strengthened ward. Sensitization is the opposite precipitate, repeated pull settling into raised Alert around a class.

**An internal gate on precipitation.** Kilgard and Merzenich paired tones with stimulation of the nucleus basalis, a source of acetylcholine closely tied to attention, and the auditory cortex reorganized around those tones; the same tones without the signal did far less. Inside a brain, an attention-linked signal is part of what licenses experience to settle into structure. Deliberate attention functions as a message to the rest of the system that this matters, and repeated messages precipitate. The pull-precipitate route is the same gate opened by salience rather than by reach.

Stronger form of the original phrase: **Lean is what prior relation has made easier for attention to do next.** With the qualification that Lean can also arise through inheritance, development, and endogenous organization.

### 7.3 Tilt and reshape

Pull and Reach are fast tilts: they alter trajectories within the present field, additively and robustly. Precipitation is reshape: it alters the geometry through which later trajectories move. The mechanics/terrain split is the causality synthesis's tilt/reshape distinction applied to attention, and TCL's fast/slow lamination in another substrate.

This has a consequence worth holding loosely but taking seriously. TCL's parametric wall makes direct reshape fragile and hard-limited: reshape a system quickly and it becomes delay-sensitive. Precipitation never attempts direct reshape. It tilts repeatedly within the robust additive window and lets condition promotion do the reshaping slowly. **Precipitation is how tilt becomes reshape while sidestepping the parametric wall.** A successful attentional intervention need not win attention repeatedly. If it can alter lean, gate, defaults, infrastructure, habit, or environmental accessibility, it eventually becomes terrain, and less active pressure is required. What once had to pull now simply lies downhill. This is why both engineered capture and good formation work slowly and robustly, and why the route itself cannot be the ethical discriminator.

### 7.4 Correspondence to the causal grammar

The six-term grammar closes into a cycle that maps onto the causal grammar:

| Grammar | Causal grammar |
|---|---|
| Lean | Condition (stabilized terrain) |
| Pull | Cause arriving (deformation of the landscape) |
| Reach | Cause issued (the system deforming toward something) |
| Pick | Effect (closure, the GCO firing) |
| Hold | Commitment maintained |
| Tend | Consequence propagating into the attended |

The tended becomes condition, and the loop closes. An attention episode is a causal episode, and the attention landscape is a causal landscape whose terrain is precipitated attention. Attention is causal in the full sense the causality synthesis gives the word.

**Load-bearing:** Lean as terrain; the braid localized by resolution; precipitation into control policy; the correspondence with the causal loop. **Hold loosely:** tilt becoming reshape around the parametric wall.

## 8. The pull-precipitate route: capture and formation

Repeated pull can settle into standing bias without the system's reach ever being involved. This route is general rather than domain-specific; it appears on at least three primitive facets (valuation, epistemic, group).

**The natural brake.** Repeated pull normally fades. Habituation is the default response to a stimulus that keeps arriving and changes nothing, so most pulls never precipitate into attraction.

**What steers precipitation toward lean rather than ward.** Four conditions are well anchored:

- **Reward pairing.** Anderson, Laurent, and Yantis found that stimuli once paired with reward keep capturing attention after they have become task-irrelevant and the reward has stopped. This is pull become lean at the perceptual level, almost exactly.
- **Intermittency.** Variable-ratio reinforcement produces the most persistent, extinction-resistant responding in the operant literature.
- **Repetition below awareness.** Zajonc's mere exposure effect raises liking with no reach anywhere in the loop. The illusory truth effect (Hasher and colleagues) is the same mechanism running on belief.
- **Population salience.** Agenda-setting research (McCombs and Shaw) found that the press shapes less what people think than what they think about: collective lean precipitated from collective pull.

Variability is the common tool that defeats habituation. The same fluctuation CFAR uses benignly to open search is what keeps an engineered pull fresh.

**Not all of it is capture.** Native grammar, a sense of home, and early taste all form by this route. Being formed by a landscape is part of how anyone becomes anyone. The route alone is not the discriminator.

**Five signatures that separate the kinds.**

1. **Provenance.** A reach-precipitate can be traced back to a chosen practice. A pull-precipitate has no originating choice to return to. "I don't know why I do this" is not evasion; there is no intention underneath.
2. **The wanting and liking gap.** Berridge and Robinson's incentive salience work shows motivational pull ("wanting") growing under repeated cue pairing while enjoyment ("liking") stays flat or declines. This is the valere/pretium split from the literacy work, visible in how the system is built. Frankfurt's account of identification states the same point philosophically: whether one endorses the desire one has.
3. **Invertibility.** Under reflection a reach-precipitate re-dissolves into the intention it came from. A pull-precipitate re-dissolves into a gap. RS needs a signal gradient to recombine along, and there is none, so reopening stalls. This is why people can see a pattern clearly and still not re-resolve it.
4. **Beneficiary.** Whose maintained gradient does the lean serve? A formed lean serves its carrier. An engineered one serves the engineer (AOMI's beneficiary occlusion). Natural pull-precipitates, such as a fear laid down by one bad encounter, may serve nobody's current gradient, only an ancestral one.
5. **Margin displacement.** Hold is finite, so a lean formed by pull occupies capacity that reach would otherwise use. AMM says saturated systems collapse onto cheap attractors, and cheap attractors are exactly the pulls that precipitate most easily. Pull-precipitate lowers margin, and lower margin raises susceptibility to further pull: a self-reinforcing trap with the same shape as the Emergence Engine's hunger loop.

**The counterfeit loop.** A recommender system watches its user and returns pulls tuned to the user's lean. That is contingent, responsive, return-arc behavior, the formal signature of mutual attention, but the tend component is missing. It inverts Tronick's still-face. The still-face is presence without return, and infants protest it. The feed is return without tend, and it draws little protest, because the contingency is real and the absence is invisible.

**The ethical line.** Pull inside a loop that tends and stays invertible is formation; a peekaboo game is an engineered pull that sits inside a caregiver's tending. Pull from a line that simulates a return arc and serves the one pulling is capture.

**EDF reading.** The engineer conditions; the precipitate is then named as the person's own preference. That is substitution operating exactly where EDF predicts it: a tendency supplied under the name of one's own disposition.

**Reopening routes.** Context change (severing the cue from its setting). Friction (adding Hold cost before the pull can close; a Truth Ceiling buffer lowering C_eff). Visibility (naming the precipitate and its beneficiary; EOTC's occlusion reduction). Margin restoration. Counter-precipitation through deliberate practice. Tending loops, which supply the gradient that re-resolution needs. This may be why relationship reopens what reflection alone cannot: dissolution needs somewhere to recombine to.

**Formation, grounded.** Formation, as one of the corpus's anchor words, becomes the question of which precipitates are allowed to form, by which route, under whose reach, and whether they remain invertible. Formation is the shaping of an attentional policy landscape, not the accumulation of preferred objects.

**Load-bearing:** the route and its anchors; the five signatures; the counterfeit loop. **Hold loosely:** the provenance/endorsement pair as a usable instrument before it is tested.

## 9. Relational topology: directed, reciprocal, mutual, joint

The first mutual-attention pass found a change of topology. One-sided attention is a line, with reach at one end and pull at the other. Add the return arc and each party's reach arrives at the other end as pull: my reach is your pull, and yours is mine. The stretch family and the seize family turn out to be one line read from opposite ends, and mutuality closes the line into a loop. Sol made the topology more granular:

```
Directed attention       A → X            A attends to X
Reciprocal coupling      A → X → A        X's changes return causally to A; X need not attend
Mutual attention         A ⇄ B            each attends to the other
Joint attention          A ⇄ B → X        A and B coordinate a shared allocation toward X
```

Reciprocal coupling is the missing middle case: a gardener and soil, where changes return causally but nothing attends back. Much of what earlier looked like mutual attention in plant and ecological cases may be reciprocal coupling, until the admission criterion (§12) says otherwise.

**What the loop makes possible.**

**Closure.** A line can deliver, but only a loop can settle. Mutual attention is the first attention structure the settlement operator applies to. Its failure already has a place in Boundary Dynamics: trade is the pair-quantity reciprocal mode, and the parametric wall marks where a reciprocal regime can no longer rebalance.

**Recursion.** Only in a loop can one party attend to the other's attending. That suggests intention may be internalized regard: one learns to aim attention by having been attended to. Vygotsky's law of cultural development runs this way (every higher function appears first between people and later within), and Tomasello's nine-month revolution gives it developmental timing, with triadic joint attention appearing before language and scaffolding it. Held loosely, but two external witnesses for a specific ordering claim.

**A shared world.** Triadic joint attention is two parties picking the same third term while each tracks that the other has picked it. That is a signal/noise partition held in common: local gluing of two picks, in Relational Localization terms. Common ground is not a region both parties happen to stand in; it is a co-held pick.

**Co-forms of the grammar.** Co-lean: standing biases shaped by each other over time (long partnerships, teams, cultures, flower and pollinator). Fused pull and reach: gaze, where being looked at captures attention. Co-pick: joint attention. Shared hold: vigil split between parties, or laminated so one holds slow while the other runs fast. Reciprocal tend: care that returns.

**The healthy window.** Coupled oscillators need enough coupling to synchronize, and too much collapses them into one oscillator and erases the lamination. The viable region for mutual attention lies between drift and fusion, and healthy mutuality preserves distinct clocks. Caregiver and infant are the canonical laminated pair: slow layer and fast layer, serve and return. Tronick's still-face paradigm is the controlled demonstration of the loop failing: the caregiver stays present but unresponsive, the infant tries to re-engage, then shows distress, then withdraws. That is a loop failing to close with the remainder borne internally, interest-frustration in its most legible form. Tronick's broader finding is that ordinary interaction spends most of its time mismatched, with coordinated states the minority and repair as the developmental engine (proportions from recall, not cited).

**Asymmetric mutuality is the general case.** Fiske's work on power and attention found that lower-power parties attend more carefully to higher-power ones, while the powerful attend less and rely more on stereotype. Attention flows up the power gradient (§14).

**Pathologies of the loop.** Fusion: the gap closes and the tension with it (Tensional Intelligence requires connection and differentiation together; AMM calls collapsing another's asymmetry relational annihilation). Severance: the gap widens past what reach can span, and the line attenuates. Capture: one party's reach becomes the other's unrelieved pull, a leash where a loop should be. Surveillance: watching without tending. Parasocial attachment: a line experienced as a loop, the return arc supplied by the imagination of the one reaching. Wrong-axis co-picking: tight synchrony on a difference that is not carrying signal (co-rumination, folie à deux, echo chambers), a joint pick that has filtered out whatever could reopen it.

**The projection claim, weak and strong.** The strong version, that all one-sided attention is a projection (a loop with one arc occluded), is held only as a population-level hypothesis. The defensible version: **many leans are fossilized arcs of prior coupling.** Some are inherited, evolved, self-generated, or produced by routes with no recoverable reciprocal arc.

**Load-bearing:** the line-to-loop change; the four-way topology; closure, recursion, and shared world as what the loop makes possible. **Hold loosely:** intention as internalized regard; the strong projection claim.

## 10. Push, signs, and the border with signal

### 10.1 Attention cannot be pushed

Attention is *ad-tendere*, a stretch, and a stretch stays anchored at both ends. A push releases: what is pushed leaves the pusher. At the moment attention were pushed it would stop being attention and become signal. **Nobody directly sends their attention into another system. They modify the field through which the other's attention resolves.**

```
Push_A → Signal → Pull_B
Pull_B ⇄ Ward_B → Resolve_B
```

An external actor can raise pressure, alter salience, monopolize channels, reshape terrain, or overwhelm modulation capacity, but the receiving attention still localizes on the receiving side. Push is the conditioning channel into another's attention, which is EDF in miniature: nobody reaches with another's reach.

**One gradient, two ends.** In a circuit the source is said to push (electromotive force) and the load to draw current, but there is one potential difference across the gap. Push at the emitter and pull at the receiver are the same gradient read from each end. This is why the noise synthesis found attention and signal hard to separate cleanly.

**Emission needs no receiver.** Pull requires two ends; push requires one. Light leaves a star whether anything is there or not. Much apparent one-sided attention may be push mislabeled: a lecture, a broadcast, a post to followers are emissions, and the attention is the pulls they produce. The flower mostly does not reach; it radiates scent, color, and electric field, while its receiving side runs pick and something like tend.

**Appeal** is push designed to land as pull, which is the pull-precipitate route in one word.

**The eye.** Ancient extramission theory held that eyes emit rays, making seeing a push; physics reversed that, but the phenomenology persisted (feeling stared at, the spotlight metaphor for attention). Attention cannot be pushed, but the signal of attention can. Tomasello's cooperative eye hypothesis argues that the human eye's white sclera evolved to make gaze direction visible to others: evolution solved the can't-push problem by making attention broadcastable. Pointing does the same thing deliberately.

### 10.2 Signing, not conversion

Three layers that ordinary use runs together:

1. **Operation.** Attention itself.
2. **Artifact.** A sign: a closure-artifact that makes the operation legible (gaze direction, dwell time, a contingent response, a named notice).
3. **Role.** Signal: what the sign does when a receiver takes it up and it biases that receiver's landscape.

SBF settled the third layer already: signal is not a substance but a role within a coupling. Nothing converts *into* a role. Attention does not become signal; it acquires signs, and the signs play the signal role.

Two tests confirm it. **Attention is not consumed when it signals:** the stretch continues at its origin while its legible trace reaches the other. **Signs of attention can be forged:** performative listening, automated "thinking of you" messages, engagement metrics with nobody behind them. Forgery requires that the sign be separable from what it signs for. The counterfeit loop (§8) works only because sign and attention come apart.

**The neuron gives the structure.** Graded input crosses threshold; an all-or-none spike (a discrete closure event, the sign) fires; released transmitter acts as signal on the next neuron's graded state. The graded potential does not become the spike; it crosses a threshold and a spike is produced. Signal and attention alternate along the chain, linked by signs, without converting into each other. This is SBF's rhythm (continuous pressure to discrete commitment) and SMFR's routing signature with attention as the incident pressure.

**What licenses the signal role, and what licenses credibility.** Legibility plus uptake licenses a sign to function as signal: a cheap glance toward a door still tells another person where attention lies. Cost licenses credibility: whether the sign is reliable, hard to fake, or indicative of substantial commitment. Costly signaling theory (Zahavi in biology, Spence in economics) belongs to the second question, not the first. Attention's finitude is what makes its allocation informative about the attender: if attention were unlimited, attending to someone would say nothing about them.

**Tethered and released signs.** Tethered signs are co-extensive with the attending and end when it ends: a gaze, a listening posture, a live response. Released signs persist after the attention stops: a handwritten letter, a remembered act, a tended garden, a worn path. A released sign is attention precipitated into an artifact, which is the kernel of truth in the conversion intuition. It is precipitation, not transmutation. A letter can mean more than a text because it holds more precipitated attention per unit of sign, and the cost is visible in the artifact.

### 10.3 Attention as signal, between and within minds

Attention normally carries information about its object. It becomes signal when a reader treats where it points as information about the attender: "she's looking at the door" is about the door; "she keeps looking at the door" is about her. Three uses separate. **Delivery:** attention that is care, the stretch carrying something across. **Testimony:** attention as evidence of care, information about the attender's lean. **Communication:** attention aimed so that someone will read the aim, including withholding (pointed ignoring; Williams's ostracism research shows being ignored registers as real harm, so legibility rather than presence is what makes attention signal).

All three have internal versions.

**Delivery, internally:** attending to a channel raises its neural gain, and an attention-linked signal gates plasticity (§7.2).

**Testimony, internally:** minds read their own attention as evidence about themselves. Klinger's current-concerns work found mind-wandering drifting disproportionately toward unmet goals; where attention keeps returning shows what has not closed. The broader pattern that open loops keep pulling is well supported even though the classic Zeigarnik effect has a mixed replication record. In corpus terms this is settlement-debt showing up as recurring pull, the internal signature the Interest-Frustration Instrument treats as a loop that cannot discharge. Treating friction and metabolic inconvenience as signal, a standing practice in this corpus, is reading one's own attentional snags as testimony.

**Communication, internally:** Global Workspace Theory describes attended content winning entry to a workspace and being *broadcast* to many specialized processes, which is pick followed by push inside one system. Rehearsal, practice, and ritual are deliberate uses of that broadcast.

**One schema, two directions.** Graziano's attention schema theory proposes that the brain builds a simplified model of its own attention and uses it to control attention, and that the same machinery models others' attention. Contested as a theory of consciousness, but the structural claim holds without that: attention control requires attention to be represented, and represented attention is signal. If so, reading attention between minds and within one are one capacity pointed two ways. The inter/intra boundary may be a resolution choice (Relational Localization): attention becomes signal whenever it crosses a boundary between subsystems that can read it. Development supports the social-first order: private speech turning into inner speech (Vygotsky), intention as internalized regard.

**Breath work** is the cleanest internal case. Breathing is under dual control, automatic from the brainstem and voluntary when taken over, so it sits on the boundary between the voluntary and the autonomic. Unattended breathing is unsigned flow in SMFR's exact sense (information crossing an unattended channel). Attention signs it; the signed breath routes into autonomic substrate through respiratory sinus arrhythmia and vagal pathways. The autonomic system does not interpret; it has operator capacity, and a sign routed into it is metabolized without being understood. That is why breath work reaches parts of the mind that cannot converse. Breath work is also a loop-gain intervention: in the panic loop, arousal speeds breathing, fast breathing drops CO2, and the sensations raise arousal further; changing the breath changes one factor in the product and the loop damps.

Routing is the general case. Conversation is routing with a return arc, where both ends produce and read signs.

### 10.4 An attentional specialization of SMFR

SMFR distinguishes unsigned flow (crossing boundaries with no closure artifact, untrackable and ungovernable) from signed flow, and names "information leaking through unattended channels" among its examples of the former. Attention governs which flows gain enough access to be signed. Attention control is governance over one's own flows: SMFR's principle that nothing can be routed that cannot be seen applies literally.

A candidate attentional specialization, following SMFR's own pattern: **sign types** include gaze, pointing, naming, and noticing; **remainder** is unattended flow, and SMFR's remainder dynamics then say interoceptive and relational neglect accumulate as unrouted flow while practices such as breath work act as metabolization. **Legibility capacity** (SMFR's L_i) is the node property that lets attention carry signs at all.

**Integrity property, candidate only.** Every SMFR specialization names one (justice for consequence routing, market integrity for commerce, truth-integrity for knowledge). The candidate here is **fidelity**, with two components: whether the sign is backed by attention actually present (sign without attention is counterfeit; attention without sign is unrouted remainder), and orientation, whether the answering the sign represents serves the attended's gradient or the attender's (§11). The name is Daniel's call.

**Load-bearing:** the tether and push as the conditioning channel; signing rather than conversion; operation, artifact, and role; signal-role licensed by legibility and uptake, credibility by cost; tethered and released signs. **Hold loosely:** delivery, testimony, and communication as the right internal categories; AST, GWT, and predictive-processing precision as convergent rather than merely compatible; the SMFR specialization and its integrity name.

## 11. Organization: Tend and the higher-order forms

Tend matters precisely because it is not required for minimal attention. A system can attend to a threat, a puzzle, a star, a sound, an adversary, a pain, or a target without tending any of them.

```
Attention ⇏ Tend
Tend ⇒ Attention
```

Tend is a temporally extended organization that repeatedly recruits the whole mechanics: Alert enough to notice relevant change; Lean that keeps the tended relation persistently significant; Pull when the tended system signals need; Reach when its state must be checked; Pick among changes that matter; Hold long enough to integrate and respond; Release when intervention is no longer called for; and return, as the relation continues. It is not "what comes after Hold."

**Open loop and closed loop.** A timer watering a plant regardless of its condition is open-loop maintenance. Tending is closed-loop: the tended system's changing condition must be able to revise the tender's next resolution. Sol's word for this is answerability, and the etymology rewards it: answer is a sworn reply (§3).

**Answerability is not sufficient.** The counterfeit loop passes it. A recommender system is exquisitely answerable to what its user reveals, and uses that responsiveness to optimize extraction. So Tend requires orientation: the closed loop must serve the tended system's own viable gradients.

**Orientation is not sufficient either.** A tender can wrongly decide what another's viable gradient ought to be. Boundary Ethics supplies the companion structure, Care = Presence × Restraint, where presence includes active availability, attention, and responsiveness, and restraint keeps interpretive or power advantage from collapsing uncertainty prematurely.

> **Tend is closed-loop answerability oriented toward the other's maintained gradient, with enough restraint for the other's changing condition to revise the tender's model of what that gradient requires.**

Tend's earlier "delivery" sense, the stretch carrying something across, now splits between reciprocal coupling (§9) and emitter-side signs (§10). Both already have homes, so nothing is lost by moving Tend to the organizational layer. The etymological tie between attend and tend is preserved without requiring every substrate capable of attentional selection to display care. At minimal scales attention may be differential allocation of access alone; at richer relational scales the same mechanics can be organized into Tend.

**Other organizations.** Inquiry organizes the mechanics around unanswered distinctions. Vigilance organizes them around possible change. Contemplation organizes them around sustained hold with minimal reach. Joint attention coordinates them across systems. Learning organizes them so that precipitation is steered. Institutional and cultural attention coordinate mechanics and terrain across many systems at once (§13). None of these needs to become a primitive operator.

**Load-bearing:** Tend as organization rather than step; the open/closed loop distinction; orientation as the clause that excludes the counterfeit loop. **Hold loosely:** the exact wording of the restraint clause.

## 12. Admission and localization

### 12.1 The criterion

A first admission criterion, "finite differential allocation among relational possibilities in a state- or history-sensitive way," was too broad. Markets allocate capital, cells allocate ATP, governments allocate budgets, rivers distribute flow. Admitting all of that would be another Great Flattening.

The corrective is to shift the object of allocation from resources to access:

> **A process is attention-like insofar as it dynamically and differentially modulates which resolvable or potentially resolvable differences can influence a finite system's ongoing resolution, in a manner sensitive to current state, history, or context.**

An earlier refinement proposed "allocation of sensitivity, what can register." Sol's objection holds: attention can operate after registration, on memories, ideas, and interpretations of already-perceived sentences. "Effective access to closure" is broader and more accurate than sensory registration.

**Tests.** Is there meaningful differential sensitivity? Are multiple possible differences available? Does the mechanism alter which differences can influence resolution? Is that modulation state-, context-, or history-sensitive? Is it more than downstream resource mobilization? Can the distinction be localized at the claimed scale?

The criterion requires neither consciousness, neurons, nor human-like behavior, and does not make every selective process attention. It is provisional.

### 12.2 Boundary matrix

| Case | Reading |
|---|---|
| A market allocating capital | Not sufficient: downstream resource allocation |
| A market selectively amplifying some information into price formation while filtering other information | Potentially attention-like at an emergent level; requires an identified mechanism |
| An immune system mobilizing cells after detection | Response, not automatically attention |
| An immune system altering which antigenic differences gain effective access to detection and response machinery | Strong candidate |
| A plant following a fixed chemical gradient | Probably directed regulation |
| A plant changing its sensitivity among competing cues by state or history, thereby changing which cue governs subsequent resolution | Increasingly plausible attentional organization |
| A human turning toward a bang | Pull influencing access and resolution |
| A human deliberately ignoring the phone while reading | Reach plus Gate/Ward modifying effective access |

**The immune system as adversarial test.** It has differential receptor sensitivity, thresholding, amplification, suppression, competition, and history-dependent future sensitivity (clonal selection and expansion change what the system will respond to later). The question stays local: where, if anywhere, does an attentional access-control pattern occur within immune regulation? Some mechanisms will qualify well; others are detection, memory, amplification, or response. Systems do not receive metaphysical merit badges. The same discipline governs plant, cellular, swarm, institutional, and computational cases.

### 12.3 Clocks and the pollinator evidence

"The flower can't attend" was category freezing (Proxy Localization's undefined Q settled early) at mammal clock speed and resolution. Measured floral responses sit on clocks the bee does not share:

**Seconds.** Flowers carry a negative potential and bees acquire a positive charge in flight. A bee's approach and landing shift the flower's electric field, beginning before contact by electrostatic induction, and the shift persists for a time. Bumblebees detect and discriminate these fields and use the post-visit change to avoid recently visited flowers (Clarke and colleagues, 2013).

**Minutes.** Beach evening primrose flowers exposed to bee wingbeat recordings or matching low frequencies raised nectar sugar concentration within about three minutes; silence and higher frequencies did nothing, and flowers with petals removed did not respond, so the flower itself acts as the ear (Veits, Hadany, and colleagues, 2019). Read through the mechanics, frequency selectivity is a gate, the sweetened nectar is something like reciprocal tend, and higher visitation closes the loop.

**Minutes to hours.** Changes in color, shape, and humidity signal depleted or pollinated flowers.

**Evolutionary time.** Co-lean between plant and pollinator lineages.

The Venus flytrap is visible to us as attending because its clock is one we resolve. From recall (Böhm and colleagues, 2016): a first trigger-hair touch starts a decaying memory, a second within roughly half a minute closes the trap, and continued stimulation counts toward committing to digestion. That is hold as a decaying rate, pick as threshold closure, and an integration window that treats a single raindrop as noise. Plants also reach at growth rate: phototropism is a stem stretching toward light.

The operators need a coupling with the right clocks, not an agent. **"Can X attend?" becomes a localization question: at which layers does X run which operations, and does the pattern pass the admission criterion there?** Attention is treated as unresolved rather than absent until the criterion is applied at the right resolution.

**Load-bearing:** access rather than resources as the object of allocation; localization rather than category verdicts. **Hold loosely:** the flytrap details; the exact reading of each floral response against the criterion.

## 13. Group attention and the complex regime

**Two and three.** Two parties close a loop and feedback begins, but a dyad can stay low-dimensional: it can laminate, settle into rhythm, and remain legible. Three parties reach generic irreducibility. Physics has the same step (the two-body problem has closed-form solutions; the three-body problem generically does not). Sociology found it independently: Simmel's triad introduces mediation, the third who profits from conflict, and divide-and-rule, and the group acquires a character independent of any one member; Bowen called the triangle the smallest stable unit of an emotional system because a stressed dyad pulls in a third. The triad is also where joint attention appears. The minimal structure for a shared world and the minimal structure for generic irreducibility may be the same structure: a specific, low-prior overlap worth marking.

**Combinatorics.** Directed pairwise attention links grow as n(n−1). Their on/off configurations grow as 2^(n(n−1)), roughly 10^27 at n = 10 before any weighting. Attention can also target attention, which adds depth to breadth (Keynes's beauty contest: choosing not what one finds beautiful but what one expects others to expect others to choose).

**Size is not what makes Zone 3.** A gas has an enormous state space and is statistically well behaved. What tips attention into irreducibility is that attention is signal: every configuration reshapes the pull landscape for everyone else, so the space rewrites itself as it is explored. Working formula: **Zone 3 ≈ combinatorics × reflexivity.**

**Zone 3 was defined through attention.** The Start Here document describes Zone 3 as attention-sensitive, with feedback loops making prediction impossible and participation necessary, and the observer's focus entangled with the system's evolution. A possible restatement: the regime where attention loops are dense enough that no position sits outside every loop. That lines up with EDF: a would-be determiner stops being external once it has to run the substrate.

**Complex Causality as Zone 3's mechanism.** Zone 3 names a regime by its epistemic signature (no shortcut; the process must be run). Complex Causality names the mechanism: causation intertwined enough that cause and effect cannot be traced cleanly in isolation, with deformation, commitment, propagation, and conditions promoted into terrain that becomes the next causes. Each of Zone 3's listed traits has a mechanism there: scale-crossing causation is condition promotion; path-dependence and memory are stabilized deformation; emergence through constraint is basins shaped by accumulated terrain. Relational density alone may not constitute an irreducibly complex regime; CC is what makes Zone 3 what it is.

**The zone ladder gets a ceiling.** The ladder ran from sparse (noise, dissipation) through structured (computable) to rich (irreducible), as though more density only meant more creativity. The causality synthesis adds a ceiling: above TCL's chaos ceiling, high coupling plus delay makes the cause-effect map decohere. That separates **forward irreducibility** (cannot be predicted ahead) from **backward attributability** (can be traced afterward). Zone 3 proper is forward-irreducible but still traceable: the top band of the operating window. Above the ceiling both fail, and dense Zone 3 degrades into a second kind of noise, from overload rather than sparseness. Mass reflexive attention with delay crosses that ceiling, attribution fails, and blame lands on the most visible low-power node rather than on the deformation source, as the causality synthesis predicts. An algorithmic feed is reshape causation applied to attention topology: it changes who can attend to whom, the parametric-wall regime of causal illegibility.

**Institutions as attention-topology constraints.** Much of what institutions do is collapse attention topology to keep groups in Zone 2. A stage with rows of seats, turn-taking rules, a chain of command, and a liturgy each forbid most of the possible configurations. Part of what CFA called Constraint is this.

**Zone 1's analog.** Reaches that leave no trace and receive no return, where nothing precipitates and each episode dissipates.

**Shared terrain and stigmergy.** Group attention need not imply a group mind with one giant spotlight. Individual mechanics can run over shared terrain. A desire path is the model: each walker makes local picks, repeated picks deform the ground, the deformation changes the cost landscape for later walkers.

```
many local Picks → shared terrain → correlated future Lean
```

The path is not literally everyone's Lean; it is shared terrain capable of producing similarly oriented Lean across many systems. Stigmergy then becomes **coordination produced when traces of prior local activity reshape shared terrain and thereby alter future local resolution.** The flower's post-visit electric field is a small instance: bee A changes the flower, and the changed flower tells bee B. The Emergence Engine is a simulated instance: agents read trail density, and frustration rises when trails run thin. Stigmergic attention may be triadic joint attention with two substitutions: the jointly picked third term made persistent, and "knowing that the other attends" replaced by reading the trace. On that reading, triadic and stigmergic attention are one structure at two resolutions, synchronous with awareness held in the parties, or asynchronous with awareness held in the medium.

Group attention then emerges from three interacting layers: individual mechanics, shared terrain, and organizations that coordinate or reshape both. Institutions, rituals, interfaces, recommendation systems, architecture, language, schedules, ranking systems, and norms all function as shared terrain. The power question follows immediately: **who gets to reshape the common terrain through which everyone else's attention resolves?** That actor need not command attention directly. Changing the hill is often easier than ordering every trajectory.

**Load-bearing:** Zone 3 as combinatorics times reflexivity; CC as Zone 3's mechanism; the ceiling and the forward/backward split; group attention as mechanics on shared terrain. **Hold loosely:** "no position outside every loop" as a Zone 3 criterion; triadic and stigmergic as one structure; the Zone 1 analog.

## 14. Power and the access economy

**The attention economy is an access economy.** "Pay attention" treats access as a resource, which is precisely the category error the admission criterion catches. Attention is not a transferable commodity, but attentional access is scarce, competitive, and consequential. What advertisers, platforms, propagandists, teachers, entertainers, institutions, friends, children, alarms, and predators compete for is privileged access to another system's resolution dynamics.

> **The attention economy trades in opportunities to influence what receives access to human closure processes.**

"Pay attention" still makes sense for a different reason: sustained access has costs (metabolic effort, opportunity cost, interference, exclusion, time, maintenance burden). What is paid is the cost of keeping some relation privileged within finite closure dynamics.

**Attention and power approach without becoming identical.** The power synthesis (PRFC) defines power through capacity to influence what gets repeated and describes deposited power becoming terrain that attracts further deposition. Attention concerns access to resolution. Power is broader: durable capacity to shape relational trajectories, repetition, routing, or conditions. Some power operates through attention and some does not; a locked door shapes behavior whether or not anyone finds it salient. But cognitive and social power often operate by gaining privileged access to others' closure processes.

**The asymmetry.** The powerful actor's access to the less powerful may become terrain, while the reverse access must be won episode by episode. One party must monitor; the other is already structurally present. One must reach upward continuously; the other arrives as standing pull. Fiske's finding that attention flows up the power gradient gets a structural reading: the less powerful must keep their closure processes open to the powerful, who need not reciprocate because their access is already part of the terrain.

> **Structural attentional power is durable privileged access to others' closure dynamics.**

This is one specialization of power, not a replacement definition.

**Load-bearing:** the access-economy reframe; structural attentional power as a specialization of PRFC.

## 15. Primitive decomposition and the RS reading

`attention*` is kept as a closure mark rather than retired. Unlike cost, the root is structurally accurate (*ad-tendere*, stretch toward); the hollowing lives in the idioms ("pay" imports *pacare* and settlement, "span" imports a stock). Its facets along the PBR basis:

**attention_O (Ontological).** A held relation, not a stock or faculty. It exists only while stretched, which is AMM's "exist = run the maintenance loop" in miniature. There is no attention without an attended. Even the stock-word "span" is a stretch-word under a different root, *(s)pen-*.

**attention_D (Dynamical).** A process under decay and habituation. Pull and reach run on different clocks; hold is the slow layer regulating the fast; terrain is slower still. Lamination, in TCL's sense.

**attention_G (Geometric/Causal).** Directional and aperture-bearing, with a breadth-depth trade set by focus. Causally, attention modulates access to closure, so where attention goes shapes where flow becomes consequential. It deforms landscapes, its own and others'.

**attention_S (Symmetric/Constraint).** Finite. Attenuation trades reach against thickness. Neglect is the opportunity-cost shadow of every pick. Typically asymmetric, and who attends to whom is a power reading. Mutual regard is the symmetric special case.

**attention_E (Epistemic/Informational).** Attention governs which resolved differences gain access to closure, and so which partition of signal and noise a system acts on. Noticing contains knowing (*notitia*, from *noscere*). Resolution limits bound what can be attended at all.

**attention_M (Meta-relational).** Intention is reach represented reflexively; attention to attention. Third parties can engineer another's pull. Engineered pull named as one's own preference is EDF substitution.

**The RS reading.** RS's three intake pathologies map directly. Under-resolution is distraction: the stretch pulled asunder, nothing picked. Over-resolution is fixation and capture: a pick held past usefulness, terrain frozen. Wrong-axis resolution is misdirected reach: sharp allocation on a difference that is not carrying signal, which engineered salience manufactures at scale. RS's prior-dissolution requirement becomes Release, and the etymology makes the kinship explicit: release and resolve are both "loosen again." The mechanics cycle Resolve → Sustain → Release → Resolve is RS's dissolution and recombination running at the scale of one episode.

**Remainder terms.** The neglected (what fell outside allocation), the attenuated (what reach stretched too thin to hold), the watched (the attended's burden under surveillance), and unattended flow (unsigned, unrouted).

## Final compression

A finite system lives inside attentional terrain shaped by prior relation, development, embodiment, inheritance, culture, and precipitation. That terrain is its **Lean**. In any episode, **Alert** sets general readiness. Differences bid for access through **Pull**; the system issues its own bids through **Reach**; both act on Lean rather than on neutral ground. **Gate**, with **Ward** as its suppressive pole, modulates which bids can effectively compete. **Pick** resolves the field into an allocation of variable aperture and multiplicity. **Hold** sustains it against decay and interference. **Release** dissolves it so the field can resolve again.

Each episode changes more than behavior. Through learning, habituation, sensitization, practice, capture, relationship, and environmental modification, its consequences precipitate into future terrain: not only direction but a control policy. Attention both follows and forms its conditions. Returned, the line becomes a loop, and loops make closure, recursion, and shared worlds possible; dense and reflexive enough, they make Zone 3. Attention is never pushed and never becomes signal; it acquires signs, and others' attention resolves through the field those signs alter. Across longer clocks the mechanics organize into inquiry, vigilance, joint attention, and **Tend**: restrained, closed-loop answerability oriented toward another's maintained gradient.

> **Attention is the dynamic differential modulation of effective access to a finite system's resolution and closure processes. It operates through episode-level pressures and gates on historically formed terrain, sustains selected differences against decay, releases them for re-resolution, and recursively participates in forming the terrain through which later attention will operate.**
