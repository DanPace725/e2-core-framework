## Claim limits

- “Resonance” means a proposed metaphor for perceived or negotiated fit / mutual comprehensibility in a particular interaction. It is not a physical field, frequency, force, or mechanism here.
- Do not equate shared coherence with truth. Any claim still needs the evidence standards appropriate to its domain.
- Contextual or relational knowledge does not make all truths subjective, co-created, or equally warranted.
- SCIA is not established as a causal threshold for meaning, truth, bonding, or phase transitions.
- Paradox tolerance means keeping apparent incompatibilities available for inquiry; it does not make contradictions jointly true.
- RAV and threshold design are conceptual prompts only, not validated measures or substitutes for explicit boundaries and consent.
- The dashboard is a historical design proposal only.
- No consciousness, subjective-experience, universal physics, or biological-law conclusion follows from this family.
