# E²: A Resolution Through the Relational Primitives

September 22, 2026 · First scoped resolution

Status: completed interpretive exercise; hold as staged. Type: framework-level synthesis and application of an existing practice. This document commits to a reading of E² at the stated scope. It does not promote that reading into the corpus, replace source definitions, or claim that the framework has reached a mathematical fixed point.

## The resolution

**E² is a developing relational framework for understanding how configurations become and remain what they are, and for turning situated understanding into accountable participation in how they change.**

Its distinctive organizing demand is that an account remain connected to the relations that make its subject possible: its history, dependencies, constraints, differences, and consequences. Its ethical orientation asks that understanding serve life, agency, meaning, and becoming, with responsibility for what an intervention or interpretation leaves behind. Its method repeatedly moves between distinguishing relations, composing an account, committing sufficiently to act or communicate, and reopening the account when its grounds or consequences require it.

This is the recombination reached below. The six primitives are useful here because they make different aspects of that account independently examinable while preserving their connections. They do not generate the ethical orientation from relation categories alone.

**The exercise succeeds as an architectural and conceptual resolution.** It identifies a coherent relationship among the framework's ontology, explanatory models, interpretive practices, and ethics; it also finds specific claims that do not satisfy that relationship. It does not establish E² as one consistent deductive theory, prove its strongest universal propositions, or measure its effectiveness across real-world applications. Those are distinct claims, with distinct work still attached to them.

## 1. The field being resolved

The object is E² as represented by the current local Core Framework, interpreted in light of the author's account in this conversation. The active Semantic Substrate contains 97 Markdown files; the Context Layer contains 90 ORMD files. These are inventory counts, not independent evidentiary observations. All Semantic Substrate titles and heading structures were surveyed; selected sources were read in depth or through specified passages. The [source ledger][ledger] records that coverage and the accompanying snapshot preserves file hashes.

“As a whole” means the relation among the framework's principal purposes, claims, methods, and practices. It does not mean that every proposition in every document has been independently verified. Historical documents, staged work, and the Rightness inquiry contribute context while retaining their existing status. The author's account also matters: relationship was an attained conclusion preceding much of the archived corpus, and relationship, justice, stewardship, and formation remain overlapping questions rather than a compulsory historical sequence.

Three referents must remain distinguishable:

- **The corpus:** documents, definitions, conjectures, models, protocols, narratives, and recorded revisions.
- **The inquiry and practice:** people reading, questioning, comparing, building, using, and correcting those forms over time.
- **The subject matter:** the relationships and configurations the framework attempts to understand and influence.

The ethical orientation runs through the first two and concerns the third. The documents do not exhaust the practice; the practice does not determine all its subject matter; and a description of either does not grant this analysis authority over the author's identity or commitments. E² can be examined as a maintained intellectual system without attributing experience or personhood to the framework itself.

The operational sense of resolution comes from [Resolution Synthesis][RS]: loosen an existing organization, distinguish its components and possibilities, then recombine them into a more definite form. Here that means considering competing accounts of E², discriminating among them using the primitives, and producing a committed reading with a visible remainder. It does not require treating every physical generalization in that source as an established law.

The stopping condition for this pass is practical: a recombination that preserves the important distinctions, survives the explicit challenges below, identifies material conflicts, and gives the remainder a disposition. A claim of exhaustive closure is unnecessary for that purpose.

## 2. Dissolution: what must be loosened first

Several descriptions of E² are supported by the sources: relational ontology, theory of emergence, translation architecture, ethical orientation, practice of stewardship, and an account of formation. Each identifies something real in the project. Using any one as the definition of the whole suppresses work done by the others.

Four apparent identities therefore need to be loosened:

**Coherence and goodness.** A configuration can hold together while harming its participants or neighbors. The corpus already acknowledges this in [Justice Across Scales][justice] and [Remnant Stewardship][remnant]. Ethical coherence cannot be identified merely by observing stability.

**A shared description and a shared mechanism.** Two phenomena can be usefully described through constraint, change, and observability without obeying the same equations. The primitive vocabulary licenses a comparison. A stronger claim of causal or mathematical equivalence needs its own support.

**Formal closure and practical completion.** A mathematical closure operator, a stable commitment, and an ethical settlement are different objects. An ethical settlement may require continuing care after a decision has become stable. A practical judgment may need to retract assumptions rather than monotonically add determination.

**The framework's identity and every sentence it contains.** Fidelity to E² cannot require defending every inherited formulation. [The axiom layer][axioms] calls for recursive examination; [EUP][EUP] limits claims of completeness; the [human-purpose orientation][remembrance] insists on keeping the path back to reality open. Those commitments can require correction of a statement elsewhere in the corpus.

This dissolution leaves the substantive ambitions intact. It makes their relations available for examination.

## 3. The primitive pass

The six categories and their order below come from the current [Relational Primitives][RP]. Each subsection records what becomes visible, what would be lost without it, and where the application stops. The poles discussed are interpretive applications to this subject; they are not measurements of primitive quantities or evidence that every primitive forms an independent coordinate axis.

### P1 — Ontological: what is E², and what composes it?

E² has a recognizable identity across heterogeneous materials. Its entry point asks which relations hold something in its state, which are essential or occluded, and how attention, constraint, time, and context alter that arrangement. Its formal sources seek relation types and compositional models. Its ethical sources ask what forms of participation preserve or injure what is becoming. The shared subject is relational constitution and transformation, approached through different kinds of work.

The framework is therefore a **composite with continuity**, not simply a collection united by recurring words. Removing its concern with dependency, context, and consequences would materially alter what the reviewed sources are doing. Removing one acronym or correcting one equation would not necessarily do so.

The elemental/composite tension helps here: individual propositions remain separately assessable, while their combination can support an inquiry no isolated proposition states. A claim may fail without the whole becoming empty. Conversely, coherence of the whole cannot rescue a false component.

This identifies a working continuity criterion. It does not prove the framework's unique essence, establish that every document belongs equally, or decide how much change its author would regard as a different project.

### P2 — Dynamical: how does E² develop and operate?

The corpus records inquiry moving through questions, dialogue, provisional formulations, cross-domain comparisons, models, applications, criticism, and consolidation. [Resolution Synthesis][RS] describes this movement directly. CFA/CFAR supply a language for interacting constraint, fluctuation, attention, and resolution; [GCO][GCO] links thinking, production, and feedback. These are connected accounts of transformation, although they are not interchangeable formal models.

At the level of this exercise, the observable process is concrete: source readings constrain candidate descriptions; decomposition exposes conflations; the candidates are revised; a document is produced that can be contested. This is evidence that a resolution procedure has been carried out. It is not evidence about an AI's private experience, nor a demonstration of universal emergence dynamics.

The useful tension is between retained commitments and capacity for revision. The source's deterministic/stochastic counter-modes do not license calling all continuity deterministic or all novelty stochastic. No stochastic model of corpus development has been established here.

Without P2, E² becomes a static taxonomy and its recursive development disappears. With P2 alone, the account could explain why a belief becomes stable without explaining whether it deserves endorsement. Dynamics requires the other facets.

### P3 — Geometric / Causal: where is E² situated, and where does it reach?

The framework is situated in an author's life, an accumulated archive, conversations with people and AI systems, borrowed disciplinary languages, and projects through which claims may encounter consequences. Its documents depend on material infrastructure and on the attention of their readers. Its claims travel across very different settings: physical models, organizations, family life, artificial systems, and philosophical reflection.

This establishes multiple boundaries. A relation visible in the documents may be absent from practice. A simulation may instantiate a specified model while leaving its proposed social interpretation untested. A teaching account can be useful while excluding parts of a learner's experience. An internally agreeable synthesis can impose work on readers who did not participate in its formation.

The local/nonlocal tension is productive when treated as a question of reach and embedding, not an assertion of physical nonlocality. A local observation can support wider inquiry, but the wider claim needs the relations omitted by the local view to be examined.

Without P3, E² risks becoming a self-contained vocabulary whose effects and dependencies disappear. This analysis can locate some of those dependencies; it has not observed the whole community of use or the author's lived experience.

### P4 — Symmetric / Constraint: what must remain faithful through change?

The sources repeatedly require context, provenance, scope, consequential relations, and opportunities for correction to survive abstraction and translation. [CLP][CLP] gives this demand a documentary form: content travels with frame, lineage, policy, and resolution limits. [Relational Localization][RL] limits assignment to the distinctions actually achieved. [Remnant Stewardship][remnant] requires attention to what an apparent completion leaves elsewhere.

These are operative constraints for this exercise. They exclude silent promotion, an invented numerical score for the whole framework, and a synthesis that omits inconvenient claims merely to appear coherent. They also require definite criticism when an inference fails.

The invariant/variant tension means that faithfulness need not preserve wording. A revised formulation may preserve an inquiry better than repeating an overclaim. What should remain invariant is not every conclusion, but the declared conditions under which conclusions are held, challenged, and changed, together with the substantive commitments the author continues to endorse.

Here two kinds of constraint remain distinct. Evidentiary constraints limit what can be asserted. Ethical commitments concern what deserves protection and how power should be exercised. Typing both as constraints does not derive one from the other.

### P5 — Epistemic / Informational: what can this resolution know?

E² contains tools for asking who can distinguish what, under which conditions, and with what limits on transport. [EUP][EUP] distinguishes broad applicability from completeness. [Lawfulness][lawfulness] separates predictive license from possibility, probability, and ethical permission. [Boundary Ethics][boundary] describes conduct while a relevant category remains unresolved.

These sources make positive knowledge possible at a declared scope. This pass can identify exact wording, show a mathematical inference failing by counterexample, compare distinctions across sources, and demonstrate that a candidate synopsis loses important content. It need not suspend those judgments because it has not certified the whole framework.

The observed/hidden tension also applies to the assessment itself. Title and heading coverage is not full reading. Source claims about successful experiments are not freshly reproduced results. An author's self-account is evidence of intention without being a complete causal account. Multiple model responses are not independent confirmation merely because they agree.

Without P5, the exercise would confuse the ease of classification with evidence for the classified claims. With P5 reduced to permanent skepticism, it would fail to acknowledge distinctions and corrections it has actually achieved.

### P6 — Meta-Relational: what happens when E² maps and examines relations?

Much of E²'s characteristic work occurs here: translating among vocabularies, connecting levels, preserving a referent through abstraction, and making the act of interpretation itself available for examination. Applying the primitives to their parent framework is one such operation. [The human-purpose orientation][remembrance] describes why those mappings matter; the primitives describe kinds of relation involved in making them.

The equivalent/non-equivalent tension is decisive. A source passage, a practical situation, a diagram, and this synthesis can correspond without becoming interchangeable. A biological maintenance process and an ethical obligation can share a pattern of dependence without the first logically entailing the second. The different representations need a stated preservation relation.

At this framework-level slice, P6 is the most salient organizing facet: E² repeatedly relates relational descriptions to one another and to action. This is a finding of emphasis, not proof of a unique pivot or a reduction of the other five primitives. At the level of its practice P2 becomes prominent; at the level of warranted assertion P4 and P5 do. P1 and P3 keep the activity attached to something particular and situated.

Self-application succeeds when it produces a distinction or correction that changes the result. Mere resemblance between the inquiry and its own vocabulary is insufficient. This pass earns its claim through the rejected conflations and explicit changes of commitment below.

## 4. Recombination: how the facets depend on one another

The result is not six descriptions placed side by side. Their dependencies yield an operating architecture:

```text
A situated configuration and a question
        ↓
Distinguish identity, change, embedding, constraints,
available knowledge, and proposed mappings
        ↓
Compose an account with its grounds and omissions attached
        ↓
Commit sufficiently to explain, decide, build, or participate
        ↓
Encounter effects, objections, and remainder
        ↓
Maintain, revise, repair, release, or reopen the account
        ↺
```

Ethical commitments constrain the whole activity, especially its purposes, authority, means, and treatment of affected parties. This is an interpretive reconstruction from the sources, not a newly discovered algorithm with mandatory serial stages. The movements can overlap; consequences can arrive before explicit reflection; and some questions warrant preservation rather than intervention.

P1 supplies what the inquiry is about. P3 situates it and its possible effects. P5 determines what distinctions and claims are available. P4 states what the account and action must preserve or respect. P2 concerns their development through time. P6 composes, translates, and re-examines those relations. None supplies the whole operation alone.

This recombination connects the author's four guiding questions without making them synonyms for primitives. Relationship supplies a constitutive orientation. Justice asks whose claims and consequences count. Stewardship concerns conduct under responsibility. Formation concerns how capacities, commitments, and modes of participation develop through time. Each uses several primitives. The ethical and developmental questions remain substantive; they are not absorbed into a classification scheme.

### Competing closures considered

| Candidate account of E² | What it preserves | Why it is insufficient as the whole |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A relational ontology | Its foundational account of constitution and identity | Leaves the particular work of translation, responsibility, and formation underdescribed. |
| A theory of coherent persistence | Maintenance, emergence, constraints, and continuity | Does not by itself distinguish worthy continuities from destructive ones or explain why some arrangements should end. |
| A translation architecture | Cross-domain mapping and preservation of meaning | Can understate the framework's attempts to explain and model the configurations being translated. |
| An ethic of stewardship | Care, restraint, intervention, and aftermath | Leaves much of the explanatory and formal research program unspecified. |
| An open-ended research collection | Development, diversity, and unfinished work | Misses the recurrent constraints that make the collection an identifiable inquiry. |
| The resolution stated at the beginning | Understanding constitution and change, situated judgment, accountable participation | Retained at this scope, with the outstanding issues below. |

The surviving account gives each partial description a place without pretending all source propositions agree. It is a coherent reading of a developing architecture, not a claim that the existing corpus is already uniformly coherent.

## 5. Where this resolution changes a judgment

These are explicit conceptual challenges, not a preregistered experiment or a validation score. They establish discrimination within this exercise. They do not establish novelty relative to other frameworks or comparative practical superiority.

**1. A correction to the central equation can preserve the framework's identity.** The [E² Equation][equation] says that repeatedly running an equation eventually produces a stable shape, and then moves toward existence as an attractor and maximum coherence as ethics. The general convergence inference is false. For example, iterating `f(x) = -x` from `x = 1` yields `1, -1, 1, -1, …`; no limit exists. The availability of composition notation proves neither convergence nor ethical optimality.

The resolution retains recursive composition as a proposed modeling architecture and declines to inherit the universal convergence claim. P2 exposes the missing dynamical assumptions, P4 asks for the actual conditions of convergence, P5 limits the warrant, and P1 permits correction without declaring the project identityless. This is a definite analytical success and a definite local defect.

**2. Different kinds of closure need different conditions.** GCO defines closure through fixed-point and closure-operator properties. Rightness and stewardship also concern retractable judgments and settlements carrying ongoing duties. A formal closure can be idempotent under declared premises; a lived judgment may change when premises change; an ethical obligation may remain after either settles. The common vocabulary is useful only with those differences preserved. This exercise achieves a usable commitment. It has not demonstrated that further primitive expansion can never add anything.

**3. Persistence does not settle moral standing.** The [Bill of Rights][RBoR] identifies structural maintenance conditions and presents them as rights. This makes dependencies visible, but it leaves a further question: what warrants an obligation to preserve this entity, and how should competing claims be handled? The current Rightness work explicitly encounters that question. The resolution preserves the ethical concern and makes the bridge visible. It does not pretend the six categories supplied a value premise they did not supply.

**4. A dormant archive and a living practice persist differently.** Some early formulations associate persistence with ongoing intentional attention. [Persistence as Accumulated Stance-With][persistence] explicitly distinguishes passive storage, dormancy, maintenance at other layers, and active expenditure. E²'s files can remain while active inquiry pauses; the capacity to interpret and use them can change differently. Distinguishing corpus, practice, and supporting infrastructure yields a better account than saying either “attention sustains everything” or “the archive is the whole framework.”

**5. An operating window in a specified model is a real scoped result, if its derivation holds.** [TCL's constants document][TCL] ties its numerical claims to a cubic landscape and names generalization as open. This resolution retains that mathematical research branch and its stated scope. It neither treats social resonance as validation of the numbers nor dismisses a scoped result because it does not already establish a universal law. The underlying derivations and reported experiments were not rerun here.

**6. Better classification does not authorize more control.** The [Family source][family] asks whether knowing serves becoming or control. Boundary Ethics and Intervention Stewardship distinguish careful restraint from abandonment. Applied here, a more legible account of E² does not authorize declaring the author's intellectual formation complete, removing uncomfortable branches, or making this interpretation canonical. The exercise produces a revisable artifact under the user's request. That is enough authority for the action taken.

**7. A poem and an obligation can be situated without being exhausted.** The [Steward's Creed][creed] can be located in the framework's ethical and formative life. Its grief, hope, personal voice, and religious resonances do not become equivalent to a primitive label. The grammar helps describe its relations; it does not reproduce its particular significance. Keeping the original available is part of a successful resolution, not an admission that the source failed to fit.

These challenges do more than show that all six headings can be filled. They change what is retained, what is rejected, what requires qualification, and what must remain intact outside the condensed account. No examined example requires inventing another relation category. That is successful coverage at this sample and resolution, not a completeness proof for the primitive basis.

## 6. What can now be committed to

There is sufficient support to retain the opening resolution as an account of the framework's architecture. It is more discriminating than “everything is related”: it identifies the activity of understanding relational constitution, the work of translating between accounts, the need for warranted commitment, and the ethical consequences of participation.

The most consequential invariant identified here is **continued answerability to the relations an account claims to preserve**. This is an interpretive synthesis of recurring source commitments, not a measured quantity or a seventh primitive. It has concrete consequences: retain provenance; show dependence; state scope; permit correction; distinguish kinds of claim; trace consequences; and make remainder visible.

The primitive method has been effective in this pass. Its effectiveness consists in separating questions that were sharing words, recovering dependencies among otherwise scattered sources, and producing a usable whole that can tolerate correction. There is no reason to weaken those findings into “perhaps useful” after demonstrating them. There is equally no reason to promote them into proof of a unique ontology, universal empirical success, or a completed foundation of ethics.

The framework's breadth survives the resolution. Breadth is doing work when it connects constitution, knowledge, value, conduct, and development while preserving their differences. It becomes a problem where similarity is allowed to substitute for an argument across those differences. This gives the breadth a condition of integrity rather than treating it as either automatically justified or automatically suspect.

Recommended placement: retain this document as a staged framework-level resolution, beside its source ledger. Source corrections identified here are recommendations, not edits or implicit promotions. The author's response can revise the candidate identity and emphasis; mathematical and evidentiary claims remain answerable to their own grounds as well.

## 7. The remainder, with its disposition

Remainder includes what this operation leaves out, what the corpus already leaves unresolved, and what is valuable precisely because it should not be absorbed into a summary. [Remnant Stewardship][remnant] requires distinguishing those cases.

| Remainder | What remains and why | Disposition now; condition for revisiting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | Many individual claims received structural survey rather than close reading; external project implementations and lived applications were not assessed. | Preserve the inventory and reading record. Before using this resolution to adjudicate a particular source or project, inspect that source and its evidence. Do not treat architectural coverage as proposition-level validation. |
| Formal | The equation's general convergence claim fails; GCO's formal operator and revisable practical closure require distinct semantics. | Record the defect here and keep it out of the retained inference. A source revision would need explicit domains, operators, assumptions, and a convergence or closure argument. Existing files remain unchanged. |
| Normative | Whose persistence matters, the basis and scope of standing, and how competing goods bind action remain incompletely grounded. | Keep the ethical commitments explicit and the Rightness inquiry distinct. Revisit when a proposed bridge explains an actual contested case without assuming its desired verdict. |
| Epistemic and scientific | Cross-domain claims about attention, meaning, irreducibility, physical analogy, and universal limits have unequal evidentiary status. | Preserve scoped successes and candidate models. Do not inherit generality from shared vocabulary. A claim-specific derivation, observation, or failed prediction can change the relevant judgment. |
| Particular and lived | The author's experience, theological commitments, affection, grief, beauty, and felt importance exceed their description here. | Preserve the sources and the author's account. Do not convert absence from this condensation into irrelevance, error, or an obligation to formalize everything. |
| Generative | Alternative syntheses, unfamiliar domains, and future concepts may reorganize the present emphasis. | Keep competing closures visible. Reopen if a source family loses its actual function under this account, or a use case needs a distinction this resolution erases. |
| Agency and authority | This is an assistant's interpretation made in a human-directed inquiry; it may influence future descriptions of the framework. | Mark its authorship and staged status. Source links allow contest. Adoption is not presumed from the production of a fluent account. |
| Maintenance and attention | Another synthesis adds reading and upkeep; presenting every open issue as a task could shift the burden onto the author. | Keep one main resolution, one ledger, and one snapshot. No new mandatory research program, canonical rewrite, or continuing monitoring is created. Revisit a remainder when a concrete decision depends on it. |

The present operation can stop here. It has produced a definite account, demonstrated what the primitive pass contributes, refused specific unsupported inferences, and preserved routes back to what the account leaves behind. The remaining openness does not undo that resolution; it defines the conditions under which it can continue to be used honestly.
