## 0. Core Claim

> Attention is the dynamic differential modulation of which available differences gain effective access to a finite system's processes of resolution and closure. It runs through episode-level readiness, pressures, gates, allocations, persistence, and release on historically formed terrain; its consequences may help form that terrain for later episodes.

The claim is about a *function at a declared boundary and scale*. Energy, time, and other resources may fund or follow that function, but resource movement alone does not identify it. Conscious intention and care are possible organizations of attention, not entry requirements. A static sensor, a resource allocation, and a response after detection do not automatically qualify.

Short diagnostic: **Can the difference be distinguished? Can it matter to this resolution? What closes?** These are distinguishable questions, not a mandatory temporal pipeline. Attention can alter effective distinguishability, and an attentional Pick may itself be a local closure.

## 1. Corpus Position

The six Relational Primitives remain the framework's grammar. Resolution Synthesis describes narrowing, dissolution, and recombination more broadly; this candidate does not reduce resolution to sensory discrimination. Signal as Bias Field already locates agency partly in conditional uptake and modulation at the signal–closure boundary. Global Closure Operator names general closure and stabilization. This candidate names and organizes a particular way differences gain influence before, within, and across local closures; it does not add another GCO.

Asymmetry Maintenance gives the persistence cost of holding a useful distinction. Complex Causality gives the cause/effect/consequence/condition loop. Condition as Typed Terrain requires that any terrain claim specify a referent, observer position, resolution, evidence, and time state. Boundary Dynamics and Flow Operators describe interfaces and what crosses them. Boundary Ethics supplies restraint where attention becomes care. The concept is cross-cutting within these parents, not a seventh primitive or seventh flow.

AFD, CFA, and CFAR are especially important lineage. They use “Attention” for physical energy movement, aggregate focus/resources, and emitted salience respectively. The access definition here proposes a narrower, testable functional question. A later source must distinguish historical AFD/CFA/CFAR readings instead of silently treating them as synonyms.

## 2. An Admission Trace

Call a process **attention-like at a specified scale** only when the following can be identified or left explicitly unresolved:

1. **Boundary and candidates.** Which system or controller is being considered, and which competing differences are potentially available to it?
2. **Resolution process.** Which interpretation, action, selection, or other closure can these differences influence?
3. **Differential access.** What changes whether a candidate can influence that process, beyond simply moving resources or producing a downstream response?
4. **State dependence.** Does access vary with current state, history, context, or an identified controller? What would be different under a comparison condition?
5. **Evidence and grain.** At what spatial, organizational, and temporal scale was the access change observed, and what observation would disconfirm it?

The trace can return **supported at this scale**, **excluded at this scale**, or **unresolved**. A qualifying subsystem does not license a claim about every property of its containing system. This is a functional classification, not a consciousness, moral-standing, or subjectivity test. A fixed filter may implement a gate without being, by itself, an attention system.

## 3. Three Organizational Layers

**Episode mechanics** concern a current competition or resolution. **Terrain** is the relatively slow configuration that makes certain routes easier or harder before the current episode. **Organization** concerns repeated episodes structured around a larger relation or purpose. The layers are relative to the focal timescale: what is terrain for a moment can itself change over a day or a generation.

| Form | Role | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alert | Nondirectional readiness or gain condition | How available is this system for a difference to matter? |
| Pull | Priority bid not issued by the currently controlling loop | What calls for access from beyond that controller? |
| Reach | Controller-issued priority bid | What does it search for, scrutinize, or privilege? |
| Gate / Ward | Selective admission, with Ward as suppressive operation | What is admitted, amplified, attenuated, or refused? |
| Pick | Local allocation, possibly graded or plural | Which candidates become influential enough for this resolution? |
| Hold | Persistence of an allocation against decay or competition | What remains live, and at what cost? |
| Release | Dissolution of a current allocation | What becomes available for re-resolution? |
| Lean | Standing access disposition, read as terrain | What has already become easier to treat as relevant? |
| Push | Emitter-side act that changes another's field | What is emitted to another boundary? |
| Tend | Longer-lived, ethically oriented organization | Whose changing condition can revise the next response? |

These are functional distinctions, not a validated minimal list, compulsory progression, or one-to-one map onto the six primitives. The earlier readable grammar “Lean, Pull, Reach, Pick, Hold, Tend” remains useful if its heterogeneous levels are disclosed.

### Lean and Gate

Lean is a standing slope of relevance. Gate is a current admission operation. One can lean toward X while warding X during a task; one can deliberately open a gate to Y despite weak prior Lean. Repeated Gate changes can contribute to future Lean, along with reward, habit, embodiment, inherited dispositions, other people, and shared conditions. Ranking, permeability, and bias can coincide at a terrain-level description without being identical at the mechanism level. A claim of equivalence needs a stated coarse-graining and an intervention that could distinguish them.

### Ward, Release, and Redirect

Ward keeps a candidate from influential access. Release loosens an allocation already in force. Redirect favors another candidate. Similar visible behavior may have any of these causes. “Ignore the phone” does not identify its mechanism without a trace. Aversive Pull may coexist with bodily avoidance; negative weighting need not be an attentional push-away vector.

### Pick and GCO

Pick is the current allocation resolving enough to constrain downstream processing. It may itself be a local closure. GCO is the general principle governing closure across relational fields, including closures wider than an attentional episode. There need not be one master Pick preceding all other closure events.

### Hold, Release, and margin

Hold spends or relies on finite maintenance capacity. Insufficient Hold can lose a distinction before it can be integrated; excessive Hold can become fixation. Release is active reopening, not merely passive decay. AMM supplies the margin question: what maintains the useful difference, and what cheap closure appears when maintenance fails? No common physical unit of “margin” across all substrates is asserted here.

## 4. Formation: Episodes Can Change Future Access

Complex Causality provides the parent sequence: a deformation may close locally, propagate as consequence, and stabilize into condition. In the attention case, repeated episodes can change Lean, Gate thresholds, Alert readiness, search templates, Pull sensitivity, Hold duration, and Release difficulty. This is **formation of an access policy**, not only accumulation of preferred objects.

```text
candidate and current terrain
  -> differential access and local allocation
  -> consequence
  -> possible stabilization into later terrain
```

Not every episode precipitates. Promotion into terrain depends on the focal process, observer, evidence, duration, and maintenance. Conditions can become stale or be un-promoted. An apparently shared path, recommender ranking, institutional schedule, or habit is not literally every participant's inner Lean. It is a condition that may produce similarly directed Lean in different systems.

Pull and Reach are different possible origins of a bid. Their origin alone does not decide whether a resulting disposition is beneficial, endorsed, revisable, or exploitative. Provenance, present endorsement, beneficiary, reversibility, and displaced maintenance burden require separate traces. A later lean-provenance instrument would need controls for mixed and unknown histories.

## 5. Relation, Signs, and Tend

One-way attention, reciprocal causal coupling, mutual attention, and joint attention are different configurations. In reciprocal coupling, changes return without the second party necessarily attending. In mutual attention, each party attends to the other. In joint attention, parties also coordinate allocation around a third term. A feedback loop does not by itself establish care or shared awareness.

An outward act may leave a **cue or sign of attention**—gaze, pointing, a contingent response, a remembered act. A receiver may take up that artifact and be influenced. The source operation, its observable trace, and its effect on the receiver are distinct. The trace can be forged or can outlast the operation. Uptake establishes influence; credibility requires further evidence about how the sign was made and whether it still tracks the operation. A fleeting gaze need not meet the trace requirements of an accountability ledger.

**Tend** is a longer-lived organization of attention, not a required final step. It is closed-loop answerability oriented toward the tended relation's viable condition, with restraint enough for that condition to revise the tender's account. Bare responsiveness can serve extraction. Orientation without restraint can become paternalism or proxy capture. Boundary Ethics's `Care = Presence × Restraint` provides the adjacent posture. The tended party's gradients must be localized rather than invented by the one attending.

## 6. Relation to CFA, CFAR, and AFD

The older documents use `A` for several non-equivalent things. This candidate distinguishes at least these roles:

| Legacy usage | Reading under this candidate |
| --- | --- |
| AFD's energy movement toward fluctuations and attractors | A possible physical realization or supporting condition in a specified system; it is not enough by itself to establish differential access to resolution. AFD also discusses context-sensitive selection and persistence, so it cannot be reduced wholesale to Pull. |
| CFA's concentration of focus, energy, and cognitive resources | A useful historical aggregate for a coarse model. A focused allocation may be a proxy for access; measured resources alone do not determine the function or its target. |
| CFAR's campaigns, prompts, visibility, and social proof | Often emitter-side actions or environmental changes that attempt to induce Pull or change terrain for a recipient. The intervention is not identical to the recipient's attention. |
| Cognition-source “conscious navigation” | A possible reflexive Reach or organized use of attention, not the whole category. |

The old CFA equations may remain as a historical model of an aggregate `A` if its observables and scope are declared. Replacing `A` with this candidate's access function would require a new model and calibration. The CFAR Rayleigh analogy concerns the resolution a system can reliably sense and control; it does not by itself measure attentional access. CFAR's controller architecture remains a proposed practice layer with its own implementation and validation burden.

The active reconciled CFAR source now keeps AFD's field and pattern-formation questions, CFA's original equations and regime language, and CFAR's resolution and controller proposal together. It also makes three boundaries explicit that this candidate must retain at promotion: preferential physical flow is not yet attentional access; the historical scalar `A` is not a validated measure of access; and an emitter-side salience input does not set receiver-side attention. The candidate adds a finer functional account, rather than claiming to replace these older mechanisms or their evidence obligations.

## 7. Limits and Open Questions

- Which specific immune, plant, computational, and institutional mechanisms pass the admission trace, and which are only detection, regulation, response, or environmental trace?
- Can effective access be observed independently of resource spend, overt response, and the closure it helps cause?
- What formal model separates current Gate, inherited Lean, and environmental terrain over multiple clocks?
- When does consequence stabilize into policy, and what makes that policy contestable and revisable?
- How should multiple local Picks and later closures be represented without turning a functional distinction into a rigid pipeline?
- Which signs of attention can support trustworthy attribution, and which are only cues with possible influence?
- How can Tend's orientation be assessed without substituting the tender's preference for the other's account?
- What part of CFA's old `A` can be empirically mapped to this functional account, and what parts require separate variables?

The priority expression in the original note, group/Zone extensions, TCL parameter transfer, developmental sequence, etymological derivations, and the proposed SMFR integrity name remain research leads. The September 23 original and its contained integration review preserve their arguments and reservations. They are not premises required to accept this candidate's core claim.
