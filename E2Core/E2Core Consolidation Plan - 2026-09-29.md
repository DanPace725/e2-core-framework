## 1. Operating rules

1. **Extract, then archive.** For each item marked Extract/Archive or Merge/Archive, compare the actual source bodies, identify unique claims and useful provenance, place retained material in a named destination with its limits, then archive the superseded active document(s). Record what was retained, what was left historical, and why. An existing synthesis is evidence of earlier work, not proof that all unique content survived.
2. **Keep source status visible.** Active Semantic Substrate and Context Layer files, staged proposals, Synthesized Core, and archives have different authority. A staged theory enters Core only through a contained integration review and an explicit status decision under `AGENT_INTEGRATION_PROCESS.md`.
3. **Reconcile both representations.** Several June merges affected ORMD while human Markdown predecessors remain active. Review substantive differences before deciding the paired result. The current reader's human/ORMD review queue is a dependency; do not mechanically regenerate or discard human Markdown.
4. **Preserve lineage and remainder.** Every consolidation pass records source paths, successor paths, lineage links, claim changes, unresolved tensions, and any claims whose evidence is insufficient. A merger may retain distinct registers or subtypes rather than flattening them.
5. **Visible titles first.** Clean reader-facing headings and title metadata in a separate pass. Filename, anchor, and link changes follow only after a reference audit. Keep version and date information in provenance, not necessarily in a visible title.
6. **No numerical end-state target.** The June estimate of roughly 75 canonical documents and its proposed folder hierarchy are historical proposals, not acceptance criteria for this pass.
