## 0. Core Claim

Relational Localization is resolution specialized to the assignment subspace.

It narrows a relational field into distinguishable entities, oriented relations, boundary states, and consequence potentials sufficient to license a meta-relational closure.

Core bound:

> A meta-relational closure cannot validly commit more precision than the resolution that feeds it.

Custody, ownership, responsibility, liability, intervention authority, and proxy authority are late P6 closures. They require prior localization.

The companion `Custody - Core Source.md` owns the operational lifecycle after a custodial position arises: scoped authority, bearer capacity, trace, delegation, transfer, breach, return, and discharge. Relational Localization retains ownership of assignment admissibility and re-localization.

## 1. Position in the Source Family

Relational Localization is the broader machinery in the Boundary Ethics / Proxy Localization source family.

Boundary Ethics interfaces with Relational Localization where assignment is not licensed and posture must carry the load.

Proxy Localization is a companion protocol for partial participation where self-account cannot arrive in the assigner's register.

Source family:

```text
Relational Localization: assignment condition
Boundary Ethics: closure-unlicensed posture
Proxy Localization: marked representation under partial participation
```

This source should carry the assignment machinery for the group.

## 2. Extension, Not Addition

Relational Localization is not a new root operation.

Resolution is the general operation: possibility-space narrows into committed actuality. Localization is resolution running in the subspace where the output is assignment-relevant structure.

The source inherits:

- Rayleigh-like thresholds;
- over-resolution, under-resolution, and wrong-axis resolution pathologies;
- observer relativity;
- prior dissolution before valid recombination;
- sign formation as a way of making flow governable.

Localization is a GCO firing on attribution-relevant components of the signal field.

## 3. State and Assignment Quality

The localization state:

```text
L(t) = <D, G, Theta, Chi, W>
```

where:

- D = distinguishability;
- G = geometric-causal placement;
- Theta = relation orientation;
- Chi = boundary coupling state;
- W = consequence-weight profile.

Assignment quality is not part of the state. It is an observer-indexed functional on the state:

```text
Q_O(t) = q(L(t); position, legitimacy, observability, occlusion, observer resolution, attribution window, effective compression)
```

The subscript matters. Two observers may read the same field from different positions and produce different assignment quality.

## 4. Assignment Thresholds

Assignment is admissible only when assignment quality meets the threshold required by the assignment class:

```text
A_x admissible iff Q_O(t) >= Q_min(x)
```

Q_min is not uniform. It rises with:

- stakes;
- irreversibility;
- power asymmetry;
- consequence weight;
- category-shaping capacity;
- entity-constitutive content;
- reduced contestability;
- higher risk of remainder dumping.

Where Q is insufficient, the lawful move is not stronger rhetoric. The lawful moves are:

- slow down;
- coarsen the assignment;
- expand capacity;
- improve observability;
- seek participation;
- preserve contestability;
- classify remainder as unresolved;
- hold Boundary Ethics posture.

## 5. Entity-Constitutive Components

Not all components of a localization state are epistemically alike.

External-resolvable components include position, causal contact, flow, boundary state, and timing. These can often be improved by instrumentation, proximity, and time.

Entity-constitutive components include intent, meaning, identity, interiority, agency, and self-account. These cannot be externally determined without substitution. They must be told, contested, participated in, or marked as unresolved.

The participatory requirement:

> Assignment over what must be told requires the telling.

Where the telling cannot arrive, assignment degrades, posture rises, and proxy stays marked as proxy.

## 6. Laundering and Remainder

The operational formula:

```text
laundering = claimed assignment precision - achieved localization resolution
```

The gap is conserved. It does not vanish because a system writes a clean label. It transfers as remainder to the assigned bearer, the excluded substrate, the future, the lowest-power actor, or the next system in the chain.

This makes Relational Localization a direct partner to Remnant Stewardship:

- Relational Localization detects unsupported assignment precision;
- Remnant Stewardship asks where the unsupported remainder lands and how it is tended.

## 7. Localization Trace

The localization trace is the enforcement mechanism.

It is a durable sign-artifact that records:

- what was assigned;
- what evidence supported the assignment;
- what remained unresolved;
- which observer position produced the localization;
- which proxy inputs entered the closure;
- what contest rights remain available;
- when re-localization is required;
- what would retire or revise the assignment.

The trace makes assignment contestable, auditable, gluable, and retirable.

Without a trace, assignment hardens into category power.

## 8. De-Localization and Stale Assignment

Every resolution has a prior dissolution. Localization inherits that requirement.

When L(t) changes, the old assignment may no longer be valid. If the sign remains after the localization has moved, stale assignment occurs.

Examples:

- records that outlive the condition they describe;
- diagnoses that become identity substitutions;
- reputations that persist after relation changes;
- custody assignments that ignore changed capacity;
- institutional categories that no longer match the field.

Stale assignment is injustice with a valid receipt.

The right to closure and the right to self-determination jointly imply standing to force re-localization when the field has moved.

## 9. Localization Conflict and Gluing

Different positioned observers may localize the same field differently. That does not always mean one is wrong.

Conflict may arise from:

- resolution difference;
- position difference;
- embedding difference;
- category instability;
- power-shaped observability;
- stale signs;
- proxy distortion.

Gluing protocols attempt to produce a shared closure from incompatible localizations without pretending to a view from nowhere.

When no global section exists, lawful outcomes include:

- a coarser shared closure;
- bounded disagreement;
- delayed assignment;
- explicit unresolved remainder.

Fabricated agreement is laundering.

## 10. Adversarial Overlay

Localization is an accountability mechanism, and accountability mechanisms can be gamed.

Attack families:

- occlusion attacks suppress Q to escape assignment;
- seizure attacks force false localization to dump assignment elsewhere.

Counter-rule:

> When low Q is manufactured, the manufacture is the assignable act.

If the underlying harm cannot yet be localized, the production of unassignability may still be localized through coordination cost, beneficiary analysis, pattern inconsistency, and exposure.

## 11. Relationship to Boundary Ethics

Boundary Ethics governs the posture required where assignment closure is not licensed.

Relational Localization says:

> No P6 closure beyond achieved resolution.

Boundary Ethics says:

> Where closure is not licensed, do not convert uncertainty into contempt, projection, seizure, or disposal.

The two are mutually constraining. Relational Localization without Boundary Ethics can become cold assignment logic. Boundary Ethics without Relational Localization can become posture without a licensing test.

## 12. Relationship to Proxy Localization

Proxy Localization extends the partial-participation branch.

Relational Localization supplies the principle:

> assignment degrades, posture rises, proxy stays marked as proxy.

Proxy Localization supplies machinery:

- deficit-location audit;
- proxy typology;
- voice-invertibility;
- guardian discipline;
- withdrawal test;
- interest-model boundaries;
- proxy-capture detection.

Proxy Localization should remain a companion source, not a replacement for the assignment machinery.

## 13. Diagnostics

Use Relational Localization when asking:

- What is being assigned?
- What resolution feeds the assignment?
- Is Q high enough for this assignment class?
- What components can be measured, and what components must be told?
- Is assignment being made sharper than the field supports?
- Is the localization stale?
- Is a proxy marked as proxy?
- Is low Q natural or manufactured?
- What trace preserves contestability?
- Where does unsupported precision transfer as remainder?

## 14. Load-Bearing and Held-Loosely Claims

Load-bearing:

- Relational Localization is resolution in the assignment subspace.
- No P6 closure may validly commit more precision than the resolution that feeds it.
- L(t) and Q_O(t) should remain distinct.
- Assignment thresholds are class-specific.
- Entity-constitutive components require participation, contest, or marked proxy representation.
- Laundering is claimed precision minus achieved localization resolution.
- The localization trace is the enforcement mechanism.
- Stale assignment is a first-class failure mode.

Held loosely:

- The exact five-mode licensing-failure catalog remains provisional.
- Q_min calibration is not yet empirical.
- The gluing formalism may or may not need full sheaf machinery.
- "Localization" is already live in SBF and should be treated as inheritance, not collision.
- The group should proceed as three separate source docs plus later integration work.
