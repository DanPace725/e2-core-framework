# The Practical Guide to Coherence Stewardship

## A Neurodivergent Survival Toolkit for Households, Relationships, and Reality

---

## 🧭 Quick Navigation Principles

**Remember**: You're not managing people. You're managing coherence fields.

1. **You are the condition, not the cause**
2. **Consequences flow naturally when you stop being the dam**
3. **Witness without watching, hold without grasping**
4. **Your coherence is not negotiable**

---

## 🔧 The Daily Practice Toolkit

### Morning Coherence Check

Before engaging with anyone:

**Ask yourself**:

- What's my current coherence level? (1-10)
- What do I need to maintain my field today?
- Where am I tempted to absorb others' consequences?

**If below 5**: Minimal engagement mode. Basic maintenance only.
**If 5-7**: Normal operations with boundaries
**If 8+**: You have surplus for complex interactions

### The 3-Touch Rule for Chaos

When something needs doing (dishes, homework, bills):

1. **First touch**: Signal availability ("The dishes need doing")
2. **Second touch**: State natural consequence ("We'll run out of plates")
3. **Third touch**: Implement consequence (paper plates, natural hunger, etc.)

You touched it three times. That's enough. Let reality teach.

---

## 🛡️ Boundary Protocols for Common Scenarios

### When Someone Is Melting Down

**DON'T**:

- Rush in to fix
- Take on their emotional state
- Explain why they shouldn't feel that way

**DO**:

- Maintain physical presence at safe distance
- State once: "I'm here when you're ready"
- Hold your own coherence like a tuning fork

**The Physics**: You're offering a stable frequency they can match when ready, not forcing them to tune.

### When Tasks Aren't Getting Done

**Traditional**: Nag → Fight → Do it yourself → Resentment
**Coherence Model**: Signal → Natural consequence → Let reality teach

**Example**:

- Laundry not done? They wear dirty clothes
- Homework not done? They face teacher/grade consequences
- Room messy? Door stays closed, no complaints about lost items

**Your job**: Not to prevent their consequences but to not amplify them with your emotional reaction.

### When Someone Wants Immediate Response

**The Pause Protocol**:

1. "I need to process this"
2. Give timeframe: "I'll have thoughts by [specific time]"
3. Actually take that time
4. Respond from coherence, not pressure

**Key**: They can feel urgent. You don't have to match it.

### Green Light (Coherence 8-10)

- Engage with others' emotions

### Yellow Light (Coherence 5-7)

- Maintenance mode
- Clear, simple boundaries

### Red Light (Coherence Below 5)

- Survival mode activated
- Minimum viable interaction
- All consequences return to sender
- Focus only on your coherence

## 🔄 The Pressure Redistribution Method

**When you feel overwhelmed by others' chaos**:

1. **Identify**: What consequence am I holding that isn't mine?
2. **Locate**: Where does this pressure naturally belong?
3. **Release**: Stop being the shock absorber
4. **Allow**: Let the pressure find its natural level

**This looks like**:

- Not reminding adults of their appointments
- Not mediating others' conflicts
- Not fixing problems you didn't create
- Not softening natural consequences

## 🏠 Household Coherence Architecture

### Daily Minimums

These maintain basic coherence:

- One meal happens (doesn't matter which)
- Everyone sleeps somewhere safe
- Basic hygiene occurs (define "basic" loosely)
- Medication/essential needs met

Everything else is bonus.

### Weekly Coherence Rituals

Pick ONE that works:

- Sunday Reset (everyone handles their zone)
- Midweek Check-in (5 minutes max)
- Friday Celebration of Survival
- Saturday Chaos Amnesty

### Monthly System Review

- What consequences am I still absorbing?
- Where can I pull back more?
- What's working? What's not?
- Adjust protocols accordingly

## 💬 Communication Templates

### Setting a Boundary

"I notice [observation]. I need [specific thing]. When [timeframe], I'll [specific action]."

Example: "I notice the dishes are piling up. I need clean plates for dinner. At 5pm, I'll use paper plates for myself."

### Declining Emotional Labor

"I care about this AND I don't have capacity right now. Let's revisit when [specific time]."

### Returning Consequences

"That sounds difficult. What are you planning to do about it?"

### Witnessing Without Fixing

"I see you're struggling. I'm here, not going anywhere. Let me know when you're ready."
