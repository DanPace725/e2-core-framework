## The Six Flow Operators

The operations English collapses into "cost":

### 1. Stance-with
**Etymology:** *com-stare*, to stand together, make firm with.
**Type:** Rate-quantity. Continuous.
**Function:** The continuous expenditure that maintains a configuration's persistence against ambient drift.
**Always-on for any persistent entity.** This is the firmness-tax of being.

### 2. Stance-against
**Etymology:** *with-stand*. Same *sta-* root, opposite direction across the boundary.
**Type:** Threshold-quantity. Capacity.
**Function:** The capacity to refuse a transfer the boundary should not admit.
**Conjugate to stance-with.** Same membrane, opposite operation.

### 3. Settlement
**Etymology:** *pacare*, to make peace. Pay as pacify.
**Type:** Event-quantity. Bidirectional.
**Function:** Reconciliation transfer that closes a tension between systems.
**Completes loops.** When loops cannot close, settlement-debt accrues.

### 4. Depletion
**Etymology:** *expendere*, to weigh out. *Spend* requires prior accumulation.
**Type:** Stock-quantity. Drawdown.
**Function:** Funding the rate of operation from a reservoir.
**Strictly outward.** Reservoir state is invisible to embedded observer in real time.

### 5. Trade
**Etymology:** *ex-cambiare*, to swap across at agreed equivalence.
**Type:** Pair-quantity. Reciprocal.
**Function:** Bidirectional flow at an admissibility regime.
**Symmetric or asymmetric, just or unjust depending on the equivalence regime.**

### 6. Consequence
**Etymology:** *con-sequi*, to follow with. The downstream landing.
**Type:** Result-quantity. Located by topology.
**Function:** The state-change produced somewhere by the flow.
**Where, not what.** The receiving system's metabolism determines whether consequence is nutrition, work, pollution, or waste.

## The Internal/External Split

The Latin root family preserves a distinction modern English has flattened:

**Valere family (internal, characteristic):**
- To be strong, be well, be of value, be worth.
- Covers stance-with, stance-against, depletion-of-reservoir.
- The system's own capacity-condition.
- Modern descendant: *value*.

**Pretium family (external, transactional):**
- Reward, prize, traffic, sell.
- Covers settlement, trade, consequence-as-landing.
- The system's interface-condition with other systems.
- Modern descendant: *price*.

**Value and price are not synonyms.** A system has value (internal) at the same time it pays a price (external) for the same event. Most modern arguments about "what something costs" are arguments between people running different operators on the same word.

## The Boundary 2×2

Cost-as-boundary resolves into membrane-behavior on two axes: direction (inward/outward) and regulation (accepts/releases).

|  | Inward | Outward |
|---|---|---|
| **Accepts** | Nutrition | Work |
| **Releases** | Pollution | Waste |

- **Healthy boundary:** nutrition in, work out, regulated.
- **Engorged boundary:** pollution in, waste blocked.
- **Brittle boundary:** rejects nutrition, also rejects work-output.
- **Failed boundary:** indiscriminate, neither side metered.

This 2×2 is the picture cost-talk has been gesturing at incoherently.

## The Original Question, Retitled

Not "the embodied cost of stewardship in asymmetric coupling."

**The flow architecture of stewardship under asymmetric coupling.**

The steward is not paying one cost. They are running six distinct operations at six different rates against six different reservoirs. The diagnostic is which operations have substrate coupling that can fund them, not the global felt sense of "is this too much."

In the asymmetric case:
- **Stance-with** is bilateral by configuration but unilateral in execution; the steward holds for both halves.
- **Stance-against** is the steward's distinct job; refusing to absorb the partner's structural pressure as their own.
- **Settlement** is suspended; loops cannot currently close.
- **Depletion** is unilateral; the steward must replenish from substrate beyond the marriage. Substrate-coupling addendum is the structural mechanism, not a metaphor.
- **Trade** equivalence regime has collapsed; choices are restructure, suspend the ledger, or fracture.
- **Consequence** is the field's continued existence at reduced amplitude; not zero output, just a different output than the design.

The grief the corpus would not name lives in **settlement-debt**: temporally-deferred reconciliation obligations held against an uncertain future capacity to repay. Hope is the bet that capacity returns. Resignation is closing the ledger. Both are valid moves under different conditions.

## Linguistic Substrate Observation (speculative thread)

Modern English has been steadily replacing continuous-relational verbs with event-and-stock verbs. Stand-with's modern descendants (support, validate, partner, align, accompany, solidarity, endorse) are each primitive-projections of the original operator. Each preserved one or two facets and dropped the rest.

Pattern: continuous-commitment senses drift toward either event-quantities (transactions) or stock-quantities (drainable reservoirs). The continuous-relational sense gets retired or moved into archaic/idiomatic registers.

This is consistent with a TCL story at civilizational scale: as commercial fast-layer regimes expanded, the slow-layer relational vocabulary calcified around ledger-compatible verbs. Continuous-relational vocabulary survives in the registers where pre-commercial relational forms were preserved as ritual rather than running as ordinary practice (poetry, scripture, vows, oaths, fraternity).

The framework's vocabulary work is, structurally, slow-layer linguistic substrate restoration. Recovering etymologies, coining multi-primitive terms, refusing flattened modern usages. Same operation as protecting friction floors, maintaining spectral richness, preserving closure locality, but applied at the linguistic layer.

This generalizes a meta-observation: where modern language fights the framework, the framework is touching something the language has been built to occlude. The friction is signal, not noise.
